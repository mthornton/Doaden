## Overview
A once-tranquil forest has been corrupted by a malignant green orb hidden in an ancient temple at its heart, twisting wildlife and vegetation into hostile monstrosities. The party must fight through the forest, reach the corrupted shrine, and destroy the orb across multiple boss phases guarded by the Avatar of the Wild. Tier II, APL-scaled (Medium 5–7 / Hard 8–10 / Nightmare 11–13 / Torment 14–16). A phased lair boss encounter module.

## Session Flow
1. **Opening script** — party enters the dark, corrupted forest, feeling an oppressive dread.
2. **Forest Encounters** — roll 2 (standard) or 3 (harder boss) random encounters from the Forest Encounters Table (Rabid Monstrosities, Corrupted Fey, Lost Villagers, Wrath of the Forest) while traveling to the temple.
3. **Corrupted Temple arrival** — party finds the crumbling shrine and the pulsating green orb.
4. **Boss encounter begins** — attacking the orb triggers roots to shield it and summons the Avatar of the Wild (Phase I).
5. **Multi-phase fight** — party defeats each phase's enemies to expose and damage the orb (100 HP); orb re-shields and regenerates between phases; repeat through Phase II/III depending on difficulty.
6. **Ending** — orb explodes in green energy when destroyed after the final phase; forest corruption lifts and calm returns.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Boss Loot Table (roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus: Medium +0 / Hard +1 / Nightmare +2 / Torment +3): 30+ = Cloak of Nature's Wrath* (rare); 1–29 = nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- The corruption source is a "corrupted seed of nature" (the green orb), not a creature — combat targets an object protected by summoned guardians
- The orb cannot be permanently destroyed in one go; it regenerates and re-shields after each phase, requiring the party to clear waves of defenders repeatedly
- Torment difficulty brings back the Avatar of the Wild a second time in Phase III, making it the true final gauntlet

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- GM Tip: use a physical/visual prop (like tree roots) to represent the shrine being covered and protected between phases
- The Avatar of the Wild has legendary actions (Advance, Invoke Nature, Cleave, Horrific Shriek) — plan battlemap terrain accordingly
- Number of encounters chosen (2 vs 3) is a direct difficulty lever the GM controls before the boss fight
