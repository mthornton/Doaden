## Overview
The party is ambushed and knocked unconscious on the road, waking up imprisoned in the pitch-dark, labyrinthine sewer complex beneath the isolated keep of Lord Nachzehrer — an exiled, obsessive vampire lord who breeds twisted "Abhorrent" bloodsucking monstrosities from abducted humans. To escape, they must repair a machine that floods the cistern and raises them to the surface, hunting down its three missing components through monster-infested sewer depths before facing the lord's caged Bloodsucker Dragon in a final battle.

## Key Locations
- The Sewers (Map A/B) — labyrinthine, pervasive-magical-darkness tunnels beneath the keep; light sources capped to 10 ft. bright/10 ft. dim.
- The Cistern (Map C) — large flooded chamber with the main escape machine; an anti-magic field from the lab above blocks all spells/magic here.
- Pumping Chamber & Monster Lair (Maps D/E) — sewer depths housing a Cryptstalker and a Bloodghast's nest.
- Noxious Tunnel (Map F) — gas-filled nest area for Bloodhounds/Bloodfiends; DC 16 CON save vs. poison on entry.
- The Junction (Map G) — enormous 180 ft.-high chasm-island chamber where all sewage converges; lair of the Bloodsucker Dragon and site of the final component.
- Lord's Laboratory (Map H) — lower lab above the cistern; exit route to the surface, filled with experiment corpses.

## Session Flow
1. **Ch.1 – Trapped:** Party wakes in a sewer jail cell with no memory of capture; escapes cell; navigates Map B (Sewer Corridor) fighting Bloodfiends; finds the Copper Lever; reaches Map C (The Cistern), an anti-magic zone, and attempts to power the escape machine — it fails, missing further parts.
2. **Ch.2 – Sewer Depths:** Party descends into the depths via Map D (Pumping Chamber, Cryptstalker kills a Crusader on sight), explores Map E (Monster Lair, Bloodghast) and Map F (Noxious Tunnel, gas + Bloodhound/Bloodfiends), collecting Copper Gears and clues toward the Electrical Charger.
3. **Ch.3 – Down the Drain:** Party is swept down a storm drain into Map G (The Junction), an island in a vast chasm; retrieves the Electrical Charger from the ruin machine; boss fight vs. the Bloodsucker Dragon.
4. **Epilogue:** With all parts collected, the cistern machine powers up and raises the party to Lord's Laboratory (Map H); a reanimated Brute Fiend attacks en route; party exits through the trapdoor to the surface, overlooking the Rivenwald woods.

## Secrets & Twists
- The party's memory of the two days before capture has been wiped — implying more happened during the ambush than they realize (open hook for the GM to develop).
- The entire sewer complex sits beneath, and was engineered as part of, Lord Nachzehrer's still-functioning "lost age" laboratory-fortress — a relic of lost technology/architecture rarely seen in Doaden.
- The Bloodsucker Dragon cannot be permanently killed while its still-beating heart remains hidden and chained within Nachzehrer's keep — defeating it in the sewers is only a temporary victory.

## Faction Notes (The Abhorrents)
- The Abhorrents are Lord Nachzehrer's degenerate vampire-bloodline creations, made by injecting abducted humans with a bestial, "impure" strain of vampiric blood mixed with genetic experimentation — unlike other vampires, they crave fresh flesh, not just blood.
- Other vampire bloodlines (the Sanguine Court and kin) shun Nachzehrer for his deviancy but still rely on and use his creations, making the relationship an uneasy, tenuous political alliance.
- The bloodline hierarchy runs from mindless Bloodfiends/Bloodsuckers (made from Bloodhound blood, used as fodder/guards) up through Brute Fiends, tamed Cryptstalkers, the Abhorrent Mage (dark researcher), and Bloodghasts (mutated, crawling horrors), all serving Nachzehrer.
- Prison Wretches are abducted humans (villagers, merchants, adventurers) used as ongoing experimental subjects in the lab — useful as pitiable NPCs or additional rescue/side-quest hooks.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- The Bloodsucker Dragon's heart (hidden in Nachzehrer's tower study) is a clear sequel hook — killing the dragon again is meaningless until the heart is found and destroyed.
- Lord Nachzehrer himself is never directly fought in this adventure — he remains an off-screen, escalating threat for a future session (see Vampire Hunt expansion idea).
- Consider whether any Prison Wretches encountered should be rescuable rather than just background dressing, per the "Taking It Further" guidance.
