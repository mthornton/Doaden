**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–10 (Normal 5–7, Torment 8–10)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The party is ambushed in the night and wakes at the bottom of a three-level infested mine near the town of New Heldon. To escape they must reach and operate an old mining elevator while fighting through the Remade — a dark-elf cult practicing forbidden flesh-shaping — and the monstrous spider-hybrids they've bred. Blocking the only way out is the Broodmother, the cult's twisted former queen. Survival hinges on an uneasy alliance with the Fleshsmith, the Remade's mother-and-maker, who will trade her blood (and a fighting chance) for the Broodmother's death.

## Key Locations
- Infested Mine — three connected levels linked by a single elevator shaft; the only way to the surface
- Upper Level — Remade dark-elf living quarters and Ceremonial Chamber; bodies are dropped down a hole here to the Middle Level
- Middle Level — transfer/dumping ground where the party wakes (Body Pit); minor cave-spider nest
- Lower Level (Spider's Den) — heart of the Fleshsmith's nest: Birthing Chambers, Feeding Ground, Fleshsmith's Throne, master elevator control
- New Heldon — nearest town, an hour's walk from the mine entrance; starting/ending hub (shares resources with the Indictum Dominus module)

## Session Flow
1. **Ch. 1 — A Revival in Darkness:** Party wakes in the Body Pit (A1), meets Grik, crosses the spider-webbed tunnels, reaches the elevator (A6/A7), and is ambushed rising toward the surface — first Broodmother appearance, then a forced plunge back down.
2. **Ch. 2 — The Cult of the Spider:** Elevator crashes into the Upper Level (locked in place). Party fights through Remade living quarters (C2/C3) to the Ceremonial Chamber (C4), then descends by rope through the Sacrifice Pit to the Lower Level since the local elevator console is destroyed.
3. **Ch. 3 — The Spider's Den:** Party navigates Acid Cocoons and the Birthing Chambers to reach the Fleshsmith's Throne (D5) — negotiate or fight for her blood and unlock the master elevator console — then survives the Final Ascent boss fight against the Broodmother.
4. **Epilogue:** Elevator reaches the surface near New Heldon; Grik succumbs to his wound moments after escaping.

## Secrets & Twists
- The Fleshsmith was once the Broodmother's priestess and aide before their falling out
- The Broodmother is literally the original Remade hive-queen, reshaped past sanity into a predator that now hunts her own children
- Deaths mean little to the Remade while the Fleshsmith lives — she can stitch the fallen back together; killing her instead ends the Remade line
- Grik hides how badly he's hurt during the final fight until the party is already back in daylight

## Faction Notes (The Remade)
- The Remade are dark-elf cultists who turned to forbidden flesh-shaping after fearing extinction; the Fleshsmith stitches the fallen back together using spider and humanoid parts
- Repeated reshaping corrodes mind and body over generations: relatively intact Dark Elf Cultists/Warriors → shambling Necro Servitors/Warriors → fully monstrous elites (Driders, Swollen, Fleshsmith's Daughters)
- Those who lose all identity to the process become "Necro Spiders" and turn on the Remade who made them
- Drinking the Fleshsmith's blood grants superhuman agility/reflexes — the Remade's only edge against the Broodmother's acid attacks

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Only the Lower Level's master console can unlock elevator locks on all three levels
- Whether the party allies with or kills the Fleshsmith permanently changes Remade behavior for the rest of the scenario
- New Heldon is shared with the Indictum Dominus (Penitent Crusade I) module — reuse that town map/resources for post-adventure downtime
