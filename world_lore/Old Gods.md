# Old Gods

The Old Gods are not really gods.  They are very powerful beings with an unknown origin.  They have been around as far back as history started.  They are not immortal, but close.  They live as long as they have mana and they must willingly use mana to perform magic. 
 
Mana is accumulated by the act of a follower worshiping them.  The more devote the more mana is generated.  However, the amount of mana a single person generates is miniscule.  A god need tens of thousands worshiping them in earnest to be a real power. 
 
Think of mana as psychic power.  The old gods are beings that feed on the emotions directed towards them.  Some believe they are decedents of psychic vampires. 
 
As the god were forgotten, many went into hibernation conserving there little remaining mana.  Some were killed by being forced to exhaust their remaining mana. 
 
But some found a way to survive.  By keeping the festivals associated with them active they are able to get a sizable mana bump once a year.  For example, Saint Nickolas would get a nice bump in mana each Christmas.  The worship is not really in earnest but it is worship in a light way but celebrated by most of the population.  
 
The old gods almost never make themselves public.  Where they to do so the Church of Azeria would outlaw their festival and stave them.  The Church is very aware of the situation. 
 
The Church does not simply ban all festivals because they festivals are ingrained into the culture.  The Church does subtly interject the beliefs of the one true god into the holidays.  With each passing decade the old gods gain a little less power because of this.  In another century there will be none left. 
 
Age of Conflict 
 
A millennia ago, the old gods had real power and were in open conflict with each other as each looked for a way to increase their followers.  Most choose to specialize in an area to attract followers.  At times the old gods would go to war with each other sending their follower into battle.  The outcome was rarely decisive as that none could risk losing too many followers.  Less followers meant less power which would make them more vulnerable. 
 
What they could do was play a political and subtle game.  Find ways to discredit another god and pursued followers to change allegiance. 
 
Another popular method was to have a strong god move directly against a weaker god.  Usually backstabbing and assignation was involved.  Once a god was weaken, a strong god could imprison the weak god they be free to convert their followers.
