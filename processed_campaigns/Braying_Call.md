**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–13 (Normal/Torment/Damnation scaling; Encounter 3 boss is Damnation-only, level 13+)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The party investigates Essenvald, an isolated village in the southern Black Forest whose people have descended into madness, secretly worshipping a sinister goat-headed entity that "brays" from deep in the woods. After confronting the Village Hag at the corruption's heart, the party must hunt a demonic Bies through the misty forest, and optionally face the demon-goddess Baphomet herself in an optional high-level boss encounter.

## Key Locations
- Essenvald — isolated village in the southern Black Forest; permanently shrouded in obscuring mist (100 ft. visibility), oil barrels scattered as environmental hazards.
- Old Windmill (A2) — 3-story structure; Crazed Scyther on top floor; clue letter on the bed.
- Rundown Cottage (A3) — boarded hovel; human remains and a child's doll hidden inside.
- Village Square (A4) — central bonfire, runestone, and dead tree; Mad Woodcutters dance in a trance; touching the runestone grants a vivid vision of Baphomet's birthing ritual.
- Old Chapel (A5) — Village Hag and Mendicant Friar confrontation site; door locks and traps the party in for the fight.
- Ruined Barn (A6) — Old Blacksmith's lair; dark, decaying interior.
- Village Hag's Hut (A7) — locked hut with symbols matching the runestone; hag is absent (she's in A5).
- Forest Path (A8) — leads into Encounter 2's Bleating Woods.
- Bleating Woods (Map B) — magically misted forest (30 ft. visibility); Kikimora's Den (B2), Forest Overlook with Chort (B3), Hanging Tree/glowing runestone (B4), Bies' Feeding Ground (B5).
- Baphomet's Lair (Map C) — optional pocket-dimension arena reached by touching the glowing runestone after Encounter 2; three demon altars on destructible rock formations.

## Session Flow
1. **Encounter 1 – Essenvald:** Party arrives to find villagers trance-dancing around a bonfire; free-form investigation of the village (windmill, cottage, square, chapel, barn, hag's hut, forest path); hostility escalates with each provocation; culminates in a locked-door boss fight against the Village Hag and Mendicant Friar in the chapel.
2. **Encounter 2 – Monster Hunt:** Village Hag's death triggers a braying roar and a mob of frenzied villagers pounding on the chapel door (breaks in 1 round); party flees/fights into the misted Bleating Woods and hunts the Bies via its bleating calls, encountering the Kikimora den and the Chort at the overlook along the way; ends when the Bies is killed.
3. **Encounter 3 – Baphomet Calls (optional):** If used, the party is drawn back to the glowing runestone at the Hanging Tree, which teleports them to Baphomet's pocket-dimension lair for a high-difficulty boss fight; ending the fight returns them to the forest with the runestone destroyed and Baphomet's corpse vanished.
4. **Wrap-up:** Return to Essenvald; if surviving villagers remain, the GM/party decide whether to purge them (no cure exists); Crusade-sanctioned campaigns grant Penitent Rewards (years off sentence + gold) for successful completion.

## Secrets & Twists
- The village's "olden ways" worship connects directly to Baphomet, the demon mother-goddess — the runestone vision in A4 reveals her birthing ritual and the village's true patron.
- The Mendicant Friar, sent originally to bring the Azerai faith to Essenvald, was himself corrupted by the same forest evil the villagers worship — a cautionary inversion of his mission.
- The Bies hunting the party in Encounter 2 is simultaneously being hunted by them — a mutual predator/prey dynamic driven entirely by noise, not sight.
- Baphomet periodically "gives birth" to Chorts and Bies as living extensions of her corruption — implying the Chort and Bies encountered in this adventure may literally be her offspring.

## Faction Notes (Dark Forest / Beastmen)
- The Black Forest is ancient and vast, stretching from the Holy Empire to the northern realms of Thule, with trees thousands of years old; old folk traditions hold that ancient nature spirits still inhabit its unexplored depths.
- Corruption spreads like an infection through isolated communities: villagers become paranoid, fearful, and eventually devoted to depraved rituals and sacrifice in service to whatever dark entity (here, Baphomet) has taken root nearby.
- Baphomet is an immortal elder demon predating human civilization, holding dominion over nature and fertility; she is in a constant state of monstrous "birthing," and her mere presence taints earth, water, air, plants, and animal life around her lair.
- Her demonic offspring — Chorts (virile goat-demon consorts) and Bies (solitary, ravenous hunters with near-impenetrable hides) — are worshipped by the corrupted as symbols of her favor and depravity.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Decide up front whether Encounter 3 (Baphomet) is in play for your table — it explicitly requires a level 13+/Damnation-tier party and is otherwise wildly out of balance.
- The "Purging the Villagers" section has no mechanical cure for corrupted NPCs — if the party wants a merciful alternative, that's homebrew territory, not RAW for this adventure.
- "Taking It Further" suggests using the Black Forest itself as a recurring regional backdrop for other threats (beastmen, witches, tree-spirits, goblinoids) — good hook bank for connecting this one-shot into a larger northern Doaden campaign.
