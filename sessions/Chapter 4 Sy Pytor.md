# Chapter 4 Sy Pytor

## A1: Front door 
As you approach the entrance a sense of unease begins to grow.  Maybe you are just nervous? 
 
Large wooden double doors.  Nobody answers the door.  If checked, it is unlocked. 
 
## A2: Vestibule 
The room smells of old wood an incense.  Under those odors is a light scent of rot.  The room is decorated with tapestries and paintings of long dead Saints and monks.  In the center of the room is a life sized bronze statue of a man in religious garb.  A placard at the base of the statue says "In dedication to Saint Pyotr, healer of madness and consecrator of the wretched." 
 
## A3: Hall 
Dark hall lit only by small windows.  More paintings of Saints and monks.  Your footsteps echo in hall. 
 
Perception check: 
10+ you hear a faint whimpering that stops shortly after it started. 
 
## A4: Dining Room 
As you open the door you are assaulted by the stench of rotting food.  This is a dining room with a large central table.  The table is set with plates and cutlery.  Food is still on the plates desiccated and rotted.  Rats scurry off the table and dash to the dark corners as you enter the room. 
 
Perception check: 
10+ The food is 2-3 months old based on the amount of rot and mold.  It appears something happened suddenly to interrupt the normal cadence of life in the sanitorium. 
 
## A5: Pantry 
Room to hold food supplied.  The supplied looked spoiled and have been ravaged by rats.  There is a staircase leading down. 
 
Perception check to search the supplies 
15+ Silverware that is actual silver (value: 2d6 silver pieces) 
10+ healers kit, 2 potions of healing 
5+ 1d10 days' worth of rations 
 
## A6: Kitchen 
Opening the door the smell of rotting food fills you noes.  The sound of rat scurrying across the floor can be heard before everything goes silent.  The kitchen is a chaotic mess.  Pot, pans, and cooking utensils are strewn about the tables and floor.  Mold covered flour covers one table.  Blood is spattered across the walls, tables, and floor.   
 
Behing the table is a corpse.  What looks to have been a heavy set man in a priest's working robe lies on the floor with a blood crusted knife in his ridged hand.  His stomach is distended and pulsing with gore bubbling from wounds.  His abdomen suddenly bursts open in a spray of gore and several gore coated rats burrow out and scurry for dark corners. 
 
All make a DC 10 Wisdom roll.  Failed rolls lose one point of sanity. 
 
Investigation check 
10+ several deep slashes on the body from a bladed weapon.  The slash across the throat is the likely killing blow. 
 
## A7: Garden 
An outdoor decorative garden.  It was probably nice at one point.  The flowers and plants are all dead.  Only the occasional weed grows.  Looking up, dark clouds are coalescing.  Tendrils of dark swirling fog a stretching down towards the sanitorium. 
 
There is a trap door on the edge of the building.  The door is a metal grate with a lock.  The opening is 2x2 and drops straight down.  No visible way to get down other that jump in.  It is too dark to see how deep or what lies at the bottom.  When the party tried to look, they are overwhelmed by the stench of rotting meat. 
 
The lock can be picked with a DC 15Slight of Hands check. 
 
In the garden are 3 cultists, 1 bruisers, 1 cage hound. 
 
## A8: Chapel 
 
You unlock the heavy door and push it open.  A black mist hangs in the air.  Heavier than what you have seen so far.  About the consistency of a light morning fog.  The smell is sour. 
 
(Take 1d4 necrotic damage each round.  Cover your nose and mouth and reduce to –2+1d4 damage.) 
 
The room is large chapel.  Lit torches line the walls.  Empty pews fill the first three quarters of the room.  At the front of the room is a stone alter.  On the Alter is a bound person with open wounds.  You can't tell if the person is living or dead. 
 
Beside the alter is a stone circle about three feet in diameter and three feet off the floor.  Black particles float out of the opening seemingly to be the source of the black fog in the room.  You can't see inside the circle from where you stand. 
 
Behind the alter stands a very tall very pale man wearing an ornate headdress.  He is chanting in a deep guttural tones.  He does not react to your entering the room. 
 
A group of 5 cultists see you and advance forward.  The leader of the group stop and address you.  "How dare you interrupt.  I can smell the stink of Saraswati on you.  Are you hear of revenge?  Upset she was out played by Aldephiaga?  Fools.  You know nothing of the Old Gods.  But don’t worry, Brudda Death will tutor you.  Books will be written on the suffering you will experience."

## A9: South bell tower 
 
Tower is 70 feet tall containing a belfry.  There is a wooden staircase leading up, but it has collapsed making it impossible to ascend. 
 
If they enter this before going upstairs: 
 
Stepping in the southern tower, you look up to see a large bell above.  The tower's wooden stairway has collapsed, making any attempt to ascend impossible.  Hearing a commotion above, you quickly duck behind a pile of loose rubble and hide yourself from view.  Two cultists drag a man in priest robes to the edge of the upper platform.  As the priest begs for his life, one cultist throws a noose around his neck.  The other end is tied to the bells crossbeam.  The other cultist draws a dagger and slashes the priests belly before kicking the priest off the ledge.  As the noose catches, the priest gurgles, his intestines dropping from his opened belly and splattering into the ground around you.  His body spasms as his life leaks away causing the bell to toll in a series of sharp clangs until the man finally shudders and stills.  Looking up, you see the cultists have gone.

## B7: Ignatius Office 
A journal is on a table.  Ignatious is nailed to the wall.  Search him and a ring of keys is found. 
 
This is the journal of Father Ignatius, head caretaker of St. Pyotr's Sanitorium. The journal describes the daily life during his leadership.  The book is mostly dull tracking patients in and out, logistical operations, and budgeting.  A few pages describe treatments he used on patients.  The early treatments were harsh.  Patients were deprived of food, water, sleep.  Whipping used to "encourage sane behavior".  As the journal goes on, the methods get more extreme over the years.  He comes to believe the insane are infested with spiritual creatures from another realm.  By the end torcher devises are being used.  He believe he can drive out the infestation with pain.  The handwriting degrades as the inhumane treatment increases.  You can feel his sanity slipping with each passage.  Near the end of the journal, he write of being able to feel the barrier between this realm and an unknown realm of evil.  He believe if he thins the barrier enough, the evil spirits infesting his patients will go back once he drives them out.
