Ending the Adventure
When Urvakh is defeated, the prison riot will
end. Depending on the state of the prison, the
bandits and mobs of inmates might choose to
flee or surrender to the guards and the party.
    How the party chooses to handle this
victory is up to them. They can parade
Urvakh's head as a macabre trophy, a grim
symbol of the fate that befalls those who dare
to challenge the Iron Gauntlet. Or they may
decide to shackle the fallen leader in irons and
send him to the inescapable Panopticon to be
held for many years. Either way, for the
bandits, the sight of their leader's defeat will
send a clear message – the rebellion is over.
    In the aftermath, the prison slowly begins
to reclaim its usual rhythm. Guards round up
the remaining rebels, some nursing their
wounds, others bound and awaiting their fate.
The warden, once freed from his captors,
resumes control of the prison. His ordeal has
not broken him, but it has instilled a newfound
respect for the precarity of his position and the
valor of the adventurers who saved him.
    With their mission in the Iron Gauntlet
prison complete, the party can enjoy a well-
deserved respite, knowing they've made a
difference. In the face of anarchy, they've held
the line, and in doing so, they've earned not
just the gratitude of the Iron Gauntlet but also
