# The Bramblefen Freed
### The thirty, and the few among them with a job to do later

> **Replaces the "Eighteen" roster from the Chapter 7 prep (Oct 2026).** None of the prep names except **Anselm** was ever used at the table. Per the DM, only the names that carry a **specific role** in another file are kept; the rest are dropped.

## What is canon *(Session 3)*

- **Thirty survived and were freed.** Eighteen of them fought in the revolt; the rest were too old or too weak and were let out with them — **Gimble opened their cells.**
- **Anselm Vogt** offered the party the service of all thirty. They **cannot go to Solteres** — half are not fully human, and there that is a legal status.
- **They were sent to Ald's Star, to say Wolfram sent them.** Erwana went ahead of them in Session 1 with the same instruction.
- Wolfram handed out Vaskar's coin to them in the yard.

**Leader:** **Anselm Vogt** *(own file, `Anselm Vogt.md`)*.
