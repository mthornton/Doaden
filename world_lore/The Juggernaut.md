# The Juggernaut

**Type:** NPC — enslaved pit-fighter (Large humanoid, human). Owned by **Lord Leofard Ironwake.**
**Base chassis:** Level 5 Barbarian (Rage, Reckless Attack, Extra Attack, Fast Movement, Unarmored Defense, Danger Sense).
**Design goal:** a boss you *survive*, not out-damage. He is overwhelming for ~4 rounds, then burns himself out. His trusted dwarf handler is the off-switch.

> **Names (rename freely):** the pits bill him as **"The Juggernaut."** His handler, the dwarf **Durgan Half-Ear**, calls him **"Mott."** Mott doesn't know his own birth name.

---

## Concept & roleplay

Mott is a man grown three times the size of a normal one — a quirk of birth that made him a monster in the eyes of buyers and a fortune in the eyes of Ironwake. His mind never grew with his body; he has the temper and tenderness of a small child in a body that can tear a door off its hinges. **Calm, he is gentle**: he likes warm bread, shiny things, and Durgan's voice. **Provoked, he is a catastrophe** — he rages and strikes the nearest living thing, unable to tell friend from foe, until his own body gives out.

**Durgan Half-Ear**, a fellow slave, is the only person Mott trusts and the only one who can steer or stop him. Durgan protects Mott like a son; Mott would never raise a hand against him. Ironwake keeps them together precisely because Durgan is the leash — and knows that killing the dwarf would turn his prize weapon into an uncontrollable disaster.

Play Mott as tragic, not villainous. He is a victim who happens to be a wrecking ball.

## Stat block — The Juggernaut ("Mott")

*Large humanoid (human), unaligned — CR 5 (1,800 XP) as a raging set-piece*

- **Armor Class** 14 (Unarmored Defense)
- **Hit Points** 90 (12d10 + 24)
- **Speed** 40 ft. (see the Rage Clock for reductions)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (+5) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 6 (−2) | 8 (−1) | 6 (−2) |

- **Saving Throws** STR +8, CON +7
- **Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +1
- **Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing **(only while in Full Fury — see Rage Clock)**
- **Senses** passive Perception 9
- **Languages** understands simple Common; speaks only a few words and Durgan's commands
- **Proficiency Bonus** +3

**Traits**

- **Unarmored Defense.** His AC includes his Constitution (already in the 14 above).
- **Reckless.** While raging, he can attack with advantage; attack rolls against him also have advantage. He almost always does this.
- **Danger Sense.** Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects he can see (traps, breath weapons, spells).
- **Simple Mind.** He automatically fails Intelligence checks and can't be swayed by logic, bribery, or complex commands. He *can* be reached through simple emotion — fear, kindness, and above all Durgan's voice (see **Bonded to Durgan**).
- **Bonded to Durgan.** Mott will **never** willingly attack Durgan, and never targets him even while raging. If Mott can see **and** hear Durgan, Durgan can spend an action to **calm** him, instantly ending the rage (no roll — this is trust, not magic). This is impossible during a **Grief-Rage** (below).

**Actions**

- **Multiattack (Full Fury only).** Mott makes two Slam attacks.
- **Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d8 + 7 bludgeoning while raging (2d8 + 5 when not). On a hit against a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength save or be knocked prone.
- **Ground Slam (Recharge 5–6, Full Fury only).** Mott smashes the earth. Each creature within 10 ft. must make a DC 16 Strength save, taking 2d10 bludgeoning and falling prone on a failure (half damage, no prone on a success). *This hits his own side too — he does not aim it.*
- **Fling (optional, in place of one Slam).** One Large or smaller creature within 10 ft. must succeed on a DC 16 Strength save or be grabbed (escape DC 16). On a later turn he can hurl a grabbed creature up to 20 ft.: the thrown creature takes 2d6 bludgeoning + falls prone, and anything it lands on makes a DC 16 Dex save or takes 2d6 and falls prone.

## ★ The Rage Clock (the core mechanic)

**Trigger.** Mott rages when: combat begins and he's ordered to fight, he takes damage, or Durgan is threatened or hurt. **While raging he uses his action each turn to move to and attack the *nearest creature he can reach* — ally or enemy alike** (never Durgan). He can't be calmed except by Durgan.

**Track the rounds since the rage began.** He burns out on a fixed timer:

| Phase | Rounds | What he can do | Drawbacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full Fury** | 1–4 | Multiattack (2 slams), Ground Slam, Fling; +2 rage damage; **resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing**; advantage on STR checks & saves; Reckless (adv. to hit); speed 40 | Attacks the nearest creature indiscriminately; can't be reasoned with |
| **Flagging** (~50%) | 5–6 | **One Slam only** (no Multiattack, no Ground Slam) | **Loses physical resistance**; loses the +2 rage damage (2d8 + 5); **disadvantage** on attack rolls and STR checks/saves; speed halved to **20** |
| **Burned Out** | 7+ | Nothing but breathe | Rage ends. He is **prone, incapacitated, speed 0**, gains **2 levels of exhaustion**, and stays down **1d4 rounds** (or until Durgan rouses him / he takes a short rest). Attacks against him have advantage; melee hits within 5 ft. are **critical hits**. Easy to bind, drag, or subdue |

**Reading the design:** a level-3 party *cannot* burn through 90 HP + resistance in four rounds — and they're not supposed to. The fight is a survival puzzle with three solutions: **outlast him** (delay/kite for 4–6 rounds and he collapses), **reach Durgan** (free or persuade the dwarf and he shuts Mott off), or **use the room** (chains, a portcullis, a pit ledge to keep him 10 ft. away while the clock runs). When he reaches Flagging he becomes killable if the party wants blood; at Burned Out he can be captured alive.

**Recovery & repeat use.**
- The clock counts consecutive rounds of rage. If Durgan calms him or he's knocked out early, the rage ends and the clock resets once he recovers.
- Each rage wrecks him. He can't rage again until he finishes a **short rest**, and a **second rage the same day starts already Flagging** (rounds 5–6 rules from round 1). A **third** the same day only reaches Burned Out. He is a weapon Ironwake is slowly using up.

**★ Grief-Rage (if Durgan is killed or gravely harmed).** Mott rages instantly and **cannot be calmed by anyone.** His Full Fury lasts **6 rounds** instead of 4 (Flagging 7–8, Burned Out 9+), and he single-mindedly hunts whoever struck Durgan. This is the doomsday scenario — including for Ironwake's own people.

## Durgan Half-Ear — the handler

*Medium humanoid (dwarf), the leash and the heart of the pair.* Use a **Commoner** or **Scout** stat block; he's no fighter. What matters is the bond, not his sword arm.

- **Calm the Beast (action).** If Mott can see and hear Durgan, Durgan ends Mott's rage. Automatic — except during Grief-Rage.
- **Rouse (action).** Durgan can deliberately provoke Mott into rage. Ironwake forces him to do this before fights; Durgan hates it.
- **The off-switch cuts both ways.** Silence Durgan, blind Mott, move Durgan out of sight/earshot, or cage him — and *no one* can stop Mott until the clock runs out. Ironwake's guards know to protect the dwarf; clever players will realize they can win the fight by **getting to Durgan**, not by killing Mott.
- **Loyalty.** Durgan will do almost anything to keep Mott safe and fed. Threaten Mott and he folds; offer them both a way out of slavery and he is yours for life.

## Using him in play

- **Ironwake's pit champion.** The obvious use: Mott is Ironwake's fighter in the Solteres pits — and the muscle behind the **rigged trial by combat** (Ysabel's arc). The party can't beat him straight; they win by exposing the trick (reach Durgan mid-duel and Mott simply *stops*, in front of the whole crowd — Ironwake's rigged victory collapses publicly).
- **A hazard, not just an enemy.** Because he hits the nearest target, he mauls Ironwake's guards too. Smart parties herd enemies between themselves and Mott, or bait him into his own side.
- **Don't TPK.** He's slow-witted and attacks whoever's *closest and standing*. A downed PC is ignored if someone nearer is up. Reward kiting, cover, and terrain.
- **The freedom payoff.** This pair is built to be rescued. Free them and Durgan becomes a fierce, loyal ally/quest-giver, and Mott a gentle giant the party protects — and can unleash in emergencies, at the real cost of burning him out. It rhymes with JonLief's whole "free the enslaved" throughline.
- **Chapter 7 tie-in.** Mott and Durgan could be the prize "stock" at Bramblefen, or already shipped to Ironwake's Solteres pits — a natural thing for **Old Cobb's ledger** to point at.

## DM knobs to tune

- **Size.** Built as **Large** (VTT-practical, ~9–10 ft.). For a truly monstrous *Huge* version, bump reach to 15 ft., HP to ~120, and Ground Slam to 15-ft. radius.
- **HP 90** is set so a focused level-3 party of 4 can drop him around the time he'd collapse anyway. Lower to ~75 if your table hits hard; raise to ~110 if you want the clock (not damage) to be the only way through.
- **Phase lengths** match your spec (Full 1–4, Flagging 5–6, Burned Out 7+). To make him even more of a glass cannon, shift to Full 1–3 / Flagging 4–5 / Burned Out 6+.
- **Names:** *The Juggernaut / Mott* and *Durgan Half-Ear* are placeholders — swap at will.
