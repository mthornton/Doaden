# Adding to party
Gimble is being added to the party in the second half of Chapter 7. He replaces the placeholder formerly called "Prisoner X."

Gimble is a Penitent and the sole survivor of the first group of Penitents sent to investigate Dol Island — **the team this party was ordered in Chapter 1 to go and find.**

## The short version of Dol

Five Penitents landed in late June 1126, in the same week the cult's 666-day wait on Saraswati ran out. They cleared the crypt, concluded they had completed the mission, and stayed on the island because they had no boat and no way to report. Then the harvest crew arrived — three ships, twenty-odd men, and a tall pale man in an ornate headdress.

They were taken alive on purpose, because the ritual needed volume and nobody comes looking for a Penitent. They were bled into the same barrels as the goddess, on the beach, over four days. **The bloodstains this party found on the sand in Chapter 2 and never investigated are theirs.**

Gimble was left for last because he was small. Four days was three too many. He got out of the cuff, hid in the treeline for two more days watching because he could not accept there was nothing he could do, and then went along the shore to where a thousand years of reef has been piling up broken ships, lashed together enough timber to be called a raft, and put out into three miles of water.

## The road to a cage

He made the mainland and wandered for days with no map and no idea which way a Church camp lay. Then he found people who offered to help. They were slavers. He fought them, well and for a long time, and lost — and fought them again during transport, and again at Bramblefen.

**Vaskar's assessment:** too willful to break, too skilled to sell to a household, and — the part that matters — a *branded Penitent*. If it ever came out that Ironwake's people had collared a servant of the Church, not even a lord that powerful could stand in front of what the Church would do about it. So Gimble could not be sold, could not be freed, and could not be killed without waste.

Then Ironwake's wagon arrived with a giant in it that needed something to hit. A sparring partner good enough to sharpen Mott and not good enough to damage him, with an iron mask so no visitor sees a face and no one sees the shoulder — and a nameless hole outside the wall by morning.

**Nobody at Bramblefen expects Gimble to survive the match.**

## Running the handover

- **The reveal is the shoulder, not the face.** A masked thing in a cage that every guard says will be dead by dark → the party gets close → the Mark of Atonement, the same sun and tear they each carry, on a slave's shoulder. They learn *what* he is before *who*.
- **He cannot speak through the mask.** Getting it off is a scene.
- **Once it's off, stop narrating him.** He's Dave's.
- **Target: out of the cage by the end of play night 2**, so Dave has a character in hand for the sundown session.

## What he brings the party

- **The answer to the first half of their Chapter 1 orders.** *Find out what happened to the first team.* Producing him alive in front of Vozruk is the only part of that mission this party has ever completed.
- **Proof a Penitent was enslaved by Ironwake's chainmaster** — leverage on a lord that has nothing to do with Cobb's ledger.
- **Corroboration for Wolfram.** Gimble saw the four-armed woman in the tomb too. Wolfram has been carrying that alone since Chapter 2.
- **First-hand knowledge of the crypt**, including rooms this party never entered — which matters if they ever spend the amulet's last charge.
