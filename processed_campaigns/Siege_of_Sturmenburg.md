**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–10 (part of the Lvl 5–13 Crisis in the North campaign)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The fortress city of Stürmenburg, a human bulwark against the north, is besieged by an unprecedented, unified horde of Beastmen. Part 1 follows the party (rangers/allies) fighting to keep the city's outer wall (Träz) standing — it falls anyway, killing Captain Arne. Part 2 picks up immediately after: the party helps retake the wall, escorts a cowardly Imperial administrator to safety, and finally kills the Beastmen warlord Grimscar, ending the immediate siege but leaving the true mastermind, the demon-pacted shaman Graybane the Bloodcaller, unconfronted.

## Key Locations
- **Stürmenburg** — Fortress city with three concentric walls: Träz (outer, wood), Sten (middle, stone), Stahl (inner, steel); each has named Great Gates (Bjärn/Ulf on Träz, Sif/Sigrid/Tora on Sten, Odr on Stahl).
- **District Träz** — Outermost ring; farms, orchards, the Lost Wood (site of an old wall breach), Tempest Tavern (party's hub — resupply, short rests), the Ironworks.
- **District Sten** — Middle ring; city guard barracks, Temple of the End Flame (Gorm, the End Pyre for burning the dead).
- **District Stahl** — Innermost ring; the Stone Keep (Lord Arminius's seat, houses the Horn of Odr), the Imperial Embassy (Varus).
- **Black Forest** — Dense woods on the road into the city; site of the opening beastmen ambush.
- **The Lost Wood** — Overgrown ruins inside a breached, unrepaired section of Wall Träz; contested skirmish ground, includes the Northern/Southern Woods Gates.
- **The Eir River dam** — Upriver blockage the beastmen built to cut off the city's fresh water; destroyed with explosive barrels in Pt.1.
- **Gate Ulf** — Southern Great Gate of Wall Träz; secured by two massive chains needing 12 men each to open; site of the final Grimscar boss fight in Pt.2.

## Session Flow
**Part 1**
1. *Ch.1 Dark Omens* — Black Forest Ambush → arrival at Gate Ulf (Hamarhelm) → Corpse Cart → Ironworks → Tempest Tavern (meet Arne, get city map & first mission) → optionally visit Gorm for a vision.
2. *Ch.2 Into the Wild* — Defend the Northern Woods Gate (wave combat + Cyrox) → escort the explosives cart to Eir Be Dammed and blow the river blockage.
3. *Ch.3 Blood and Flame* — Return to find Wall Träz breached, Horn of Odr sounding; Arne is killed by a titanic beast; fight a Cyrox at the gate, deal with a Beast Totem hazard, rescue peasants from a burning granary; Hamarhelm kills a second Cyrox; party falls back to Wall Sten.

**Part 2** (picks up immediately)
1. *Ch.1 Counterstrike* — War meeting with Arminius; Uldibad restores the party; clear Wall Träz section-by-section in a pincer with the Blitzvakt to retake Gate Bjärn.
2. *Ch.2 Cowards & Cravens* — Escort Varus through the burning city, free captives from a bonfire/cage site, fight off wave-spawned beastmen while protecting Varus/townsfolk, clear a debris blockage to escape.
3. *Ch.3 Head of the Goat* — Reach Gate Ulf; Grimscar kills Uldibad and challenges the party; boss fight vs. Grimscar.
4. *Epilogue* — Hamarhelm refuses to abandon the dying Uldibad; Varus stabs Uldibad in frustration; Hamarhelm nearly crushes Varus's head — the party's Final Choice decides Varus's fate; +1 Renown with the Templar Rangers if the siege was defended well.

## Secrets & Twists
- The Beastmen have been acting with unnatural intelligence and coordination — this is secretly Graybane the Bloodcaller's demonic influence, not natural Capra cunning.
- Lord Arminius secretly resents the Empire that took him hostage as a child and would rebel given the right opportunity — hidden from Varus and the Empire.
- Administrator Varus's intercepted letter reveals the Empire and Church of Azerai intend to let Stürmenburg fall without real aid.
- Grimscar has united traditionally warring Beastmen sub-species (Capra, Aurox, Centaurs/Armogors, Cyrox, and even Ghorox) under one banner for the first time ever — orchestrated behind the scenes by Graybane.
- The Epilogue's Final Choice (save Varus or let Hamarhelm kill him) is a genuine branch point with lasting political consequences.

## Faction: The Beastmen
- A multi-species horde: **Capra** (goatmen — the numerous, devious rank-and-file: Scouts, Hunters, Raiders, Marauders, and shaman Herd Keepers), **Aurox** (minotaur-kin, once dominant but now enslaved/subjugated by the Capra), **Cyrox** (huge one-eyed brutes chained and goaded into rage as living siege weapons), and **Ghorox** (even larger, near-unstoppable war machines).
- Signature monsters: **Gorehounds** (mutant boar/hound/demon hybrids that also pull war chariots), **Boargores** (scavenger beasts), **Armogors** (demonic centaur-like raiders), **Dreadgore** (a monstrously mutated Gorehound commander), **Aurox Battlesmiths** (forge/repair pillaged gear for the horde).
- Warband command: **Grimscar the Beastlord** leads the largest, first-ever unified tribe of Beastmen the North has seen, driven by ambition to found a bestial empire.
- Hidden power: **Graybane the Bloodcaller**, head of the Capra Herd Keepers and the demon-pact-bound leader of the "Bloodbrand Cult," is the true source of the horde's new cunning and demonic mutations — Grimscar's guiding hand and puppet-master.
- Capra rank progression for encounter-building: Scout (CR2, disposable scout/skirmisher) → Hunter (CR3, archer) → Raider (CR3, frontline) → Marauder (CR3, elite brute) → Herd Keeper (CR3, shaman spellcaster).

## Campaign Context
- These two parts form Chapters 2–3 of **"Escalation: Crisis in the North"** (Bestiarum Games, Doaden setting, Lvl 5–13); they pick up directly after the previous chapter, *Aurox: The Bloodfever*, whose party choices affect Gorm's reaction and rewards here.
- Ends with Graybane the Bloodcaller still at large as the setup for the campaign's next chapter, plus optional "Taking It Further" revenge encounters (the Ghorox-type beast that killed Arne; Dreadgore) for extending the siege.
