**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A peaceful village's inhabitants are transforming into insectoid thralls under the psychic call of a Hive Queen. The party must fight through the infected village, hex-crawl a subterranean insect hive, and slay the queen to end the transformation curse. Tier II, APL-scaled (Medium 5–7 / Hard 8–10 / Nightmare 11–13 / Torment 14–16). Any region of the Empire of Azerai.

- Area 3: Woodcutter's Shed — dying Hive Victim NPC + Hidden Hatch (auto-found with journal page, else DC 20 Perception) leading down to the hive
- Chittering Hive Hexcrawl (Map 2) — subterranean tunnel network with Danger Level/random event mechanics
  - A) Descending Ladder — entrance from the woodcutter's shed hatch
  - B) Swarming Grounds — Hazardous Area; staging ground, always Normal-encounter-biased (13-14 event roll)
  - C) Larval Hive — Hazardous Area; always on High Alert (treated as event roll of 8)
  - D) Ascension Grounds — Hazardous Area; elite insects, easy encounters upgraded to normal, normal to challenging
  - E) Queen's Chamber (Map 3) — final battle area with a Sunken Pit hazard (15 ft fall, DC 14 Con save vs. poison gas, 10/3d7 damage)
  - F) Escape Ladder — hidden passage back to the surface (a hunter's shed in the woods)

## Session Flow
1. **Opening ambush** — party is ambushed in the infected village by chitin thralls, walking hives, and a locust guardian.
2. **Village investigation** — explore Strange Clusters, Ramshackle Hovel (Torn Journal Page + treasure), and the Woodcutter's Shed (tragic NPC encounter + Hidden Hatch).
3. **Descend into the Chittering Hive** — hexcrawl through Map 2 using the movement/Danger Level/event roll system (Slow/Normal/Fast pace tradeoffs).
4. **Navigate special areas** — Swarming Grounds, Larval Hive, and Ascension Grounds each carry unique hazard rules and encounter modifiers.
5. **Queen's Chamber showdown** — final battle against the Hive Queen (scaled by difficulty) on the earthen platform with a toxic Sunken Pit hazard.
6. **Escape** — party finds the hidden Escape Ladder back to the surface via a hunter's shed.
7. **Epilogue** — resolution varies by hook (Dark Cult reward from city council; bittersweet Villager's Plea homecoming; eerie silent return for Queen's Call).

## Treasure & Rewards
- Ramshackle Hovel hidden compartment — 4d100 gp + gold locket (100 gp)
- Lucky Find table (roll 1d4 on event roll 17-20): Hidden Alcove (safe rest spot), Treasure Chest (8d100 gp, DC 10+1d6 lock), Dead Hero's remains (spell scroll/potion/gp/uncommon magic item), Cultist Altar (temporary boons: Focus/Power/Dominance/Replenishment)
- Queen's Chamber loot (roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus): 30+ = Staff of Swarming Insects; 1–29 = nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- The village's affliction is caused by the Hive Queen's psychic "calling" — villagers/cultists are drawn to descend and transform
- The Woodcutter's Shed conceals the hive entrance; finding the Torn Journal Page bypasses the DC 20 Perception check
- Ascension Grounds is where cultists voluntarily undergo transformation into the queen's "elite" — a body-horror set piece

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Danger Level resets to 0 after each event roll check; increases based on pace (Slow +3, Normal +4, Fast +5) and terrain (Normal Area +1, Hazardous Area +3)
- "High Alert" state (from event roll of 8, or always active in the Larval Hive) makes an event roll of 8 result in a Hard encounter
- If the queen's threat feels too easy/hard mid-session, shift the adventure difficulty tier on the fly (built into the module's design)
