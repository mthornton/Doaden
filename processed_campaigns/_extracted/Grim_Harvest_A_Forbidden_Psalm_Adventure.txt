                                       Adventure Summary:
                              GRIM HARVEST
        Deep within the cracks and caverns of the yawning earth lie unexplored chambers filled with
    vile denizens. In the shadows, goblins scuttle like wretched vermin, their wicked grins revealing
    an insatiable hunger. Driven by primitive instinct, they have carved out their domains within the
    heart of the earth, surfacing to raid nearby human settlements, dragging off their captives to kill,
    and dumping the bodies into mulch piles which fertilize their subterranean fungal crops. Their
    mushroom farms flourish in the damp recesses, spreading tendrils through the earth, growing large
    which suck the nutrients from the grotesque piles of flesh. Here, in this grim domain, the fierce and
    cunning goblins will defend their territory with unyielding ferocity, ambushing intruders from the
    shadows and granting no mercy to the unwary.

        In this scenario the warband/s must investigate a rash of disappearing townsfolk. Finding the
    goblin menace behind the phenomenon, they must delve into the subterranean lairs to exterminate
    the threat and liberate any survivors. As the warband/s delve deeper into the monsters’ lair, they
    must take care to avoid traps, ambushes, and be wary of the hulking trolls that lurk deeper in
    the shadows.

    Chapter 1: GOBLIN INFESTED TRAVERSE
    After descending into the lower, goblin-infested tunnels, the warband/s will be forced to
    confront the bulk of the goblin forces in a dark and precarious cavern.

    Chapter 2: TROLL LAIR
    Having braved the treacherous goblin-infested tunnels, the warband/s now finds themselves on the
    cusp of a deeper, more menacing challenge. In this cavernous den resides a monstrous troll, and
    hiding in the shadows are more of the menacing goblins.

    Chapter 3: THE GOBLIN KING
    Grothak Wyrm’Killa, the goblin king, stands as the pinnacle of goblin brutality and cunning.
    Guarded at all times by his Bugbear Enforcers, he is a testament to the raw destructive ferocity of
    the goblin species. He wields his wicked blades with relentless precision, doggedly pursuing his foes
    and slashing away until nothing remains but a gruesome splattering of blood and tangled ribbons of
    flesh and viscera.

    Grothak’s rule is absolute, his authority unquestioned among his kin. Under his command,
    the goblin horde has become a relentless wave of devastation, feared throughout the scattered
    kingdoms of goblin kind. His prowess in battle is legendary, earning him the title Wyrm’Killa after
    he butchered a dragon in single combat.

    The sight of Grothak, standing upon the shredded head of his draconic foe must have been a sight
    to see, enough to instill fear in the hearts of the bravest warriors. To face the goblinb king is to face
    the culmination of goblin savagery.

                                   Scenario 1:
                                   TRAPPED

    In recent weeks, food production in the northern territories has reached concerning lows. The
military forces stationed nearby are growing malnourished and weak, undermining the stability
in the region. As such, the matter has garnered some official attention, warranting the dispatch of
investigative forces.
   Reports tell of numerous disappearances among the rural populace, decimating the labor force
and leaving many crops untended. Strangely, the remaining villagers speak of signs of struggle and
small mushrooms that are left scattered around the last known locations of the vanishing victims.
Some mushrooms that are left scattered around the last known locations of the vanishing victims.
Some claim to have heard vile chittering of creatures in the dead of night, finding that more people
have gone missing by the next morn.
    Local investigators have tracked the creatures responsible for the abductions to a cave entrance.
However, all those who have entered said cave, have disappeared, never to resurface. Unwilling
to suffer the losses of more specialists, the lords of the land have decided to send in a more
expendable force to resolve the issue and cleanse the hollow of further threats.
    You step cautiously into the gaping maw of the dark and ominous cave, where the villagers claim
the creatures return to with their captive victims. A stench hangs heavy in the air, a putrid blend of
decaying flesh and damp earth. The flickering light of your torches cast eerie shadows on the cavern
walls, revealing crude drawings and symbols etched into the stone, depicting giant beasts of prey
hunted by small bipedal figures.
    The occasional drip of water echoes ominously as you descend deeper into the narrow tunnels,
amplifying your unease with each lingering note. Gnarled stalactites reach down from above
like skeletal fingers, ready to snatch at any reckless interlopers. Pools of blood, intermittent but
unmistakable, trail along the cave floor, leading further into the bowels of the earth.
    Yet, there is a strange subterranean beauty to this place. Patches of fungi adorn the walls, at
times clustering into mesmerizing, bioluminescent tapestries. Mycelium bunches form vibrant
patterns that flicker in shades of cloying greens, purples, and blue, casting disorienting shadows
upon the twisting tunnels.
    You press on, squeezing through the narrow and claustrophobic tunnels, determined to
complete the mission. Venturing into the darkness, you pull yourself along the rough ground for a
tortuous hour, until you see a glimmer of light ahead. Following it, you come to the opening of a vast,
vaulted cavern. Stepping inside, an awe-inspiring yet chilling vista unfolds before you. The ceiling
looms high, adorned with an intricate network of massive mushrooms, their caps the size of buckler
shields, casting unnerving shadows across the cavern walls.
   Wooden platforms stretch across a wide chasm, connected by precarious makeshift bridges that
sway and groan with each gust of stale air. Faint chittering and raspy breathing echoes in the gloom,
from where you spy a shifting movement. You ready your weapons, your senses on high alert.

                                  TROLL LAIR
         The tunnel opens to a large cavern, the rocky ground a perilous floor riddled with gaping chasms
    and jutting stalagmites. In the darkness, a monstrous silhouette shifts at the far side of the chamber,
    its attention absorbed in a gruesome feast. Each resonating crunch of flesh and bone accompanied
    by a satisfied guttural growl.
        Large stalagmites rise from the earth, offering potential cover, while the shadowy pits lurking at
    the edge of your vision warn of a deadly fall. Amidst the grisly spectacle, a faint stirring from the pile
    of bodies suggests a desperate flicker of human life, perhaps the same person you heard earlier.

    ence but heals 1HP per round even if downed and can only be killed outright by a firebased attack
    such as one by a torch}. If a warband member with this curse is killed by a troll spawn a new Feral
    Cave Troll.
