## Who he is *(Channing's backstory)*

Raised his whole life in the service of the **Church of Azerai**, on tales of holy warriors. Trained for the Church's military and proved **unusually talented with a blade**. When **magic began to manifest alongside his swordsmanship**, he accepted it as **another blessing from the Emperor** — and has never questioned that.

**Confident, determined, deeply devout, and certain he is destined for greatness.** Frustratingly sure of his own judgment. Not interested in charm: he pushes forward with conviction, speaks plainly, and expects people to see the value of his plans. **He means well; his certainty reads as arrogance.**

His posting to the **Office of the Lost Flock** felt like a detour from the heroic future he imagined — but serving under **Baltus Thole** is a chance. His orders: **accompany a group of Penitents, help them when he can, observe them, and report back to the Church.** He sees no contradiction in any of it.

**He wants to do good, and he wants to be remembered for doing it.** He thinks this journey may be the start.

## His patron — as Cassian understands it

His magic came from **an old man who appeared to him**: very old, plainly holy, who said he was **Bishop Osric, the Emperor's top bishop**, and that the Emperor had granted him his long life **as a reward for his faith and devotion.** Cassian believes every word — it is exactly the kind of thing his upbringing told him the Emperor does for the faithful.

- **They first met during his training**, in the training-house chapel, late at night, when Cassian was praying alone.
- **Osric gave him a string of prayer beads.** When Cassian needs guidance he prays with them, and sometimes he is answered.
- **Osric has appeared to him a couple of times since — and each time, nobody else around him noticed the old man at all.**
- The full story, for Channing: **`Cassian Zev - The Bishop in the Chapel.md`**.

## What the campaign already establishes

- **Apprentice to Baltus Thole** *(`world_lore/Baltus Thole.md`)*. Arrived with him at Bramblefen after the fight in Chapter 7; **Baltus left him with the party** to assist and guide them, saying mildly that he thought they were out of their depth.
- **Unbranded.** Apprentices carry no Mark, **feel no pull, and are never assigned a file**; the apprenticeship typically runs five to eight years, and **an apprentice can walk away** *(`world_lore/The Office of the Lost Flock.md`)*.
- **He has not been taught the severance rite** — that comes at or after the branding.
- **Baltus has taken one apprentice before him**, years ago, who is a Shepherd now.
- **Free and Church-sanctioned** — the one member of the party who can walk into a magistrate's office, hire a cart, or ask a question in Solteres without a noblewoman or a brand.
- **He carries a warning from Baltus about Rowan**: a child with no guardian but a Penitent is an orphan, and orphans are raised (apprenticed) by the Church. He has never seen it done; these are Baltus's stories *(`world_lore/What To Do With Rowan.md` §2)*.
