# Chapter 7, session 2

You are standing in the inner yard of Bramblefen, a one-time prison converted to house enslaved people awaiting auction.  The yard is mostly mud with sparse patches of grass and weeds.  Directly in front of you is a large stone building three stories high.  This is the prison known as The Deeps.  The building is discolored with patches of moss growing in the cracks.  You can feel the despair radiating outward, powered by the misery of the thousands of souls imprisoned inside over the centuries.  The feeling you get from the building is reminiscent of the feelings you had at St. Pyotr's.  Much less intense, but recognizable.

There are three other buildings around the courtyard.  A two-story house stands to the right of The Deeps.  The house stands out on account of being the only building in good repair.  To your right is a small blacksmith and armory.  It does not appear active.  No smoke comes from the forge's chimney.  The front door is chained and locked.  To your left is another barracks for housing the enslaved.  Smaller and less intimidating than The Deeps, but unpleasant nonetheless.

You came here believing Bramblefen only had four or five guards.  You have counted eight so far, and those are only the ones you can see out in the open.  You have an out-of-date ledger created by the slave bookkeeper Old Cobb that has a two-month-old entry for JonLief's family, Elizabeth and Rowan, but no entry for what happened after they arrived.  Erwana remembers that Elizabeth was sick, but she knows nothing else.  Your goal is to find them, then give Ysabel the ledger in exchange for her help.  But on your arrival at this desolate place you learned Lord Ironwake, the owner of this facility, has his champion, The Juggernaut, here for some warm-up fights.  He has a match tonight against a feisty gnome that the guards seem particularly excited to watch.

The yard is bustling with activity.  A few guards are directing a group of slaves to assemble a wooden fence made of sharpened timbers.  It appears they are constructing a circle in the center of the yard.  The work is half complete.  The guards are brutal in their treatment of the slaves.  The work is interrupted by the guards beating the slaves for imagined disobedience.  From the laughter of the guards you can tell the beatings are primarily for the entertainment of the guards, with each trying to outdo the other.

A half-orc slave is tied to a post with a drunken guard clubbing him between drinks.  "Don't worry about that, orcs can take a beating.  It's not likely to do any permanent damage," says the guard escorting you.

Work stops temporarily as the slaves notice the party.  The break lasts no more than a second before the guards begin punishing the slaves for the work stoppage.  However, one slave does not immediately go back to work.  He drops the timber he is carrying with a look of shock and astonishment on his face.  A guard pounces — beating the man to the ground and continuing to kick him as he curls up into a ball.  In the brief moment the slave's face could be seen, Wolfram recognized the man.  He can't place the face with a name, but it must be somebody he knew from Westfield.

Wolfram acted quickly and approached the downed slave.  "I want to inspect this man.  He might be somebody we want to purchase," he stated as he knelt down.  The guards, unsure how to react, stepped back.

The man opened his eyes.  He was around 60 years old and had the physique of a man who had known hard labor.  "Wolfram!  What are you doing here?"

"I'm sorry, I recognize you but don't remember who you are," says Wolfram.

"We don't have much time.  My name is Anselm.  I worked for your father.  After your arrest—"  Anselm's whisper is interrupted by a kick from the guard.

JonLief immediately starts a conversation with the other slaves in hopes of giving Wolfram more time to talk to Anselm.  The plan works.  JonLief asks the slaves about an elf woman and child.  The guards hear this and step in, telling JonLief these slaves are busy and that they should first go see Vaskar.

This was the distraction Wolfram needed.  He bent over and had just enough time to hear Anselm say, "After your arrest I went to the Bishop and told him what I heard.  About your reasons.  The Bishop never even wrote down what I said.  I'm sorry, but we need your help.  We are going to break out of here tonight.  Everybody will watch the fight.  Once it starts, we are going to attack the guards and earn our freedom or die trying.  There are weapons in the armory.  If you could get some of them for us, it would make a big difference."

A guard stepped over and kicked Anselm in the side of his head, ending the conversation.  "Sir, you need to see Vaskar before you inspect any of the slaves."

Wolfram and JonLief walk back to the party and quietly share what they learned with everybody.

The party is greeted by a guard in front of Vaskar's house.  The guard tells them that Vaskar has already been informed of their arrival and will see them now.  However, the guard notes the unusually large number of people and tells Lady Ysabel her people will need to stay outside.  Lady Ysabel counters by bringing her assistant, JonLief, and Virgil.

"Wolfram, stay with the group.  Be sure to keep my handmaiden Mina safe," says Ysabel as she enters the house.

Wolfram leads the group to the side of the yard to an area shaded by the wall.  To the guards it appears he is simply taking the group to a plase to wait in comfort.  However, what he has done is gotten the group close to the Armoury.  He examins the door from a distance.  The lock and chain appear well made and sturdy.  He knows he can't break either without tools and a lot of time.  However, the door itself is wood and looks to have suffered from a bit of rot.  He thinks he might be able to bowl through it if the need arrises.

While the party is waiting and Wolfram is planning how to breakdorn the Armoury door Ysabel, JonLief, and Virgil enter Vaskar's house.  Inside the house they see what is apparently a man in a chair covered by a tarp.  There are splatters of blood on the wall and the man can be heard breathing laboriously under the tarp.  Vaskar offers no acknowledgement of the odd sight, simply beckons them to follow him to the upstairs balcony.

Once they reach the balcony, Vaskar booms out, "Welcome to Bramblefen, Lady Ysabel," in a condescending tone.  "I am so happy to see you.  Are you here for more slaves for your factories?  Or maybe to purchase a new Champion?  I hear you may need a new one."

"You don't need to worry about my Champion.  Yes, Ulfric is dead.  However, I have other champions.  And the Emperor knows I am innocent.  His light will shine down on any champion I select, and he will prevail in the Trial by Combat!" Ysabel fires back.

"Ha ha ha.  Nobody has a chance against The Juggernaut!" chuckles Vaskar.

"I'm not here to discuss my personal issues.  I need some slaves for my factories.  Specifically, I need seamstresses.  Also, I need a small person who can crawl inside the looms to make repairs.  A skinny child would be ideal," asks Ysabel.

"We have no women at all.  I should have another shipment of them in a month or two.  Sold every woman I had a short while back to a creepy group," explains Vaskar.

"Creepy?" asks Ysabel.

"Yea.  Men in dirty hooded robes.  Their skin was covered with scars.  Nasty stuff.  They never said why they wanted all those women and I never asked.  But I did charge a premium, so a good day's business," boasts Vaskar.

"You are a gentleman as usual, Vaskar.  What about a child?  Any for sale?" inquires Ysabel.

"Naw, don't get many children here.  Don't have any.  I need to get back to work.  My guards will show you out," says Vaskar.

"No, wait.  Maybe some of the slaves you do have worked as tailors.  I want to inspect your stock," states Ysabel.

"Fine, whatever.  If you are trying to spy on The Juggernaut you don't need to sneak around.  He is in The Deeps," Vaskar says with a smile.  "My guard will escort you around the property."

Before they leave, a patch of green just outside the outer wall catches the attention of Virgil.  The area outside the walls is just as desolate as inside.  Mud with an occasional patch of weeds.  However, there is one spot that is different.  There is a circle burnt into the ground, about twice as wide as a person is tall.  In the center grows bright green foliage.  It is the only healthy-looking green thing in sight.

Before Virgil can ask about it, the group is ushered back inside, down the stairs, and out the front door.  As they exit they can't help but glance at the person under the tarp, still wheezing.

The guard first takes the group to the smaller prison.  Once inside they see that this holding area is low security.  There is a section with eight cells.  Each cell has a wrought iron door.  There is a large lever that opens all the doors at once when pulled down.  Beside the lever is a ring with many keys hanging off of it.

While Ysabel stops at each cell to talk to the slaves about their skills, JonLief takes this opportunity to ask about his family.

Speaking in Elvish, he asks an elf who is chained to the wall, "Greetings, friend.  I am so sorry you are enslaved.  If I can, I will try to help you.  I am here searching for a half-elf woman and young boy.  They were reportedly here a couple of months ago.  Do you know of whom I speak?"

"Sorry, I don't.  I have been here for three months but we don't get to socialize much, if you know what I mean.  I have rarely been out of this cell.  Sorry, friend.  I certainly would appreciate anything you could do to help me," says the elf.

JonLief tried the same conversation with a half-elf in another cell.  The half-elf spoke Elvish very poorly and JonLief switched to Common.  However, this person knew no more than the first.

Once Ysabel was finished inspecting all the slaves, the party moved on to The Deeps.

Inside was a line of cells, each with a locked door of iron bars.  At the end of the hall were two large wooden doors with a small window.  Two guards stood in front of the doors.  These men looked different from the other guards.  They gave off an air of competence and professionalism.  These must be the special guards sent by Lord Ironwake to escort The Juggernaut.

Ysabel starts inspecting the slaves as before.  JonLief seeks out slaves with elf blood to question.

The first couple of conversations JonLief has bore no fruit, but the third offers promise.

"Yes, I know who you are talking about.  They were in the cell beside me.  Right there, between my cell and Old Cobb's cell.  She was really sick.  Just laid in her cell all day, not moving much.  The boy would plead with her to eat but she did not seem to care about anything.  After a few days some guards came and took her away.  Never saw her again.  That same day they came back and took the boy up into the tower.  I never saw them bring him back down, but I assume they did," says the elf slave.

"When was this?  Have you heard anything more?" anxiously asks JonLief.

"Sorry, I don't know exactly when.  The days and weeks bleed together here.  This was after all the women were bought and before the Tabaxi slave escaped.  That's all I know," says the slave.

Luthien has spent this time looking around and trying to make jokes with the slaves.  They were not amused.  When Luthien reached the end of the hall and peered into the last cell — the cell the elf slave said was Old Cobb's — Luthien sees a very small man wearing an iron mask.  He has a coat on, but his arms are inside the coat, the sleeves wrapped around his body and tied together.  There are no holes in the mask for the man to see out of or speak.

"Who is this?" asks Luthien.

A guard steps over and explains this is the slave that is going to fight The Juggernaut tonight.  The whole camp is excited.  They are all wagering on how many pieces The Juggernaut will tear the little man into.

Luthien sees his opportunity and turns on the charm.  He talks with the guard about the finer points of gambling and how to extract the best possible terms.  He then turns the conversation to an elf woman and child.

"Yea, I remember them.  Don't get many kids here.  The woman was really sick.  When we saw some green patches starting to grow on her skin, Vaskar had us take her out back and burn her.  We don't want The Green spreading here!" explains the guard.

"The Green?" asks Luthien.

"You not heard of it?  Rumor is there's a plague out east.  People start sprouting leaves then die.  Don't want that here!" says the guard.

"What about the boy?" asks Luthien.

"Children are valuable and he looked healthy.  Vaskar had us isolate him in the tower for a week to make sure he was not sick too.  Once we knew he was healthy, Vaskar sold him.  You would have to ask Vaskar for the details on the sale.  Well, Vaskar or Old Cobb, but I don't think Old Cobb will survive the beating Vaskar is giving him," says the guard.

"Why is Vaskar beating Old Cobb?" asks Luthien.

"I don't know.  Been at it all day though," says the guard.

"Hey, what's in the room at the end?  The room behind the two mean-looking guards?" asks Luthien.

"The Juggernaut!  You wanna see?  Go take a look," says the guard.

Luthien peers through the small window in the door.  It is dark, with the only source of light being what shines through the small window in the two doors.  At first he can't comprehend what he is seeing.  He realizes the massive boulder in the corner is the back of a very large man.  As described by Ysabel, this man is easily the size of four large men.  The man is turned to the wall and making little sounds.  After listening for a moment Luthien realizes the man is playing.  He has a doll made from scraps of straw tied together.  Luthien makes some baby talk to get the creature's attention.  The Juggernaut hears him and cowers into the corner.

Suddenly, from the shadows, a stout dwarf rushes to the door shouting "Leave him alone!  He has never hurt anybody" and slams his palm against the window.  The Juggernaut then also charges forward, bellowing in rage.

Luthien yelps and stumbles back, startled.

In that last glimpse Luthien could see The Juggernaut was wearing a large full-face helmet of iron covered with spikes.

The guard Luthien was talking to is doubled over in laughter.

With that the party leaves The Deeps.  They see the ring is now complete.  The sun is almost set and the match will start soon.

On the way back across the yard Wolfram stops at the blacksmith and armory and takes a close look at the door.  The chain is heavy and the padlock is newer than anything else in the compound.  He has no way to pick it and says so.  The door itself is another matter.  It is old and the wood is damp at the bottom, and he thinks he can put his shoulder through it if it comes to that.

Luthien does not like the noise a broken door would make.  He begins working on a way to conjure a key instead.

The party has already worked out that the key to the armory is almost certainly on the ring hanging beside the lever in the smaller prison.  They decided against taking it.  A missing ring of keys would be noticed, and being caught with it would cost them more than the weapons were worth.

"We still have time before the match.  I want to go see the place my wife was burned.  Virgil, please accompany me," says JonLief.

The two go out to the burned circle.  Virgil examines the growth.  It is out of place and strange, but it will need deeper study to learn anything.  He takes a couple of samples, then digs in the ground to gain access to the roots.  While digging he discovers the bones of Elizabeth.

JonLief drops to his knees in silent prayer and vows to learn the truth.

Somberly they return to the yard.

Vaskar steps out of his house holding the limp body of an old man and throws the body out into the yard.  "This is what happens to people that betray me!"  The man is obviously dead.  "Poor Old Cobb," says one of the guards.

The yard is now full.  The guards have brought out all the slaves to watch the match.

The prisoner is brought out first.  He is still wearing the mask and binding coat.

Next comes The Juggernaut and the dwarf.  The size difference between The Juggernaut and his opponent is stunning.  The masked prisoner is barely as tall as The Juggernaut's thigh.

The guards remove the mask, and it can now be seen that the slave is a gnome.  Next they unbind and remove the jacket.

The party stares in shock.  The gnome has the brand of a Penitent!
