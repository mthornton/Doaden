# Virgil

Virgil was an alchemist of the Alchemic Order who became obsessed with studying fungi, believing they were the key to humanity’s next stage of evolution. He performed experiments on corpses to test fungal symbiosis and argued constantly with other alchemists, convinced that he alone understood the future of mankind. While traveling with an Order caravan through Azerai territory, several rivals secretly exposed his research to the local Church authorities, not because the experiments were unusual for the Order, but because his arrogance had made him many enemies. The Church declared his work blasphemous, imprisoned him, and sentenced him to serve with the Penitent. Virgil still believes his research is correct and sees his punishment as proof that others fear the truth he has discovered.
