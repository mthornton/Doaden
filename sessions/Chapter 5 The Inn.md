# Chapter 5 The Inn

## 1. Ill fated lovers 
Baron Corvin Ashwinter up in a room at the Inn.  He came wearing the cloths of a commoner using the alias Godric.  With him is Lady Marguerite de Valpreux.  Corvin is a single man in his early twenties.  He is a baron, but not one with wealth or power.  Marguerite is the daughter of Lord Alwith de Valpreux and is betrothed to Baron Emeric Blackholt.  She is in her early twenties while Emeric is in his mid forties.  Emeric is a powerful landholder.  Corvin and Marguerite are in love, but her father does not support.   
 
They will spend the evening in their room believing nobody recognized them.  The elf barmaid did recognize them and sent work to Emeric in expectation of a reward for information. 
 
Truth is this whole thing is a ruse.  Emeric is in debt to Lord Ironwake.  In exchange for forgiving his debt, he has agreed to kill Ulfric.  The only opportunity is this night at the Inn.  He enlists Marguerrite and Corvin.  They are lovers and her father does not approve.  She is from a rich family.  Corvin is a Barron in name, but that title is all he owns.  Emeric agrees to take him into service and make him heir if he helps in the plot.  This would instantly make him an acceptable match for Marguritte. 
 
Emric works out the betrothal to Marguritte.  It is rushed with Emric claiming he need to produce an heir before he is too old to do so.  The plan is to catch the young lovers and cause a commotion.  While that is happening, Emeric's man will sneak upstairs and kill Ulfric. 
 
To get Ulfic upstairs, a barmaid was paid to slip him a sleeping tonic.   
 
This all goes to plan.  The only unplanned part was the thug stealing the knife from a party member. 
 
Clues: 
a. Emeric enters with two thugs.  One thug throws Corvin down the stairs followed by Emeric.  The second thug does not follow until after the bar brawl starts. 
The party can get plenty of gossip from the bar maids if they just ask.  The engagement is the talk of the town.  It was out of the blue and the age gap is substantial.  The rumors of Emeric being broke must have been untrue for him to have paid what must have been a substantial dowery. 
The barmaid that gives Ulfirc the sleeping potion is the same one that steals the knife early in the evening.  The party will not know this but should remember the barmaid borrowing the knife.
