                      The World of Doaden



W
    elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted
    monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very essence of
                                   existence.

It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of
the glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie with
                                     one
              another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.

     It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be honored in
                               songs of minstrels.

 For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake invariably leave
 them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless husks and shadows of
                         their once-glorious selves.

Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn tenacity,
                   a last flickering flame within the dark.

 A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of eternity,
                a momentary victory before the endless night.

    Leveling. PCs gain levels by completing scenarios. From
    levels 5 to 8, they gain one level for every 2 adventures. From
    levels 9 to 13, they gain one level for every 3 adventures. The
    maximum level is 13, but parties continue to acquire gear
    and can enjoy the most challenging and dynamic encounters,
    and exclusive content for Damnation Mode. If you include
    your own adventures in between monthly scenarios, consider
    leveling your party quicker.

Exploring the Strange
The Ashen Wastes hold many secrets, valuable treasures, and
powerful artifacts, hidden amongst the strange ruins from
Doaden’s past age. Some of these places have sunken below the
dust, preserved in the state of their existence as the Demoncore
detonated. In these echoes of the past, one is often reminded
of the great tragedies of the lost, seeing the ashen remains of
human silhouettes smeared on the walls and floors, or even the
petrified bodies that crumble into ash when touched, the last
remnants of those caught in the blast.
    Other places have been exposed to the unbridled arcane
energies of the Ashen Wastes for centuries, warping into
strange and alien things, with features that defy physics and the
laws of nature. Even the natural landscape has become twisted
and bizarre, changed by the arcane fallout. Floating pyramidal
structures, fractal geological cuboids, perpetually swirling red
sand valleys, and many other odd phenomena are common
here.
    The Church of Azerai and the Penitent Crusade often send
missions into the Wastes to discover and extract lost knowledge
and items, sending teams to delve into these lost temples and
ruins of the past. Some teams deployed for weeks or months
on end, and rumors among the ranks spread about strange
cadences of time. Occasionally, a team that has been deployed
for a single week returns years later, aged just as they were as
they left. Sometimes teams speak of being trapped and enduring
decade-long hardships, returning in a state of emaciation, with
long and unruly beards, just a few days later.
If the party wishes to continue exploring, you may devise
various bizarre structures and shelters mutated by the arcane
fallout.
