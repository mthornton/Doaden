The upper floor of the warden's office is eerily silent compared to the chaos of the rest of
the prison. The thick wooden door opens under your touch, revealing a dimly lit room.
The rich scent of incense and old parchment hangs in the air, a stark contrast to the grime
and stench that pervades the rest of the prison. As your eyes adjust to the gloom, you
see a figure towering over a frail, bound man slumped in a chair.


Urvakh, a brutish man clad in crude, blood-stained armour, is interrogating the
warden. His scarred face contorts into a sadistic grin as he leans in close, pressing the
edge of a wicked blade against the man's neck. "Give me the key, old man," he growls,
his voice as cold as iron. The warden, though pale and beaten, maintains a defiant glare,
refusing to submit to the bandit's cruelty. The warden is struck down by the bandit who
then turns to face you.
