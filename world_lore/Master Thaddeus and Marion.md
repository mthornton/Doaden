# Master Thaddeus and Marion

Members of the Alchemical Order.

**Thaddeus** is human from the waist up. From the waist down he is a single long, ringed, boneless **tentacle** — grey-green, thick as a man's thigh where it joins him, tapering away to nothing — which he keeps coiled twice around Marion's shoulders and torso, on the outside of her clothing. She carries him; he rides her; neither of them makes anything of it anymore.

At a glance, and from across a room, he reads as an enormous worm wearing the top half of a scholar, and nearly everyone assumes worm. Thaddeus has stopped correcting people. It takes longer than it's worth and comforts nobody.

**Marion** is human, tall and stout, and does not speak. She cannot: her mouth and throat are built like a squid's beak. She communicates telepathically, which she does well, and with anyone — not only her husband.

## Backstory

Thaddeus comes from a long line of alchemists, and his education was guided by his parents. He believes alchemy is the key to making the world a better place.

When he was nine his mother died of a disease nobody could name — most likely a consequence of her own experiments. He was raised after that by his father, a knowledgeable and kind man, but nothing replaced what he had lost.

He became obsessed with extending human life by enhancing the body's own capacity to resist disease. That research led him to giant squid, which are known to live in excess of a thousand years and appear not to sicken at all. His work was to extract the essence of a squid's biology and apply it to a human being.

It showed enough promise to make him a rising star in the Order. It was around then that he met **Marion** — brilliant, socially awkward, working on telepathy. Her theory was that every creature has the innate capacity for it and needs only a chemical key.

They shared a lab. They fell in love, married, and merged the research. Marion theorized that squid communicate mind to mind: if Thaddeus could put squid into a man, that man might come out both disease-resistant *and* telepathic.

As the results got more promising, Thaddeus made the arrogant decision to test the compounds on himself and on Marion. She insisted on being included. He was proud enough of his own cleverness to allow it.

It did not go as hoped. They got the telepathy, and it works beautifully — better than speech, by his account. Marion lost her voice entirely. Thaddeus lost the lower half of his body to the tentacle.

They did not extend anyone's life, which had been the entire point.

## Ch5–Ch6: what the party knows

- **At the Calf & Ass (Ch5)**, Virgil asked outright about the transformation. Thaddeus gave the polished version he gives strangers: a vanity experiment, badly judged, gone wrong. Small self-deprecating laugh. He has told it enough times that he half believes it.
- **On the road to Solteres (Ch6)**, a few nights in and with some trust built, he took Virgil aside and told him the truth above. The lie, he said, was embarrassment, not deception.
- He then told Virgil why he and Marion were really travelling: **Underwood** (see `world_lore/Underwood.md`), a farming village a day's ride outside Haleyon, two years into a spreading affliction — green skin patches thickening into nodules and then into spore-pods, with the infected growing steadily less afraid, protective of the pods, and addicted to eating their fruit. The Church refused aid, citing the war in the north, and issued a quarantine order it has never enforced. The village offered payment and the Order sent Thaddeus and Marion, on the grounds that they have personal experience of transformation.
- They were riding to meet **Willowbread**, a colleague bringing samples and notes on something similar found growing further east.
- **Thaddeus asked Virgil to join them directly**, and offered to put in a word that would restore Virgil's standing with the Order. **Virgil declined**, citing the party's standing Church orders and the three people he had decided to stand with. Thaddeus did not push.
- **In the Porcine raid**, three beastmen broke the line and reached Marion, who has no combat training and could not run while carrying her husband. She was beaten nearly to death and had to be stabilized by the party. Thaddeus could do nothing but hold on to her.
- **Willowbread died** of wounds already dealt before the party arrived, after giving Thaddeus a rough location east and warning him of a **coven of witches** growing the pumpkins. His group stole samples by distraction rather than confront them, and his last words were an insistence that this had been the right call.
- Virgil's analysis of the salvage: the flesh is entirely inert; **all of the magic is in the seeds**, and it is three schools braided together — Enchantment, Necromancy, Transmutation. His conclusion, said out loud in front of everyone: *this isn't a disease, someone built it.*
- Thaddeus and Marion turned back toward Underwood with the notes and the salvage. The parting was warm on both sides.

## Running them

- Thaddeus is the most educated person the party has met, and they have mostly asked him about his skin. He knows a great deal about the Old Gods and has never been asked. (`sessions/Open Questions.md` lists this as one of four questions the party could answer right now if they bothered.)
- He has lied to the party once already, about himself, out of shame — which is exactly the kind of thing that makes a person wonder what else. The party has noticed.
- Marion is not scenery. She can speak into anyone's head, which means she can talk to a PC privately, in a crowded room, without her husband hearing it if she chooses. This has not been used yet.
