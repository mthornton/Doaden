**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
This document explains how to use the Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive — a collection of 34 adventures built over 3+ years — as a flexible toolkit for running campaigns in the dark-fantasy world of Doaden, rather than as a fixed, prescriptive campaign path.

## How the Archive is Organized (bullets)
- Adventures are grouped by Level and Experience Type: mostly self-contained one-shots, plus a few multi-part Escalation Arcs and Sagas.
- One-shot adventures are split into 3 level-scaling groups: Level 1–10, Level 5–13, and Level 5–20.
- Starting Adventures: **Burial Isle** and **Rise of the Jailer** are purpose-built onboarding adventures — simple, quick to run, and designed to introduce players to each other and to Doaden.
- **Man Eaters** and **Calden Keep** are two-part "Sagas" — earliest 5E adventures, each with a single recommended level (not a bracket) and built in two parts that benefit from time passing between them.
- **Escalation Arcs** are longer multi-part adventures charting major moments in Doaden's history:
  - *Crisis in the North* — Empire vs. the largest Beastman Horde in history, siege of Sturmenburg; includes "Bloodfever" (Aurox Minotaurs, ideal starting point), "Purgation," and "Under Cover of Darkness" (post-siege aftermath, best run after time away).
  - *Eternal Legions Rise* — journey from the Penitent Crusade's bastion into the Ashen Wastes to confront the Eternal Legions; "Dreadlights" (an Imperial Engineer escort mission through the Deadmire to the Black Fortress) is the ideal introduction.
- Additional Statblock & Lore booklets (no pre-made adventures) supplement the Archive with new characters, factions, and foes for homebrew encounters.

## How to Run / Combine Adventures (bullets — sagas, escalation campaigns, one-shots, level scaling)
- The Archive is a toolkit, not a script — order adventures however you wish; level brackets are guidance, not requirements.
- Use the **Path of Penitence (PoP) Campaign Guide** as the foundational framework for stitching adventures together.
- Use Hexcrawl-style travel sections between adventures to build hooks, atmosphere, and character background/desire exploration collaboratively with players (e.g., seeding Abhorrent encounters before a main adventure for tension and grudges/phobias).
- One-shots can each span multiple play sessions when embedded in a larger campaign, acting as narrative anchors; encourage GMs to invent their own material between them.
- Saga adventures (Man Eaters, Calden Keep) work best with a deliberate time gap between Part 1 and Part 2 — use the level recommendation to gauge when to return.
- Escalation Arcs can be run as standalone one-shots but need more significant adaptation to work outside their full arc context.
- Place names/NPCs in adventures (e.g., "Guerrin" in the Man Eaters siege) are swappable — reskin locations to raise stakes for your own party's established world.
- Use Page 43 of the Path of Penitence Campaign Book for guidance on leveling and atonement calculations upon quest completion — applicable to any adventure, including homebrew ones.

## Key Terms & Conventions (bullets)
- **PoP** = Path of Penitence, Bestiarum's campaign system for running a persistent dark fantasy campaign.
- **Penitent Crusade** — the in-world faction that can assign missions/hooks to the party.
- **Tier** ratings (e.g., Tier II, Tier III) and **APL** (Average Party Level) scaling with named difficulty bands (Medium/Hard/Nightmare/Torment, or Hard/Nightmare/Torment/Hell for higher-tier content) are used throughout the Archive to scale adventures to party strength — GMs can shift difficulty even mid-adventure.
- **Doaden** — the shared dark-fantasy campaign setting; its map/lore has evolved over the Archive's 3-year development, so contradictions between older and newer adventures are expected and intentional to embrace.
- **Escalation Arc** vs. **Saga** vs. **One-Shot** — distinct adventure formats with different structural expectations (see above).

## Things to Remember (bullets)
- Canon has evolved over time (narrative and map changes); when adventures contradict each other, pick what resonates with your table rather than treating everything as strict canon.
- The newer, more freeform Hexcrawl Map mechanics (from the Campaign Book) are the recommended default over the older fixed Doaden map.
- Statblock & Lore booklets are meant to inspire custom encounters/stories, not replace full adventures.
- Community/support resource: Bestiarum's Discord server for questions, tips, and sharing actual-play stories.
