                           THE WORLD OF DOADEN




W
             elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted monsters, where
             an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very essence of existence. It is a world of decay
             and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of glorious purpose now struggle to
             survive as cults and dark gods vie with one another to consume the last vestiges of
             humanity.

In Forbidden Psalm: The Tainted lands of Doaden you will find:

• Three scenarios created for the fantastic monsters from Bestiarum Games;
• Rules for monsters.

 These scenarios should be played in order.
 These scenarios should be played in solo or coop only.

You will need a copy of the Forbidden Psalm Core Book

S
           omething sinister lurks below the seaside town of Saltwich. Laurent Gilmiere, a cleric of the
           Church of Azerai and arcane researcher, was staying at the local inn while researching a
           source of powerful magical emanations. The Church has not heard from him in several
           months and fears the worst.
             What happened to Gilmiere, and what disquieting nightmares had he found reflecting in the
depths of Saltwich’s dark waters?
  Your warband will investigate the disappearance, uncovering his strange and maddening tale.

  Your warband have been hired by the Church of Azerai to track down a cleric, a middle-aged man by
the name of Laurent Gilmiere. Gilmiere was an accomplished scholar and arcane researcher, having
made a number of recent discoveries on the source energies of magic.
  Father Armitage, his mentor, explained to you that Gilmiere was last traveling south to follow a trail of
powerful emanations that he had discovered. Gilmiere was usually diligent about keeping in touch,
providing regular updates about his work.
  However, the last letter they received from him arrived several months ago. Father Armitage fears the
worst as the silence is alarmingly uncharacteristic of him. It should not be difficult to identify Gilmiere.

  He wears the yellow robes of the Azerai scholars and a silver pendant hangs around
his neck in the shape of a gull, a seabird that once adorned his family’s crest before he joined the priory.
In exchange for returning Gilmiere safely to the church, or confirming his fate, Father Armitage has
promised you each 15 gold.

  You have been traveling along the southerly roads for weeks. Once the grand roots of the old empire,
they are now nothing more than cracked cobblestones and withered dirt. After days without
encountering a single traveler, you come to a small seaside hamlet, a collection of lichen-crusted shacks
that descend from a ridge down to the shore. Perched high above on a nearby cliff sits a lonely abbey, its
walls cracked and crumbling.

  You head towards the Inn to see if anyone is there to help you in your investigation. The rotting wood
inn is filled with dusty tables and chairs. Taking a seat, you see a cauldron bubbling over a small
hearthfire along the far wall, filled with an oozing gray sludge that gives off a slightly sour aroma. In the
far corner of the room, a narrow staircase leads upward.
  Next to it, a hunched man sits at a counter, on top of which lies a small envelope bearing the red wax
seal of the Church of Azerai. The man watches you warily, but shuffles over as you sit. He moves with
an uneven gait, his swollen feet squelching on the wood floor. He is bald with a narrow head and
bulging eyes, and his skin gives off a sickly pale sheen.
  As he approaches, you are overwhelmed by an intense fishy odor.

    Dealing with him you find a note that leads you to Forlorn Abbey to continue your investigation.

                              SCENARIO I: THE ABBEY



y
          ou descend a gravel ridge into the lower levels of Saltwich, finding the houses in much
          the same derelict state; made of warped planks nailed haphazardly at slanted angles.
          The cobbled road is slick with saltwater and strewn with strands of seaweed.
           You step off the road into a small graveyard when a sudden clang fills the air; the discordant
          peeling of a large bell.
            You look to the source of the sound, finding a stone chapel at the far end of the graveyard.
At the top of the bell tower, a drunkard who you previously saw on your way into town flails about,
ringing the bell madly and yelling to the sea.

 “See me! See me! Come and get me!”

  With each ring, a screeching shiver shoots up your spine and rattles your skull, stabbing like sharp
daggers into your forehead. From the town, you hear wailing shrieks as angered men and tentacled
monstrosities begin to emerge from the squalid shacks.
  Turning towards the chapel, you find a wide broken window that has been boarded up by loose
wooden planks and rusted nails.

GOAL

  The warband must break down the boarded window in area D2 that leads to the chapel interior and
stop the Drunkard from ringing the bells.

REWARD

 You did not die!

SETUP AND TREASURE

 Set up as shown in Map 1.
  Each square is 1 inch by 1 inch. In area D2 you should place walls to represent the Chapel, with at
least 1 window.
  A model within 1 inch of the Wall can make a Strength test DR12 to remove a plank that blocks their
way into the Chapel.
  When 8 Planks have been removed the warband can leave the board via D2.

DEPLOYMENT

 All the warband models that are participating in this scenario should be placed in D1.



                                                                                                         5
THREATS

 In D3 (randomize which D3) you should at the start of the scenario place 1 Fisherfolk and 1 Strangler.
On each round at the start of the monster activation phase you should place another 2 Fisherfolk.

SOLO PLAY

As above but do not spawn Fisherfolk on Odd Rounds and only 6 planks need to be removed.

Battle ends when all warband members are dead or off of the board.

RPG TEXT BOX

When the party arrive read:

  Screams echo through the streets as the swarm of monstrous villagers begins to swell.
  They pour from their houses and shamble towards the chapel, drawing forth hooks and knives,
enraged by the ringing bell.
  You frantically yank the last wooden panel from the window and jump through the sill, into the
darkened mass hall of the chapel.

When the party makes it into the chapel read:

  You look around the musty chapel.
  To your left, at the far end of the hall is a sturdy door leading to the inner sanctum.
  Across from you, past rows of shattered pews, is a boarded window like the one you broke in from.
  At the end of the hall to your right is an enormous wooden double-door entrance secured by a thick
iron bar lock.
  Breaking it down you silence the bells. While in the chapel your party sees visions they cannot explain,
endless horrors.
  But for now, some peace.
  As suddenly as they came, the degenerate swarm of villagers disperses, returning to their darkened
alleyways.
  Looking to the ground, you find a crumpled note.

T
           he note your warband found leads you to the fishery where you will need to acquire a boat to
           take to the Sunken Temple or the Luminous Grotto.
           You see a long warehouse along the edge of the beach.
           The wide building is constructed from planks of shoddy, rotting wood, and the floor is a
           mossy cobblestone.
           Sets of large footprints laced with faintly glowing blue algae lead into the fishery from the
shore.
  A villager stands at the front door, pacing back and forth. Inside of the warehouse, you see several
small rowboats stacked along the walls.
  They look to have been used recently, still dripping water.
  Along their undersides are clusters of the same glowing blue algae.
  At the other end of the warehouse, you can make out a large figure sliding a cleaver through the belly
of a dead man, slicing him open with one clean cut and allowing his entrails to fall to the ground.
  A ladder rests on the side of the building, leading to a small roof.
  Get in kill those in your way and get a boat as quickly as you can.

GOAL

Kill the creatures and get a boat.

REWARD

A boat to make your way to the Sunken Temple or the Luminous Grotto.

SETUP AND TREASURE

    Your party has entered the fishery via the roof tops.
    The board should be set up as in Map 2.
    Each square is 1 inch by 1 inch.
    Fisherfolk should be placed in the area below G1.
    A Shambler should be placed in G4. In G5 Place two bodies that may be searched for a random item.

DEPLOYMENT

 Warbands should deploy in G2 area.
 Warbands will need to navigate down the walls into G4, to do so they must pass an Agility test or fall.
 Falling models take 1d4 damage.

                       SCENARIO III: TO THE DEPTHS



Y
        ou row out to a smooth black spire that rises above the waves, noticing that the dark
        contours of the temple descend down into the abyssal depths of the sea.
        Drawing near, you find the water lapping at a small circular chamber, no larger than a garden
        gazebo.
        You tie the boat to a pillar and step inside, finding the place empty.
        The gusting wind whistles quietly through the room as sea birds caw above.
You are about to leave when you hear something.
  A subtle whisper or just a foreboding feeling, you are unsure, calling you down, down into the black
abyss below.

  Rowing out to the glowing area below the Saltwich cliffside, you find the entrance to a cave partially
filled with seawater.
  Paddling inside, you find the tunnels are illuminated by glowing blue algae, which explodes in all
directions, covering the walls and saturating the water surface.

  The cave tunnel extends for some time before ending at a small beach.
  You tie your boat to a dry pillar of coral and prepare to venture deeper into the narrowing tunnel.
Many tracks in the sand lead back and forth around a bend to the right. Straight ahead, the tunnel opens
into a wide cavern, from which you hear slow, lumbering footsteps that shake the rocky ground with
each step.

GOAL

Kill the N’Gorroth Giant and discover what happened to Gilmiere.

REWARD

 The warband that slays the N’Gorroth Giant gains 10 XP, each participating warband gains 15 gold.
 For killing the Pastor or Priest the warband gains another 5xp per one killed.



SETUP AND TREASURE

 The room should be setup as shown in Map 3, each square is 1 inch by 1 inch.
 H1 is the deployment area. H2 contains 4 corpses that can be looted.
 H3 Is where the N’Gorroth Giant resides and H4 contains 1 Abyssal High Priest and 1 Abyssal Pastor.
 Between areas H3 and H4 is a drop that warbands cannot descent until the Giant is slain.
 The whole area is considered to have partial darkness.

DEPLOYMENT

As described above.

RPG TEXT BOX

When the party arrive read:

  Lumbering about the center of the enormous cavern is a monstrosity of abhorrent proportions.
  It is a giant humanoid shape, but its face is a bulbous glob of pulsating flesh, riddled with staring,
milky eyes.
 Large sacs on its neck heave slowly as it breathes.
  Elongated tentacles hang from this body, slinking about on the floor behind it, feeling the contours of
the cavern walls and leaving behind trails of sickly mucus.
 It paces back and forth, dragging a barnacled brass bell along the ground behind it

When the players kill the N’Gorroth Giant read:

 The behemoth abomination falls to the ground, its tentacles flailing as the bell clatters against the cold,
hard floor.
 It sputters and gasps, writhing in anguish until its strange guttural shrieks die down, and it stills.
 The black poisonous clouds evaporate slowly, and the eyes covering the wall of the cave disappear,
revealing a marble gateway constructed in an ancient architecture you have never seen before.

When the Players enter H4 read:

  You pass through an ancient archway, jumping down into a shallow, rocky pit of water that rises to
your ankles.
  A hunched figure stands before you with the stretched facade of a human face pulling around its head
like a tight mask. It wears the robes of an Azerai priest, and around its neck dangles a silver pendant of
a gull in flight.
