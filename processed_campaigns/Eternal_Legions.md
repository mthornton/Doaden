**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–13
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Two-part dungeon-crawl-into-siege campaign set in the Ashen Wastes, the blasted heart of the fallen Old Empire. The Black Fortress (Penitent Crusade) has lost contact with 20 Crusaders investigating the ruined Cathedral of Bones. The party is sent to find them, discovers the Eternal Legions (an undead army of the Emperor's former soldiers) reviving General Kourrakas, and is forced into a desperate running retreat that ends with defending a ritual to decrypt an ancient tablet (the Cypher) while Kourrakas himself bears down on them.

## Key Locations
- Black Fortress — Penitent Crusade's last bastion, spans the border between the Western Reaches and the Ashen Wastes; mission briefing/provisioning hub.
- Rustwind Pass — the stretch of Ashen Wastes containing the Cathedral of Bones; corrosive sandstorms.
- Cathedral of Bones — ruined Old Empire cathedral; surface level (A1-A4), hides the catacomb entrance; General Kourrakas's original burial site.
- The Catacombs / "Ash & Bones" (B1-B5) — Atrium Cubicula, Great Gallery, Sisters' Burial, Arcosolia Tombs, Gladius Gallery; locked behind 4 golden keys held by 3 Ossificators.
- Forgotten Depths / The Immortal Tomb (C-D) — mass grave chamber party falls into; Kourrakas's empty sarcophagus ("Here lies Kourrakas the Unyielding, Decimator of Worlds").
- Hall of Bones (E) — cathedral nave, site of the Siege Breaker boss fight and escape.
- Obelisk Valley / The Twilit Obelisk — pyramidal structure that decrypts the Cypher; ringed by 4 energy-feeding cuboids; site of Encounters 1 and 3.
- Cliffside Temple Ruin — Crusade outpost above the valley, safe from dust storms; site of Encounter 2 (Crusade Fall).
- The Necropolis — the Grave King's buried tomb-city, unexplored endgame location.
- The Swirling Sea — sandy region deep in the Wastes; home of the "Great Bone Patch" (thousands of drifting Malformed Constructs).

## Session Flow
**Echoes of War**
1. Ch.1 – The Cathedral of Bones: travel to Rustwind Pass, explore cathedral surface (A1-A4), Cloister Garden fight, recover golden key, descend via unlocked spiral stair.
2. Ch.2 – Ash & Bones: fight through the catacombs (B1-B5) to collect 3 more golden keys from Ossificators, unlock the final stairway.
3. Ch.3 – Forbidden Knowledge: floor collapses into the Forgotten Depths/Immortal Tomb; recover remaining Crusader badges, survive the wave-based Immortal Tomb ambush, escape via arcane lift, fight the Siege Breaker in the Hall of Bones.
4. Epilogue: party sees the full Eternal Legion army (200,000+) marching, Kourrakas among them, and flees into the dust storm — directly into Lord of War.

**Lord of War**
1. Encounter 1 – Beginning the Cypher Ritual: shelter with Rusken's outpost, descend to the Obelisk Valley, protect the scholar and clear bone-covered cuboids while Crusade soldiers hold the perimeter against scaling waves; ends at 60% decryption.
2. Encounter 2 – Crusade Fall: ascend to the temple ruin during a dust-storm sprint (DC 15 Athletics or take 4d6 damage), find Rusken's team already killed by the Silent Emissary, fight it off on the Temple Plateau.
3. Encounter 3 – Kourrakas: return to the valley to find the archeological team slaughtered; hold off/stall the regenerating Kourrakas while finishing the Cypher to 100%; escape via the Ballista Wagon.

## Secrets & Twists
- The party is always too late to stop Kourrakas's resurrection — he's already left the cathedral before Ch.3 ends.
- Kourrakas cannot be permanently killed in this adventure; he collapses to indestructible bones and rises again — the real win condition is completing the Cypher, not the kill.
- Rusken and his crusaders are killed off-screen (via psychic mind-blast, eyes/brains liquefied) by the Silent Emissary while the party is stuck on the far side of an arcane flare-up.
- The Cypher is only 1 of 3 tablets needed to rebuild the Demoncore, the weapon that destroyed the Old Empire — the Church of Azerai secretly wants it recreated.
- The Eternal Mother, mastermind behind the Legion, is theorized to be the former Mother of the Azerai church, present at (and transformed by) the original Demoncore detonation.

## Faction: The Eternal Legions
- Originally the God Emperor's chosen soldiers from the Valley of the Sun, raised from birth to never retreat, never surrender, and treat fear as death; conquered Doaden and founded the Holy Empire.
- Risen as undead by the chaotic energies of the Ashen Wastes; bones mutated into carapace-like armor stronger than steel.
- Driven by the will of the mysterious Eternal Mother; roam the Wastes in endless retinues with giant bone-harvesting columbines that reclaim the dead to swell their ranks.
- Ranks include: Immortals/Immortal Flagbearers/Lieutenants (soldiers, CR 3-5), Harvesters and Ossificators (former Azerai priestesses, CR 5-7, shape bone and raise new troops), constructs (Malformed Constructs, Amalgamations — mechanical, not true undead), Venators (crossbow snipers), Cataphracts (cavalry), Hellions (flying ambushers), Praetors and Lord Magistrae (commanders/judges), Silent Emissary (herald/negotiator, now only brings war), and the boss-tier Siege Breaker and Kourrakas the Unyielding.
- True undead troops (Immortals, etc.) retain will/intelligence and resist charisma/wisdom effects; constructs do not and are purely mechanical.

## Campaign Context
- These two adventures are Chapters 2 and 3 of the "Escalation: The Eternal Legions Rise" campaign (Lvl 5-13); Chapter 1, "The Dreadlights," is summarized separately.
- Echoes of War (Ch.2) and Lord of War (Ch.3, the finale) form a direct continuous narrative — Lord of War picks up immediately where Echoes of War's epilogue ends, mid-flight from the Cathedral of Bones.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Kourrakas's army (200,000+ strong) is still marching toward the Black Fortress — it was only stalled, not stopped, at the end of Lord of War.
- Two more Cypher-like tablets are unaccounted for; the Church of Azerai's Demoncore ambitions are a slow-burn moral hook for later sessions.
- Lord Magistrae, Praetor, and Kourrakas himself are flagged as future "Hit Team" targets to delay the Legion's advance.
- The Grave King and the Necropolis remain an unexplored, extremely dangerous endgame location.
- Lunden the Engineer (from the Deadmire adventure) is available as a recurring friendly face at the Black Fortress if that adventure was played.
