# Mycena Mortifera
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/B_One-Shot Adventures (Lvl 1-10)/Mycena Mortifera (Lvl 1-10)/Mycena Mortifera (Lvl 1-10).pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A one-shot dungeon crawl into a fungal cave system beneath a deserted village. Reanimated, fungus-infected villagers (zombie thralls) attack the party at the village outskirts; tracing the blue spore trail leads down three levels of an infected cavern to destroy the Mycena Mortifera, a mind-controlling mycelium. Scales via APL: Beginner (1–2), Easy (3–4), Medium (5–7), Hard (8–10).

## Key NPCs
| Name | Role | Motivation | Location | Notes |
|------|------|------------|----------|----------------|
| Mycena Mortifera | Sentient fungal core / final boss | Spread mind-controlling spores, defend itself via minions | Cavern Level 3, Mycelium Cavern (area 9) | AC 10, 150 HP; destroying it ends telepathic spore control, cleanses affected creatures over 24 hrs |
| Fungal Shepherds* | Undead spore-thralls guarding the core | Poison/debilitate intruders, protect the Mortifera | Mycelium Cavern & Spore Lake | Use ranged poison spores; sacrifice self-safety to protect the Mortifera |
| Fungal Knights* | Undead elite guardians | Knock party into the ravine, defend the core | Mycelium Cavern | Use parrying strike and fungal growth (mushroom cover/hazard zones) |

## Key Locations
- Infected Village — abandoned village where reanimated zombified villagers first attack; investigable for supplies/clues (blacksmith shop, herbalist's hut).
- Cavern Level 1 — Entrance (deep hole to Spore Lake below), Fungal Farm (edible/medicinal mushrooms, guarded), Jagged Tunnel (stalagmite hazard), Stairway Descent, Infected Dwellings (loot).
- Cavern Level 2 — Spore Lake (glowing spore-saturated water, ambush point), Stairway Descent 2.
- Cavern Level 3 — Stalactite Chamber (falling-rock hazard), Mycelium Cavern (final boss room with a 15-ft spore-filled ravine).

## Session Flow
1. Opening: party finds the deserted village at night, attacked by fungal-infected zombies.
2. Investigate the village (Investigation checks) for clues and supplies; find the spore trail into the woods.
3. Descend Cavern Level 1 — Fungal Farm fight, Jagged Tunnel hazard, optional loot in Infected Dwellings.
4. Descend Cavern Level 2 — cross the Spore Lake, fight zombies/fungal shepherds.
5. Descend Cavern Level 3 — navigate the Stalactite Chamber, enter the Mycelium Cavern.
6. Final battle vs. fungal shepherds/knights defending the Mycena Mortifera; destroy or harvest the core.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Village blacksmith shop (DC 10+ Investigation): 1d4 torches, 50 ft. rope, hammer, 1d8 pitons, 1d100 gp.
- Herbalist's hut (DC 15+): 1 restorative ointment, 1d100 gp.
- Fungal Farm: harvestable mushrooms = 1d4 rations + 1d4 potions of healing (DC 15 Nature).
- Infected Dwellings (Perception DC 5/10/15): oil barrels; 2d100 gp buried; 1d4 potions of resistance.
- Final loot roll (4d10 + difficulty bonus): 28+ yields a Parrying Buckler* (uncommon); otherwise nothing.

## Secrets & Twists
- The zombified villagers are not classic undead in origin — they are fungus-controlled thralls infected by the Mortifera's spores.
- Any creature that dies while affected by the Mortifying Spores rises as an undead servant of the Mortifera within 1 hour.
- Killing the Mortifera doesn't instantly cure victims — the spore effect withers and fades over the next 24 hours.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Track which PCs failed the Mortifying Spore save (disadvantage on saves for 24 hrs) as they approach Level 3.
- Decide in advance whether the party harvests a Mortifera sample (for an alchemist reward hook) or destroys it outright.
- Fungal shepherds can be repositioned to force ranged engagement across the Spore Lake — plan terrain accordingly.
