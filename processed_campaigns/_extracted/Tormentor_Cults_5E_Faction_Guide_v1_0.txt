              The Tormentor Cults
   In the darkest corners of Doaden, a seed of corruption takes root, a whispered collective of shadowed
figures meeting in the darkest underbellies of towns and cities to commit atrocious acts of inhumanity,
leaving in their wake mutilated corpses of their tortured victims. Dedicated to the explication of agony,
this seed would grow over the centuries to become the Tormentor Cults, a secretive collective bound by
their worship of a malevolent entity known as the Prison Lord. Their dark lord resides not in our world but
in the extraplanar Prison Realm—a domain writhing with the psychic energies of agony and despair. The
cults serve as the earthly heralds of this dreadful realm, committed to a macabre agenda of merging their
lord's domain with our reality.

   The origins of the Tormentor Cults can be traced back to the immediate aftermath of the Cataclysm, a
disastrous event that shattered the very fabric of the world and left Doaden reeling in fear and
uncertainty.

   In these desperate times, the Prison Lord’s whispers cut through the chaos, promising power through
pain and retribution against oppressors. These whispers found eager ears among the disenfranchised and
downtrodden — those crushed under the weight of a ceaselessly hostile world, and those who wished
simply to see the lands burn and to revel in chaos and anarchy. The objectives of the Tormentor Cults are
as insidious as they are catastrophic. Driven by the belief that truth can only be achieved through
suffering, they seek to thin the veil between our world and the Prison Realm.

   Through blood-soaked rituals and unholy pacts, they summon forth abominations and spread
corruption as they attempt to bring about a convergence of our world and the realm of suffering. This
apocalyptic vision drives the cults to commit acts of terror and violence, destabilizing the already fragile
order of Doaden in preparation for their dark lord's ascension.

vv                   Beliefs and Practices
                       At the heart of the cult’s insidious influence lies
                     a twisted creed centered on the glorification of
                     suffering. They seek dominion through suffering
                     and the infliction of torment – both on oneself and
                     on others. In the throes of agony, both physical and
                     mental, the veil between the material world and
                     the Prison Realm thins, allowing for communion
                     with their dark master.

                       By orchestrating grotesque scenes of torture and
                     mutilation, they seek to replicate the conditions of
                     the Prison Realm, making their dark lord's influence
                     more palpable in the material world.

                       The screams of their victims are offered up as
                     solemn prayers of worship, appeasing their dark
                     lord’s appetite for agony and violence. These
                     sacrificial rituals, if grand enough and charged with
                     powerful arcane energies, begin to corrode the veil
                     between worlds, bringing the Prison Realm into
                     reality.


                     The aim of the Tormentor Cults is the convergence
                     of the material world with the Prison Realm,
                     creating a singular plane of existence governed by
                     the Prison Lord and fully subjugated by his will. This
                     catastrophic vision is the driving force behind their
                     most heinous acts, as they believe that only
                     through the total merging of realms can true order
                     be achieved — an order based on the universal
                     dominion of chaos and suffering. Central to the
                     cults’ practices are their rituals, which range from
                     blood offerings to grotesque ceremonies involving
                     human sacrifice. These rituals are designed to open
                     rifts between worlds, allowing the horrors of the
                     Prison Realm to seep into Doaden. Each ritual is
                     both a spiritual act and a strategic move, aimed at
                     destabilizing the region and expanding the cults'
                     influence.

                Leaders
                The shadowy orchestrators behind the relentless
                advance of the Tormentor Cults are the Harbingers,
                master tacticians and strategic leaders who direct
                the cults’ most sinister agendas. These formidable
                figures are the dark generals of their ranks, each
                imbued with the Jailer's insidious will and a
                profound understanding of the dark magic that
                binds the material world to the Prison Realm.
                Where a typical cultist attack might be marked by
                chaotic savagery and wild rites, the presence of a
                Harbinger signals a shift towards meticulous
                cruelty and terrifying precision.

                   Their operations are not merely local
                disturbances but regional threats, as they deploy
                coordinated assaults that combine brutal efficiency
                with arcane potency. Under the command of a
                Harbinger, the cults execute large-scale rituals and
                strategic strikes that significantly erode the barriers
                between worlds, bringing Doaden ever closer to a
                catastrophic convergence with the Prison Realm.
                Each Harbinger's influence is a palpable shadow
                over the land they control, turning what were once
                isolated incidents of horror into orchestrated
                campaigns of fear and subjugation, marking them
                as one of the most formidable enemies behind the
                cult’s sinister machinations.

Progressive Rifts. The goal of the cult is always to
open a rift to the Prison Realm. Weaker bands of
cultists open small rifts that affect only the
immediate area and are only powerful enough to
bring forth a handful of lesser fiends, while more
organized bands open devastating rifts that corrupt
entire cities or regions and draw forth godlike
entities from beyond. This brings a unique flavor to
each adventure depending on how large the cult
operation is. The greater the rift, the more of the
Prison Realm will bleed over to reality, causing
horrifying physical and psychological effects on any
parties that manage to enter the area. As the cult's
ritual nears completion, the effects on reality
become more pronounced and dangerous. This
progressive alteration of the environment serves to
heighten tension and stakes as the party
approaches the final battle. Use the Progressive
Rifts table to determine how serious these effects
are, or as inspiration to create your own. The table
includes a description of the rift’s effects on the
area as well as a suggestion for the effects that you
may roll, which make it difficult for the party to
invade the area. The party should roll for these
effects periodically as stated in the table.

Minor Rift, while in a longer campaign, each

of the rifts. This escalation mimics the size of the

Adherents of the Cult
 The mortal followers of the Tormentor Cults are the backbone of the Jailer's dark army, driven by a
fanatical belief in the power of suffering. These individuals come from all walks of life, drawn together by
their shared devotion to the Prison Lord and his promise of a new world order through pain and torment.
The cultists believe that through the infliction of suffering, they can thin the veil between the material
world and the Prison Realm, bringing their dark lord's realm into reality.

Cultists are typically encountered in small cells or larger groups, depending on their mission. They are
deeply embedded in the societies they seek to corrupt, often posing as ordinary citizens by day and
performing dark rituals by night. Their presence is marked by subtle signs of corruption—strange symbols,
eerie chants, and unexplained disappearances. In their hideouts, cultists surround themselves with
macabre artifacts and twisted remnants of their rituals, creating an atmosphere of dread and unease.

In towns and cities, cult adherents can be encountered subtly influencing local politics, spreading rumors,

    Like the whispering draft of air that offers only

is a terror despite its subtlety. Leading his cult from
the shadows, this roguish lieutenant engages in a
shadow war of trickery, theft and assassination.
The brutal murder of a Magistrate in their home,
the theft of the entire town guard’s wages, or
strange and devious traps, left at the site of a
sacrifice; these are the tools by which the Oubliette
sows discord and suffering in praise of his dark
masters.

Tactics
The Oubliette fights with the cunning and precision
of a rogue. He is a master of assassinations,
ambushes, and guerrilla tactics, striking swiftly and
disappearing before his enemies can react. His
cultists mimic his approach, using the shadows to
their advantage, setting traps, and employing hit-
and-run tactics or deploying poisons in the right
time and place to eliminate key targets. They often
leave behind gruesome displays of their victims, a
signature of the Oubliette's influence, to spread
fear and uncertainty. A campaign featuring the
Oubliette would involve uncovering hidden
threats, avoiding deadly traps, and facing a foe who
finds ways to strike back when the party least
expects.

The Ascendants
While most mortals are cast into the Prison Realm unwillingly, the Tormentor Cultists aspire to tread upon
the accursed ground of that infernal plane. Through the culmination of their dark rituals, this opportunity
is granted. Stepping into the Hecatomb’s roiling pools of blood, they are drawn into that nightmarish
reality, where they are blessed with the maddening majesty of their Lord’s kingdom. While most never
return, some are cast back through the Hecatomb, suffused with their master’s energy. These individuals
are changed, ascended, becoming beings of both worlds and direct conduits to their master’s predatory
will. Though these Ascendants are capable of communication, their remaining spark of mortal essence is
trapped within a labyrinth of madness, borne from witnessing eons of dark secrets and terrible suffering
within the Prison Realm. Consorting with these beings risks a similar fate.

The Ascendants return as nightmarish embodiments of the Prison Lord’s will, cruel servants and
instruments of terror, twisted reflections of the suffering and torment that permeates the realm beyond.

Summoned through blood-soaked rituals and sacrificial offerings, these creatures spread chaos and fear
wherever they go, dragging more souls into the Jailer's grasp. Ascendants vary in form and function and
are often summoned to guard key locations, hunt down enemies of the cult, or enforce the cult’s will
during major rituals. Their presence is always a sign of doom and mere sight of these fiends can drive
mortals to madness, their forms so horrifying that they defy natural comprehension.

In gameplay, the fiends serve as formidable adversaries
for the players. They can be encountered in various
settings, from cult strongholds to remote ritual sites. Their
abilities and tactics vary, providing unique challenges and
requiring the players to adapt their strategies.

Collector Entities
 While the Ascendant beings of the Tormentor Cult still bear vestiges of their lost humanity, those entities
wholly confined within the Prison Realm are utterly alien to the human mind. Known as ‘Collectors,’ they
encompass a bewildering array of forms and characteristics.
  The Collectors are integral to the peculiar ecology of the Prison Realm, serving as agents in the relentless
consumption of anguish. Any unfortunate souls sacrificed and cast into the Prison Realm inevitably
become fodder for these strange entities.

  Despite their diversity, the behavior of Collector entities is uniformly instinctual. They are not beholden
to the intelligence of higher beings, instead possessing an insectile drive that more closely mirrors the
denizens of some rotting log than some mighty demonic court - to feast upon and process matter for
unthinking infinity.
  To behold the Collectors is to glimpse the true essence of the Prison Realm—a mindless and indifferent
predator whose singular purpose is to consume and thrive.

abilities enable them to swiftly home in on the
scent of potential sacrifices for their dark master.

The Jailer
   Among the most formidable subjects of the Prison Lord, the Jailer embodies the essence of abject
terror and insatiable greed that permeates the grim expanse of its realm. It reigns supreme within its
domain, the Fortress of Anguish—a macabre labyrinth of iron-barred cells teeming with agonised screams.
The souls of those sacrificed to the Jailer are forever trapped here, their tormented cries echoing endlessly
through the spiralling halls, fuelling the entity's essence for eternity.
         To even just entreat with the Jailer is a momentous blessing for the zealous followers of the
Tormentor Creed. Its cryptic and violent messages, transmitted through the veil, are meticulously
deciphered by deranged mystics and translated into dreadful directives for the Tormentor Cults. These
orders disseminate slowly across the myriad Cult cells throughout Doaden, igniting waves of violence and
terror that further nourish the Jailer and its dark master.

At the apex of their power, when the Tormentor Cults manage to rupture a significant tear between
Doaden and the Prison Realm, they may draw the attention of the Jailer itself. Emerging from a pool of
blood and viscera, the Jailer manifests as a titanic force of violence—a living avatar of the Tormentor Cult’s
dark creed.
What ensues after such a summoning is an orgiastic rampage of violence and destruction. Driven by an
insatiable hunger for fresh souls, the Jailer bursts forth from its dank lair, demolishing stone walls and
shattering wooden palisades in its relentless pursuit of prey. Only the most formidable heroes or entire
brigades of the Penitent Crusade have been recorded to confront and subdue the horror, casting it back
into the dread realm from whence it came.
