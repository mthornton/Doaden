## Session 1 — Played 16 Aug 2026 (≈2.5 hrs)

**At the table:** JonLief, Virgil, Wolfram, Luthien. *(No Gimble yet.)*

### The campfire *(the last night on the road, two days from Solteres)*
- Ysabel's household introduced: **Perrin, Dov, Symon, Cael, Mina** *(see `world_lore/Ysabel's Household.md`)*. **Dov looks up to Wolfram**, and the beginnings of a friendship.
- **Ysabel's plea, refused for the fifth time.** The Juggernaut is "three times the size of" Wolfram. Magic is allowed in a trial. **Trials are the finale of a festival day in the coliseum**, after plays, acrobats and animal acts, and always end in a death.
- **Ysabel's history told in full:** smuggled cloth → Ironwake's tax → she cheated him, he seized a shipment → she built a weaving mill on Bramblefen slaves (margins doubled) → **opened a dress house that was best in the city within a season** → cheated him a second time → **he had her niece poisoned and framed her.**
- The plan that night: see her to Solteres, part ways, report to the Church there.

### The rope at dawn
- **Erwana** caught in the leavings. Ysabel calls her "it" and wants to hand her to Ironwake's men; JonLief kneels and asks where she came from.
- **Cobb's Ledger changes hands.** Erwana's story: Old Cobb, the tunnel under the drainage grate, and the book he told her to take to a Church in *another* city — not Solteres, which looks the other way about slavery. She cannot read.
- **JonLief reads it:** last entry ~6 weeks ago; **two months ago, an elf woman and her son — Elizabeth and Rowan.**
- **The pendant:** Erwana recognises it. The woman was in a cell near hers, very sick, coughing; guards took her and the boy out one day.
- **The scarred buyers:** every woman in the holdfast was sold in one lot to hooded men covered in scars, shortly after the elf woman and boy were taken. Erwana was the only one left behind ("smelled too bad").
- **Ysabel on slavery**, unprompted and unembarrassed: non-humans are beneath humans; Terms is a public good — **"Dov is mine for three more years."**
- **Dov on Bramblefen:** held a few days before Ysabel bought him; **four or five guards**, brutes not soldiers; Vaskar looked fierce.
- **Erwana freed** with a pack of food and 5 silver, and sent to **Ald's Star — "tell Ald that Wolfram sent you."**
- **The deal:** Ysabel fronts a buyers' cover at the gate and helps free JonLief's family; **she gets Cobb's Ledger once everyone is clear of Bramblefen.**

### Arrival — 5:30 pm
- **Eight guards visible**, not four or five. Slaves building a ring of sharpened stakes.
- The gate guard: **the Juggernaut is here** for a warm-up match **at sundown** against a stubborn gnome slave. *(Sundown fixed at **8:00** at the table.)*
- Inside: a drunken guard clubbing a half-orc at a post. **Wolfram half-recognises one of the slaves.**

## Session 2 — Played early Sept 2026 *(exact date not recorded)*

### The yard
- **The Deeps feels like St. Pyotr's**, weaker. Every PC felt it.
- **Anselm Vogt.** Wolfram "inspects" him. He served Wolfram's father, **went to the Bishop after Wolfram's arrest and told him Wolfram's reasons; the Bishop never wrote it down.** The slaves will **break out tonight during the fight** and need weapons from the armory. JonLief covered by asking the other slaves about an elf woman and child.

### Vaskar's house
- Ysabel, JonLief and Virgil go in. **A man under a tarp in a chair, wheezing, blood on the wall** — Old Cobb, unacknowledged.
- On the balcony Vaskar needles Ysabel about Ulfric. She asks for **seamstresses and a small child** to crawl inside the looms.
- **Vaskar:** no women — **sold them all to "men in dirty hooded robes, skin covered in scars," at a premium.** No children.
- ⚠️ **Ysabel: "some of the slaves you do have may have worked as tailors — I want to inspect your stock."** *(The Ashgate buyers' selection procedure. See `world_lore/The Art-Items.md` §5.)*
- **Virgil spots the burn circle** outside the north wall: bright green growth in a scorched ring.

### The tour
- **Small prison:** eight cells, **one lever opens them all**, and **a key ring hangs beside it.** The party decided **not** to take the keys.
- **The Deeps:** two of **Ironwake's professionals** guard the end door. An elf slave in the cell next to Cobb's: the woman lay still and **did not seem to care about anything while the boy begged her to eat**; she was taken away; the boy was taken up the **tower** the same day.
- **The masked prisoner** in Cobb's old cell: iron mask, arms bound inside his coat. The guards are betting on how many pieces he'll be in.
- **Luthien works the guard:** green patches on her skin → **Vaskar had her burned outside the wall — "don't want The Green here."** First mention of **the Green** (a plague out east). **The boy was isolated in the tower for a week, then sold** — "ask Vaskar, or Old Cobb, but Cobb won't survive the beating."
- **Luthien looks in on the Juggernaut**: an enormous man playing with a straw doll, who cowers. **Durgan** rushes the window — *"Leave him alone! He has never hurt anybody!"* — and Mott charges, bellowing, in a spiked iron helmet.
- **The armory:** heavy chain, new padlock — **but the door is rotten at the bottom.** Wolfram can shoulder it. Luthien starts working on conjuring a key instead.

### The burn circle
- **JonLief and Virgil go out to it. Virgil takes samples, digs to the roots, and finds Elizabeth's bones.** JonLief prays and vows to learn the truth.

### Sundown
- **Vaskar throws Old Cobb's body into the yard: "This is what happens to people that betray me!"** Cobb is dead.
- The masked prisoner is unmasked in the ring: **a gnome with the brand of a Penitent.** *(Curtain.)*

## Session 3 — Played 4 Oct 2026. Closes the chapter.

**At the table:** all five — **Dave joins as Gimble Beren.**

### The fight
- Luthien puts a turning-aside working on Gimble. **Mott stops, cannot make himself want the kill, tears up a barricade section and throws it into Ironwake's two professionals.** Gimble goes through the hole.
- **Wolfram shoulders the armory door off its hinges.** The slaves run for it.
- **Durgan's decision: "Mott. Kill the guards."** Mott starts on them.
- Anselm hands out weapons; Wolfram holds the door. Virgil walks a column of light across the guards by the pens. JonLief works round the outside with a sword.
- **Gimble opens every cell in the small prison** — the old and the weak come out. *(His first act in the campaign.)*
- **Mott exhausts himself and collapses; the balcony guards beat him; Durgan throws himself on top of him** and takes the blows.
- The slaves pull the ring down by hand and **kill every guard still standing. 19 of 20 dead.**

### Vaskar's trade
- **Vaskar grabs Ysabel and retreats into his house** with **the last surviving guard.**
- Wolfram negotiates. **Vaskar trades Ysabel, unharmed, plus the location of the Working Ledger, for his freedom.** The last guard volunteers the location too.
- **The party debates killing him anyway. Wolfram will not let anyone break their word.**
- **Vaskar, leaving: Ironwake will probably kill him** for losing the fen and the slaves.
- **Luthien strips Vaskar's bag** (rations and a lot of coin). **Wolfram drops a small handful back in** and pushes him out the gate, then **gives the rest to the freed.**
- ✅ *(Stated at the table later)* **Vaskar took a horse from the stable outside the walls.**

### After
- **The barrel in the Deeps:** iron-hooped, pitch-sealed, too heavy — **the same blood** as Dol and St. Pyotr's. **Virgil takes a sample; the rest drains into the floor.**
- **Luthien plays for the freed and sees a glowing woman with four arms, then six,** at the edge of his vision. **He does not realise who she was.**
- **Anselm offers the service of all thirty.** They cannot go to Solteres (half are not human). **They are sent to Ald's Star, "Wolfram sent us."**
- **The ledgers split as promised:** **Cobb's Ledger → Ysabel.** **The Working Ledger → the party.** It names Rowan's buyer. ⚠️ **JonLief has not read it yet — he reads it at the start of Chapter 8.**
- **Durgan and Mott leave north** before dawn, for a mountain range Durgan knows.
- **Baltus Thole breaks the gate in, with his apprentice.** The party tells him everything. **He is pleased.** He tells them **a Penitent has latitude to act beyond orders in the Emperor's service.** He approves the plan: **Rowan first, then the women or the barrels.**
- **Virgil leaves for Thaddeus and Underwood** to find the cause and cure of the Green. **Baltus approves**, says the party is out of its depth, and **leaves his apprentice with them.**
- The party sleeps inside Bramblefen.

### Table changes this session
- **Retcon — slave count:** **thirty survive**, eighteen of whom fought; the rest were too old or weak and were freed with them. *(The prep's "Eighteen" roster is gone; the named freed who still matter are in `world_lore/The Bramblefen Freed.md`.)*
- **Player change:** Virgil's player wanted a new character. **Virgil is now an NPC with Thaddeus; the player now plays Baltus's apprentice** (name and backstory TBD).
- **Level:** the party is **level 4** at the end of the chapter.
