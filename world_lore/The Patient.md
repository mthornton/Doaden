# The Patient
### Herald of the Tormentors — the woman the party left in an unlocked cell
#### Hanne Vetter, potter, of no fixed place

> ⚠️ **DM ONLY.** This is the consequence of Chapter 4, and it is the best one available.
>
> **She runs the holding east of Underwood where the Ashgate women are kept.** Arc Plan Chapter 13.
>
> See `sessions/Chapter 4 Sy Pytor.md`, `The Penitents Chronicle.md` §VI, `The Ashgate Purchases.md`, `The Three Faiths.md`, `Elizabeth and the Green.md`.

---

## 1. What the party actually did

**18 August 1126, St. Pyotr's Sanitorium, the torture chamber.**

They found a woman alive among the clutter and the empty healing-potion bottles, and the bottles explained her: she had been there for weeks, taken to the edge of dying and healed back from it, and taken there again. Scars of every age layered over one another.

*"No more. No more. I beg you. Let me die."*

**They cut her loose and healed what could be healed.** They watched the flesh close under their hands while she stared past all of them at nothing.

What they could not fix was the rest of it. **She flinched from kindness exactly as hard as she flinched from anything else**, as though she could no longer tell the difference. She could not walk unassisted. She could not be reasoned with, or reassured, **or asked her name.**

So they took her down the corridor to an empty cell, **put her inside, left the door unlocked, and stacked what rations they could spare within reach of her hands.**

Then they went back to work.

> *"Every one of them carried that out of the building. Not one of them has entirely put it down."*

### ⚠️ Three things to keep straight

1. **They did not do this to her.** Ignatius did, and then the cult did. **The party healed her, fed her, and left the door open**, and they argued about it, and they hesitated. **Do not let her be an accusation.** She never accuses them of anything, and that is worse.
2. **She never learned their names and may not know their faces.** She stared past them. ⚠️ **What she remembers is the healing and the food**, not the people. She would recognise the *act*, not the men.
3. **Nobody in that party ever got her name.** ⚠️ **So somebody else named her** — and her real one is sitting in a book they are carrying. **See §3a.**

## 2. 🌑 What came for her

**The party left. The building did not get quieter.**

She was alone in an unlocked cell in an ancient asylum where a mad priest had spent years deliberately wearing the barrier thin, with a black fog still pouring out of a stone ring two corridors away.

**Brudda Death came to her the way he came to Elizabeth** — in a nightmare, in the dark, to a woman with nothing left. *(`Elizabeth and the Green.md` §2.)*

### The offer

**He did not offer her death.** He cannot, and would not; he does not kill. **He keeps.**

What he said to her was the only honest thing anyone had said to her in a month:

> *"You asked to stop being hurt. I cannot give you that."*
>
> *"I can give you the other side of it."*

**And he kept his word exactly.**

| She asked for | She received |
|---|---|
| To stop being hurt | ⚠️ **Pain no longer means anything to her.** She is not immune to injury. She bleeds. **She simply does not receive pain as pain any more** — it arrives as information, like a change in the weather. |
| An end | **A vocation.** |

> ### 🔑 Two women, two bargains, one entity
>
> **Elizabeth traded herself for her son.** She knew the terms, she negotiated one of them, and she got what she was promised, and the price was that she stopped being able to love him.
>
> **The Patient traded herself for herself.** And got what she asked for.
>
> ⚠️ **Both deals were honoured to the letter. That is what Brudda Death is.** Put these two documents side by side before you run her, because the party may eventually be in a position to notice the pattern — and then somebody will wonder what he would offer *them.*

## 3. ⚠️ And she let the other one out

**The party made two decisions in that building about two cells.**

In the patient cells there was a man, screaming himself hoarse to be released. The party looked at him and something did not sit right and **they left him locked.** They were correct. He had tortured the woman chained to the wall beside him — *"look at the gift of suffering I gave her, it lasted two night falls"* — and he raked his own face open in front of them to prove his devotion, and begged to be allowed to **"see the rising."**

**He was a Tormentor. He is still alive.**

> **When she came out of her cell, she opened his.**

He has been with her ever since. **He is her first adherent, and he is the one who named her**, because the cult needed a word for a woman who had endured more than any of them had ever managed to inflict on themselves.

> ⚠️ **Their two Chapter 4 decisions have had a child together.** One door left unlocked, one door left locked, and the thing on the other side of the locked one is now second in command to the thing on the other side of the unlocked one. **Do not explain this at the table. Let a player remember him.**

## 3a. 🏺 HANNE VETTER — the name in the ledger

### The entry

**She is in the Working Ledger. The party has been carrying her name since the night Bramblefen fell.**

> *One woman, sold singly. **Hanne Vetter.** Potter, skilled, own wheel. Ashgate. Carriage to St. Pyotr's Sanitorium.*
>
> **Three months back.**

### 🔑 Why this needs no roll

**The party has stood inside that building.** They know exactly what was in the torture chamber at St. Pyotr's, because they healed her and left food in reach of her hands.

> **They have both halves already.** A name with a destination, and a memory of what was at that destination. ⚠️ **Do not gate this behind a check** — let it land the moment somebody reads the line, quietly, in a room, while looking for eleven other women.
>
> **Then they carry it for however many sessions it takes to reach her.** And when they get there they already have the name and the only question is whether they use it. **That is a decision, not a die roll.**

### ⚠️ And the consequence nobody will see coming

**St. Pyotr's was an Ashgate destination.** Which means it was the Tormentors' Catalyst site **before** the holding east of Underwood.

> **The party walked into it in Chapter 4, killed five cultists, broke the ritual, and left alive knowing exactly where it was.**
>
> ⚠️ **The building is still standing. Nothing was burned.** What the cult lost was not the site — it was **privacy**, which is the same way they lost Dol *(`The Barrel Trail.md` §2)*. A secret operation that has run for thirty-eight years by being boring does not keep working people to death in a building four armed strangers can find again.
>
> **So they moved the Catalyst work east and left the door where it was.** The stone ring cannot be carried; the women can. ⚠️ **That is why the eleven from Bramblefen were carted inland instead** — and it is why the Hecatomb is still at St. Pyotr's waiting for the finale. **The party caused the relocation, has never known it, and can work it out from two lines in a book.**

### 🏺 What being a potter does to her

**This is the part that ties everything together, and she believed it before any of this happened.**

A potter knows that **unfired clay is nothing.** Greenware is mud that remembers being mud — fragile, useless, and it dissolves in the first rain. **It has to go in the kiln or it was never a thing at all.** And the firing has to be *slow*, because heat applied too fast cracks the piece, and you lose it.

> **She has not changed her beliefs. She has changed her material.**
>
> Everything she does to those sixty women, she does in the sincere conviction that **she is finishing them.** Not hurting them — **firing** them. Slowly, patiently, exactly as hot as they can take and no hotter, because she is a craftsman and she does not waste pieces.
>
> ⚠️ **That is why she calls it mercy, and it is why she cannot be argued out of it.** You are not debating cruelty. You are debating a potter about pottery.

### 👐 Her hands — describe this, do not roll for it

**She has scarred herself everywhere she can reach. The Tormentors do; it is sacrament; she is better at it than any of them.**

> ⚠️ **Except her hands. Her hands are completely unmarked.**
>
> Every other inch of her is a map. **The hands are clean**, and she has never been able to say why, and the one time her own adherents asked she changed the subject.

**The party can see this.** No check. Just describe a woman covered in deliberate scars whose hands are perfect, and let somebody notice.

### 🏺 And there is a wheel in that holding

**Somewhere in her rooms there is a potter's wheel, and clay, and finished pieces.**

**She never stopped.** She would not call it art — she would say she is keeping her hands in — and the work is **extraordinary.** Better than anything she made before, because she has a great deal of patience now and nothing frightens her.

> ### ⚠️⚠️ And here is the loop closing on itself
>
> **Making something beautiful is an act of worship, whether the maker has heard the name or not.** *(`The Three Faiths.md` §2.)*
>
> **Hanne Vetter is one of Saraswati's unwitting congregation. She always was — that is why Ashgate bought her.** And she is *still* generating mana for the goddess whose blood her own order is burning as fuel.
>
> 🔑 **If a player picks up one of her pots and says it is beautiful, that is worship, and it feeds Saraswati.** The party can admire a Herald's pottery and be doing the one thing the cult exists to prevent. **Nobody in that building knows. Say nothing. Let it be true.**

## 3b. ⚠️ THE MAN WHO MADE HER — and the party failed to kill him too

**Correction worth having clearly: the party did not kill the herald at St. Pyotr's.**

> *"They struck the herald hard enough to break the ritual, but he proved strong enough to escape out the back of the chapel before they could finish him off."* — `Party History.md` *(now in `_archive/`)*

**The very tall, very pale man in the ornate headdress is alive.** He is the one who smelled Saraswati on Luthien before anyone in the party had heard her name. **He is the Herald the Arc Plan has them killing in Chapter 13.**

### 🔑 And he is the one who found her

He fled his own chapel, wounded, with four strangers in his building. **He came back — days later, weeks later — to a sanitorium with five of his people dead in it.**

**And in a cell near the torture chamber he found a woman who had endured more than he had ever managed to inflict on himself, sitting with the man she had let out.**

> ⚠️ **He recognised what she was immediately. He brought her into the order. He is the reason she is a Herald.**
>
> **The party's two Chapter 4 failures found each other in that building.** The man they could not finish and the woman they could not save.

### Which one of them is in charge now

**Recommend: she is, and he does not like it.**

He made her, and she has surpassed him, and he is the only person in that holding old enough in the order to resent it. ⚠️ **That is a crack the party can get a knife into** — and it means the Chapter 13 site has two bosses who do not entirely want the same things.

**It also means he is the only person there who remembers the party's faces** *(along with the man from the locked cell — see §3)*. **Two witnesses, both of whom hate them, neither of whom she will listen to about it.**

## 4. Who she is now

### 👁️ First sight

**She is healthier than the last time they saw her, and that is the most disturbing thing about her.**

Fed. Upright. Walking without help. Clean, in the plain grey of the order. The scars are all still there, every age of them layered over each other — **and there are new ones, neat, deliberate, and only in places she can reach.** *(The Tormentors practise on themselves. She is better at it than any of them.)*

⚠️ **And her hands are untouched. See §3a.**

**And she is lucid.**

That is the horror and you should let it land slowly. She was babbling. She screamed things that were not words. **The madness did not go away. It organised.**

### 🗣️ Voice

**Run her in the exact register of every other institution in this campaign.** Ysabel, the Church, Vaskar, the Office of the Lost Flock: **bewilderment first, then the category argument, then cold.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sound** | Calm, patient, warm. **She has a great deal of time.** |
| **Register** | A nurse explaining a necessary procedure to a frightened person. |
| **Doctrine** | **She calls what she does mercy**, and she means it, and she is giving people exactly what she was given. |
| **Never** | Raises her voice. Hurries. Threatens. **Enjoys it.** |
| **Tell** | ⚠️ She does not flinch. From anything. A knife goes into her and she keeps talking, and she finishes the sentence. |

**Four lines to have ready. She talks like a craftsman, because she is one.**

> *"Unfired, you are mud that remembers being mud. It washes off in the first rain and nothing is lost, because there was nothing there."*

> *"It has to be slow or you crack. I lost a great many pieces learning that."* — ⚠️ **and she is not talking about pots.**

> *"I know precisely how much you can take, and I know it from the inside, and I will not take more than that. Nobody who was careless with me is in charge of you. **I am.**"*

> *"You will be hard. You will ring when you are struck, and nothing after this will ever be able to hurt you." (quite gently) "I am not doing this to you. **I am finishing you.**"*

### ⚙️ Why she is extraordinary at the work

**The Tormentors need the Catalyst of Suffering sustained, not spent.** *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md` §2.)* Prolonged, continuous, year on year — which requires keeping people alive and in agony indefinitely, which is a *skill*.

> **She spent weeks on the receiving end of the most sophisticated version of that in the world, and she survived it, and she was paying attention.**

**She is better at it than Father Ignatius ever was.** Ignatius was a believer with a theory. **She is a practitioner with a case study.** She knows where the edge is because she was held on it.

⚠️ **The sixty women in that holding are alive because she is very, very good at her job.** Say that out loud when the party gets there.

## 5. 🔑 THE CRACK — and it is the party's own doing

**One thing in her life does not fit her theology, and she has never been able to put it down.**

> **Four strangers who owed her nothing closed her wounds, left food within reach of her hands, and left the door unlocked.**

**She ate the rations.** Every one of them. She has thought about that more than she has thought about anything else, and it does not fit anywhere in a doctrine built on suffering as a gift, and she has constructed a great many explanations for it and none of them hold.

| And this is the mechanism | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **Pain stopped meaning anything. Kindness did not.** | The one thing in the world that can still reach her is somebody being gentle with her, **and it is agony.** |
| 🏺 **And in her own language it is a category error** | **You cannot mend greenware. You can only mend a thing that has been fired.** Kindness is water on unfired clay — it should do nothing, it should wash straight through, **and it does not**, and she has no framework for that at all. |
| **Which the party already discovered** | In that room, in Chapter 4. *"She flinched from kindness exactly as hard as she flinched from anything else."* **It was true then and it is the only thing still true.** |

> ### 🎯 So the way to beat her is the thing they already did and thought had failed.
>
> **Make that mechanical.** See the stat block.
>
> **And she does not know who they are.** Not until one of them is kind to her again — and then she will, immediately, completely, and she will not be able to bear it.

## 6. ⚔️ Stat block — The Patient

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil — CR 9 (5,000 XP)*

**Reskin of the Herald of Agony** *(`Tormentor_Cults_Faction_Guide.md`)*, rebuilt around pain-immunity and the refusal to kill.

- **Armor Class** 15 (reinforced grey habit)
- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
- **Speed** 30 ft. — she never runs

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 17 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 17 (+3) |

- **Saving Throws** WIS +9, CHA +7, CON +7
- **Skills** Medicine +13, Insight +9, Persuasion +7, Religion +7
- **Condition Immunities** frightened, charmed
- **Senses** passive Perception 15 · **Proficiency Bonus** +4

### Traits

- **★ Beyond Pain.** She does not receive pain as pain. **She never loses concentration from taking damage, is never frightened, and no effect that functions through agony, intimidation or torment has any effect on her.** She does not react to being wounded in any visible way. *(Tell the players she did not flinch. Every time.)*
- **★ She Will Not Kill You.** ⚠️ **Her attacks and effects cannot reduce a creature below 1 hit point.** Ever. **She has no interest in killing anyone** and she regards a death as a failure of craft. *(This is the whole character. A party fighting her cannot be killed by her — and will discover that being kept alive is the threat.)*
- **The Edge.** She knows, exactly, where it is. **She has advantage on attack rolls against any creature below half its maximum hit points**, and she can tell a creature's current hit point total by looking at it.
- **Twelve Weeks of Instruction.** Advantage on Medicine checks, and she can stabilise or revive a creature as a bonus action. **She uses this on the party.**
- ⚠️ **VULNERABILITY — Kindness.** When a creature within 10 ft. performs a **genuine, costly act of kindness toward her** — healing her, freeing someone in front of her, feeding her, saying her name gently — she must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw.** On a failure she is **incapacitated until the end of her next turn**, and takes **no reaction** and **no lair action** that round. **A second failure in the same encounter and she stops fighting entirely.** *(Not a trick. The real crack. **Let the party find it and let it work.**)*

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Instrument attacks, or one Instrument and *The Gift.*
- **Instrument.** *Melee:* **+8** to hit. *Hit:* **2d6 + 4 piercing.** Cannot reduce below 1 HP.
- **★ The Gift (recharge 4–6).** One creature she can see within 30 ft. **4d8 psychic damage** and a **DC 17 Wisdom** save; on a failure the target gains **one level of exhaustion** and is **restrained** until the end of its next turn, having briefly understood what she is offering. **Cannot reduce below 1 HP.**
- **Hold Them Here (1/day).** All allies within 60 ft. may use *stabilise* as a reaction for 1 minute. ⚠️ **This is how a holding keeps sixty people alive for two years.**

### Reactions

- **Patience.** When a creature drops to 0 hit points within 30 ft., she may stabilise it. **She will do this to the party.** *(The first time it happens the table will not know how to feel, which is correct.)*

### 🏚️ Lair actions — the holding

**In her own house, on initiative 20:**

1. **The Accumulated.** Every creature not of the cult makes a **DC 15 Wisdom** save or takes **3d6 psychic** damage. **Two years of it is in those walls.**
2. **The Attendants.** Two **scarred adherents** arrive from an adjoining room. *(Cultist, CR 1/8 — and they do not flinch either.)*
3. **Thin Ground.** ⚠️ The barrier here is worn through *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md` §8c — the torture is the drill)*. Until the next lair action, **all healing in the lair is halved and the dead do not stay down the first time.**

### If it comes to a fight

**She does not fight to win and she does not fight to escape.** She fights to **keep**, and she is entirely willing to lose a building.

**She will not pursue a fleeing party.** She will ask where they think they are going and then let them go, and she will still be there.

## 7. Can she be reached?

**Yes. At real cost, and it is not easy, and it should be possible.**

*(⚠️ The Chapter 7 review specifically warned against a campaign where every rescue fails. **This is where you prove it doesn't.**)*

**What it takes:**

1. **Kindness, repeatedly, under fire** — see §5 and the stat block.
2. **Somebody saying her name.** ⚠️ **Hanne.** They have it — it is in the Working Ledger *(§3a)* — and saying it out loud to her face will do more than any spell. **Four independent routes to it, so this cannot be missed:**

   | Route | |
   |---|---|
   | ⭐ **The Working Ledger** | In their own pack. *One woman, sold singly. Hanne Vetter. Potter, skilled, own wheel. Ashgate. Carriage to St. Pyotr's.* |
   | **Cobb's Ledger** | Ysabel has the same line. **A reason to go back to her that has nothing to do with liking her.** |
   | **St. Pyotr's admission register** | Still in that building, and they have to return there for the Hecatomb. |
   | ⚠️ **The man from the locked cell** | He named her, so he heard it. **He would never surrender it — but he might use it as a taunt**, which hands it over for free. |
3. **Freeing the sixty in front of her.** She has built her entire theology on the necessity of what she does. **Undoing it in her sight is the argument.**

> ### ⚠️ And what she gets, if they succeed, is not a happy ending.
>
> **She becomes a person again, and that person has to live with the holding.**
>
> Pain starts meaning something again — all of it, hers and everyone's. Brudda Death kept his word; **taking the gift back takes the whole gift.** She asked to stop being hurt and the only route out of that bargain is back into it.
>
> **She may ask them not to.** That is a far better scene than a fight, and the party should have to choose.
