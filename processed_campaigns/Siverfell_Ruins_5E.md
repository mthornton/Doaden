**Level range:** 5-16
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A haunted, semi-open dungeon crawl through the drowned town of Siverfell, sunk in a sunless underground ravine and accessible only via two 1,000-ft towers. The town is cursed by the Queen of Sorrows (ghost of Lady Ninaeth), whose grief over her dead child Lilac has spawned undead and madness-inducing cursed waters. Party can explore for lore/treasure, fight through to the opposite tower, and/or lift the curse by dealing with Lilac's remains. Difficulty scales by APL: Medium (5-7), Hard (8-10), Nightmare (11-13), Torment (14-16).

## Key Locations
- North Tower (1a) — entry point; rusted iron gate (DC 15 STR or destroy at AC 19/30 HP — smashing alerts wretches in 1b)
- North Outskirts Upper/Lower (1b/1c) — deranged wretches and cultists; mostly passive unless provoked
- Haunted Bridge (2) — mournwraith/banshee ambush at the midpoint
- North Isle (3) — Town Square (3a, scavenging), Secluded Ruin (3b, Annals of Siverfell I), Ruined Town Hall (3c, Annals II + banshee ambush), Ruined Tower (3d, Lady Ninaeth's arcane-locked sanctum with necromancy scrolls and a viewing platform), Flooded Causeway (3e, DEX-save gauntlet over cursed water)
- Mid Gate (4) — 50-ft wall dividing outer/inner Siverfell; guarded by the strighast; lever accessible only from the far side (swim/climb/magic to cross)
- Town Center (5) — Grand Plaza (5a, scavenging + Annals III), Ruined Residences (5b, Queen of Sorrows boss encounter)
- Float Town (6) — floating market platforms; scavenging (Annals IV) and a rowboat for crossing the water safely
- Island Temple (7) — isolated shrine; crypt holds Lilac's cursed remains, the source of the town's haunting
- South Landing (8) — exit point; second tower back to the surface (DC 15 STR gate, AC 19/30 HP)

## Session Flow
1. Hook (Lost Histories / Cursed Spirits / Bridge Collapse) brings the party to the North Tower.
2. Descend the North Tower, open/smash the gate (DC 15 STR or AC19/30HP — smashing risks aggravating wretches).
3. North Outskirts — navigate deranged wretches and cultists (1b/1c).
4. Haunted Bridge — mournwraith/banshee ambush crossing to North Isle.
5. North Isle — explore Town Square, Secluded Ruin, Town Hall, and Ninaeth's Tower for lore, loot, and Annals I-II; cross the Flooded Causeway.
6. Mid Gate — bypass/open the wall (swim through cursed water, climb, or magic) and survive the strighast-led ambush.
7. Town Center — Grand Plaza scavenging (Annals III); Ruined Residences — Queen of Sorrows boss fight.
8. Optional: Float Town — scavenge, find the rowboat (Annals IV); Island Temple — investigate/resolve Lilac's cursed remains.
9. South Landing — ascend the second tower to conclude the adventure (curse lifted or not, per party choice).

## Treasure & Rewards
- Town Square scavenging (5+/10+/15+/20+): gold, 1d4 potions of greater healing, 1d4 gems (500 gp each), a potion of resistance
- Ninaeth's Tower research (2x DC 15 checks): spell scrolls of Bestow Curse and/or Blight
- Grand Plaza scavenging (5+/10+/15+/20+): Annals of Siverfell III, potions of greater healing, gold, a signet ring (750 gp)
- Float Town scavenging (5+/10+/15+/20+): working rowboat, Annals of Siverfell IV, gold, 1d4 gems (500 gp each)
- Queen of Sorrows Boss Loot (roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus): 30+ Cloak of Mourning* (rare); 1-29 nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- Defeating the Queen of Sorrows does not end the curse — she will reappear later unless Lilac's remains are dealt with at the Island Temple
- The Queen's grief-curse is what draws in and drives mad the "deranged wretches" haunting the outskirts — they are pilgrims of sorrow, not hostile by nature
- Disturbing Lilac's bones angers any remaining spirits (especially the Queen, if still alive) and provokes an attack

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Cursed Waters rule applies anywhere in Siverfell: touching/lingering in the water risks necrotic damage (scaling Beginner 1d8 to Hard 4d8) plus a DC 10 WIS save against Short-Term/Long-Term/Indefinite Madness on a failed DC 15 DEX save
- Descending or ascending either tower takes ~30 minutes each way
- Undead throughout (mournwraiths, howling banshees, strighast, Queen of Sorrows) share resistances to acid/cold/fire/lightning/thunder/nonmagical weapons and immunity to cold/necrotic/poison plus most conditions
- Mid Gate can only be bypassed by swimming (triggers Cursed Waters), climbing (DC 20 DEX per 10 ft or fall), or magic (Fly, Gaseous Form)
