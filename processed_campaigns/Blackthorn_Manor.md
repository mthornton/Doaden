**Last processed:** 2026-07-23

**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Lord Geddyn's once-noble knights have become "beast knights," twisted and corrupted after their master's pact with the mother of beasts. The party must infiltrate the walled, fortified Blackthorn Manor, fight through its knights and beast-corrupted denizens, and stop a ritual in the master residence summoning Ydir, a malformed spawn of the mother of beasts. A self-contained one-shot; scales APL 1–10.

## Session Flow
1. **Arrival** — party reaches the fogbound, walled manor; portcullis is shut
2. **Grounds exploration** — Outer/Inner Courtyard patrols, optional NPC allies (Oola, Iyk, Brog) provide info, keys, and minor aid
3. **Investigation** — Library's Scroll of Ydir and family history reveal the ritual and Geddyn's fall
4. **Escalation** — Great Hall and Knight's Quarters encounters as the party pushes toward the manor's heart
5. **Black Hall showdown** — Knight Lieutenant boss fight guards the master residence
6. **Master Residence finale** — stop the cultists mid-ritual before Ydir fully manifests

## Treasure & Rewards
- Footlocker Loot Table (area 4, roll d10 per unlocked footlocker): weapon+1/shield+1/ammo+1, beast knight plate armor (disguise utility), Blackthorn Manor Map, pouch of 1d100 gp, or trifles
- Hidden relic — golden chalice with rubies, 250 gp (Chapel altar, DC 15 Perception)
- 2 spell scrolls — False Life, Ray of Enfeeblement (Chapel, area 7)
- Potion of healing — Stables saddlebags (DC 15 Perception)
- Knight Lieutenant loot table (4d10 + difficulty bonus): **Ring of Eldritch Parry*** (uncommon) on 28+, otherwise nothing
- Brog's blacksmithing service — upgrades one mundane weapon/armor to +1 if he's freed

## Secrets & Twists
- The manor's outer walls are covered in "beast vines" — sentient carnivorous plants that entangle and pierce anyone touching/crossing them (piercing damage scales with difficulty)
- A secret entrance exists behind the master residence (area 13) for a stealthy approach or emergency escape
- Iyk's forehead scar reads "liar," carved by the knights after he refused to abandon his old faith — a quiet tragedy the party can choose to address
- Defeating all cultists in the master residence is sufficient to end the threat — Ydir's incorporeal, flickering form vanishes without needing to be fought directly as a separate boss

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Three alternate adventure hooks (Royal Edict, Wanted Poster, Personal Vengeance) reframe the ending's emphasis — confirm which one the DM used before the final session
- Lord Geddyn himself is never encountered — a loose thread for a potential sequel or campaign tie-in
- The "mother of beasts" itself/its full nature and origin is left unexplored/unexplained — open hook for future development
