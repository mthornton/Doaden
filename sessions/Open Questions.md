# Open Questions
*For the party. Everything below is something you know that you don't know.*

**Where we stand:** four Penitents of the Church of Azerai, branded, owing five hundred years each. Sent to Dol Island to find a missing team and eliminate whoever was operating there. We did neither. We have been out of contact with Vozruk for months, we are two days from Solteres escorting a woman we barely know, and nobody has told us what to do next.

---

## The woman in the tomb

- Two dozen bodies lie in a sunlit cave on Dol Island, dead about two years. No weapons. Homemade jewelry. **Who were they, and what were they doing on that island?**
- Wolfram saw a woman chained inside a cracked, bleeding tomb. She looked back at him — frightened, and sorry. Then she was gone. **Where is she now? Was she ever there?**
- Cultists were bleeding that tomb into barrels and shipping them out. **What is the blood for, and where did that ship go?**
- A cultist we caught bit off his own tongue and choked to death rather than answer one question, and died smiling. He shouted **"Hail Adephagia!"** first. **Who is Adephagia, and what does she want badly enough that dying is the easier option?**
- At St. Pyotr's, a cultist accused us of chasing revenge for **Saraswati** being *"outplayed"* by Adephagia. **Who is Saraswati — and who did they think we were?**

## Brudda Death

- The same cultists promised that **Brudda Death** would *"tutor"* us in suffering. They said the name like a favour they were doing us. **Who is he?**
- Father Ignatius, the caretaker of St. Pyotr's, wrote that he could feel the barrier between this world and **another realm** growing thin, and that he meant to wear it through so the spirits infesting his patients would be drawn back. Then somebody nailed him to a wall. **Did he succeed?**
- The black fog over St. Pyotr's killed a bird the moment it touched it. **What is it, and is it still there?**
- We broke a ritual in the chapel, but the herald in the headdress got away. **Where did he go, and did we actually stop anything?**
- **We never opened the trapdoor in the garden.**

## The people

- A very old man sat down with us at the Inn. Nobody else in a crowded room could see him. He thanked us for stopping the summoning at St. Pyotr's — which we hadn't told anyone about — and left. **Who was that?**
- The hounds that killed Alice and Red had iron bars and spikes worked into their bodies. That is not something that happens to an animal. **Somebody made those. Who, and why send them at a cabbage farm?**
- Master Thaddeus lied to us once already, about his own body, out of embarrassment. **What else has he not told us?**
- We never found the Penitent team sent to Dol before us. We never found the crew of the *Hreowan* either. **Do people just stop existing out here?**

## Underwood

- A farming village a day from Haleyon has been turning green for two years — patches, then pods, then spores, then death. The infected stop caring and start protecting the pods. **What is doing this?**
- The magic is in the **seeds**, not the fruit: enchantment, necromancy and transmutation braided together. That is not a disease. That is a design. **Designed by whom?**
- A **coven of witches** is growing them somewhere east. Willowbread died getting that much out.
- The Church refused to send help because of **a war in the north**. **What war? Since when?**

## Us

- Camp guards wear the Mark of Atonement, faded. So Penitents can be released. **Has anyone ever actually seen it happen?**
- Vozruk has had no word from us in months. **Does he think we're dead, or does he think we ran?** Which one is worse for us?
- Fid gave Luthien the Ball of Undeath against Vozruk's direct order, and slipped Wolfram a second knitting spider behind his back. **Why is the camp's inventor quietly arming us?**
- We are servants of a Church that has outlawed slavery, walking toward a city that sells people in the open. **What are we allowed to do about that?**

## What we're carrying

| | |
|---|---|
| **The amulet** | **One charge left.** It goes one place only: the pentagram in the crypt on Dol, from anywhere. *When would we ever want to be there?* |
| **The book** | *Myths of the Old Gods*, taken from the St. Pyotr's library. **Nobody has read it.** |
| **The map** | Off a dead cultist. It got us to St. Pyotr's. Is there anything else on it? |
| **The keys** | Father Ignatius's ring. We never found out what half of them open. |
| **The guitar** | Bone. Taken off a pile in the armory. Nobody ever asked whose it was. |
| **The Ball of Undeath** | Thrown right, it only hurts the dead. Thrown wrong, it hurts us. |
| **Two knitting spiders** | Wolfram has both. |
| **Six jars of pickled cabbage** | Taken from a dead man's house. We are not proud of this. |

## Questions we could actually answer, if we bothered

1. **Read the book.** It has been in the pack since St. Pyotr's.
2. **Ask Ysabel about Ironwake.** We have been walking beside her for two weeks and have asked her almost nothing about the man trying to destroy her.
3. **Ask Thaddeus about the old gods.** He is the most educated person we have met and we mostly talked to him about his skin.
4. **Ask anyone in Solteres about the barrels.** Everything that moves through that city goes through somebody's books.

## What we said we'd do

- Wolfram told Ald there were no more beasts near his village. He has not been back.
- We agreed to see Lady Ysabel to Solteres. She has asked all four of us to fight for her. All four of us said no.
- We were ordered to find out what happened on Dol Island and eliminate whoever was operating there. **That order has never been rescinded.**
