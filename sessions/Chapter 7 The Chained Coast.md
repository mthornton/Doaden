# Chapter 7: The Chained Coast

**Party:** 4 players — three at level 3, one level-4 druid (APL ≈ 3)
**Reskin of:** `processed_campaigns/Prison_Breakdown_5E.md` (Iron Gauntlet → the slaver holdfast **Bramblefen**)
**Core beat:** JonLief learns his wife and son were never killed — they were taken by slavers, and the trail runs to Solteres and Lord Ironwake.

> **DM reskin map (at a glance):** Iron Gauntlet prison → **Bramblefen Holdfast** (fen slaver compound). The prison "riot" → a **slave uprising** the party can exploit. Urvakh the Betrayer → **Vaskar the Brand**, the Chainmaster. Warden Ingfar (tortured captive who holds the key info) → **Old Cobb**, an enslaved former slaver-clerk who knows where the "old stock" was sold. The Ghost → **the Silent One**, a caged killer. Grolo (self-serving informant) → **Denrick**, a turncoat overseer. The unreachable Panopticon (ticking clock) → **the slave galley *Maw*** departing downriver — where the trail leads next.

---

## 0. DM Notes — the truth behind the hook

JonLief believes his clan leader's warriors hunted down and killed his exiled wife and son. **They didn't.** The warriors found them, but killing a healthy woman and child was worth less than selling them. They handed the pair to a slaver ring for coin and reported the deed done. That ring feeds the **Bramblefen Holdfast**, a fortified processing camp in the fens outside Solteres, which supplies "household stock" to wealthy buyers in the city — chief among them **Lord Leofard Ironwake.**

**A synthesis worth confirming with the player:** JonLief's wife was "not acceptable" to his noble clan. If she was **non-human or half-human**, that single fact explains *both* why the clan rejected the marriage *and* why the slavers (who, per Chapter 5, deal only in non-humans and half-humans) took her alive instead of killing her. It ties his personal tragedy straight into this arc's villains. Ask the player if they're happy with that reading before the session.

**Suggested names (rename freely):** wife **Maren**, son **Rowan** (a young child at exile; now roughly 8–12 depending on your timeline). Clan: leave as "his clan" or name it at the table.

## 1. Getting the party there (from the Solteres road)

The party is escorting Lady Ysabel to Solteres. You don't need them to care about her trial — you need them to cross paths with the slavers. Pick whichever on-ramp fits:

- **The freed runaway (recommended).** A half-elf slave, collar still on, staggers onto the road at dusk, hunted. She's fled Bramblefen. If saved, she describes the compound, the pens — and, if JonLief is present and shows the old token/crest he carries, she **recognizes a woman and boy who were "old stock," sold on to a city lord's house.** That single line lights JonLief's fuse.
- **The auction notice.** In a waystation or Solteres tavern, a discreet handbill advertises a "livestock" auction at Bramblefen — non-human stock only. Ysabel's people quietly avoid the topic; a barmaid mutters that "Lord Ironwake's steward always gets first pick."
- **The ledger scrap.** Among goods on the road (or lifted from the Chapter 5 slaver merchants, if they survived), a torn manifest lists sales going back years — including an entry a sharp PC could connect to JonLief's family.

Whatever the on-ramp, the goal is the same: **the party goes to Bramblefen to free captives, and JonLief goes to find the truth.**

> **Read-aloud — first sight of Bramblefen:**
> *"The road gives way to black fen. Ahead, a timber holdfast squats on a mud island, ringed by a palisade of sharpened stakes and a single watchtower. Smoke and the stink of tar and unwashed bodies drift on the wind. From somewhere inside comes a sound you'll not soon forget — the flat, rhythmic crack of a lash, and under it, chains. Then, faintly, shouting. Something inside is coming apart."*

The shouting is the start of a **slave uprising** — your cover, and your clock.

## 3. The Holdfast — area by area

Ten linked areas, same skeleton as Prison Breakdown. The slave uprising rages in the background: slavers are distracted, and freed captives will help if the party arms/leads them.

1. **The Gate.** "Toll guards" (disguised slavers) try to turn the party away — a merchant caravan, nothing to see. Insight/Perception exposes the collars and cudgels. Push in → ambush.
2. **The Auction Yard.** Muddy pen-yard with an auction block. Overturned in the uprising; a hidden knot of slavers ambushes from behind stacked cages.
3. **The Whipping Post (the Gallows reskin).** An overseer is flogging a captive as an example while the revolt spreads. Rescue the captives here → they give the compound's layout and where "the old clerk" is held. Slavers here are busy and won't aid the yard fight.
4. **Overseers' Barracks (Guardhouse reskin).** Off-duty slavers drinking; a captive locked in a sweatbox. Free them for the **keys** (armory + pens) and word of the **drainage tunnel** and **the Silent One** in the cages.
5. **The Armory + Watchtower.** Locked (barracks key, or DC 15 Dex / break DC 20). Ambush inside; the watchtower lets a PC scout Vaskar's numbers at the Chainmaster's hall.
6. **The Pens (Cell Block A reskin) — JonLief's search.** Rows of cages of the enslaved. **This is JonLief's scene.** He can free people, ask after a woman and boy, and — at the end cage — find **Old Cobb's** empty cell and his hidden ledgers (see Section 5). Denrick is caged here and will "help."
7. **The Deep Cages (Cell Block B reskin).** Worst of the holding; two overseers beating a mob of captives. Freeing them can unlock **the Silent One's** cage.
8. **The Chainmaster's Hall (Warden's Office reskin).** Walled yard (locked gate) → ground floor (Vaskar's guards) → upper room where **Vaskar tortures Old Cobb** for the names of leaked buyers. The boss beat.
9. **The Drain (Secret Tunnel reskin).** Smuggler's drainage channel from outside the palisade to behind the Hall — bypasses the yard. **Denrick's trap trail:** a spiked sump (DC 15 Dex), a rolling barrel of tar (DC 15/10), and a rat swarm if the party carries Denrick's "lucky" sack.
10. **The Galley *Maw* (Panopticon reskin — do NOT resolve).** At the fen dock, a slave galley is loading the "premium stock" to sail downriver to Solteres before dawn. The party almost certainly can't stop it this session — it's the **ticking clock and the road to the next chapter.** Whoever the trail points to (Section 5) may be aboard or already gone with it.

## 5. The emotional core — what JonLief finds

Old Cobb's ledgers (Area 6) + Old Cobb himself (freed in Area 8) are the payload. In the manifests, an old entry matches JonLief's wife and son: taken years ago by "clan men," processed at Bramblefen, sold as household stock. **Cobb remembers them** — and knows where they went. Choose the beat that fits your table (all three keep the arc alive and point at Ironwake):

- **A — Split and onward (recommended).** Maren was bought years ago by **Lord Ironwake's household in Solteres** (she may still be there — a servant, or a quiet hostage). Rowan, older now and "spirited," was recently sold on and is **aboard the galley *Maw*** or just shipped. → JonLief has a living lead in Solteres *and* a desperate clock. Threads straight into the Ironwake arc.
- **B — The gut-punch.** Cobb confirms Maren died in the pens years back (he can show where she's buried in the fen), **but the boy lived** and was sold to Ironwake's estate. Grief + a reason to march on Ironwake.
- **C — Both alive, both gone.** Both were sold to Ironwake's household and are in Solteres now. Cleanest path to a rescue/confrontation next session; least tragic.

> **Read-aloud — the ledger (adapt names):**
> *"Cobb's finger, trembling, stops on a line in faded ink. 'A woman and a boy. Brought in by hard men who called it clan business. She wouldn't stop asking after a man named JonLief.' He looks up at you. 'They didn't kill your family, friend. They sold them. And I wrote it down.'"*

**The Ironwake seed.** Whichever option you run, the ledger and Vaskar's strongbox contain the arc's connective tissue: a **writ of protection stamped with Ironwake's chained-fist signet**, and a standing order that Ironwake's steward gets "first pick of household stock." Bramblefen operates under Ironwake's protection — and his house **bought JonLief's wife.** Now the party has a personal reason to bring Ironwake down, which loops back into Ysabel (his other victim) as a potential ally, not a burden.

## 6. Treasure & rewards

- **Armory search** (1d20/PC): 15–19 a *potion of healing*; 20 a *+1 weapon/armor/shield*; else nothing.
- **Vaskar's strongbox:** ~300 gp in mixed coin, the **Ironwake writ**, sale ledgers, and a set of **master collar-keys** (frees any captive; symbolically potent for JonLief).
- **Denrick's stash** (Area 9, DC 20 Perception / DC 15 with a clue): 500 gp — if the party ever trusts him enough to find it.
- **Freed captives:** a pool of grateful NPCs — future contacts, informants in Solteres, or a couple who can fight alongside the party in the finale.

## 7. Secrets & twists

- **Denrick is bait.** His "helpful" tunnel intel is real, but he sold its location to Vaskar and seeded traps, hoping the party dies so he can loot them. His "lucky sack" gift draws the rat swarm and boosts enemies' Perception against the carrier — **do not tell the players.**
- **Vaskar took a "brand" for himself.** He wears a trophy collar off a warrior he broke — possibly kin to the Silent One, which is why the Silent One goes for him first if freed.
- **The galley won't wait.** The *Maw* sails at dawn regardless of the fight. If the party dawdles clearing every cage, the premium stock (and the freshest lead) leaves with it — a genuine consequence, not a fake-out.

## 8. Where it leads (next-session hooks)

- **To Solteres & Ironwake:** the writ + Cobb's testimony put a personal target on Ironwake. JonLief wants his wife back; the party now has cause to care about the rigged trial by combat — and Ysabel becomes a natural ally against a shared enemy.
- **The galley *Maw*:** chase it downriver, or race it to the Solteres docks.
- **Freed-captive fallout:** a camp of newly-freed non-humans needs protection/relocation — and slavers (and Ironwake) will want them silenced.

## 9. DM decisions to lock before you run it

- Confirm with the player: is **Maren** non-/half-human (the reading that ties clan + slavers together)? Keep or replace the names **Maren / Rowan**.
- Pick the **Section 5 option (A/B/C)** for the family's fate.
- Decide how the party first hears of Bramblefen (**Section 1** on-ramp).
- Decide whether Denrick and/or the Silent One get freed — it sets the boss difficulty.
