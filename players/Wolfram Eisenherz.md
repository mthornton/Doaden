## Class Features

**Divine Sense** — 3 uses per long rest. As an action, until the end of your next turn you know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within **60 feet** that is not behind total cover, and whether any place or object within 60 feet has been consecrated or desecrated.

> ⚠️ **At Ald's Star, Wolfram told Ald he would feel creatures like the cage hounds "from a couple of miles off."** That is far beyond Divine Sense as written. Treat it as a DM ruling — either a granted boon, or Wolfram overstating his confidence to reassure a grieving man. **Decide which, because he made a promise to a village on the strength of it.**

**Lay on Hands** — a pool of **15 hit points** per long rest. As an action, touch a creature and restore any number of points from the pool. You may instead spend 5 points to cure one disease or neutralize one poison.

**Fighting Style: Protection** — when a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your **reaction** to impose **disadvantage** on the attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.

> *Chosen because it is literally what he does — stepping between Ald's pitchforks and the party, holding the line over JonLief in the chapel at Saint Pyotr's. If Todd remembers taking Defense instead, that's +1 AC (19) and no reaction.*

**Divine Health** — immune to disease.

**Spellcasting** — Charisma. **Spell save DC 12** · **Spell attack +4**.

### Oath of Devotion (Level 3)

**Channel Divinity — 1 use per short or long rest:**

- **Sacred Weapon.** Action. For 1 minute, add your Charisma modifier (**+2**) to attack rolls with a weapon you're holding. It emits bright light 20 ft., dim 20 ft. more, and counts as magical.
- **Turn the Unholy.** Action. Each fiend or undead within 30 ft. that can see or hear you must make a **DC 12 Wisdom save** or be turned for 1 minute.

**Oath Tenets:** Honesty · Courage · Compassion · Honor · Duty.

## Background — Noble

**Feature: Position of Privilege.** People of high birth treat you as one of their own; commoners make way.

> **Dormant, and dangerous.** Wolfram is a branded convict a very long way from Westfield. Invoking his name works on anyone who knows the Eisenherz crest and rebounds badly on anyone who knows what he was convicted of. **Anselm Vogt at Bramblefen knows the crest on sight** — he rode under Konrad — and that is the single most important use of this feature in the campaign so far.

## Who he is

Son of **Baron Konrad Eisenherz** of **Westfield**, in the Western Diocese. Raised to arms and raised devout, and by his own account he "frequently hunted monsters that terrorized our farmsteads" back home — which is the only part of his past he has volunteered to strangers.

**He killed a bishop.** Bishop Thurstan, for what he was doing to a girl. The Church buried him for it. The folk version that travels among slaves and soldiers — *the baron's son who killed a bishop for what he was doing to a girl* — is truer than the version the Citadel tells, and Anselm Vogt gave a statement about it to a clerk in the Sunlit Citadel who never wrote it down.

He prays. He prayed out of habit his first night in the camp and noticed nobody around him looked up.

**Personality.** Steady. Slow to speak and hard to move once he has decided. He has a particular calm register he uses on frightened or furious people, and it works.

**Ideal.** The oath is not the Church's to give or take back. He kept it when it cost him everything, and he will keep it here.

**Bond.** The three people branded beside him. He has been quietly building a verdict on each of them since the first night and has not finished with any of them.

**Flaw.** He gives his word too readily, and then it holds him. He promised Ald there were no more beasts near his village. He has not been back.

## How he's been played

- **He steps in front of things.** The pitchforks at Ald's Star. The doorway at Saint Pyotr's. Between the party and whatever is coming.
- **He talks first.** Hands open, palms out, voice level — it defused Ald, and it is why the village helped them at all.
- **He is honest to his own cost.** He told the party about the second knitting spider unprompted, the same evening Fid slipped it to him, because he was not willing to be part of doing something in the dark.
- **He refuses on principle.** Wouldn't help Virgil spy on Ald's Star and walked off rather than argue about it.
- **He notices the wrong ones.** The brand pressing against something old. The dread coming off the tomb on Dol — he walked into it and looked, and saw a four-armed woman in chains look back. He hasn't talked about it since.
- **His kills are decisive.** One blow through the gap in the cage hound's iron plating, straight down to the ground.

## ⚠️ Two calls to make before Todd uses this

**1. Is Wolfram actually a Paladin?**

Everything he has done at the table points that way: sensing that the cage hounds were "not of this realm," the single-blow kill, praying, the protective instinct, and `CLAUDE.md` describing him as "paladin-coded."

**But your Run Sheet's end-of-night checklist tracks spell slots for Luthien and Virgil only.** If Wolfram had been casting, he'd be on that list. So he may well have been played as a **Fighter 3** with the extraplanar sense as a DM-granted boon from the Mark.

**If Fighter 3 is right,** keep the ability scores, AC, and equipment exactly as above and change:
- **HP 28** stays (d10 hit die, same maths)
- Drop spellcasting, Divine Smite, Divine Sense, Lay on Hands, Divine Health, Channel Divinity
- Add **Second Wind** (bonus action, 1d10+3 HP, 1/rest) and **Action Surge** (1/rest)
- Add a **Martial Archetype** — **Champion** (crit on 19–20) is the closest fit to how he fights
- Saves become **Strength and Constitution**, not Wisdom and Charisma

**2. Ask Todd what he remembers before showing him this.** Ability scores especially — if he recalls a 17 or 18 Strength, or a higher Charisma, use his memory over my arithmetic. A player's recollection of his own character is better evidence than reconstruction from a session log.
