# Notes from Master Thaddeus' Journal
 
Underwood is a farming village a days ride outside Haleyon.  Being situated in the Ambrosian Fields region, the lands are fertile and the village has thrived. 
 
It was a typical farming village until two years ago.  Some farmers started to get a green patches on their skin.  Nobody had ever heard of this before.  Other than the green patches, those effected felt fine. 
 
As time when on, The green patches turned to small mole like nodules.  They were not painful and seems to have had no other effect. 
 
By the time a year had passed the green nodules grew into spore pods like you would see on plants.  The pods would grow, eventually open, and spores burst out.   
 
At this point, the village began to panic and reach out for help.  The Church denied requests for help as they had nobody available due all the resources being consumed in the War in the north.  What the Church did do it order the Village to be quarantined.  However, they did not to enforce this.  Normally this would not work, but, in this case, nobody in the village wants to travel away from the village so the order was not necessary. 
 
When the Village offered payment to the Alchemical Order, my wife and me were sent.  As you already know, we have some experience with transformations.  Our experience is related to animals, but biology is biology. 
 
The problem continues to advance.  Most of the population shows some sign of the infestation.  For some, the pods cover much of their body.  It is also starting to effect their personalities.  The more the infestation advances, the less the infected cares.  They start to get protective of the pods.  They also seem to be addicted to eating the fruit of the pods. 
 
A few have died.  The infestation took over their whole body.  When it is close to the end, they go to a field to lay in the dirt and become part of the land.

# 🟢 The name — "the green"

**Canon: ordinary people call this disease *the green*.** Lowercase, definite article, no ceremony. It is what the rumour calls it, and the rumour is the only thing most people will ever have.

> *"He's got the green."*
> *"That village has the green in it."*
> *"They say there's green out past Haleyon."*

**Nobody agreed on it.** It is what you call a thing when you have seen it once, at a distance, and want to stop talking about it.

### Who says what

| | What they call it |
|---|---|
| **Farmers, carters, innkeepers, soldiers** | **The green.** Universally. |
| **Underwood's own people** | They do not use a name. **They say "it," and they are not frightened of it any more**, which is the worst thing about them. |
| **Those far gone** | Called **"the greened"** by others. Never by themselves. |
| **The Church** | ⚠️ **Nothing. There is no Church word for it**, because officially there is nothing to name — they refused aid and issued a quarantine they never enforced. **The absence of a name is itself the tell**, and a priest who suddenly has one has been told something. |
| **The Alchemical Order** | Wants a proper name and has not settled on one. Thaddeus writes *the affliction*. |
| **Virgil** | Will object to "the green" as imprecise, at length, **and will end up using it like everyone else.** |

### How it travels

**The name moves faster and further than the disease.** That is the useful part.

- It arrives in places that have never seen a case, so the party will hear it **long before** it means anything, and from people who are wrong.
- **It gets attached to things that are not it.** A green rash. Moss on a wall in a wet year. A bad harvest. A man who has gone quiet. **Let the party chase two false ones before a real one.**
- **In Solteres it is a traveller's story**, not a fact. In the Ambrosian Fields it is why you do not take the north road.

### ⚠️ The collision worth remembering

**Ald's Star.** The whole village grows a cabbage strain that turns the people who eat it **a beautiful green**, and Ald was *proud* of it, and the party has met them.

**They are entirely healthy and they are entirely doomed by a word.** Whenever the rumour reaches that valley — and it will — those people are green, and a frightened region has a name for green people.
