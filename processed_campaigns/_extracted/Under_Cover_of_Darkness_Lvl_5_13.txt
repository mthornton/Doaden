W
         elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and
         twisted monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the
                            very essence of existence.

It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes
of the glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie
         with one another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.

   It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
   The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be
                       honored in songs of minstrels.

 For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake invariably
 leave them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless husks and
                 shadows of their once-glorious selves.

  Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn
            tenacity, a last flickering flame within the dark.

   A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of
         eternity, a momentary victory before the endless night.

Ending the Encounter: Bestiarum Campaign
If you’re running a Bestiarum Campaign, consider the
following options for an outcome:
    If the party manages to escape, Lord Azriel could meet them
later to congratulate them on managing to invade the camp and
successfully steal the standard, and following their escapade,
would tell them to point out the flaws they spotted on the
encampment’s defense. But, if the party fails to escape and is
captured by the Crusade, Lord Azriel could intervene on their
behalf, explaining their deal and letting them go.

It’s a Trap! A magical alarm system has been set on
the Standard. If the party interacts with it, moving it
in any way, the alarm will set off, alerting the whole
encampment to the party’s presence. The alarm can
be detected with the spell Detect Magic. The alarm
can be disabled with the spell Dispel Magic with
a DC 13 / 14 / 15 spellcasting ability check. On a
failed check, the alarm is set off. If the alarm is trig-
gered, proceed to Encounter 2.

A9) Horse Pen
A round enclosure used to train the Crusaders’ war
horses. The area is very large, allowing the horses to
move freely while also providing boundaries to the
structure. Only a few horses are present here.

A10) South Gate
The walls surrounding the south-most encampment
area are 30 ft. tall with two elevated platforms inside,
giving sight to the road outside, where Crusaders
stand guard. Metal gates close off the camp. The
gates are objects with AC 19 and 100 Hit Points and
resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing
damage. Any successful hits against the gate produce
a loud echoing noise, alerting all creatures within a
30 ft. radius. Also, the gates are shut tight, requiring
the Camp Leader’s Key to be opened or a DC 19 /
21 / 23 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. The fol-
lowing enemies are present in this area:
