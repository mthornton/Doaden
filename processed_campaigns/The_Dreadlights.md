## Overview
Escort mission through the cursed marshland of the Deadmire (Western Reaches). The party protects Lunden, a genius engineer traveling in his mechanical carriage the Blasten-Vagen, to the Black Fortress for the Penitent Crusade. En route the Harrow Viaduct is out, forcing a detour into the deep mire, where the reanimated dead and hypnotic "dreadlights" hunt travelers.

## Key Locations
- The Deadmire — vast cursed marshland across the Western Reaches; former battlefield now filled with the reanimated dead; ~1 week to cross
- Vias Umbrae — basalt road built by the Old Empire, maintained by the Penitent Crusade, safer route to the Black Fortress
- Harrow Viaduct — bridge over the Blackwater River; collapsed, forcing the party's detour (start of adventure)
- Sloughfen (The Old Village) — abandoned town where the party scavenges repair parts; Dread Necromancer's tower sits on a hill (area B5)
- The Black Fortress — Penitent Crusade stronghold at the edge of the Ashen Wastes; adventure's endpoint
- Hangman's Tree, Hrodr's Castle, Chapel of Sorrows — optional Deadmire landmarks for extended play (Taking It Further)

## Session Flow
1. Setup: Blasten-Vagen crashes near the Harrow Viaduct detour; the dead rise and dreadlights appear.
2. Ch1 The Dreadlights: survive an 11-round wave fight at the Sodden Road while avoiding dreadlight charm/drowning.
3. Ch2 The Old Village: explore Sloughfen, fight scattered undead, gather 4 repair parts; risk waking the Dread Necromancer.
4. Ch3 Deadman's Fear: Lunden repairs the carriage; an 8-round chase against the Corpse Cart back to the Vias Umbrae Road.
5. Epilogue: arrive at the Black Fortress/Penitent Crusade camp; rewards distributed based on Lunden/crate/carriage survival.

## Treasure & Rewards
- 25 gold pledged by Lunden for safe delivery (minus 1 gold per lost Precious Crate, minus 10 gold if the Blasten-Vagen is destroyed, 0 gold if Lunden dies)
- +1 Renown with the Templar Rangers on successful completion
- The Sprengstoff — unique magic pistol (very rare, requires attunement by a creature with 16+ INT; +2 to attack/damage, uses INT instead of DEX) gifted by Lunden if he survives

## Secrets & Twists
- The dreadlights are theorized to be spirits of the countless dead soaked into the Deadmire's soil from ancient wars — true nature is never confirmed in-text
- The Dread Necromancer's bone instrument is psychically linked to every dreadlight in the region — destroying him collapses the Old Village's lights
- Hrodr, the Cripple's origin is disputed in-world (captive giant / circus beast / hidden noble offspring) — left to GM's choice, never resolved

## Faction Notes (Deadmire)
- The Deadmire's undead ("The Restless," "Deadwalkers") are the risen soldiers/peasants of centuries of pre-Empire wars whose blood soaked into and cursed the land
- All Deadmire undead share Undead Fortitude, Decrepit Senses (only see/hear to 40 ft.), and Flesh Driven (once they sense a living creature, they track it for 24 hrs regardless of distance)
- Rough hierarchy: Restless/Deadwalker Archers (CR2) → Deadwalker Soldiers (CR3) → Deadwalker Batterers/Dread Horsemen (CR5 elite) → Dread Necromancer (CR7 commander) → Corpse Cart/Hrodr (CR10-12 bosses)
- The Necromancer's bone instrument is the region's control node linking him to the dreadlights — implies a singular intelligence behind the Deadmire's curse
