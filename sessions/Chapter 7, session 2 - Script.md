Chapter 7, session 2

# Part 1
You are standing on the inner yard of Bramblefen a one time prison converted to house enslaved people awaiting auction.  The yard is mostly mud with sparse patched of mud and weeds.  Directly in front of you a large stone buidling three stories high.  This is the prison knows as The Deeps.  The building is discolred with patches of moss growing in the cracks.  You can feel the despair radiating outward powered by the misery of the thousands of souls imprisoned inside over the centuries.  The feeling you get from the buildign is reminecent of the feelings you had at St Pytor's.  Much less intense, but recognizable.

There are three other buidings around the courtyard. A two story house to the right of The Deeps.  The house stand out on the account of being the only building to be in good repair.  To your right is a small blacksmith and armoury.  It does not appear active.  No smoke from the forge's chimney.  The front door is chained and locked.  To your left is another barracks for housing the enslaved.  Smaller and less intimidating that the The Deeps, but unplesant none the less.

You came here believing Bramblfen only had four or five guards.  You have counted eight so far and those are only the one you can see out in the open.  You have an out of date ledger created by the slave bookeeper Old Cobb that has a two month old enrty for JonLief's family, Elizabeth and Rowan, but no entry for what happened after they arrived.  Erwana remembers that Elizabeth was sick, but she knows nothing else.  Your goal is to find them then give Ysabel the ledger in exchange for her help.  But on your arrival to this desolate place you learned Lord Ironwake, the owner fo this facility, has his champion, The Juggernaut, here for some warm up fights.  He has a match tonight aginst a feisty Gnome that the guards seem sparticulary excited to watch.

The yard is bustling with activity.  A few guards are directing a group of slaves to assemble a wooden fence made of sharpened timbers.  It appears they are constructing a circle in the center of the yard.  The work is half complete.  The guards are brutal in thier treatment of the slaves.  The work is intrupted by the guards beating on the slaves for imagined disobediance.  From the laughter of the guards you can tell the beatings are primarily for the entertainment of the guards with each trying to out do the other.

A half orc slave is tied to a post with a drunken guard clubbing the slave between drinks.  "Don't worry about that, orcs can take a beating.  It's not likely to do any permenent damage." says the guard escorting you.

Work stops temporarily as the slaves notice the party.  The break last no more than a second before the guards begin punishing the slaves for the work stoppage.  However, one slave does not immediatly go back to work.  He drops the timber he is carrying with a look of shock and astonishment on his face.  A guard pounces - beating the man to the ground and continues kicking him as he curles up into a ball.  In the brief moment the slaves face could be seen, Wolfram recognized the man.  He can't place the face with a name, but it must be somebody he knew from Westfield.
