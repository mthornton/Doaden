# Notes from Master Thaddeus' Journal
 
Underwood is a farming village a days ride outside Haleyon.  Being situated in the Ambrosian Fields region, the lands are fertile and the village has thrived. 
 
It was a typical farming village until two years ago.  Some farmers started to get a green patches on their skin.  Nobody had ever heard of this before.  Other than the green patches, those effected felt fine. 
 
As time when on, The green patches turned to small mole like nodules.  They were not painful and seems to have had no other effect. 
 
By the time a year had passed the green nodules grew into spore pods like you would see on plants.  The pods would grow, eventually open, and spores burst out.   
 
At this point, the village began to panic and reach out for help.  The Church denied requests for help as they had nobody available due all the resources being consumed in the War in the north.  What the Church did do it order the Village to be quarantined.  However, they did not to enforce this.  Normally this would not work, but, in this case, nobody in the village wants to travel away from the village so the order was not necessary. 
 
When the Village offered payment to the Alchemical Order, my wife and me were sent.  As you already know, we have some experience with transformations.  Our experience is related to animals, but biology is biology. 
 
The problem continues to advance.  Most of the population shows some sign of the infestation.  For some, the pods cover much of their body.  It is also starting to effect their personalities.  The more the infestation advances, the less the infected cares.  They start to get protective of the pods.  They also seem to be addicted to eating the fruit of the pods. 
 
A few have died.  The infestation took over their whole body.  When it is close to the end, they go to a field to lay in the dirt and become part of the land. 
 
We spent months investigating.  So far, we don't know why this is happening.  We are meeting Willowbread.  He found someting similar is some plants located farther East.  He is bringing me his notes and some samples.  I hope this new information will give me the knowledge to save these people.
