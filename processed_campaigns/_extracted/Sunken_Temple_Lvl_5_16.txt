                Tide Wall Puzzle

Tide Cubes. The indents in the wall are meant
to fit tide cubes – two representing high tide
(displaying three waves) and two representing
low tide (displaying one wave). The missing
cubes are hidden away in the various
branching tunnels connected to the chamber.
The party must retrieve them before they can
solve the puzzle. Each tide cube is 1 cubic foot
in size and weighs 150 pounds.
