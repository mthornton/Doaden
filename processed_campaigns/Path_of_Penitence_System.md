## Setting Overview
Doaden is a grimdark 5e setting by Bestiarum Games. Long ago the First Emperor united the world into the Old Empire; the Cult of Azerai worshipped him as a god and built him the **Demoncore**, a sun-powered superweapon. It detonated in the imperial capital (the "Devastation"), incinerating continents and plunging the world into a demon-and-monster-infested dark age. The surviving **Empire of Azerai** is a harsh theocracy ruled by the Church of Azerai from the **Sunlit Citadel**, seat of Grand Ecclesiarch **Vitalinus**, who sits the melted **Malformed Throne**. Every PC is a **Penitent**: a citizen convicted of a crime (or who swore an Oath of Penance) and sentenced to up to 500 years of atonement, forced into the **Penitent Crusade** — the Empire's meatgrinder army — to fight cults, undead, demons, and monsters on suicidal missions in exchange for reduced sentences. Tone: bleak, oppressive, morally compromised institutions, low-value-of-life; explicit "safe space" content warning for the table (discuss sensitive/traumatic themes before play).

## Key House Rules / Subsystems
| Rule/Subsystem | What it changes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mission Structure** (3 stages) | Frames every adventure module as a "mission" for the Crusade | A. Setup (optional: Discovery + Exploration), B. Adventure (the module itself), C. Reward (years atoned + rank). Debriefings with Vozruk build the narrative thread. |
| **Leveling & Atonement** | Replaces XP tracking with milestone leveling tied to a sentence-countdown mechanic | Party levels up after completing missions = their current level (core book) or per the Mission Rewards Table (QSG). Each mission also removes **Years of Atonement** from the party's 500-year sentence per the Adventure Difficulty Table (2–8 yrs, scaling Beginner→Hell). |
| **Adventure Difficulty & Tiers** | Scales module encounters to party level | 7 difficulties (Beginner 1–2 → Hell 17–20), grouped into Tiers (Core book: Tier I 1–10/II 5–16/III 8–20; QSG: adds Tier IV, ranges differ slightly — Tier II 3–13, Tier III 8–20, Tier IV 11–20). Party of 3-or-fewer/6-or-more shifts difficulty ±1. Suggested level cap: 10 (core book) or 11 (GM QSG) — sources disagree slightly, pick one for your table. |
| **Crusade Rank Advancement** | Narrative/mechanical progression ladder separate from character level | Condemned → Supplicant → Aspirant → Champion → Redeemed (final, exits the Crusade). Tied to # successful missions (0/10/25/60/100) or simplified to level (1/6/10/15/17). Rank gates gear access, attendant slots, war-wagon eligibility, and NPC attitudes. |
| **Crusade Camp Economy** | Adds a Crusade-run vendor ecosystem (Blacksmith, Quartermaster, Temples, Stables, Barracks, Engineering Sanctum) at every major city/fort | Blacksmith: weapons/armor/silvering (100g); rank-gated magic weapons (Supplicant=uncommon +1, Aspirant=rare +2, Champion=very rare +3). Quartermaster: adventuring gear w/ rank discount (0/10/20/30%). Temples of Azerai: hire cleric/wizard spells up to 4th level (10–250 gp by spell level) + free long-distance "sending" comms. Stables: mounts, barding, boarding. Barracks: room & board (1gp/day) + hireable **Attendants** (Azerai Devotee, Crusader Scout, Husk) at daily fees, count capped by rank. |
| **Hexcrawl Exploration** | Optional 15-mile hex travel/survival layer for the Setup Stage | Daily navigator makes Survival check vs terrain DC (Easy 10/Difficult 15); failure = lost, move via d6 scatter. Food/water/exhaustion rules (paraphrased 5e). Caravans (4g/hex, no random events). d100/d10 regional event tables per diocese (weather, enemy encounters by type, settlements) plus Active Searching (d10 roll, find on 1–2 or a related result). |
| **War Wagons** | Adds a mobile HQ/battle-vehicle subsystem | Commission cost 15,000gp/21 days; Gargantuan vehicle (AC18, 200hp, 2 mod slots, rank-gated to 3+). Modular "Mods" (armor plating, boosters, turrets, etc., 3,500–20,000gp). Combat rules for driving (steering/speed checks), collisions (1d10 bludgeoning/10ft moved), Machine Failures table (d10, triggered at 10%+ dmg from one hit), chase encounters w/ Doaden Chase Complications table. Runs on **detroil** fuel (1 gal/day, 50-gal tank). Requires Supplicant rank to unlock. |
| **Loyalty Rewards (subscriber content)** | Bonus unlockable NPCs/encounters for Bestiarum subscribers, tied to subscription length | 3mo: Vozruk + Crusade Emblem magic item; 3mo (S2): Death Knight (CR14, can be slotted into undead random encounters, drops a *true resurrection*); 6mo: Keeper of Knowledge + Mysterious Scribe (CR8/CR6 pair, drop spell scrolls); 9mo: Grand Magus Xavriel (CR16) + War Phoenix (CR20), a capstone Champion-rank test encounter. |

## Character Creation Notes
- Standard 5e character creation plus one step: choose your **Sins & Crimes** backstory (roll d6 on the Sins & Crimes table or invent one) — Heresy, Occultism/Cultism, Sedition, Corruption/Contamination, Grand Murder, Tithe Evasion — resulting in a 500-year sentence regardless of severity (a loaf of bread can get the same sentence as plotting rebellion).
- Alternative: **Oath of Penance** — character joins the Crusade voluntarily (zealotry, knowledge-seeking, hunger, monster-hunting, etc.) rather than via conviction.
- "Bad" characters are explicitly supported: the Crusade doesn't discriminate by race/class/alignment/background — warlocks, criminals, violent characters are welcomed and their "vices" reframed as useful in service to the Empire.
- Race: setting is human-dominant; non-humans (elves, dwarves, "stranger species") exist but are rare/isolated — consider a Background Lore region or invented homeland for non-human PCs.
- Magic/Casting: not deity-based — magical ability "courses through the individual" innately or through training; PCs are not required to follow the standard 5e class-flavor fluff (a cleric needn't worship a god), and can narratively lose/regain their power source same as normal 5e.
- Optional "Digging Deeper" backstory prompts: guilt/innocence, trial fairness, how conviction changed their life, motive, remorse.

## Factions & Powers of Doaden
- **Church of Azerai / Empire of Azerai** — theocratic state religion, the only legal faith; rules via the Church, enforces the Mark of Atonement branding and the Husk-conversion punishment for escapees.
- **Grand Ecclesiarch Vitalinus** — absolute ruler of the Empire, seated on the Malformed Throne in the Sunlit Citadel.
- **Cult of Azerai** — the ancient secretive priesthood that gifted the First Emperor the Demoncore; origin of the modern Church.
- **Penitent Crusade** — the Empire's army, staffed largely by conscripted Penitents; led by **Primus** generals; the party's direct chain of command is their **Redeemer**, **Vozruk** (stat block CR9, lawful neutral human commander — the party's core recurring NPC).
- **Grand Magus Xavriel** — war chancellor of the Empire, leader of the Azerai circle of mages, rides a War Phoenix; ruthless toward perceived weakness even among Crusaders (loyalty-reward capstone NPC/encounter).
- **The four Dioceses** (each led by a bishop): **Western** (chivalric remnants of Averlon/Frankonia, capitals Château Larmegris & Bryxford), **Northern** (barbarian giant-blooded warrior culture, capital Stürmenburg, tolerated shamanic religion), **Eastern** (demon/witch-haunted swamps nearest the Demoncore epicenter, capital Vostograd, viewed with suspicion by the rest of the Empire), **Southern** (fertile coastal trade region, capitals Solteres & Marinvall, hides syndicates/cults/pirates).
- **Foreign realms** (rarely visited, flavor only): Thule (elemental raider clans, north), Akhamia (desert demonology, southeast), Nikan (ancestor-worshipping honor culture, far east), Zafarika (jungle/savanna spirit warriors, south), Teo'Xacan (blood-sacrifice rainforest civilization, west).

## Bestiary Notes
- The core book includes ready-to-use stat blocks rather than a full bestiary: **Vozruk the Redeemer** (CR9), **Husk** (CR0, mind-wiped slave/soldier — hireable Attendant, 25gp/day), **Azerai Devotee** (CR1, hireable Attendant, cleric-caster) and **Crusader Scout** (CR1, hireable Attendant, gives Survival bonus while traveling), plus the four Loyalty Reward monsters: **Death Knight** (CR14), **Keeper of Knowledge** (CR8) & **Mysterious Scribe** (CR6, linked "Vengeance" pair), and **Grand Magus Xavriel** (CR16) + **War Phoenix** (CR20).
- Modules published monthly by Bestiarum (the "Liber Obscura") supply the actual dungeon/encounter bestiary content per adventure; this rules book only anchors the recurring campaign-framework NPCs above.
- Random enemy-encounter tables (by diocese, d20, categorized by creature type — Beasts/Humanoids/Undead/Fiends/Fey/Dragons/etc.) are provided for hexcrawl travel, not for specific stat blocks — GM supplies appropriate CR monsters from any source.

## Quickstart: running your first session
- Read order: give players the **Player QSG** (or run a session-0 readthrough); GM reads **both** QSGs. Player QSG includes a short "World Summary" GMs can read aloud if players skip the homework.
- Roleplay **Vozruk** consistently — he's the emotional throughline of the campaign; personality/gender/name are GM's choice (stern drill sergeant vs. caring tactician are the suggested poles).
- Structure every mission with a **Briefing** (hook + hidden danger) and a **Debriefing** (reward + rank check-in) around the adventure module itself.
- **Getting started options**: (1) run the two starter modules *Burial Isle* → *Rise of the Jailer* (levels 1–3, linked cult/demon-summoning plot), (2) jump into any monthly module, or (3) run a **session-0** origin scenario — three ready-made prompts included: *Great Escape/Escape Plans* (Mines of Tartari breakout), *Cult Ambush/Ah You're Finally Awake* (prison wagon rescue by Vozruk), *Worst of the Worst/I'm Putting Together a Team* (evil-aligned party recruited from the Bastilla prison).
- Module defaults: party size 3–6 (4–5 ideal), one adventuring day per module (short rests only, no long rest until complete), milestone XP via the Mission Rewards Table.
- **Common issues** guidance: swap in new PCs via Vozruk reassigning them to the squad (new recruit or lone survivor of a wiped team); Vozruk runs *many* squads that never meet, which is the GM's built-in excuse for drop-in players, replacement PCs, and campaign continuity; optional "Hardcore Mode" starts new PCs at the full 500-year sentence.

## Things to Remember
- Every subsystem beyond core mission-briefing/debriefing (Hexcrawl, Crusade Advancement, War Wagons, Loyalty Rewards) is explicitly **optional** — "Focused" groups can run modules only; "Sandbox" groups use everything.
- Mission rewards = two currencies: **character level** (milestone) and **Years of Atonement** (sentence countdown) — track both after every session.
- War wagons cannot be brought on the Adventure Stage itself (narrative restriction) — they're for the exploration/travel layer only.
- Husks are both a **setting threat** (fate of escaped Penitents) and a **game mechanic** (cheapest hireable Attendant) — useful dual-purpose lore/mechanic hook.
- Source text has two minor internal inconsistencies worth flagging if it matters at the table: level cap (10 in core book vs. 11 in GM QSG) and Tier II/III/IV level ranges (core book only has 3 tiers; QSG has 4 with slightly different bands).
- Loyalty Reward content (Vozruk's stat block bonus item, Death Knight, Keeper of Knowledge/Mysterious Scribe, Grand Magus Xavriel/War Phoenix) is subscriber-gated bonus material — usable anytime once "unlocked," not required for a base campaign.
