## HOW YOUR TURN WORKS

Each turn you get **all three** of these:

**1. MOVEMENT — up to 30 feet.** You can split it up (move 10, attack, move 20).

**2. ONE ACTION.** Pick one:
- **Attack** — swing your longsword or throw a javelin
- **Cast a Spell** — most of yours take an action
- **Lay on Hands** — heal someone you touch
- **Divine Sense** — detect fiends/undead nearby
- **Channel Divinity** — Sacred Weapon or Turn the Unholy
- **Dash** — move your speed again
- **Disengage** — move away without provoking attacks
- **Dodge** — attacks against you have disadvantage until your next turn
- **Help** — give an ally advantage on their next roll
- **Hide**, **Search**, **Ready**, **Use an Object**

**3. MAYBE A BONUS ACTION.** Only if something specifically says "bonus action." For you that's **Shield of Faith** and **Sanctuary**. Most turns you won't have one.

**Plus, at any time — ONE REACTION per round**, even on other people's turns:
- **Protection** (your fighting style — see below)
- **Opportunity Attack** — when an enemy you can see leaves your reach, you get one free melee attack

**AND: after you hit with a melee attack, you may DIVINE SMITE.** This is free — it costs no action. See below.

## DIVINE SMITE — your big move

**When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack**, you may spend a spell slot to add radiant damage on top of your normal damage.

| Slot spent | Extra damage | vs. undead or fiends |
|---|---|---|
| One 1st-level slot | **+2d8 radiant** | **+3d8 radiant** |

**Three things to remember:**

1. **You decide AFTER you know you hit.** Roll to hit first. If it lands, *then* choose whether to spend the slot. Never waste a slot on a miss.
2. **It costs no action.** It's part of the same attack.
3. **You can do it more than once per turn** if you have slots — but you only get one attack per turn at level 3, so in practice it's once.

> This is almost certainly what cut the iron-plated hound in half at Ald's Star.

**Your slots are the same pool for smiting and for casting spells.** Three total. Most sessions you'll want to smite with them.

## CLASS FEATURES

### Lay on Hands
**Pool: 15 hit points per long rest.**
**Action.** Touch a creature and restore any number of hit points from the pool — you choose how much. You can split it up across the day however you like.

You can instead spend **5 points** from the pool to **cure one disease** or **neutralize one poison**.

*Doesn't work on undead or constructs.*

### Divine Sense
**3 uses per long rest. Action.**
Until the end of your next turn, you know the **location** of any **celestial, fiend, or undead** within **60 feet** that isn't behind total cover. You also know what type it is. You detect consecrated or desecrated places and objects in that range too.

*You don't see through their disguise — you just know something is there and what kind.*

### Fighting Style: Protection
**Reaction.** When a creature you can see attacks a target **other than you** that is **within 5 feet of you**, you can impose **disadvantage** on that attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.

> This is your signature. It's why you stand next to people. **Watch for it every round** — it's easy to forget, and it's one of the strongest things you do.

### Divine Health
You are **immune to disease.** Nothing to track.

### Channel Divinity — 1 use per SHORT or long rest
Choose one when you use it:

**Sacred Weapon** — **Action.** For 1 minute, add **+2** to your attack rolls with a weapon you're holding *(so your longsword becomes **+7** to hit)*. The weapon glows with bright light 20 ft. and dim light 20 ft. beyond that, and counts as **magical** for overcoming resistance. Ends early if you drop the weapon or are unconscious.

**Turn the Unholy** — **Action.** Each **fiend or undead** within **30 feet** that can see or hear you must make a **Wisdom save (DC 12)**. On a failure it is **turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage**. A turned creature must spend its turns trying to get as far from you as it can, can't willingly move within 30 ft. of you, can't take reactions, and can only Dash or try to escape.

## SPELLCASTING

**Spell Save DC 12** · **Spell Attack +4** · **Charisma is your casting ability**

**You have three 1st-level slots.** They refresh on a **long rest** (not a short rest). Smiting and casting both draw from these three.

**You know these five spells and can cast any of them.** Two are always available because of your Oath and don't count against your limit.

---

### ⚡ CONCENTRATION — read this once, it matters

Three of your spells need **Concentration**. Rules:

- **You can only concentrate on ONE spell at a time.** Casting a second one ends the first. *You cannot have Bless and Shield of Faith running together.*
- **If you take damage while concentrating**, make a **Constitution saving throw (+2)**. The DC is **10, or half the damage taken — whichever is higher.** Fail and the spell ends.
- Being knocked unconscious ends it. Casting a non-concentration spell does not.

### 🛡️ PROTECTION FROM EVIL AND GOOD — *always prepared (Oath)*
**1st level · Abjuration · Action · Touch · Concentration, up to 10 minutes**
*Components: V, S, M (holy water or powdered silver and iron, consumed by the spell)*

One willing creature you touch is protected against **aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead**. For the duration:

- Those creatures have **disadvantage on attack rolls** against the target.
- The target **can't be charmed, frightened, or possessed** by them.
- If the target is already charmed, frightened, or possessed by one, it gets **advantage** on any new save against that effect.

> **This is a very strong spell in this campaign** — the cage hounds, whatever was in the crypt on Dol, and whatever the cultists keep trying to summon are all likely candidates.

---

### 🕊️ SANCTUARY — *always prepared (Oath)*
**1st level · Abjuration · Bonus Action · 30 feet · 1 minute**
*Components: V, S, M (a small silver mirror)*

You ward one creature within range. Any creature that tries to **attack the warded creature or target it with a harmful spell** must first make a **Wisdom save (DC 12)**. On a failure, it must **choose a different target or lose the attack/spell entirely**.

- Does **not** protect against area effects like a fireball.
- **The spell ends immediately** if the warded creature attacks, casts a spell that affects an enemy, or deals damage to anyone.

> Cast it on someone who's about to go down, or on a helpless person you need to keep alive. **Not** on yourself if you plan to fight.

---

### ✨ BLESS
**1st level · Enchantment · Action · 30 feet · Concentration, up to 1 minute**
*Components: V, S, M (a sprinkling of holy water)*

Choose **up to three creatures** within range (you can include yourself). For the duration, whenever one of them makes an **attack roll or a saving throw**, they roll a **d4** and add it to the result.

> Probably the best use of a slot in a big fight — three people getting +1 to +4 on every attack and every save for a full minute. **Cast it on round one, then smite with the other two slots.**

---

### 💚 CURE WOUNDS
**1st level · Evocation · Action · Touch · Instantaneous**
*Components: V, S*

A creature you touch regains **1d8 + 2** hit points.

*No effect on undead or constructs.*

> **Usually Lay on Hands is better** — it's a bigger pool, it's exact rather than random, and it doesn't cost a slot. Save Cure Wounds for when your Lay on Hands pool is empty.

---

### 🛡️ SHIELD OF FAITH
**1st level · Abjuration · Bonus Action · 60 feet · Concentration, up to 10 minutes**
*Components: V, S (a small parchment with a bit of holy text)*

A shimmering field surrounds a creature of your choice within range, giving it **+2 AC** for the duration.

> Bonus action, so you can cast it *and* attack in the same turn. Ten minutes is a long time — good to put up on yourself before kicking a door in. But remember it competes with Bless for your concentration.

## RESTING

**Short rest — 1 hour.**
- Spend **Hit Dice** to heal: roll a **d10 + 2** per die spent, up to 3 dice.
- **Channel Divinity** comes back.
- Spell slots and Lay on Hands do **not**.

**Long rest — 8 hours.**
- All hit points back.
- All spell slots, Lay on Hands, and Divine Sense back.
- Regain up to half your Hit Dice (1 die).

> ⚠️ **Chapter 7 has no long rest in it.** Whatever you spend early, you're still missing when you meet the man in charge. Budget your slots.

## IF YOU DROP TO 0 HIT POINTS

You fall unconscious and start making **death saving throws** on your turns: roll a d20, no modifiers.
- **10 or higher = success.** Three successes and you're stable.
- **9 or lower = failure.** Three failures and you die.
- **Natural 20** = you wake up with 1 HP. **Natural 1** = counts as two failures.

Any healing at all — including 1 point from Lay on Hands — brings you back conscious. **A knitting spider used immediately can stop you going down in the first place.**

## COMMON SITUATIONS

**Advantage** = roll two d20s, take the higher. **Disadvantage** = roll two, take the lower. They cancel each other out.

**Attacking someone you can't see** = disadvantage. **Attacking a prone target in melee** = advantage.

**Grappling or shoving** = Athletics **+5** against their Athletics or Acrobatics.

**Opportunity attack** — if an enemy walks out of your reach, you get one free melee swing as your reaction. *(But you only get one reaction per round — spending it here means no Protection.)*

## A FEW OPENING MOVES THAT WORK

**Big fight, several enemies:**
Round 1 — cast **Bless** on yourself and two allies. Then attack every round after and **Divine Smite** with your last two slots on the biggest thing.

**One dangerous enemy:**
Charge in, attack, **Divine Smite** on the first hit that lands. Stand next to your squishiest ally and use **Protection** every round.

**Undead or something demonic:**
**Divine Sense** first if you suspect it. Smites do **3d8** instead of 2d8 against them. **Turn the Unholy** can scatter a whole group.

**Somebody's about to die:**
**Lay on Hands** is an action and it's exact — you can spend just 1 point to get someone off the floor, or dump 15 into someone badly hurt.

**Protecting a civilian:**
**Sanctuary** on them (bonus action), then stand in front of them and use **Protection** as your reaction.

## WHO WOLFRAM IS

Son of **Baron Konrad Eisenherz** of **Westfield**, in the Western Diocese. Raised to arms and raised devout. Back home he hunted the monsters that came at the farmsteads — the only part of his past he offers to strangers.

**He killed a bishop.** Bishop Thurstan, for what he was doing to a girl. The Church buried him for it. The version that travels among slaves and old soldiers — *the baron's son who killed a bishop for what he was doing to a girl* — is truer than the one the Sunlit Citadel tells.

He prays. He prayed out of habit his first night in the camp and noticed that nobody around him looked up.

**Personality.** Steady. Slow to speak, hard to move once decided. He has a particular calm register he uses on frightened or furious people, and it works.

**Ideal.** The oath is not the Church's to give or take away. He kept it when it cost him everything, and he will keep it here.

**Bond.** The three people branded beside him. He has been quietly forming a verdict on each of them since the first night and hasn't finished with any of them.

**Flaw.** He gives his word too readily, and then it holds him. He promised Ald there were no more beasts near his village. He hasn't been back.

### Oath of Devotion — the tenets you swore
**Honesty** — don't lie or cheat. Let your word be your promise.
**Courage** — never fear to act, though caution is wise.
**Compassion** — aid others, protect the weak, punish those who threaten them.
**Honor** — treat others with fairness, and let your honorable deeds be an example.
**Duty** — be responsible for your actions and their consequences, protect those in your care, and obey those who have just authority over you.

*Breaking these knowingly and repeatedly is the kind of thing that costs a paladin his powers. Michael will warn you long before it gets there.*

### Things you've done that the table remembers
- Stepped between Ald's pitchforks and the party with your hands open, and talked a furious old man into helping you.
- Told the party about the second knitting spider unprompted, the same evening you got it, rather than be part of doing something in the dark.
- Refused to help Virgil spy on Ald's Star and walked off rather than argue.
- Walked into the dread coming off the cracked tomb on Dol Island and looked inside. A four-armed woman in chains looked back. **You haven't talked about it since.**
- Found the gap in the cage hound's iron plating and put your sword through it, down to the ground.

### One thing you should know about your own character
At Ald's Star you told Ald you'd feel creatures like those hounds **"from a couple of miles off."** Divine Sense only reaches 60 feet. Either something broadened it — or you overstated your confidence to comfort a grieving man, and a village is sleeping easier because of it. **Michael will decide which. Either way, you made a promise.**
