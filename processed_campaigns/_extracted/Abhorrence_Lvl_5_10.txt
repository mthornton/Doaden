                   The World of Doaden

W
    elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted
        monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very
                            essence of existence.


It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of
the glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie with
           one another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.


    It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be honored
                            in songs of minstrels.


For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake invariably leave
them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless husks and shadows of
                         their once-glorious selves.


    Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn
              tenacity, a last flickering flame within the dark.


A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of eternity,
               a momentary victory before the endless night.

Sewer Exploration
  The depths of the sewers are labyrinthian and perilous, with
various flooded areas and gas filled chambers. A damp, sunless
place, which the foul beasts and monstrous experiments of Lord
Nachzehrer have turned into their lair. If you’d like to craft an
open exploration of the area, keep the following suggestions in
mind.
  The party may explore the tunnel at their own leisure,
visiting the key areas in which ever order they want, and
retreating when they deem necessary. Exploration should be
encouraged, so most of the time in the sewer depths should be
spent in relative calmness, building that constant fear of being
jumped by a creature at any moment. You may create random
encounters and add new sections to the sewer as you see fit, in
order to create these spikes of fear.
  Most rooms and tunnels in this area share the following traits:
  Large Area.
   The Depths level is a large space, rising wapproximately 40
ft. to the ceiling, and 30 ft. in width in most tunnels.
  Darkness.
  All areas of the Depths are in complete darkness.
  Damp.
  The tunnels are mostly covered in sewage, with some flooded
parts going up to waist height, making traversal difficult and
unpredictable.
  Ominous.
   A foul stench of rot and decay litters the air, becoming
stronger near the storm drains. Stillness and eerie silence make
every noise echo through the tunnels, with occasional screeches
and fading stridulations of quick, hard steps resounding from
deep within the complex.
