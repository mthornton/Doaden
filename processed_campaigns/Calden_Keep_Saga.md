# The Calden Keep Saga (Calden Keep → The Undying Lord)
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 2_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/C_Saga - Calden Keep (Lvl 5 & 5-10)/ (Calden Keep Lvl 5; The Undying Lord Lvl 5-10) + Calden Keep Part 1 & 2 Lore
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5 (Part 1) → 5–10 (Part 2)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Ages ago, the tyrant Æthelric ruled Calden Castle with dark magic granted by an entity known as the Judge, until his oppressed peasantry rose up, slaughtered his court, and sealed the inner Keep, leaving it to rot. Part 1 (*Revenants*) sends a party of rangers to investigate revenants pouring out of the ruined outer castle and culminates in defeating the Wolf of Calden and the hate-fueled Goliath. Part 2 (*The Undying Lord*) picks up later: a Penitent Crusade agent hires the party to sneak into the now-awakened, shadow-realm Keep and retrieve the legendary Sol Blade, forcing them to navigate a deadly dungeon crawl and ultimately choose whether to kill Lord Æthelric (requiring the sacrifice of his innocent daughter) or earn his favor (requiring betrayal of an ally).

## Key Locations
**Part 1 — Calden Castle (outer grounds):**
- Verden — ashen ruined town where the adventure begins; investigation clues + final tracking clue
- The Forlorn Tower — optional, unstable ruin in the forest en route to Calden
- The Gates of Calden — massive ruined portcullis entrance
- A) Servant's Quarter — Vyla's hovel; Servant's Gate (locked, needs Bouvolin's Keys) leads to the Zwinger
- B) Market District — the Bazar and Herbalist's Shop (loot rolls)
- C) Peasant's Quarter — the Inn (Safe Area, long-rest friendly), hidden basement scroll
- D) Artisan's District — Blacksmith & Armorer shops (magic loot + scrolls)
- E) Noble's Quarter — Town Square (Gorvich ambush), Noble's House (Safe Area)
- F) Military Quarter — Officers' Hall (map + scroll), Wall Tower (2nd way to gate winch)
- G) Chapel — undead rise on entry; hidden Crypt beneath altar (Bouvolin's body/keys)
- H) Ruins — mostly empty, shorter travel routes but riskier (raises Goliath Modifier)
- The Zwinger — grassy ramp between outer walls and inner Keep; site of 3 combats + Wolf of Calden fight
- The Access Stairway / main gate winch — used to reopen the gate and escape

**Part 2 — Calden Keep (dungeon crawl):**
- Calden City — stealth infiltration area (gate tunnel, palisade wall, military compound, trapdoor to tunnels)
- Section A: Underground Tunnels — Obsidian Crystal (respawn point), Cadaver Pit, Boulder Trap, Nameless Prisoner's Alcove, Bottomless Chasm, Spiked Bludgeons, Entrance to Old Calden, Ur's Tomb & the Sol Blade (A12)
- Section B: Torturer's Dungeon — Spike Pit Room, Prisoner's Cell (B3), Saw Trap Room, Jail Hall, Assistant's Workshop (boss), Mass Jail/Torment Collective (boss), Falling Floor Traps, Torturer's Lair (boss)
- Section C: Southern Corridor — Mechanical Room (gear puzzle frees the Prisoner), Executioner's Gallows (boss)
- Section D: Eastern Chapel & Graveyard — Chapel (Obsidian Crystal + ritual), Graveyard (Resurrected Goliath boss)
- Section E: Northern Hall Level 1 — Grand Foyer, Grand Ballroom, Library (Sol Blade lore + Rhaban's letter)
- Section F: Northern Hall Level 2 — Royal Lobby (Morwen), Throne Room (Æthelric + Morwen final boss), Princess' Chamber (Aislinn)
- Old Calden — ancient underground city of the "Earthen Men," home of the Judge; only Ur's Tomb is currently accessible

## Persistent Rules
**Part 1 (R1–R8):**
- R1 Random Encounters — hourly roll while exploring Castle Areas; Safe Areas reduce odds
- R2 Tracking Time — heavy fog limits daylight to 10hrs/14hrs night; most actions cost ~1 hour
- R3 Tracking Food — 10 days of rations; exhaustion accrues once rations run out
- R4 Heavy Fog (Obscured Visibility) — dim light by day (disadvantage on sight Perception), full darkness by night unless countered by torches/darkvision
- R5 Searching the Castle — DC18 Investigation (DC16 if <4 players) per hour to uncover each Point of Interest
- R6 Resting — short rests cost 1hr; long rests need 8 uninterrupted hours and are much safer in Safe Areas
- R7 Safe Areas — Peasant's Quarter Inn & Noble's House; drastically lower monster-encounter odds (1-in-20 vs 4-in-20/hr)
- R8 Goliath Chases — traveling between Castle Areas risks a scripted chase vs. the Goliath, tracked via an escalating "Goliath Modifier"

**Part 2:**
- Fog Barrier / Trapped in Calden — magical fog seals the Keep; only escape is killing Æthelric or earning his favor
- Obsidian Crystal Resurrection — PCs respawn at their last-touched crystal at full HP, but gain Soul Corrosion
- Soul Corrosion — stacking (non-cumulative) debuff from death/long rests; Level 7 splits the character into a hostile Player Shadow + powerless Spirit Ember (Soul Shrooms remove corrosion; optional Permanent Death rule)
- Keep Reset — enemies/traps reset every 8hrs or after a long rest; bosses and looted treasure do not respawn
- Torment Mode — parties leveled 8–10 use the harder "Torment" enemy additions/boss statblocks; 5–7 parties use Normal Mode

## Session Flow
**Part 1 — Calden Keep: Revenants**
1. Ch.1: Investigate Verden's ruins (Investigation clue table), track revenants 5–7 days to Calden (random travel events, rations), optional Forlorn Tower, night ambush (Crossbowmen + Hunting Hounds), arrive at the Gates of Calden.
2. Ch.2: Scripted Goliath Chase introduces the castle; free exploration of Castle Areas A–H under Persistent Rules R1–R8, uncovering keys/scrolls/clues (Bouvolin's Keys, Large Iron Key, letters) needed to reach the Zwinger.
3. Ch.3: Enter the Zwinger, fight through 3 combats culminating in the Wolf of Calden (loot Glaive of Vengeance + Breastplate of Hatred), raise the main gate winch, final confrontation with the Goliath at the gate, escape and debrief with the rangers.

**Part 2 — The Undying Lord**
1. Ch.1 (Cloaks and Daggers): Meet Primus Hintralius, accept the Sol Blade contract, stealth mission through Calden City with Deliverer operatives, break into the tunnel trapdoor beneath the barracks.
2. Ch.1.5: Interim rules/history/character reference chapter (Judge/covenant lore, Persistent Rules, Æthelric/Morwen/Aislinn/Prisoner backgrounds) — no narrative beats.
3. Ch.2 (The Depths): Traverse the Underground Tunnels (Section A) to the Obsidian Crystal and Ur's Tomb, then the Torturer's Dungeons (Section B), defeating the Torturer, his Assistant, and the Torment Collective; meet and eventually free the Nameless Prisoner.
4. Ch.3 (The Cursed Keep): Ascend through the Southern Corridor (Section C, free the Prisoner via the gear puzzle, defeat the Executioner), the Eastern Chapel & Graveyard (Section D, defeat the Resurrected Goliath), and Northern Hall Levels 1–2 (Sections E–F, gather Sol Blade lore in the Library, meet Morwen and Princess Aislinn), culminating in the choice to kill or appease Lord Æthelric.
5. Epilogue: Escape through the tunnel to Calden City; standoff with Hintralius' operatives over the Sol Blade determines the party's final reward/consequences.

## Secrets & Twists
- The Goliath(s) in both parts are amalgamations of trapped, hate-filled souls of the slaughtered — not truly sapient monsters, but the residue of the uprising's violence
- Æthelric's power comes from a Faustian pact with a dark entity called **the Judge**; his final unpaid obligation was sacrificing his own infant daughter, which he refused — dooming Calden to its eternal shadow-realm curse
- **Grand Duke Rhaban** originally brokered Æthelric's covenant with the Judge (revealed only via a letter in the Library)
- The legend of Ur the Ascended is false: the true account (*The Lonely Path*) reveals Ur ignited the Sol Blade by sacrificing his own wife's pure soul, not by "saving" her from a demon — the same sacrifice the party must weigh with Princess Aislinn
- The **Nameless Prisoner** is not a fully trustworthy ally — he secretly wants Aislinn dead to make Æthelric suffer, and will manipulate the party toward that outcome
- The **Prisoner's Lucky Pebble** he gives the party is secretly cursed (-1, not +1) — a DM's Eyes Only detail
- Time inside Calden Keep is distorted: no matter how long the party spends inside, no more than 24 hours pass in the outside world
- If a PC's Soul Corrosion reaches Level 7, their soul splits into a hostile Player Shadow (which stalks the Keep) and a powerless Spirit Ember — this is literally what happened to the Shadow Monk and Shadow Warlock encountered as random enemies

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Old Calden (the underground city of the "Earthen Men") and the Judge itself are explicitly left unexplored — flagged as future adventure material
- The Sol Blade's fate depends entirely on the party's Epilogue choice (hand it over vs. keep it and be hunted by the Crusade indefinitely)
- Track each PC's Soul Corrosion level individually throughout Part 2 — it doesn't reset except via Soul Shrooms or Soul Redemption
- Shadow Monk/Shadow Warlock encounters are separate from room-specific fights — roll 1-in-20 on every room entry and every rest throughout the Keep
- If the party played Part 1, tie-ins are built in: recruited by Hintralius due to prior Calden experience, and the premade Barbarian Odrig "Iron Jaw" has an established connection via the Wolf Banner clue
- The identity of the mysterious servant girl who guided the party during Part 1's Goliath Chase was never resolved on-page
