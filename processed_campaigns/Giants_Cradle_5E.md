**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5-16
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A mountain-climbing one-shot: the party ascends the snow-capped Giant's Cradle to stop an ettin tribe terrorizing the countryside (livestock kills, missing travelers). The climb is a linear gauntlet of environmental hazards and set-piece ettin fights, culminating in a boss fight with the ettin grandsire at the summit. Difficulty scales by APL: Medium (5-7), Hard (8-10), Nightmare (11-13), Torment (14-16).

## Key Locations
- Grassy Basin (1) — starting area; Investigation reveals a mauled sheep, giant humanoid footprints, and an overturned merchant cart with jeweled trinkets
- Frozen Lake (2) — must be crossed carefully; timed ice-crack mechanic (1d6 failure threshold per PC, DC 10 Wisdom Survival each minute, DC 15 DEX save to avoid falling in)
- Secluded Cave (3) — ettin younglings feasting on a dead hiker; can be fought, snuck past, or ambushed; hiker's gear yields rope/climber's kit/gold
- Frozen Gorge (4) — wooden bridge guarded by ettins; an ettin is actively eating a second hiker as the party arrives
- Upper Slope (5) — 6-event hiking gauntlet (d8 Hiking Challenges table: icy ledge, blizzard, hidden crevasse, rockslide, crumbling path, deepfreeze); 5th challenge is fixed — a 50-ft glacial wall with the third dead hiker and a falling-ice hazard at 40 ft
- Temple Ruins (6) — defiled druidic temple (twin eagle statues) where the ettin shaman performs corrupting rituals
- Snowy Summit (7) — ettin grandsire's lair; a meat rack holds a dozen sheep and a dozen human corpses, including the fourth/last hiker carrying a valuable map case

## Session Flow
1. Adventure hook (Shepherd's Plea / Lost Hikers / Ominous Prophecy) draws the party to the base of Giant's Cradle.
2. Grassy Basin — Investigation check for clues (sheep remains, giant tracks, merchant cart loot).
3. Frozen Lake — timed ice-crossing hazard.
4. Secluded Cave — ettin youngling encounter; loot hiker's gear.
5. Frozen Gorge — ettin bridge encounter (witness a hiker's death).
6. Upper Slope — 6 hiking-challenge gauntlet (d8 table), including fixed glacial-wall climb with a dead hiker and valuable map.
7. Temple Ruins — ettin shaman encounter; temple corruption lifted on victory.
8. Snowy Summit — boss fight vs. ettin grandsire; find the final hiker's map among the trophy rack.
9. Resolution — return to whichever hook NPC (shepherd, scholar, or druid) for the appropriate reward.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Grassy Basin cart: 1d4 jeweled trinkets, 100 gp each
- Secluded Cave loot: 2x 50 ft hempen rope, a climber's kit, 1d100 gp
- Glacial wall map case: detailed map of Giant's Cradle, sellable to a cartographer for 700 gp
- Hook payoffs: Shepherd rewards an uncommon magic item or trinket; scholar pays 700 gp for the map; druid offers spiritual closure (no material reward)

## Secrets & Twists
- The "lost hikers" the party may be seeking are found dead one by one along the route — first eaten in the cave, second eaten at the bridge in real time, third frozen on the glacial wall, fourth (their leader) on the grandsire's meat rack with the valuable map
- The temple corruption and the ettin threat are connected — the shaman's rituals are actively worsening the mountain's spiritual state

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- It takes 1 hour to walk between each numbered map location; adventure assumes an early start for a full day's climb
- Difficulty Modifiers table adjusts DC/damage dice/falling distance for hiking hazards: Medium +0/+0/+0ft, Hard +1/+1/+10ft, Nightmare +2/+2/+20ft, Torment +3/+3/+30ft
- Frozen Lake cold damage scales: Medium 2d6, Hard 3d6, Nightmare 5d6, Torment 6d6
- Ettin stat blocks: Ettin (CR 6, HP 136), Ettin Grandsire (CR 11, HP 201, legendary), Ettin Shaman (CR 8, HP 152, spellcaster), Ettin Youngling (CR 2, HP 75, bite/claw only, no greatclub)
