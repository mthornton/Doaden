## 2. ⚙️ What they are for — and why the cult *buys*

**They are the Catalyst.** The mana comes out of Saraswati in barrels; **the suffering has to come out of people**, and it has to come out of them **slowly.**

That is the whole reason this is a *purchase* and not a raid.

> **You cannot stockpile suffering. You can only sustain it.**
>
> A massacre produces a great deal of agony very briefly and then nothing at all. **The rite needs a continuous supply**, which means people who are alive, held, and hurt again tomorrow — and the day after, and the year after.

**So the cult needs a procurement pipeline, and it has had one for thirty-eight years.**

- **They need them alive on arrival.** Hence buying from holdfasts rather than taking off a road.
- **They need nobody to come looking.** Hence slaves — and hence *women* in Solteres, where a woman who is Stock has no standing, no family entitled to ask, and no entry in any register but a merchant's book.
- **They need them to keep arriving.** Hence thirty-eight years of unremarkable, well-paid, boring commerce.

> ⚠️ **This is the same logic the cult used on Dol Island with the first Penitents** — *taken alive on purpose, because the ritual needed volume and nobody comes looking for a Penitent.* **It is one organisation with one method, and the party has already seen it work.**

## 3. Why artists — three answers, stacked

**Do not explain this. Let them stack it themselves.**

### a) To starve her *(the strategic reason)*

Saraswati lives on **mana generated by people appreciating great work.** `Saraswati and Adephagia.md`. **Every artist removed from the world is a tap closed.**

Thirty-eight years of quietly buying up the people who make beautiful things is not cruelty. **It is a siege.**

### b) To find her tools *(the operational reason — and the best one)*

⚠️ Before the final battle Saraswati spent nearly all her remaining power making **a set of art-items** — brushes, chisels, instruments, pens — scattered across the world. *(`Saraswati and Adephagia.md`.)*

**An artist who is suddenly, inexplicably better than their own practice accounts for is holding one.**

> **So: buy the artists. Ask them questions. The ones who are merely good are fuel. The one who is impossibly good has a thing you want.**

⚠️ **Hanne Vetter was merely good.** Skilled, with her own wheel, worth a line in a ledger — **and nothing more than her own talent.** She was fuel. **And then she turned out to be something else.** *(`The Patient.md`.)*

⚠️⚠️ **And this is exactly what Corbin Wainwright is doing.** He is *"employing people to research and learn about other artists in search of the other magic items"* — `Corbin Wainwright and Orla Miller.md`.

> **Corbin and the cult are running the same search by different methods.** He is doing a benign version of an atrocity, he does not know it, and **he is an artist of growing reputation in a city whose artists are disappearing, holding one of the items, and he has not left.**

### c) Because they feel it more *(the horrible reason — optional)*

**Only use this if you want the cult to have an aesthetic.** A person who makes things, prevented from making them, held in a room for years, suffers in a way a labourer does not — and the men in the robes believe that, and have measured it, and have a preference.

**It is the single most repellent thing available in this document.** It also explains the scars on the buyers: *they are devout and they practise on themselves.*

## 5. Where they are

**The Working Ledger gives a direction, not a place.** Add this line and let them chase it:

> *"Eleven head, lot sold whole. Ashgate. Carriage inland, east road past Haleyon. Settled by draft, Vance & Oltmer."*

### ⚠️ And there is an earlier Ashgate line in the same book

**Three months back. One name. A destination the party has personally stood inside.**

> *"One woman, sold singly. **Hanne Vetter.** Potter, skilled, own wheel. Ashgate. Carriage to St. Pyotr's Sanitorium."*

> ### 🔑 Two things fall out of this and both are large
>
> **1. St. Pyotr's was an Ashgate destination** — the Tormentors' Catalyst site *before* the holding east of Underwood. ⚠️ **The party did not destroy it. They compromised it** — walked in, killed five cultists, broke the ritual, and left alive knowing where it was. **The building still stands and the stone ring is still in it.** What the cult lost was privacy, exactly as on Dol. **So they moved the people east and left the door where it was.** ⚠️ **That is why the eleven from Bramblefen were carted inland instead**, and why the Hecatomb is still at St. Pyotr's. The party caused the relocation, has never known it, and can work it out from two lines in a book.
>
> **2. Hanne Vetter is the woman they healed and left in an unlocked cell** at St. Pyotr's on 18 August. ⚠️ **She is now the Herald who runs the holding those eleven women were sent to.** *(`The Patient.md` — read it before this thread goes anywhere.)*
>
> **No roll to connect it.** They have a name with a destination and a memory of what was at that destination. **Let it land while somebody is reading, in a quiet room, looking for eleven other people.**

⚠️ **East of Underwood. Which is a day outside Haleyon. Which is where Elizabeth caught the green.**

### The site

**A holding, not a temple.** Somewhere that was built to keep people and has been repurposed: an old estate, a flooded quarry, a shuttered mine, a monastery whose order died out. ⚠️ **Recommend something that already had cells**, because the cult did not build this — **it bought it, the same way it buys everything.**

**What is there:**

- **Sixty-odd people**, held, in varying states, in a place designed to hold rather than to kill.
- **The men in the scarred robes**, who are devout, unhurried, and treat it as work. *(The same register as the beach on Dol — they talked about other things while they did it.)*
- ⚠️ **Two bosses, and the party owes a debt to each.**
  - **The Patient — Hanne Vetter.** `The Patient.md`. Potter, bought by Ashgate, broken at St. Pyotr's, **left alive in an unlocked cell by this party.** She runs the holding. There is a wheel in her rooms and the work on it is extraordinary.
  - ⚠️ **The pale man in the ornate headdress** — the herald from Chapter 4 who **escaped out the back of the chapel** when the party could not finish him. Arc Plan Chapter 13's target. **He found her, and brought her into the order, and she has since surpassed him, and he resents it.**
- **Art-items.** At least one, taken off an artist who never understood what she had. *(Arc Plan Ch13: "Recover an art-item.")*

**Ready-made option:** the Arc Plan's **coven of witches inland** is already framed as a cult cell feeding the Hecatomb, with `Yagas_Brew_5E.md` sitting in the catalogue for it. ⚠️ **If you use it, override that module's "everyone is already dead" ending** — see §4, and see the Chapter 7 review, which flags that exact repetition.

## 6. 📜 The paper trail — how they actually get there

**Three steps, all of them in documents the party already holds or can reach.**

| Step | What it gives |
|---|---|
| **1 — The Working Ledger** | The lot, the date, the direction, **and the name of the counting house that guaranteed the draft.** |
| **2 — The counting house** | ⚠️ **This is the real lead and it is a social problem, not a dungeon.** *Vance & Oltmer* (or your name) is a respectable Solteres firm that has guaranteed letters of credit for Ashgate for decades. **They do not know what they are financing. They know exactly how much they are paid not to ask.** A ledger, a clerk, a guarantor's signature, an address for remittances. |
| **3 — Ironwake's transport records** | **Everything that moves in or out of Solteres is taxed.** Eleven women in a carriage on the east road is a consignment with a weight, a date, and a destination. *(Arc Plan §5 — the same records that hold the barrels. **See §8.**)* |

> ⚠️ **Cobb's Ledger, now in Ysabel's hands, holds thirty-eight years of the same guarantor names.** If the party ever wants the whole pattern rather than one lot, **they have to go back to her** — which is a reason to care what happened to her that has nothing to do with liking her.

### And the names behind Ashgate

**Ashgate is a filing error that became a fact.** The buyers never gave a name because **they do not have one they would say out loud** — but the counting houses needed *something* on the instrument, and somebody, decades ago, wrote down whatever Cobb's predecessor had written down, and it propagated.

> **So "Ashgate" appears in the books of respectable Solteres firms, as a client, with a credit history.**
>
> **It has been a legitimate commercial entity for thirty-eight years and it has never existed.** That is a better horror than a secret name, and it is the sort of thing that gets a counting-house clerk very quiet when you point at it.

## 8. 🔗 How this ties to the barrels

**Three ways, and the third one is the big one.**

### a) Same tax records

**Ironwake taxes everything.** The barrels and the women moved on the same roads, through the same system, paid for by the same kind of instrument. ⚠️ **One raid on his records answers both questions**, which is exactly why Chapter 10 is Ironwake.

### b) Same destination logic

**Mana in barrels. Suffering in people.** The rite needs both in the same place at the same time. ⚠️ **So wherever the barrels are going, the people are going too** — or the two streams converge at the end.

**Decide which:**

- **One site.** Everything goes to the Hecatomb. Simple, and makes the finale the only target.
- ⭐ **Two sites, one convergence.** *(Recommended.)* **The women are held and harvested inland, east of Underwood. The barrels are stored elsewhere. Both are shipped to St. Pyotr's for the summoning itself.** That gives the party two separate places they can hurt the cult *before* the finale — which is exactly what the Chapter 7 review asked for: let prior investigation change the last confrontation.

### c) ⚠️⚠️ The green

**This is the connection I would build the whole act on, and it is already sitting in your canon.**

- **The green is caused by the rift** — the Prison Realm bleeding into biology. `Elizabeth and the Green.md` §6.
- **Father Ignatius spent years at St. Pyotr's deliberately thinning the barrier — with torture.** He believed pain drove the infestation out. He was wrong about why and **right that it worked.** `Chapter 4`, `Player Open Questions.md`.
- **Underwood began turning green about two years ago** — the same season the Ashgate purchases surged.
- **And the women are being held and tortured, continuously, a day's ride from Underwood.**

> ### 🔑 Therefore:
>
> **The torture is not just fuel. It is the drill.**
>
> Sustained suffering **thins the barrier** wherever it is done. The cult is not gathering a resource; it is **boring a hole**, and the green is what leaks back through.
>
> **Underwood is not a plague. It is a symptom of the room next door.**

**What that unlocks:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **It answers the party's own open question** | *"What is doing this?"* — and the answer is a place they can walk to. |
| ⚠️ **It makes stopping the women's suffering a cure** | Not for the infected. **For the cause.** Free those sixty people and the drilling stops. **That is a victory with a visible result**, and this campaign badly needs one. |
| **It validates Ignatius** | He was mad and he was not wrong, and that should be deeply unpleasant for Wolfram. |
| **It gives Virgil his answer** | He went to Underwood to find the cause of the green. **The cause is a holding full of tortured artists sixteen miles away.** ⚠️ **Virgil may well find it before the party does** — which is a letter arriving at exactly the right moment in Chapter 9 or 10. |

## 9. If the party goes now instead of after Rowan

**They might. Be ready and do not block it.**

**The honest answer is that they cannot get there yet, and the reason is geography, not DM fiat:**

- The site is **east of Haleyon** — the wrong direction entirely from Solteres, and weeks of travel.
- **Rowan is in Solteres, now, with a man who has a deadline.**
- ⚠️ **And the women have been alive for seven weeks and will be alive in two more.** The cult is not in a hurry. **That is the whole point of the method.**

> **Let JonLief or the apprentice make that argument.** It is true, it is grim, and it is the kind of arithmetic this party has had to do before.

**What they can do right now, in Solteres, without leaving:**

1. **Find the counting house.** §6. It is a city errand and it is the actual next step.
2. **Ask Corbin about artists who got better than they should have.** §3b. ⚠️ He will go very quiet.
3. **Get Ironwake's transport records** — which is Chapter 10 and they are not ready, and finding that out is useful.
