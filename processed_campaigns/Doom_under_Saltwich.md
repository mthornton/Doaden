**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–10 (Normal 5–7, Torment 8–10)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
An investigatory adventure set entirely in the isolated seaside town of Saltwich. The party is hired to find Laurent Gilmiere, a missing cleric of Azerai who was chasing "magical emanations," and instead uncovers Saltwich's horrifying secret: its people carry dormant N'Gorroth blood and periodically metamorphose into fish-hybrid servants of the slumbering Deep One, Ab'zal. Gilmiere himself was a hidden descendant of Saltwich stock, and the party's search leads them from town investigation into open combat with the cult and a final confrontation offshore.

## Key Locations
- Upper Saltwich — Town Square, Floating Sailor Inn, and the cliffside Forlorn Abbey (view of the offshore Sunken Temple spire)
- Lower Saltwich — Sodden Graveyard, Ruined Chapel (bell tower), Lighthouse (lures ships onto the reefs), Fishery (gutting warehouse and rowboat access point)
- Luminous Grotto & Sunken Temple — offshore, boat-only; algae-farming sea cave, the N'Gorroth Giant's chamber, and the Adoration Room where Ab'zal is worshiped
- Sunken Temple spire — only the highest chamber is above water except during the rare Ab'zal's Awakening

## Session Flow
1. **Ch. 1 — Upper Saltwich:** Interview the Drunkard and Innkeeper Marshley, search the inn (Gilmiere's Notes 1–3 + map), and the Forlorn Abbey (Note 4, spot the Sunken Temple spire).
2. **Ch. 2 — Lower Saltwich:** The Drunkard rings the chapel bell, triggering back-to-back wave encounters (Graveyard → Chapel). Search the Lighthouse and Fishery (Notes 5–6) and acquire rowboats.
3. **Ch. 3 — The Luminous Grotto:** Row to the Sunken Temple's exposed chamber, then the Grotto itself — fight through the algae farm to the N'Gorroth Giant boss fight, then confront the Abyssal High Priest and Gilmiere in the Adoration Room.
4. **Epilogue:** Party pieces together Gilmiere's fate from collected notes and returns his gull amulet to Father Armitage for the reward.

## Secrets & Twists
- Innkeeper Marshley is himself a hidden Strangler covering for the whole town
- Saltwich's food staple, blue algae, is cultivated on the corpses of murdered travelers
- Gilmiere was a secret descendant of Saltwich stock — his "research trip" was actually his N'Gorroth blood calling him home
- The Abyssal Pastor the party sees first in the Adoration Room is an illusion; the real Gilmiere is invisible nearby

## Faction Notes (The N'Gorroth)
- An ancient undersea society predating humanity, once building marble temple-cities across Doaden's deep oceans in worship of the Deep Ones — now decaying like the rest of the world
- Saltwich's fisherfolk carry diluted N'Gorroth blood; in mid-adulthood many are compelled to return to their ancestral shore and metamorphose
- Transformation hierarchy: common Fisherfolk → Stranglers (weak specimens) → Shamblers/Trawlers (strong specimens) → Abyssal Pastors (arcane-gifted, rare) → Abyssal High Priests (ancient, tongueless, telepathic); N'Gorroth Giants are suspected to carry Deep One blood directly
- If the N'Gorroth's gods, the Deep Ones, ever fully awaken, it is said to spell the end of Doaden

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- The Sunken Temple's full interior stays submerged and inaccessible until the next (unscheduled) Ab'zal's Awakening
- N'Gorroth genes can lie dormant for generations and surface unexpectedly in people with no known ties to Saltwich — a ready hook for future NPCs or even a PC
- Short Term Madness (d6-based fumble effects) triggered by failing the chapel bell encounter can linger for days if left unaddressed
