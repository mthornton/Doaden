# Mad Mage's Tower
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/B_One-Shot Adventures (Lvl 1-10)/Mad Mage's Tower (Lvl 1-10)/Mad Mage's Tower (Lvl 1-10).pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A one-shot dungeon crawl: the town of Neredge is stalked by nightmarish beasts from the reawakened tower of a once-renowned mad mage. The party ascends the tower's five magically warped floors, each guarded by a puzzle or encounter, to reach and slay the mage's monstrous creation on top. Scales via APL: Beginner (1–2), Easy (3–4), Medium (5–7), Hard (8–10).

## Key NPCs
| Name | Role | Motivation | Location | Notes |
|------|------|------------|----------|----------------|
| The Sentient Codex | Magical guardian (floating book) | Protect tower by binding intruders to a draining contract | Floor 1 | Offers knowledge for a signature; cursed contract drains HP each round until book destroyed or curse removed |
| Wandering Keyholder | Deformed ex-apprentice of the mad mage | Wanders performing odd alchemical tasks | Floor 3 | Carries keys to the stair-trigger device and treasure chests; scales acolyte → mage by difficulty |

## Key Locations
- Floor 1 (Ground Floor) — Grand entrance hall/library guarded by the Sentient Codex; staircase unlocks once Codex is defeated.
- Floor 2 — Circular room with three maiden statues, glass orbs, and a "two liars, one truth" logic puzzle; wrong orb explodes in fire; optional 10-minute descending-ceiling timer.
- Floor 3 (Laboratory/Living Quarters) — Multi-room floor with the Wandering Keyholder, an armillary Stair-Trigger Sphere (2a), spell scroll (2b), treasure bag (2c), potions (2d), unlocked/locked chests (2e/2f), supply closet (2g), and a Construct Storage Room (3).
- Floor 4 — Simple room with 4 numbered treasure chests, some of which are mimics; real key chest is always marked "2."
- Floor 5 — Vast ruined chamber of broken cell-enclosures where the mage's escaped monstrosity lairs; far wall caved in to the outside.

| Thread | Status | Notes |

| Ascend all 5 floors | Sequential — each floor's puzzle/encounter must be solved to unlock the next staircase | Floors do not conform to normal space (magically enlarged interiors) |

## Session Flow
1. Opening hook: villagers' pleas, an escaped experiment, or arcane-artifact rumors draw the party to the tower.
2. Floor 1 — negotiate/defeat the Sentient Codex to open the ascending stair.
3. Floor 2 — solve the Maiden/orb logic puzzle (optionally under a 10-minute ceiling-descent timer).
4. Floor 3 — explore the lab, deal with (or avoid harming) the Keyholder and dormant Construct, loot side rooms.
5. Floor 4 — pick the correct treasure chest (#2) among mimics to get the key and open the stone door.
6. Floor 5 — final battle vs. the tower monstrosity; find the mage's notes and a teleporting hand-mirror; return to Neredge as heroes.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Floor 3, area 2b: Spell scroll, level determined by 1d4 (cantrip–3rd level).
- Floor 3, area 2c: Small bag of treasure — 4d10 gp + 1d4 gems (50 gp each).
- Floor 3, area 2d: 1d4 potions of healing + 1 potion of poison.
- Floor 3, area 2e (unlocked chest): 4d10 gp.
- Floor 3, area 2f (locked chest, DC 15 Dex/thieves' tools or keyholder's key): 8d10 gp.
- Floor 3, area 2g (supply closet): 6d10 gp + 1d4 gems (50 gp each).
- Floor 5 loot roll (4d10 + difficulty bonus): 28+ yields a Nightmare Chimera Skull* (uncommon); otherwise nothing.
- Ending: mage's research notes and a magical handheld-mirror teleportation device.

## Secrets & Twists
- The Sentient Codex doesn't want to kill outright — it tries to bind PCs into a slow life-draining contract via signature.
- Two of the four Floor 4 chests are always mimics (or greater mimics on Hard); the chest marked "2" is guaranteed genuine because mimics can't duplicate a unique number without an original.
- If a shield guardian is used as the Floor 3 construct, the keyholder secretly holds its amulet and has stored a spell inside it.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Confirm which difficulty band (Beginner/Easy/Medium/Hard) matches the party's APL before each floor, and note it can be shifted mid-adventure if needed.
- Track which PCs signed the Codex's contract — cursed PCs take level-equal damage per round until cured/book destroyed.
- If running the maiden puzzle with the timer, track real elapsed time (10 minutes) separately from in-game time.
- The wandering keyholder must not be harmed carelessly, or the dormant construct in area 3 awakens and rampages.
