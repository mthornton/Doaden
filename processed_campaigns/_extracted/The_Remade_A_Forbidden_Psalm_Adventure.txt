              FOREWORD: THE WORLD OF DOADEN



W
            elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted monsters,
            where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very essence of existence.
            It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of
            glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie with one
another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.



In Forbidden Psalm: The Tainted lands of Doaden you will find:

• Three scenarios created for the fantastic monsters from Bestiarum Games;
• Rules for monsters.

These scenarios should be played in order.
These scenarios should be played in solo or coop only.

In all Scenarios the Darkness Condition is in effect.
Each warband gets 2 free torches to use each Scenario.



You will need a copy of the Forbidden Psalm Core Book to play:
https://www.forbiddenpsalm.com/forbidden-psalm

          he night is dark and quiet. The Warbands small campfire sends ashes floating into

  As they prepare to sleep, a sudden gust of air brushes across the camp.

                          SCENARIO I: AWAKEN



T
            he Warband wakes up in complete darkness to the sound of dripping water and a
            soft, slimy sensation beneath them.

          Each warband members whole body feels sore and tight, as if rigid from paralysis. As
they slowly regain control of their arms, a member of the warband reaches out in the utter
darkness, feeling the shape of their backpack nearby. They draw a torch from the pack and
with great effort fumbling with a piece of flint and tinder, manage to light it.

  The yellowing light pushes back the darkness, revealing the walls of a shallow pit around the
warband. The pit is filled with corpses; humans, animals, and strange beasts that the warband
has never seen before.
They are in various stages of decomposition, with clear signs of blunt damage from an
apparent fall.

 Climbing out of the pit, the warband finds a cave system with a tunnel path leading forward.
Cobwebs hang from the ceiling and the ground is strewn with small piles of bones.

 Somewhere in the dark, the sound of stones falling to the ground. There is a soft hiss followed
by fading stridulations of quickly, hard steps.



GOAL

    Find a way out.


REWARD

    You did not die!


SETUP AND TREASURE

 On a board 24 inches by 24 inches set up a tunnel system as shown in Map 1. Do not worry
about being accurate a rough tunnel system marked out with string, tape or terrain will do.

A1 – Body pit
Where the warbands are deployed. Bodies can be searched here twice by each warband as
per treasure rules.

A2 – Tunnel system
Contains 1D4+number of warbands participating, Cave spiders.

6
A3 - Bottomless hole
When a warband approaches this read the following.
I… I wouldn't go down there… if I were you.”
Here is where Grik joins the warband which killed the most enemies in A2, in the case of ties roll
off.
Once Grik joins a warband he is controlled by that player.

Grik has the following stats:

Strength: -1
Agility: 0
Presence: 0
Toughness: 0

He is equipped with a makeshift one-handed weapon and torch.
He has no flaws or feats.

A4 – Webs
Models must make an Agility test to make it through the webs, on a failure they lose their
movement and are stuck. They need to as an action make a strength test to break free of the
webs. Once a model breaks free or passes the tests, they ignore the webs for the rest of the
scenario. If they fumble a Cave Spider spawns in the webs within 1 inch of the model that
fumbled.

A5 – Elevator (around 12 inches by 12 inches)
Before the elevator will move a warband member in A6 must pull the leaver to remove the
breaks for the elevator. After the breaks are removed, the elevator will begin to ascend in
three rounds, at the end of the third round. Any models not on elevator are lost until scenario
3. When the Elevator begins to ascend read the section titled Escape.

A6 – Control room
When the lever in this room is pulled place in A7 1x Drider and 1x Flesh smith’s Daughter.

A7 - Webs
Models must make an Agility test to make it through the webs, on a failure they lose their
movement and are stuck. They need to as an action make a strength test to break free of the
webs. Once a model breaks free or passes the tests, they ignore the webs for the rest of the
scenario. If they fumble a Cave Spider spawns in the webs within 1 inch of the model that
fumbled.


ESCAPE

When the elevator begins to ascend models can no longer leave the area marked A5. Each
round at the end of the round, roll a D6 and on a result of a 6 the Brood Mother appears, or if 6
rounds have passed since the elevator began to move the Brood Mother also will appear.
                                                                                                  7
Until then every other round at the start of the monster activation phases 1x Swollen and 1x
Necro Warrior appears in a random corner of A5.
The Brood Mother appears at the start of the round in which she can appear, place her in the
centre of A5 and move all models in her way to the edges of the elevator.


DEPLOYMENT

All the warband models that are participating in this scenario should be placed in A1.


SOLO PLAY

Reduce the rounds on the elevator to a D4 and on a roll of a 4 or after 4 rounds then place
the Brood Mother.

The Scenario ends at the end of the round the Brood Mother first appeared.



RPG TEXT BOX

WHEN THE PARTY MEET GRIK READ:

Looking back you see a hunchback figure, frail and malnourished, peering out warily from
behind the tunnel corner. He is emaciated, his milk-white skin so translucent that you can see
the blue veins beneath it. His hair has mostly fallen out, just a few long strands of silky gray left
on his scalp. His form is misshapen and twisted, and it is impossible to tell his age.

He steps forth, shielding his wide eyes from your torchlight with one hand.

WHEN THE LIFT BEGINS TO MOVE READ:

You pull the lever and the wooden platform below you begins to shake. Metal gears grind
against one another, making a sharp whine as the machine begins a slow ascent. Looking up,
you cannot make out the ceiling. The tunnel rises up into complete darkness.

As the elevator climbs, you notice webs on the walls, growing ever thicker, and hear skittering
about in the darkness above. You draw your weapons.

Suddenly you hear a loud shriek as a mass of distorted monstrosities emerge from holes in the
walls. They holler in a cacophony of high-pitched screeches and lunge toward you.

WHEN THE BROOD MOTHER APPEARS READ:

As you wipe your blades of green ichor, you look upward, finally seeing the top of the cave.
You are so close to freedom.
8
But to your horror, you see a slick white mesh glistening off your torchlight, a dome of webs
covering the entire cave and a massive shadow skittering upon it. Bulging black eyes emerge
into the firelight followed by a gargantuan arachnid monstrosity. Huge legs ending in
humanoid hands support the creature's massive weight.
Its abdomen is a stitchwork of corpses and pulsating sacks dripping a foul concoction. Each
drop sizzles as it falls to the elevator platform, eating through the wood, leaving behind
nothing but black corrosion.
With a howl, the thing lunges onto the elevator. The platform shudders and stalls under its
weight.

AT THE END OF THE SCENARIO READ:

Grik shrieks, running from his hiding place behind a stack of chains. He yanks the elevator
lever, sending the machine in a frantic downward fall. The giant spider loses its grip on the
platform, hanging suspended in the tunnel above.

You plummet away, slamming moments later into the ground, losing your footing and crashing
into the wooden platform as it comes to a sudden halt.

                     SCENARIO III: SPIDER DEN



T
          he Warband descends the eerie descending tunnel lined with layers upon layers of
          webbing. As they reach the bottom of the hole, they drop to firm ground, finding
          themself in a large, hollow chamber connecting to sets of branching pathways,
          each disappearing into the darkness.
You hear faint skittering echoing through the shadows.



GOAL

Free any members of the warband lost in scenario 1. Fight your way to daylight.


REWARD

Any warband that has at least 1 surviving member at the end of the scenario gains 10xp and 1
random relic.


SETUP AND TREASURE

On a board 24 inches by 24 inches set up a tunnel system as shown in Map 3. Do not worry
about being accurate a rough tunnel system marked out with string, tape or terrain will do.

A1 – The entrance to the hole.
Where warbands are deployed.

A2 – Abandoned camp
Place two boxes here per warband that can be searched as per treasure rules.

A3 – Feeding ground
Here the party find any warband members lost in scenario 1 along with a member of the
Penitent Crusade. The warband members are stuck in webs along the walls and can be freed
with a successful strength check. In this area also place 2 Cave Spiders.
The Crusader is trapped in an Acid Cocoon. His lower body has been completely dissolved
already and there is no way to save his life. But he can be looted as per treasure rules.
If his cocoon is disturbed in any way, it will explode immediately and any creature within 1
inch. must succeed on an Agility test to avoid the spray or take 1d6 Acid Damage. The
crusader dies in the process.

A4 – Birthing Chamber
Models must make an Agility test to make it through the webs that cover this entire area. On a
failure they lose their movement and are stuck. They need to as an action make a strength
test to break free of the webs. Once a model breaks free or passes the tests, they ignore the
14
webs for the rest of the scenario. If they fumble a Cave Spider spawns in the webs within 1 inch
of the model that fumbled.
Warbands can avoid the web by jumping from wooden platform to wooden platform.
Place 1 Drider in this area per warband.

A5 – Fleshsmiths Throne
When a warband member enters this area place the FleshSmith on their throne in the area
marked A5. This creature is not hostile unless a warband member attacks it. A warband
member who is within 1 inch of the Fleshsmith as an action is granted blood to drink (see
Fleshsmiths gift below).

‘The Fleshsmith always stays in this area, unable to move from her seat. She is surrounded by
flesh materials that she uses in her reshaping process.

When the warbands first encounters her, she will offer an alliance, giving her blood freely if
they agree to slay the Broodmother and leave her in peace. If they agree, no other hostiles
bar the Brood Mother will attack them for the remainder of the scenario.’

If they Kill the Fleshsmith she can be searched for 2 random relics.
This area also contains the controls for the lift. As an action a warband member can call the
lift, which will arrive at the start of the next round. Grik is on the lift and re-joins his warband.

A6 – Elevator
Once the Elevator is called and arrived warband members can embark on the elevator. As an
action a warband member can pull the lever on the elevator and the elevator will begin to
ascend at the start of the next round. Any warband members left behind are killed.
As the elevator ascends the Brood Mother appears in the middle of the elevator, the
warbands must kill her to escape.



DEPLOYMENT

All warband members should be setup in A1.

Scenario end when the Brood Mother is dead.

FleshSmiths Gift:

Models that take the FleshSmiths gift are immune to the acid damage of the Brood Mother.
However, they take -1 Presence.

WHEN THE PLAYERS ENTER A3 READ:

A pile of corpses is stacked up at the far end of the small room. They are all naked, their
bodies thin and emaciated, their skin leathery and tight, as if drained of all liquid.

Hanging above the corpses is a greenish cocoon, wrapped tightly around a man’s body. The
man’s head sticks out of the top, clad in the unmistakable facemask of the Penitent Crusade.
He raises his head and whispers.

“I can’t feel my legs… help me.”

WHEN THE PLAYERS ENTER A4 READ:

Webs cover the entire floor of the large cavern. Several cocoons dangle from the ceiling to
hang a few feet from the ground, pulsating with a sickly green glow. They rock gently in place,
moving back and forth and dripping ichor that sizzles into the ground.

Monstrous-looking arachnids move about the place tending to the cocoons and chittering to
each other in a strange, inhuman language.

Along the left side of the cavern, a wooden column walkway rises above the webs and travels
around the room. However, it is shattered in several areas and several of the cocoons hang
dangerously close to the wooden planks.

WHEN THE PLAYERS ENTER A5 READ:

You enter a strange chamber. The floor is littered with corpses. Many are missing limbs or
appendages, and disconnected arms, heads, and torsos lie in organized piles.

Sitting in a bizarrely serene image of repose next to the body parts is an enormous form
resembling a beautiful woman, but from her back sprout a multitude of arachnid limbs,
lovingly rolling a body and slicing it open, extracting its intestines and inner organs.

The woman turns to you as you enter the chamber, her many arms deftly unraveling the
innards and placing the body down with the others. She speaks in a quiet, sad voice.
“So it is you who disturb my children. Please, may we share words?”

WHEN THE ELEVATOR BEGINS TO ASCEND READ:

You kick the last of the spider bodies off the elevator platform, its legs shattering as it bounces
off the rocky tunnel walls before disappearing into the darkness below.
