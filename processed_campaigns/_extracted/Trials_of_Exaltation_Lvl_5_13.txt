              World of Doaden

          elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands

W           and twisted monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction
                    corrodes the very essence of existence.

   It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and
heroes of the glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark
   gods vie with one another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.

  It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
  The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be
                      honored in songs of minstrels.

 For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake invariably
 leave them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless husks and
                 shadows of their once-glorious selves.

 Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn
           tenacity, a last flickering flame within the dark.

  A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of
        eternity, a momentary victory before the endless night.

    Ashurr, the City of Wonder
    The city of Ashurr is located in central Akhamia. It is a thriving
    metropolis filled with wonders; grand bazaars, libraries,
    hanging gardens, exotic markets, crystal fountains, vibrant
    harems, and much more. The Tiridus river’s blue-green waters
    flow along its outer banks. Reed-bound boats bob along the
    river’s surface, flowing into the city, bearing goods from afar.
    Canals and aqueducts bringing water to sprawling districts of
    sun-soaked clay buildings. The streets are lined with green
    foliage, trees, and shrubs. Yet of all the wonders, two stand out
    above the rest.
        Balazar’s Palace. Balazar’s palace is a gleaming sight.
    Its outer walls shine with stunning blue lapis lazuli and bright
    veins of gold. Idols and statues line the halls and magnificent
    tapestries drape every wall. The floors are lain in vibrant mosaic
    patterns. Fountains gush with crystalline water that flows into
    fruiting gardens. Tables are laden with exotic fruits and roasted
    meats, and the halls ring with dulcet tones of harps and the
    rapturous cries of orgies.
        The Ziggurat. An enormous ziggurat towers over the city,
    where Akhamians come to worship. It is the holiest of places
    for those on the path of enlightened suffering, and no outsiders
    are allowed inside. The only one who know what lies inside are
    the very exalted few who are chosen by Balazar himself.

    Balazar, the Lord of Excess
    The demon prince Balazar signifies gluttony and depravity. But
    he is also a collector of knowledge and all things, from artifacts
    to treasures, or spells and incantations. He rules from his high
    court in the city Ashurr. From this gilded throne, Balazar
    accumulates all manner of luxury, wealth, and pleasures. Many
    come from far and wide to suckle on the bounties of food,
