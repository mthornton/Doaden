# Open Questions
*For the party. Everything below is something you know that you don't know.*

**Where we stand:** five Penitents of the Church of Azerai, branded, owing five hundred years each — and one Shepherd's apprentice who isn't. Sent to Dol Island to find a missing team and eliminate whoever was operating there. We found the team, or what was left of it: Gimble. We still have not eliminated anybody. Virgil has gone to Underwood. A Shepherd found us at last and told us we were allowed. We are two days from Solteres with Lady Ysabel, and a book in our pack with a name in it that none of us has read.

---

## Rowan

- Vaskar sold the boy after a week alone in the tower. **His ledger names the buyer. Who is it, and is Rowan still there?**
- The slaves said Elizabeth stopped caring about anything — even the boy, even when he begged her to eat. **That was not the woman JonLief married. What happened to her?**
- She had green patches on her skin before they burned her, and green is growing out of the place where they did. **Is that the same thing that is happening in Underwood? How did it reach her?**

## The women, and the scarred men

- Every woman at Bramblefen was sold in one lot to hooded men with skin covered in scars, old and new. They paid a premium and never said why. **Who are they, why only women, and where did they take them?**
- Erwana was the only woman they didn't take. **What were they looking for that she didn't have?**

## The blood

- We have now seen it three times: going aboard a ship on Dol, in a stone circle at St. Pyotr's, and in a barrel in the Deeps at Bramblefen. **Who was the Bramblefen barrel waiting for — and how many more are there?**
- The book says an Aspect of Brudda Death needs **massive mana** and **a Catalyst of Suffering.** We know what the blood is for. **Where is the suffering coming from?**
- The Deeps felt like St. Pyotr's, only weaker. **Why?**

## The woman with too many arms

- Wolfram saw a woman chained inside a cracked, bleeding tomb on Dol. She looked back at him — frightened, and sorry. Then she was gone. **Where is she now?**
- Two dozen bodies lie in a sunlit cave on Dol, dead about two years. No weapons. Homemade jewellery. **Who were they, and what were they doing there?**
- At Bramblefen, while Luthien played for the freed, there was a glowing woman at the edge of his sight. Four arms. Then six. She left when the song did. **Who was she?**

## St. Pyotr's

- Father Ignatius wrote that he could feel the barrier to **another realm** wearing thin, and meant to wear it through. Then somebody nailed him to a wall. **Did he succeed?**
- The black fog killed a bird the moment it touched it. **What is it, and is it still there?**
- The herald in the headdress got away. **Where did he go, and did we actually stop anything?**
- **We never opened the trapdoor in the garden.**

## The people

- A very old man sat down with us at the Inn. Nobody else in a crowded room could see him. He thanked us for stopping the summoning at St. Pyotr's — which we hadn't told anyone about. **Who was that?**
- The hounds that killed Alice and Red had iron worked into their bodies. **Somebody made those. Who, and why send them at a cabbage farm?**
- Vaskar walked out of Bramblefen saying Ironwake would probably kill him for it. **Did he?**
- Anselm told the Bishop why Wolfram did what he did, and the Bishop never wrote a word of it down. **Why not?**
- We never found the crew of the *Hreowan.* **Do people just stop existing out here?**

## Underwood

- A farming village a day from Haleyon has been turning green for two years — patches, then pods, then spores, then death. The infected stop caring and start protecting the pods. A Bramblefen guard called it **the Green**, and said it was a plague out east. **What is doing this?**
- The magic is in the **seeds**, not the fruit: enchantment, necromancy and transmutation braided together. **That is a design. Designed by whom?**
- A **coven of witches** is growing them somewhere east. Willowbread died getting that much out.
- The Church refused to send help because of **a war in the north.** **What war? Since when?**

## Us

- Camp guards wear the Mark of Atonement, faded. So Penitents can be released. **Has anyone ever actually seen it happen?**
- Baltus found us. **Does Vozruk know where we are now — and what will Baltus tell him?**
- Baltus said a Penitent may act beyond his orders to serve the will of the Emperor. **How far beyond? And who decides afterwards whether we did?**
- Fid gave Luthien the Ball of Undeath against Vozruk's direct order, and slipped Wolfram a second knitting spider behind his back. **Why is the camp's inventor quietly arming us?**

## What we're carrying

| | |
|---|---|
| **Vaskar's ledger** | His own working book, current to the day the fen fell. **Names the man who bought Rowan. Nobody has read it yet.** |
| **The amulet** | **One charge left.** It goes one place only: the pentagram in the crypt on Dol, from anywhere. *When would we ever want to be there?* |
| **The book** | *Myths of the Old Gods*, from the St. Pyotr's library. JonLief has read it and told us what's in it. Written by a Church abbot, and it says the old gods were real. |
| **The map** | Off a dead cultist. It got us to St. Pyotr's. Is there anything else on it? |
| **The keys** | Father Ignatius's ring. We never found out what half of them open. |
| **The guitar** | Bone. Taken off a pile in the armory. Nobody ever asked whose it was. |
| **The Ball of Undeath** | Thrown right, it only hurts the dead. Thrown wrong, it hurts us. |
| **Two knitting spiders** | Wolfram has both. |
| **Six jars of pickled cabbage** | Taken from a dead man's house. We are not proud of this. |

*Given away:* **Old Cobb's book** went to Lady Ysabel, as promised.

## Questions we could actually answer, if we bothered

1. **Read Vaskar's ledger.** The answer to the most important question on this page is in our own pack.
2. **Ask Gimble everything.** He was on Dol before us and in that cage for weeks. We have barely started.
3. **Ask the apprentice what a Shepherd actually does.** He's standing right there.
4. **Ask anyone in Solteres about the barrels.** Everything that moves through that city goes through somebody's books.

## What we said we'd do

- **Rowan first** — then use the ledger to find where the women went, or where the barrels were going. We told Baltus that.
- We agreed to see Lady Ysabel to Solteres. She has asked all of us to fight for her. All of us said no.
- Wolfram told Ald there were no more beasts near his village. Since then we have sent him Erwana and **thirty freed slaves**, on Wolfram's name, without asking him first.
- We were ordered to find out what happened on Dol Island and eliminate whoever was operating there. **That order has never been rescinded.**
