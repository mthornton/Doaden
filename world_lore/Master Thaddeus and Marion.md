# Master Thaddeus and Marion

Members of the Alchemical Guild  Master Thaddeus is a human that has transformed into a giant worm.  Marion is his wife, is human, and does not speak.  They can communicate telepathically.  Marion is a tall stout woman.  Thaddeus is usually wrapped around her body on the outside of clothing. 
 
Thaddeus comes from a long line of Alchemists.  His education was guided by his parents.  He believes that alchemy is the key to making world a better place. 
 
When he was 9 year old, his mother passed from disease.  The cause of the disease is unknow, but was likely a consequence of her experiments in alchemy.  He was then raised by his father, who is a knowledgeable and kind man, but nothing could replace the bond Thaddeus' bond with his mother. 
 
Thaddeus became obsessed with finding ways to extend human life by enhancing the body's natural ability to fight off disease.  His research led his to study giant squids.  Squids are well known to often live in excess of a thousand years and seemingly don't suffer from disease at all.  Thaddeus' research was to extract the essence of a squid's biology and apply to a human. 
 
His research showed a lot of promise and became known as a rising your star in the Alchemical Guild.  It was at this time he met Marion.  A brilliant, but socially awkward young woman.  Her research was focused on telepathy.  She believed that all creatures had the innate ability to communicate telepathically, it just needed a chemical unlock.   
 
Thaddeus and Marion worked out of the same lab.  As young people tend to do, they fell in love and were married.  Their marriage also applied to their research.  Marion theorized that all squids communicate telepathically.  If Thadeus could be successful applying the essence of a squid to a human, that might just make them resistant to disease but also telepathic. 
 
As the research became more and more promising, Thaddeus made an arrogant decision to test his concoctions on himself and Marrion.  Things did not work out as hoped.  One a positive, they did develop the ability to communicate with each other through telepathy.  However, Marrion lost the ability to speak.  Her mouth and throat transformed into the beak of a squid.  Thaddeus had it even worse.  His entire body transformed.  Not into a squid, but into a tentacle.
