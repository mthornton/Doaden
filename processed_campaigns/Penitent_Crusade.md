**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/B_One-Shot Adventures (Lvl 5-13)/ (Indictum Dominus Lvl 5-10; Purgation Lvl 5-10; Under Cover of Darkness Lvl 5-13) + Penitent Crusade Part 1–3 Lore
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–13
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A three-part anthology following the Penitent Crusade, the Church of Azerai's zealot military order, across three unrelated one-shots that share a faction rather than a continuous plot:
1. **Indictum Dominus** — the party investigates cult ritual murders in the town of New Heldon under Crusade martial law; the "trusted" abbot running the investigation is the cult leader.
2. **Purgation** — a rogue Iron Maiden (Nefryna) burns the northern town of Briga with a captured fire giant and Crusade wave-troops; the party is hired to stop her before it sparks open war.
3. **Under Cover of Darkness** — the party infiltrates High Lord Azriel's (supreme Crusade commander) siege encampment outside Stürmenburg to steal the Crusade Standard, then escapes a mounted chase.
Part 1 Lore file (`Penitent_Crusade_Part_1_Lore.txt`) is empty/placeholder — no lore content extracted from it; Part 1's faction fluff lives instead in Indictum Dominus's own Appendix A.

## Key Locations
- **New Heldon** (Part 1) — growing town built on Lost Age ruins; Old Heldon Cathedral, New Heldon Monastery & Menhir Stone, Dragon's Eye Mine, Stonemasons Guild Hall, Weaponsmith's Shop, Guardsman Barracks, Town Hall, Hagmeat Tavern, Old Garden Ruins, Farmtown Mead Hall, the Market — full player-hub town, reusable for future adventures.
- **Old Heldon Cathedral** (Part 1) — ruined Lost Age cathedral atop a mountain cliff; site of the Jailer's first battle; collapsed Grand Bell; hidden stairwell down to the caverns.
- **The Hekatomb / Underground Caverns** (Part 1) — subterranean ritual site beneath the Cathedral, reached via the Dragon's Eye Mine cave-in; site of the Jailer's second, "lair" battle; Mercereau's secret cult HQ.
- **Briga** (Part 2) — small hillside northern town, burned during Nefryna's unsanctioned purge; Briga Streets and Briga (Town) Square are the two combat maps.
- **Crusade Encampment near Stürmenburg** (Part 3) — walled military camp (Map A) with 10 numbered zones (gates, barracks, Commander's Tent, Standard Yard, horse pen, checkpoint); Map B "Rolling Road" is the escape chase route.
- **Stürmenburg** — walled northern city under siege by Azriel; never entered on-screen, referenced as the political backdrop for Parts 2 & 3.

## Session Flow (per part)
**Part 1 — Indictum Dominus:** Act 1 — party hired via letter, investigates 5 scattered murder scenes (Stonemasons Hall, Weaponsmith's Shop, Guardsman Barracks, Farmtown Mead Hall, Market), interviewing/interrogating suspects, reporting daily to Aquilon. Act 2 — a coded scripture note reveals timing/location of the last two murders; ritual completes regardless; Aspect of the Jailer rises and is fought at the Cathedral (Stage 1), then chased down through descending caverns (Stage 2, in its lair). Act 3 — Mercereau's guilt is exposed; Aquilon turns on the party to bury the secret; escape via Cathedral (brutal Crusade gauntlet) or old mine tunnel.

**Part 2 — Purgation:** One-session boss-fight adventure. Chapter 1 — fight through burning Briga Streets (3 wave encounters: Flagellants, Azerai Mage + Flame Defender, Purifying Machine). Chapter 2 — first battle with Surtr in the town square, using chained ballistae. Chapter 3 — an Azerai Mage reignites and heals Surtr; second, enraged fight ends the scenario.

**Part 3 — Under Cover of Darkness:** Encounter 1 — stealth infiltration of Azriel's walled camp (10 zones, guard patrols, brazier/oil-barrel alarm mechanics) to reach the Standard Yard and steal the Crusade Standard (magically alarmed). Encounter 2 — if the alarm triggers, an 8-round chase on an Armored Transport Wagon pursued by High Lord Azriel (flying in on his sphinx) and Repentor Cavalry.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Part 1: 20 gp retainer per party member up front, +80 gp on completion of the investigation (per the Crusade's Player Letter).
- Part 1: After the Jailer's final defeat, roll once on the DMG Treasure Hoard: Challenge 5–10 table.
- Part 2: +1 Renown with the Templar Rangers per party member on mission completion (spent on Order-cell benefits per the Bestiarum 5e GM Guide).
- Part 3: Commander's Tent (A4) has two locked chests — one with 3x Potions of Greater Healing, one with 2d8 gold; opened with the Camp Leader's Key (held by the Knight Redeemer) or a DC 16/18/20 Sleight of Hand check.
- Setting-wide reminder: gold/items circulate at only 1/10th normal D&D rates; magic items are extraordinarily rare.

## Secrets & Twists
- Abbot Mercereau, New Heldon's beloved caretaker abbot, is secretly the Tormentor Cult leader driving the entire ritual murder plot (Part 1).
- Aquilon, the party's own patron, is bound by Crusade law to execute the party once Mercereau's Church-embarrassing secret comes out — "helpful ally" becomes final boss (Part 1).
- Cultists tortured by Alain will never give up Mercereau's real name — only a GM-chosen decoy — so players can be misled even by "successful" interrogations (Part 1).
- Nefryna's motives in Purgation are deliberately left blank for the GM to fill in (retaliation, zealotry, ambition, or calculated strategy) — no single canonical reason (Part 2).
- The Standard in Part 3 is a trap: touching/moving it without disarming its magical alarm instantly escalates to the full chase encounter.

## Faction: The Penitent Crusade
- Military arm of the Church of Azerai; recruits mainly from criminals and sinners "atoning" under the Order of Atoners' Path of Redemption; soldiers wear expressionless masks and are bound to silence under penalty of death.
- Two parallel command tracks per regiment: a Primus (military/tactical authority, e.g. Aquilon, Azriel) and an Atoner (spiritual/doctrinal authority) — often in tension with each other.
- Ranks referenced across the trilogy (roughly weakest→strongest): Husk, Flagellant, Irredeemed, Bastion, Quickblade, Supplicator, Deliverer, Azerai Mage, Locusts, Flame Defender, Knight Redeemer, Flameblade Knight, Repentant/Repentor, Scarlet Centurion, Repentor Cavalry, Penitent Bulwark, Iron Maiden, Repentor Primus, Atoner, High Lord Azriel (supreme commander); Durand and Purifying Machine are Crusade siege engines; the Aspect of the Jailer is the Tormentor Cult's summoned foe, not a Crusade unit.
- Common folk regard the Crusade with fear more than gratitude — "salvation" from the Crusade or death from monsters is the realm's grim choice, per Doaden's setting fiction (Dreyen Solius, Journal of the World's Ending, quoted in both Part 1's Appendix A and both Faction Lore documents).

- Empire vs. Stürmenburg/the North political conflict is unresolved at the end of Part 3; war or peace hinges on Harvunn's decision regarding Hamarhelm.
