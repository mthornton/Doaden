# Rise of the Jailer
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/A_Starting Adventures (Lvl 1-3)/2_Rise of the Jailor (Lvl 1-10)/Rise of the Jailer (Lvl 1-10).pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The party tracks a band of cultists to a forsaken chapel in the woods, where thirty years ago a boy named Tommit led fellow institutionalized children in murdering the clergy to complete the Libation of Agony, a ritual pact with an extradimensional demon called the Jailer. Now the cult has found a powerful enough energy source (the buried god's blood from *Burial Isle*) to give the Jailer a physical form. The party must investigate the chapel, reconstruct and perform the Libation of Agony themselves, and follow the cult into the Jailer's hellish Prison Realm to fight it before it escapes into the world. Scales APL 1–10; the final boss fight is intentionally brutal for low-level parties.

## Session Flow
1. **Arrival** — party tracks cultists through the woods by night to the Forsaken Chapel
2. **Level A investigation** — explore ground floor, find the ritual symbol bodies, loot Room of Sentencing (A4), get ambushed at A10 and recover Tommit's Journal, unlock Golden Reliquary
3. **Level B (optional)** — climb belfries for Brother Remus's body, keys, and 4th ritual symbol
4. **Level C** — descend to the Cellarium, meet Mad Mort, gather remaining ritual pieces in the Treatment Room (C2)
5. **The Libation of Agony** — party performs the ritual themselves using available bodies and the journal's instructions
6. **The Prison Realm** — transported to fight the Jailer across up to 3 phases; Tommit flees mid-transformation
7. **Ending** — victory returns the party as heroes (Jailer banished, not destroyed); defeat returns them battered but alive, with dread knowledge the Jailer will return

## Secrets & Twists
- The fight against the Jailer is explicitly designed as likely unwinnable for parties below level 8 — if defeated, the module intends the party survive and escape rather than die, carrying forward the dread that the Jailer will return
- The Jailer's belly is an "agonizing digestion" prison — swallowed PCs take ongoing acid damage and heal the Jailer each of its turns; escape requires a DC 21 Strength check
- Blood Orbs spawned in Phase II/III grant an instant short rest but curse the drinker (-10 max HP for 24 hours) — a risky mid-fight resource
- Tommit himself escapes/transforms off-screen and is not fought in this module — set up as a recurring villain

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- PoP framing: this module follows immediately after *Burial Isle*; advance the party to level 2 and grant a long rest before starting, and to level 3 (plus 4 years off their Crusade sentence) after completing both modules
- Vozruk debriefs the party after each mission in a PoP campaign — good beat for building that relationship
- The Jailer surviving sets up future Doaden adventures; keep note of whether the party won or fled for continuity
