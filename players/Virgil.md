# Virgil

> ### 🍄 DM — Virgil now holds an alternate victory condition
>
> **Rowan is immune to the green** *(bought from Brudda Death by his mother — `world_lore/Elizabeth and the Green.md`)*, and **Virgil is the only person alive who could read that immunity and reproduce it.** Diagnostic → vaccine → possibly a cure.
>
> Under the containment rule the pandemic is a weapon that only fires when the rift opens, so **a vaccine defuses it before the finale.** The party could lose at the Hecatomb and still save the world.
>
> Three things this closes at once: **he was right** — a controlled version of the thing is exactly his thesis, and the Church gave him five hundred years for saying so; **it reopens Thaddeus's offer** to earn back his Order standing, which he turned down in Ch6; and **it is the one thing that could plausibly buy a discharge.** Baltus Thole has watched one person walk out of the Office free in twenty-two years. Virgil could be the second.
>
> ⚠️ **And it costs Rowan** — repeated access to a traumatised child, from a man who is not gentle and does not really see him. **JonLief crossed a continent for that boy.** Neither PC is wrong. Do not resolve it for them.

Virgil was an alchemist of the Alchemic Order who became obsessed with studying fungi, believing they were the key to humanity’s next stage of evolution. He performed experiments on corpses to test fungal symbiosis and argued constantly with other alchemists, convinced that he alone understood the future of mankind. While traveling with an Order caravan through Azerai territory, several rivals secretly exposed his research to the local Church authorities, not because the experiments were unusual for the Order, but because his arrogance had made him many enemies. The Church declared his work blasphemous, imprisoned him, and sentenced him to serve with the Penitent. Virgil still believes his research is correct and sees his punishment as proof that others fear the truth he has discovered.
