# Adephagia

## Background
Identifies as a woman, but you would never be able to tell from the looks of her. 
 
The Goddess of Gluttony.  Apephagia never had many followers, but peoples natural greed and lust fed her well.  She is the only old god that still has real power.  As long as people are people, she will always have a steady supply of mana. 
 
But, as true glutton, that is never enough.  She wants to have more and for others to have less. 
 
Her appearance is that of a monster.  She is terrible jealous of Saraswati.  Saraswati barely acknowledged her existence until Adephagia openly moved against her. 

# Folk song about Adephagia

The old night hag is a woman of might 
Her stomach is deep as a bottomless night 
 
She'd eat a whole hog before breakfast was done 
And treat every plate like a race to be won 
 
She'd finish the ham, cheese and pies 
And plan the next feast with a glint in her eyes 
 
But the moment the pudding was scraped from the bowl 
A new different hunger would seize her soul 
 
She's not picky, winks at every man in sight 
And vanish in haste toward a chamber of night 
 
No time for whispers, no time for romance 
She'd pin them to the bed and off with their pants! 
 
Her need for affection was swift and profound 
With very few limits or safe sacred ground 
 
When the act is done her legs will seal 
And her exhausted lover is her very next meal!
