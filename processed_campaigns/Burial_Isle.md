# Burial Isle
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/A_Starting Adventures (Lvl 1-3)/1_Burial Isle (Lvl 1-10)/Burial Isle (Lvl 1-10).pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The party is shipwrecked (or arrives deliberately) on a deserted island where a group of cultists is excavating an ancient Scythrian tomb, harvesting the corrupted blood of an evil god entombed within. The party must delve through trapped shrines and undead guardians to stop the cult before the god's blood (and possibly the god itself) is loosed on the world. Scales APL 1–10 via Beginner/Easy/Medium/Hard difficulty bands throughout.

## Key Locations
- Shipwreck Shore (Map A) — starting beach; wrecked ship, drowned cultist corpses, pools of corrupted "necrotic blood," a bone pile trap, and the excavation tunnel entrance
- Floating Cavern (Map B) — underground cavern with a floating rock island reached by rope bridge; recent cultist campsite; rune-door puzzle guarding the tomb entrance; hidden alcove with 12 sarcophagi of tomb guardians
- Buried Tomb (Map C) — the Scythrian burial tomb proper: flagstone floor-trap passage (C1), saw-blade trap hallway (C2), Chamber of the God with the god's sarcophagus (C3), Graves of Warriors with poison-gas geysers (C4), Chamber of Kings with a guardian watcher statue (C5), a small treasure alcove (C6), Chamber of the Guardian / final barricaded exit (C7), tunnel back to a second beach (C8)

## Plot / Quest Threads
| Thread | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Stop the cult from harvesting the god's blood | Core objective | Resolved (or not) at the Standard Ending beach encounter |
| Solve the tomb door rune puzzle | Gate to Buried Tomb | Solution: touch runes middle, left, then right — spelling "soul"; clue found on a dead cultist's note at the door |
| Solve the flagstone floor trap | C1 | Safe stones spell "soul" in mirrored (reverse) order per the cultist's note ("mirror soul to escape death") |
| Fate of the entombed god | C3 | The god vanishes from its sarcophagus 5 minutes after the party inspects the crack in the coffin — an evil is unleashed upon the world |

## Session Flow
1. **Shipwreck Shore** — party wakes shipwrecked, investigates wreck/bodies/blood pools, scavenges crates, survives the bone-pile trap, finds the excavation tunnel
2. **Floating Cavern** — crosses rope bridge, finds cult campsite, solves the rune door puzzle (or triggers guardian ambushes), descends to the guardian alcove
3. **Buried Tomb** — navigates the flagstone floor trap and saw trap, reaches the Chamber of the God (evil is unleashed), fights through Graves of Warriors and Chamber of Kings, clears the final Chamber of the Guardian
4. **The End** — escapes via C8 tunnel to a second beach; Standard Ending fights cultists loading the god's blood onto their ship, or (PoP variant) arrives too late and the cultists escape, bridging into *Rise of the Jailer*

## Treasure & Rewards
- Shield +1 — Bone Pile, A4 (trapped)
- Vial of necrotic blood* — can be carefully filled from the pools at A3
- 6d10 sp and a potion of healing — possible results from Supply Crate searches, A2
- 2 potions of healing + 8d10 sp — small wooden chest, C2 saw trap hallway
- Gems/jewelry/baubles (10 cp–10 gp range) — Graves of Warriors searches, C4
- 4d10 gp jewelry/gemstones per opened sarcophagus — Chamber of Kings, C5
- 1d4 gems worth 50 gp — Small Alcove searches, C6

## Secrets & Twists
- The evil god's presence in the sarcophagus is a red herring by the time the party investigates closely — it disappears, implying it has been freed/relocated rather than destroyed
- The Guardian Watcher Statue in C5 instantly destroys any undead of CR 5 or lower within 30 feet and its mirror can cast *calm emotions* on a viewer — a useful ally-of-convenience, not a threat
- A dead cultist's note hidden in the door puzzle area doubles as the key to both the B3 door puzzle and the C1 floor trap

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- If run as the Path of Penitence starter adventure, the party is meant to arrive **too late** — cultists escape by boat with the barrels of blood, directly setting up *Rise of the Jailer* (advance party to level 2, long rest, before starting it)
- Standalone/non-PoP games use the Standard Ending instead, where the party can foil the cultists directly
