## Overview
The party travels to Ashurr, in the desert land of Akhamia, to bargain with the demon prince Balazar for the Cypher Tablet — one of three fragments needed to build a world-ending weapon (the Demoncore). Balazar agrees to hand it over only if the party survives three sadistic trials — of Body, Mind, and Soul — testing combat prowess, puzzle-solving, and moral conviction.

## Puzzles / Challenges
- **Statue Puzzle (B1):** Three empty pedestals reference the Maiden ("virtuous... sleeps with her lover"), Mother ("nurturing... poisons her child"), and Crone ("wise old... leads us astray") — a blasphemous riff on the Azerai faith's holy triunity. Solve by placing the Maiden statue (left), Mother statue (middle), and Crone statue (right); statues are crafted via the three Alchemy Altars.
- **Alchemy value system:** Sulphur = 3, Mercury = 11, Salt = 7 (deduced via the balance scale + "Principles of Alchemy Vol. 1" book on the table). Values add linearly (e.g., 2x Mercury = 14).
- **Left Altar — Common Elements → Maiden Statue:** match bowl values to named elements (Alumen = 14, Ferrum = 18, Realgar = 21) using the alchemy book; several valid ingredient combos given in the source.
- **Middle Altar — Solstice Stars → Crone Statue:** find the Snake/Crab/Phoenix constellations via the Giant Telescope (B3) or the astrology book (B4); place values matching each constellation's star count (Snake = 21, Crab = 25, Phoenix = 17).
- **Right Altar — Answer to All → Mother Statue:** requires the number 42, found on the Dead Crusader's bloodied note (B5); split a combined value of 42 across the Sulphur/Mercury/Salt bowls per the altar's element restrictions.
- **Failure state:** 30-minute in-world timer (a real hourglass is suggested); if it expires unsolved, the orb yanks the party back to Balazar's court and the mission fails (no tablet). Bouffon can sneak in to give hints, or flip the hourglass, if the party gets stuck.
- Raw puzzle-note scan (OCR asset) is fairly garbled in places (e.g. "The ne Three"); the clean companion text file confirms the three pedestal riddles verbatim — defer to that for read-aloud text.

## Session Flow
1. Arrival: party travels to Ashurr, meets Balazar's servant, is led through the palace to Balazar's court.
2. Balazar's offer: he'll trade the Cypher Tablet for entertainment — three trials of Body, Mind, and Soul.
3. Trial of Body: three escalating gladiator rounds at the Mysterious Orb; short rests between rounds; Bouffon hands out healing potions after Round 1.
4. Transition: the Keeper of Secrets opens a portal via the golden orb to the Observatorium.
5. Trial of Mind: timed puzzle gauntlet — solve 3 alchemy altars to create the Maiden/Mother/Crone statues, then place them on the correct pedestals.
6. Return to Balazar's court via the orb.
7. Trial of Soul: Balazar orders the party to kill Bouffon; the party's choice resolves the trial and yields the Cypher Tablet.
8. Optional epilogue/hook: explore Ashurr's Grand Bazaar, or set up a future raid on Balazar's ziggurat lair.

## Treasure & Rewards
- The Cypher Tablet — quest reward for completing all three trials (regardless of Bouffon's fate)
- 2x Potions of Greater Healing — given by Bouffon after Gladiator Round 1
- Balazar's "troves of treasure and artifacts is endless" — the GM is explicitly invited to reskin any desired item/relic as the party's goal if not running the tablet/Demoncore plot (homebrew hook)

## Secrets & Twists
- Balazar's court body is only a magical illusion/projection — his real body is hidden deep beneath his ziggurat, immune to attack in the court
- Balazar already knows the tablet's importance and foresees the Empire's inevitable repeat of history (another Demoncore, another apocalypse) — he sees the party as pawns in a "predestined play" and finds this deeply amusing
- The Statue Puzzle's riddles are deliberate blasphemy against the Azerai faith (the Empire's religion), quietly mocking the Maiden/Mother/Crone as unfaithful, abusive, and misleading — a detail sharp PCs may pick up on
- Bouffon is being deliberately gaslit by Balazar, who praises him sarcastically while planning to have him killed for sport — the Trial of Soul reveals this to Bouffon in real time

## Faction Notes (Court of Balazar)
- The Court is built on "Enlightened Suffering" — flesh, pleasure, and pain are treated as facets of the same path to transcendence; the exalted willingly undergo flaying/mutilation as a spiritual "gift"
- The military arm ("the Exalted": Archers, Spears, Gladiators, Cabal Assassins, General Atraxes) actively raids Penitent Crusade outposts in Akhamia and seeks to liberate the Empire-occupied city of Ir Khalem
- The Keeper of Keys and Keeper of Secrets are a linked demon pair — Keys guards the physical keys, Secrets reads souls and chooses which reality-gate key fits a supplicant's fate; neither can be deceived
- Balazar himself: demon prince of gluttony/depravity, "Master of Guile" (illusions/obfuscation), CR25 Archon-tier boss with Legendary Resistance, a Gift of Enlightenment curse, and a Hideous Laughter fear effect — reserved for the most powerful parties
