          THE WORLD OF DOADEN


W
       elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted
                        lands and twisted
       monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes
                   the very essence of existence.

It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men
and heroes of glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults
and dark gods vie with one another to consume the last vestiges
                         of humanity.

It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen
                            empire.
The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers
              to be honored in songs of minstrels.
For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake
invariably leave them disheartened, broken, nothing more than
   soulless husks and shadows of their once-glorious selves.

Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a
                      stubborn tenacity,
           a last flickering flame within the dark.
A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black
                       abyss of eternity,
        a momentary victory before the endless night.

    THE RESTLESS




             The Restless are a common sight in
           the Deadmire.
             Unlucky civilians that were
           slaughtered at the whims of one lord
           or another, their bodies were casually
           discarded on the ground or left to
           hang on trees by the hundred. Now,
           these soulless husks know no rest.
             They are driven by insatiable hunger
           to feed on the flesh of the living, in
           a futile attempt to feed their hollow
           souls.

     LUNDEN, THE ENGINEER
       Lunden is a genius engineer that studied in Allstein for years. The Penitent Crusade has urgent need
     of him at the Black Fortress, their stronghold that stands against the evils of the Ashen Wastes. The
     party must escort Lunden through the Deadmire to the Black Fortress. Everything that happens at the
     Black Fortress is highly classified and not even Lunden knows what they need him for.


                                                          THE BLASTEN-VAGEN
                                                            Lunden drives a strange, horseless carriage
                                                          he calls the “Blasten-Vagen.” It is pulled by a
                                                          metal machine that eats coal and breathes fire.
                                                          The design is his own, and just one of his many
                                                          inventions. The carriage is laden with 10 large
                                                          Precious Crates, each is a wooden box that
                                                          weighs approximately 25 pounds and is packed
                                                          full of his schematics, blueprints, mechanisms,
                                                          and inventions. These boxes contain the sum of
                                                          Lunden’s life work. He would be heartbroken to
                                                          lose them or his precious Blasten-Vagen.
