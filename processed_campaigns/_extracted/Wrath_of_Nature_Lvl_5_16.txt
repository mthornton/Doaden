FOREST ENCOUNTERS TABLE
    d4   Random Encounters

    1    Rabid Monstrosities. The party encounters a pack of corrupted beasts, their eyes glowing
         with a strange green fire. These creatures are now monstrous, grotesque versions of
         themselves, mutated and changed, and driven to hostility by the dark forces of the forest.
         By difficulty, the creatures faced are as follows.
             • Medium: 1 basilisk and 1 chimera.
             • Hard: 2 basilisks and 1 chimera.
             • Torment: 3 basilisks and 2 chimeras.
             • Nightmare: 3 basilisks and 3 chimeras.

    2    Corrupted Fey. The party is ambushed by a group of fey inhabitants that have been infected
         by the corruption seeping from the forest's heart. Their once passive nature has been
         replaced by hostility and a dark mischief, driven by a vehemence for all intruders.
         Depending on the difficulty, the creatures faced are as follows:
             • Medium: 3 centaurs and 2 druids.
             • Hard: 5 centaurs and 2 druids.
             • Torment: 5 centaurs, 2 druids, and 3 green hags.
             • Nightmare: 9 centaurs, 3 druids, and 3 green hags.

    3    Lost Villagers. The party stumbles upon a group of villagers who have succumbed to the
         wild curse. Their bodies have twisted into permanent were-creatures, acting on animalistic
         instinct rather than human reason. They see the party not as potential saviors, but as
         threats to be eliminated. Depending on the difficulty, the enemies faced are as follows:
             • Medium: 3 werewolves.
             • Hard: 2 wereboards and 2 werewolves.
             • Torment: 3 werebears and 2 werewolves.
             • Nightmare: 3 werebears and 4 werewolves.

    4    Wrath of the Forest. As the party progresses deeper into the malignant woods, the very
         forest itself begins to rise against them. Awakened trees, shambling mounds, and an
         assortment of awakened shrubs, all twisted by the corruption, launch an all-out assault on
         the party. Depending on the difficulty, the creatures faced are as follows:
             • Beginner: 5 awakened trees.
             • Easy: 4 awakened trees and 1 shambling mound.
             • Medium: 4 awakened trees and 1 treant.
             • Hard: 2 awakened trees, 2 shambling mounds, and 1 treant.
