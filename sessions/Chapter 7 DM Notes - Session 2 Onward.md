## 2. THE CLOCK — you told them sundown is 8:00

**Stated at the table in Session 1: sundown, and the match, at 8:00.** They were inside the gate at 5:30. **That is two and a half hours of game time** — enough for roughly five or six scenes at your table's pace, with the crunch landing in the last half hour rather than immediately.

| Time | What's happening |
|---|---|
| **5:30** | Party inside. Yard working. Whipping post active. Ring half-built. **Vaskar is told a buyer has arrived within a minute of the gate opening.** |
| **~6:00** | Vaskar receives them in the Hall. Courteous. Lies for ten minutes. **Cobb is in that building.** |
| **6:30 – 7:15** | **The party's window.** The Pens, the stock, Denrick, Corvant, Anselm, the yard. This is where most of Session 2 lives. |
| **~7:15** | Ring finished, hammering stops. Off-duty Ironwake men wake and drift out. **Cobb's interrogation goes quiet — Vaskar has decided the old man is empty.** |
| **~7:45** | ⏳ **Vaskar leaves the office for the arena. COBB DIES HERE** if nobody has reached him. |
| **8:00** | **Sundown. The match.** Every eye on the ring. **Anselm's signal lands here.** |
| **After** | Vaskar's move on Ysabel — if all three conditions hold (§9). |

**What the extra hour buys you:** the party can realistically get the Hall reception, the Pens, and Anselm, *and* still have a run at either Cobb or the Deep Cages. **What it does not buy them is both.** The dilemma is intact; it just arrives at 7:15 instead of on arrival.

**Say the hour out loud.** Every time they burn a scene, tell them what time it is. Two and a half hours feels like all evening until it suddenly isn't, and the moment the hammering stops is the moment they should feel it.

**⏳ The lever that matters:** Vaskar cannot kill Cobb while he is at the rail. **Anything that keeps him in his office keeps the old man breathing** — a buyer to receive, a price to negotiate, an alarm to answer, a noblewoman who has to be handled courteously. **Ysabel's cover story is a life-support machine for Old Cobb and nobody in that party knows it.** If they keep her talking to him, they are saving a man they haven't met.

## 4. VASKAR — he is expecting them, and Cobb is in the room

Per the original prep (Branch A): Vaskar is notified within a minute, recognises Ysabel on sight, and assumes she has come to get a look at Ironwake's champion before the trial. **He is very nearly right** — and he does not know the real reason, which is better.

**He will receive them courteously in the Chainmaster's Hall, and he will lie to them for ten straight minutes.**

**⚠️ And Old Cobb is tied to a chair in that building.**

This is now the best scene in the chapter, and the party has walked into it holding a teacup.

**How to run it:**

- Vaskar is calm, transactional, genuinely curious about what people want. He never raises his voice. He asks Ysabel what she's after and prices it out loud.
- **Cobb is in the next room, or in the corner under a rag, depending on how cruel you feel.** Somebody in that party will hear something. A chair leg. A cough. Breathing that isn't one of theirs.
- Vaskar will not acknowledge it. If asked directly, he says something entirely reasonable about a sick servant, and moves on, and watches to see who keeps looking at the door.
- **He is counting them.** Six armed people for a woman buying mill hands is one more than the arithmetic wants. He notices. He does not say so.

**What the cover gets them, if they play it:**

| They ask for | They get |
|---|---|
| To inspect the stock | **The Pens.** JonLief's scene. Free access, with an escort. |
| Records of a previous purchase | *"The clerk handles the books."* → the Hall, and the working ledger in the office. **This is the trail to Rowan's buyer.** |
| An elf woman and a boy, by description | Vaskar remembers. He will say the woman died of fever and the boy was sold on. **He will not volunteer to whom** unless he sees profit in it. |
| To see the gnome | **Refused.** Not for sale, needed tonight. Pressed, he'll offer three other slaves instead and change the subject smoothly. |
| To stay for the match | **Delighted.** He wants Ysabel where he can see her. |

## 6. GIMBLE, UNDER A COVER STORY

The party knows a gnome is going to die at sundown. They do not know he is a Penitent, and under a buyers' cover **they have no obvious reason to go looking in the Deep Cages** — that's where Ironwake's champion is kept, and it's the hardest room in the compound (one Fen Guard on the outer door, two of Ironwake's men inside on Mott's cell).

**Three ways in, pick whichever they reach for:**

- **Buy him.** Ysabel asks to purchase the gnome. Vaskar refuses — he is not for sale, he is needed tonight. **A refusal is more interesting than a yes.** It tells the party this one gnome matters more than money, which is exactly the wrong thing for a slaver to admit.
- **Watch the match.** He is walked out to the ring, masked, in rags, at sundown, in front of everyone. **This is where the brand gets seen** — one bare shoulder, the sun and the tear. Have a PC catch it as he's led past. That is the cleanest version and it costs the party nothing to be standing there.
- **Go and look.** Someone slips the yard during the pre-match noise. Harder, louder, better if they earn it.

**The reveal order does not change: what he is, then who he is.** Mark first, name later.

**⚠️ And the moment they see that brand, the cover is finished** — no Penitent is going to stand in a crowd and watch another Penitent be killed for sport. Expect the infiltration to end there, and be ready for it to end there. **That is a good ending for it.**

**Target: Gimble out of the cage before the end of Session 2**, so Dave has a character in hand for the finale.

**If they free him, there is no match** — no other opponent exists — which means no crowd, no stage, **and Anselm loses his signal.** See §9 and §10.

## 7. ANSELM AND WOLFRAM — the new complication

Wolfram half-recognised a man in the work party. That man is **Anselm Vogt**: the old Westfield sheriff who rode under Wolfram's father Konrad, who knows the Eisenherz crest on sight, and who gave a statement about Wolfram to a clerk in the Sunlit Citadel that was never written down.

**The prep assumed they'd meet in the Pens, quietly, with Wolfram free to be himself. That's not the situation any more.**

**Anselm is going to recognise him back, in the open yard, in front of guards, while Wolfram is pretending to be a merchant's hired sword.**

**Play the danger:**

- Anselm is not a fool and does not blurt things. He is, however, sixty and exhausted and has been waiting four months for exactly one piece of luck.
- **Run it as a near-miss first.** He looks up. He stops working. A guard shouts at him and he goes back to hauling. **Now Wolfram knows he's been seen, and doesn't know by whom.**
- The real meeting should be the party's to arrange — a moment behind a building, a word passed through the work party, Wolfram getting himself sent to fetch something.
- **When it lands, use the boxed text in `Anselm Vogt.md` verbatim.** *"Eisenherz. I gave a statement about you to a clerk in the Citadel. He never wrote it down."*
- **Then: no Insight roll.** Tell the table plainly this man is telling the truth.

**What he gives, freely** — and this is where the party learns their information is a month stale:

- **Twenty men.** Fourteen of Vaskar's, six of Ironwake's, and where each one stands.
- That it was **four or five** until the giant arrived. *(Dov was right. Dov is just out of date, exactly like Erwana was about the tunnel.)*
- Four of Ironwake's asleep in the barracks, two on the big one's cell — and that those six are why he hasn't risen yet.
- Where the armory keys hang.
- That the big one is gentle until the dwarf makes him otherwise.
- **That the masked one dies tonight.**
- That Denrick talks to the overseers and should be trusted with nothing.

**Then he asks Wolfram the question that matters:** *"Who's in charge of you?"*

## 9. YSABEL — updated

**Her two problems are still separate. Nothing in Session 1 changed that.**

**Problem one — being detained here.** Vaskar takes her after the match and holds her for Ironwake; she forfeits by absence and becomes his property. Requires all three: **the match happened**, **the revolt was put down or never fired**, and **the party never engaged Vaskar.** Break any one and she walks.

> **Freeing the gnome breaks the first one by itself** — and the party is now very likely to free the gnome. Plan on her not being detained.

**Problem two — the trial in two days.** Untouched by any of the above. She has no champion; Ironwake has the Juggernaut. **Only removing Mott changes that** — killed, crippled, or walked out of the fen with Durgan. Kindness to Mott where Durgan can see it is the key; Durgan folds instantly for Mott's sake.

**New this session — the ledger debt.**

She gets the book **when everyone is clear of Bramblefen.** Which means:

- **The party carries it through the entire raid.** It can burn, be dropped, be taken off a body, or be found on them if anyone is searched. If Vaskar ever gets his hands on it, the thing he has been torturing Cobb for a week to find walks into his office by itself.
- **She is invested in the raid working**, and will spend her own people to make it work.
- **The debt comes due on the way out**, at the worst possible moment, and the party can refuse to pay it. Decide in advance what she does about that with five servants and no leverage. *(Recommended: nothing at all, coldly, and it costs the party an ally in Solteres for Chapter 8.)*
- **If she's detained or killed here, the deal dies with her** and the party keeps the book by default. **Do not point that out to them.**

## 10. THE MATCH, THE SIGNAL, AND HOW THE NIGHT BREAKS

**Anselm was waiting for the one moment every eye in the compound is on the ring.** If the party frees Gimble, that moment never arrives, and Anselm has no signal.

**So what does he do?**

- **If the party has talked to him:** he takes his cue from them. Tell them so. **They choose the moment**, which is the best possible version of this — the revolt becomes a tool in their hands rather than a thing that happens to them.
- **If they haven't:** he waits, and watches, and fires the instant something loud happens — a fight in the yard, an alarm, the Deep Cages going wrong. **Which means the party will trigger it accidentally and not be ready.**
- **If nothing loud happens at all:** he holds. The night ends with eighteen men still in cages and a party walking out with a gnome. That's a real ending and it should sting.

**Vaskar can substitute an opponent.** He has eighteen slaves and a crowd expecting a show. If you want the match — and therefore Anselm's signal, and therefore Vaskar's move on Ysabel — to survive Gimble's rescue, Vaskar simply picks another body. **Yarel Stoke**, the half-orc pit fighter, is the obvious choice and would half-kill Mott before going down. *Optional. Only use it if losing the match costs you a structure you want.*

## 12. A SHAPE FOR SESSION 2

Not a script. An order that works.

1. **The yard, five minutes.** Let them look. Let them not intervene at the whipping post. Establish the cover as a thing that costs something to keep.
2. **Vaskar receives them.** Ten minutes of courteous lying, with something breathing in the next room.
3. **The Pens.** JonLief asks after a woman and a boy, five times, and gets nothing. Denrick starts talking. **Corvant Hale explains what Vaskar is.**
4. **Anselm sees Wolfram.** Near-miss first. Then the real conversation, and the true guard count, and *"who's in charge of you?"*
5. **7:15 — the hammering stops.** The ring is finished, the barracks empty out, and guards start drifting toward the ring. **Say the time.** This is the moment the evening stops being open.
6. **Cobb, or the gnome, or both — and they probably cannot have both.** This is the chapter.
7. **Curtain:** Vaskar stands up and reaches for the door. Or the mask comes off and there's a brand under it. Either is a good place to stop.

**Note on pacing:** Session 1 took 2.5 hours to cover an evening, a morning and a walk. At that rate this is a **four-session chapter, not three.** Plan for it rather than rushing the back half.

## 13. OPEN DECISIONS

1. **The clock is fixed at 8:00** — you said it at the table, so it's canon. The open question is only whether Vaskar's courtesy to Ysabel is what delays him past 7:45 and saves Cobb. **Recommended: yes, and never tell them.**

6. **Does Ald take Erwana in?** Not needed this chapter. Needed before the finale.
