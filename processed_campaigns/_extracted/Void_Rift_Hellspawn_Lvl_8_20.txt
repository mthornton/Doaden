Encounter Waves
Before you begin combat give the party a
moment to get their bearing and explore the
area. They should have a basic understanding
of the geography and points of interest in the
combat area, as well as a brief moment to
discuss tactics before the fight starts.
    When the party is ready, spawn the
enemies listed in the Wave 1 Enemies section.
During this encounter, several waves of
enemies will attack the party. After each wave
is defeated, give the party a minute to
reposition or activate the blood shrine before
starting the next wave.

Spawning Enemies. When you spawn
enemies, place them around the map, crawling
up onto the combat area from the edge of the
platform, flying down from the hellish skies, or
entering in any other way you think is the most
cinematic!

Boss Loot
If the party defeats the boss, they may
collectively roll 4d10s. Based on the
cumulative roll score, they find the item
corresponding to the highest score they reach.
Add a bonus to the cumulative roll score
depending on the adventure difficulty level.
