# The Office of the Lost Flock

> The arm of the Church of Azerai that goes after Penitents who stop reporting. Five of them matter to this campaign; **Baltus Thole** is the first. This file holds what they all share — the rest get their own pages as we build them.

---

## The name

Vozruk said it to this party's faces on their first morning: *"Welcome, new lambs for the flock."*

He was quoting. It is what the Church calls them, without irony, in its own paperwork. And so the men sent after the ones who wander off are, in the same paperwork, **Shepherds**.

Nobody in the Office finds this funny. That is the point of them.

**On the road they are known by the colour.** A long cloak in deep violet — a hue the Church reserves, expensive to dye and impossible to mistake. Nothing else about them is standard: no badge, no chain, no livery. Just the colour, and it is enough. **People step off the road for a purple cloak the way they step off it for a plague cart.**

---

## The lambs

The Office strikes a small silver lamb for each Penitent a Shepherd was given and **failed to bring home.**

Not the saved ones. **The lost ones.** A man who does his duty has done his duty, and the Office does not commemorate duty. It commemorates what a Shepherd could not prevent.

The lamb is stamped on the flank with a mark for **what took them** — beastmen, water, fever, slavers, heretics, *ran*, or *unknown* — and it is handed to the Shepherd when the file closes. What he does with it after that is his own business.

### ⚠️ Almost nobody wears them

**The lambs are issued. They are not a uniform.**

Most Shepherds keep them the way a man keeps things he does not want to look at. A box at the bottom of a chest. A pouch in a saddlebag. Left on an altar somewhere with a coin. Thrown off a bridge, quietly, on the way out of the diocese. Nobody in the Office asks and nobody in the Office checks.

**Baltus Thole sews his onto his cloak.** All thirty-one, in rows, collar to hem, since his fourth year — and he walks into every village in the Southern Diocese wearing a census of every person he could not save.

> **The other Shepherds think this is too much.**
>
> Not cruelty — they are not unkind about it. They think he has taken a private weight and made it a public one, and that a man who needs to be *seen* carrying it has started carrying it for a reason other than the dead. They call it zeal, and in the Office that is not entirely a compliment.
>
> **And there is a second reason nobody says out loud.** There is exactly one other man in the Office who wears his lambs. *(See `Ivo and Wendel Kessler.md`.)* Every Shepherd who has met both of them has noticed. None of them has ever mentioned it to Baltus.

**Baltus's are never removed.** Not if the case is later mended, not if the body is found and buried properly, not ever. The failure happened. *(That rule is his, not the Office's. The Office has no rule, because it never occurred to the Office that anyone would wear them.)*

> **At the table:** a purple cloak means Shepherd. A purple cloak **that chimes** means Baltus Thole, and the party should learn to tell the difference. **Describe the sound before you describe the man** — a heavy, irregular chiming, like a purse of coins carried by someone with a limp.

## ⚙️ The Mark of Atonement is a leash

**This is not new. It has been true since the forge in Chapter 1 and there have been hints at the table.** It is written down here because the Shepherds are the first people who will say it out loud.

The brand is not only a brand. It is **consecrated at the forge**, and it binds the bearer to the Church in a way that can be read.

- A Shepherd must be **assigned the file.** The bond is to the Church, not to him; the rite of assignment is what lets him feel it.
- At **dawn prayer**, once a day, he receives **a direction** for each quarry on his file. A pull. Not a distance, not a place — a bearing, the way a compass gives you north.
- It works **within a diocese.** Cross into another and the pull goes quiet, which is why Penitents who genuinely escape go *far*.

### ⚰️ The dead still pull

**A Shepherd does not know whether his quarry is alive until he is standing over them.**

Death does not end the bond. The brand goes on pulling out of a corpse, exactly as strong, with nothing in it that says *this one is finished* — **until the body rots past holding it.**

Roughly: a body left in the open goes quiet in **a month or so.** Cold ground, salt, deep bog, or a dry crypt will hold a pull for **years.** Something deliberately preserved may never go quiet at all.

> **This is the cruelty of the whole system and it should be played for exactly that.** Every walk toward a pull is a walk toward someone who is *probably* dead, and the Shepherd cannot know, and so he goes anyway, every time, at speed.
>
> **This — not the fourteen unknowns — is what has hollowed Baltus Thole out.** Two hundred mornings spent hurrying toward someone who had already been dead for three weeks.

### 🔥 The brand fades with the debt

**The strength of the pull is the size of what you still owe.**

A brand is at its brightest the day it is struck and dims across the length of the sentence, in proportion. A Shepherd who has held a file a week can tell you roughly how much time is left on it. They all learn to. None of them enjoy it.

- **This party owes five hundred years and has served two and a half months.** Their pull is about as loud as a pull ever gets. **This is why Baltus was glad** — four bearings, all of them screaming, on a file that had sat closed for weeks.
- A Penitent near the end of a short term is **nearly untraceable.** The Church does not consider this a flaw. A man who has almost paid is a man who is almost free, and the leash loosening as the debt shrinks is, to the Church, the mercy in the design.
- **When the debt is discharged the brand goes inert.** The scar stays. **That is the door**, and it is real, and Baltus has seen it open.

### What actually breaks it

1. **The Shepherd's ceremony.** The brand is cut away under a rite the Office teaches only to Shepherds. **This is the sole clean severance and one of the Office's real secrets.** *(It is also exactly why `Hedda Merrow.md` works — she is a Shepherd, so she has it.)*
2. **Time.** The sentence runs out.
3. **Rot.** The body goes. Not a severance so much as an ending.

**What does NOT break it:**

- ❌ **Cutting the brand out.** Burning over it, flaying it, taking the whole shoulder — **none of it works.** The bond is in the person, not in the skin. A man who mutilates himself to escape it has ruined himself for nothing and will still be found. *(Penitents try this. It is one of the sadder things a Shepherd walks in on.)*
- ❌ **Losing the limb.** Same answer.
- ❌ **Dying.** See above.

**And one thing that hides it without breaking it:**

- 🌫️ **Ground shielded from the divine.** Consecrated against, or desecrated past reach — the pull goes silent while you are on it and returns the moment you leave. *(The catacombs on Dol Island where a goddess is chained. The black fog over St. Pyotr's. Assume both went dark, and assume both came back.)*

### What this explains

- **Why the Church can afford to brand people and let them wander a continent.** It doesn't need walls. It has already put the wall in your shoulder.
- **Why Vaskar is terrified of holding a branded Penitent.** Not a moral problem or even a legal one. **A collared Penitent is a needle the Church can find**, and when it comes looking it will find Bramblefen. He is not afraid of the Church's wrath in the abstract. He is afraid of a specific man in a purple cloak walking up the fen road.
- **Why nobody has come for this party in two and a half months.** Nobody had the file. See below.

### Why the Church doesn't simply know

There are thousands of Penitents. **Nobody is watching them.** The bond is inert until a Shepherd is assigned, and a Shepherd is assigned only when someone files a unit as overdue — which takes months, and often never happens at all, because the Redeemer who sent them is not eager to write down that he lost four.

And when a Shepherd *is* assigned, the pull tells him almost nothing. A living man and a three-week-old corpse feel identical. **Silence could mean rotted, out of diocese, on shielded ground, sentence discharged, or severed — five very different answers and one identical nothing.**

So: **the Church genuinely does not know that most of the missing are dead.** A clerk writes *presumed lost in service.* A lamb is struck, stamped *unknown*, and handed to a Shepherd who puts it in a box.

## 🪜 The three phases

A Shepherd is made in three stages, and **the middle one is a branding.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | **Unbranded. No pull. Never assigned a file.** Works alongside a master, goes where the master cannot, does the talking, carries the load, and learns the trade by watching it done. **Typically five to eight years**, and it ends when the master says it ends. | **He can walk away.** That is the whole point of making it long. |
| **Acolyte** | Branded at the closing ceremony, and **the brand is what gives him the pull.** From that day he **works alone**, takes his own files, and starts his own count. | **Ten years, minimum.** Most of a Shepherd's career is here. |
| **Master** | Ten years an Acolyte. May take an apprentice. | Baltus (22 yrs), Hedda (16), Sault, both Kesslers. |

### ⚙️ The Shepherd's brand

**The Church brands its shepherds with the same iron it brands its lambs.** Same forge, same rite, **same shoulder.**

The mark is not identical — a **Penitent** carries a sun with a tear falling from it; a **Shepherd** carries the sun with a small lamb beneath it — but at a glance, across a room, on a man stripped to the waist, **they look like the same thing.** That is not an accident and nobody in the Office pretends otherwise.

> **This is the piece of the Office that says the most about it.** They do not simply administer the leash. **They put it on themselves, deliberately, in a ceremony, as a promotion.**
>
> And when a Shepherd tells a Penitent *you are not a prisoner, you are being saved* — **he has the same burn on the same shoulder, and he chose his.**

**What the brand does for him:** it is what lets the rite of assignment reach him. **No brand, no pull, no files.** An apprentice is not a junior Shepherd with a small caseload. He is **not yet a Shepherd at all**, and the Office is exact about this.

**What the brand does to him** — *(optional, and a very good secret if you want it)*: a consecrated mark on a shoulder is a consecrated mark on a shoulder. **The Office can find its own.** Nobody discusses it, every Shepherd knows it, and it is precisely why a Shepherd who goes wrong cannot simply walk into another diocese and disappear. *(See `Hedda Merrow.md`.)*

### What an apprentice is worth

Because he is unbranded, an apprentice can do things no Shepherd can, and the Office uses him for exactly that:

- **He is not recognisable.** No colour, no cloak, no lambs. He can drink in the room, ask the questions, and walk out.
- **He cannot be felt, followed, or recalled.** By anyone.
- **He carries no lambs, because none of the losses are his.** His count starts at the brand. **An apprentice's coat is clean and it means nothing yet.**

### ⚠️ And the severance rite is not taught to apprentices

**The ceremony that cuts a Mark of Atonement free** — the one clean way out of the leash, the thing `Hedda Merrow.md` is built on — is taught at the branding, or after it. **An apprentice does not have it.**

> **If a party ever has an apprentice in it, that is the shape of his whole arc.** He is travelling with people who were branded against their will, he is the only unbranded person among them, **he will one day be asked to volunteer for the same iron** — and the thing that could free his friends is on the far side of that ceremony.

## What they actually do

Most of the work is not hunting. It is walking toward a bearing and finding out what is at the end of it.

| What's really going on | How often | What the Shepherd finds |
|---|---|---|
| **Dead** — killed on a mission, nobody reported it | Most files | A pull like any other, for weeks. He hurries. **He arrives at a body**, and a lamb is struck *unknown*. |
| **Long dead** — the body has gone | Common | Nothing at all, from the first prayer. Indistinguishable from four other answers. The file closes on a guess. |
| **In trouble** — captured, enslaved, wounded, stranded | Uncommon | The only work that ends with **no lamb struck at all.** This is what the Office was founded for. |
| **Ran** | Rarest | Also a pull. Ends differently. |

**A Shepherd does not believe he is a jailer.** He believes he is the only person in the world still looking for you. Given how the campaign's other institutions talk about the people they hold, **run him in exactly the same register as Ysabel and the Church** — bewilderment first, then the category argument, then cold. *You are not a prisoner. You are being saved. I have come to take you home.*

## The five

| | Who | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Baltus Thole** — the hammer, thirty-one lambs, fourteen of them marked *unknown* | **Built** — `Baltus Thole.md` |
| 2 | **Hedda Merrow** — the daggers, forty-seven lambs, and eleven of them still breathing | **Built** — `Hedda Merrow.md` |
| 3 | **Provost Emeric Sault** — the bare cloak, the satchel of unassigned files, the barbed mace that puts the brand *back* | **Built** — `Emeric Sault.md` |
| 4 | **Ivo Kessler** — the broadsword, fifty-eight lambs and nineteen holes where lambs used to be | **Built** — `Ivo and Wendel Kessler.md` |
| 5 | **Wendel Kessler** — twelve lambs, two hand crossbows, one loaded to kill and one loaded to save | **Built** — `Ivo and Wendel Kessler.md` |

**Note on Sault:** he is not a fourth hunter so much as the reason hunts happen. Baltus and Hedda both answer to him; the bond is inert until *he* assigns a file, and he carries the unassigned ones in his satchel. He has done the arithmetic on Hedda's numbers and buried it, because her reputation is worth more to the Office than the truth.

**Note on the Kesslers:** they are not assigned files and they are not written down. Provost Sault releases them verbally — a place and a direction — when there is strong evidence a Penitent unit has gone in too deep with an evil of enormous scale. **Their arrival is not a hunt, it is a verdict**: the Office has already decided the Penitents are unrecoverable and the thing holding them matters more. Nobody is coming to collect the brothers either.

**The five are built.** Baltus takes the charter seriously and it has hollowed him out. Hedda has quietly started letting some of them go. Sault decides who is hunted at all. The Kesslers are what the Office does when it has stopped pretending the charter applies. **Each is a different argument about the same brand, and they can all be right by the Office's own rules.**

## Open decisions

1. **Who reported this party overdue?** *Recommended: not Vozruk.* A clerk, a ship's manifest, or the governor of the Southern Diocese noticing that a chartered boat never came back. **Vozruk finding out his own unit was filed as strays by somebody else is a scene worth having in Chapter 8** — and it tells the party something about him either way.
2. **How long is a file open before it closes?** Affects how much rope a running Penitent has.
3. **Do Penitents know the brand can be tracked?** *Recommended: no, and it should land as a genuine shock.* Nobody tells the lambs.
