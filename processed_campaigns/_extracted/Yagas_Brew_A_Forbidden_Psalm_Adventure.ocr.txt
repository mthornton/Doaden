[ADVENTURE SUMMARY:

GAS BREW

The village of Wrozek is trapped in an eerie silence. It was once a small community of
peat-harvesters in an isolated woodland swamp, yet all the residents have mysteriously
disappeared.

Only two young children remain, who beseech travelers to save their vanished community,
claiming the evil witch, Baba Yaga, has kidnapped their families. The party must track
down this vile witch to save village before it is too late. Yet they have been deceived, the
plight of the town and the children are all part of Yaga’s elaborate trap to lure in and
kidnap unwary adventurers.

depravity where once- righteous men and heroes of glorious purpose now struggle to

humanity.

SETUP AND TREASURE

The board should be set up like a derelict village. Deployment areas are on opposite sides of the
table and 6 inches deep running the length of a 2ft-by-2ft board. Players roll off to determine who
decides which deployment area they get.

Players should take it in turns to place 8 children per player. One of these is a real child. A model
within 1 inch of a child can make a Presence test as an action. On a pass roll a D4 and ona result
of a 4+ the child is real. Once found a real child is removed from play as they run to safety.
Children should be placed at least 3 inches from another child and 3 inches outside of a players
deployment zone. The children not tested move 1D6 in a random direction at the end of each
round.

However, on any other dice result place a Domovoy.

DEPLOYMENT

All models should be deployed as above.

THREATS

As above

SOLO PLAY
As Above

The Scenario ends when all children have been tested and only one Domovoy remains alive.

COUNTING DEAD MEN'S FINGERS

You enter the accursed woods to hunt down the insidious Baba Yaga. The trees are a tapestry of
gnarled branches and shadows, and the passage between them twists and turns like the threads of
a hag's loom. The air is heavy with the scent of decay, punctuated by the occasional, sickly-sweet
whiff of some unseen bloom. As you travel deeper, a thick fog descends, making it difficult to see.
The oppressive undergrowth rises from the damp, moss-ridden soil. Thick roots and vines grasp at
your ankles like gnarled hands.

There is a silence here, a horrible quiet that feels almost tangible, broken only by the far-off cry of a
raven, the mournful hoot of an owl, or the unsettling rustle of something unseen moving through
the underbrush. The absence of normal life fills you with a sense of dread, as though you have
ventured into a world that is not your own. And yet, you push forward. You are in the hunt now,
and there is no turning back.

After what seems like eternity marching through the murky woods, you come to an eerie grove. The
ruin of an ancient altar remains at the centre, sinking slowly into the surrounding water. A cauldron
full of green ichor bubbles, placed at the centre of the circular ruin. The cauldron suddenly begins
to bubble violently as the gust of wind blows through the area. Shadows twist, coalesce, and there,
from the ether, Baba Yaga materializes...

MONSTERS

All monsters in Forbidden Psalm: The Tainted Lands of Doaden treat other monsters as friendly.

Domovoy
HP: 8 Morale: 6 Shortsword 1D6 Armor:o

Special: If only one Domovoy remains on the map they flee the battlefield.

BABA YAGA'S CAULDRON
HP: 20 Morale: - Grab * Armor:2

Special: If a model is within 1 inch of the Cauldron, instead of attacking {including attacking back}
the Cauldron tries to add to its stew. It grabs a model, and the model must make an agility test. On
a failure they are put into the stew and take 1D6 damage that ignores Armor.

Models in the stew as an action can try and escape by rolling a strength or agility test. However, if
they remain in the stew the next time the Cauldron activities it deals another 1D6 damage that
ignores Armor to all models inside the stew.

BABA YAGA ~ JHE DEATH HAG

HP: 32 Morale: - Glaive 1D10 Armor:1

Special: Takes only half damage from all attacks. Each round Baba Yaga points at a model {this is
in addition to attacking} and that model must make a Death save vs DR6. On a failure they are
killed.
