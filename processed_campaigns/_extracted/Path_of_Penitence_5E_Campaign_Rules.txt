Grimdark Setting
Doaden is a dark world. Humanity is locked in
an inescapable battle with evil, and the Empire
is a shattered remnant of what it once was, a
dwindling power set upon on all sides by its
enemies. The land is rife with monsters and
even within the walls of civilization, devious
cults operate in shadows, working tirelessly to
tear down what little safety that remains.
    Safe Space. Stories told in Doaden may
often involve morally challenging, sensitive, or
traumatic themes. As you play through your
adventures here, it is a good idea to make sure
everyone in your gaming group feels safe and
is comfortable communicating openly about
any issues they might have with the adventure
content. Consider holding a meeting before
the first session to discuss any sensitive or
traumatic topics to avoid. It will also be good
to check in periodically with each other to
make sure everyone is happy and enjoying the
campaign. Remember to always respect each
other and that a character’s actions are not
reflective of the player behind them.

The Greater Good. The Crusade cares little
for the lives or wellbeing of any innocents that
may be caught on its way towards victory. The
eradication of evil takes precedence above all
else and there is little room for indecisiveness
or compassion. A will of steel and a decisive
sword are needed to keep order, and the death
of civilians, or the destruction or confiscation
of their property, is viewed as a necessary evil
in the fight for survival.
    Whole towns have been burned to the
ground, the scorched bodies of the innocent
and guilty alike piled into heaping mounts of
flesh – all to eradicate hidden cells of cultists
or other threats. When it comes to these
decisions, the Primus, the generals of the
Crusade, have great freedom to execute
whatever strategy they deem most effective to
protect the Empire. Though some may take a
nobler approach in attempts to protect the
