**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
The party is hired by the Rangers faction (Vozruk, "the Storm Crow") to find a missing 6-ranger unit last seen near the northern town of Ubsola. They arrive during Bal'Morra, a barbaric fire-sacrifice festival, then defend the town from a Beastmen raid, and finally track the raiders to the Slaughterhorn Tribe's lair — a beastman clan being torn apart from within by the Bloodfever plague and a rising demon-pact cult.

## Key Locations
- Ubsola — small northern religious-center town, permanent population a few hundred, swells during Bal'Morra; surrounded by forest and craggy mountains.
- Yngvald's Longhouse — feast hall, seats 80; site of the Bal'Morra sacrifice buildup.
- Slaughterhorn Ambush site — town edge/ridge area (Map A) where the raid occurs.
- Slaughterhorn Lair — two-day journey NW of Ubsola; a cave system split into Bloodbrand-controlled areas (B1–B7) and the besieged Resistance holdout (B8–B12).

## Session Flow
1. **Ch.1 – The Festival of Light:** Arrive at Ubsola during Bal'Morra prep; talk to Torvik/Yngvald; optionally scout tracks; attend the feast; party member forced into the throat-slitting choice at the bonfire sacrifice.
2. **Ch.2 – Slaughterhorn Ambush:** Immediately after the ceremony, Beastmen raiders attack; defend villagers and huts; fight ends when the Rockgut Raider dies, revealing the dead ranger's letter/map; Yngvald rewards the party and suggests a long rest.
3. **Ch.3 – Into the Slaughterhorn Lair:** Two-day journey to the cave lair; navigate Bloodbrand-controlled rooms (B1–B7) and/or the Resistance holdout (B8–B12); free Malcor and/or the Chieftain; confront the Bloodbrand Matriarch.
4. **Epilogue:** Return to Ubsola/New Heldon/Guerrin for reward; ending narrated depends on whether the party massacred the tribe, saved the Resistance, or sided with the Bloodbrand Cult.

## Secrets & Twists
- The Bloodfever is theorized to be a cross-species mutation of a human disease, contracted from the Aurox's desperate consumption of human flesh — the humans and Beastmen are unknowingly inflicting the same plague on each other.
- The missing rangers misjudged the whole tribe as irredeemably demonic based on witnessing only the Bloodbrand faction, leading Malcor to conclude all Beastmen must be purged — a prejudice the party can choose to validate or correct.
- The Bloodbrand Matriarch may offer to leave humans in the region alone if the party helps her finish off the Resistance — an ambiguous, possibly untrustworthy bargain left for the GM/party to judge.

## Faction Notes (The Aurox / Minotaurs)
- The Aurox are the dominant Beastmen species — long-lived (centuries), bovine-humanoid hybrids; other minor species include the goat-like Capra and solitary lionfolk Leonids.
- They worship Mologh, a primordial earth spirit of both blessing and calamity; the Mouths of Mologh are clan shamans conducting sacrifice-based rites, and the belief holds that "Mologh's Fire" will one day end and renew the world.
- Populations are collapsing from a wasting disease causing stillbirths/infertility plus the new Bloodfever plague (aggression → frenzy → coma → death); starvation is pushing tribes south into human lands and even to eating human flesh.
- The Bloodbrand Cult is a schismatic faction that pacts with dark gods/demons, self-mutilating with brand-carving, in exchange for control over the Bloodfever and necromantic power to raise fallen Aurox as Yoken — they see this as the only path to avoid extinction, against the traditionalists' waning faith in Mologh.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Ending is entirely party-choice-driven: massacre the tribe (Malcor happy, rangers pleased, but conflict continues elsewhere), save the Resistance (best "good" outcome, tribe relocates away from humans), or side with the Bloodbrand Matriarch (morally ambiguous, Malcor and the Rangers turn hostile to the party).
- If the party can't communicate in Gaurric, they cannot negotiate with the Chieftain, Mouth of Mologh, or Matriarch at all — consider whether any PC has a background reason to know the language, or lean on Comprehend Languages.
- Bloodfever is explicitly a recurring faction-level threat across Doaden's Beastmen clans, not unique to the Slaughterhorns — usable as a hook for future Aurox-related sessions.
