# The Man Eaters Saga (Man Hunt + Red Dawn)

**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5 (Part 1) → 9 (Part 2)
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Part 1 ("Man Hunt") follows a level-5 party tracking the fugitive Ivon Horack west of Verden Abbey, only to stumble into the hunting grounds of the Man Eaters — cannibal tribes devoted to a dark entity called the Carniphage/Carrion King — and end up captive at one of their rituals. Part 2 ("Red Dawn – Siege of Guerrin"), set at level 9, picks up the wider threat: the Man Eater tribes have swollen into a "Wild Hunt" of thousands and lay siege to the fortified town of Guerrin, with the party helping defend it in stages until dawn and a final confrontation with the Man Eaters' monstrous god-avatar. Playing Part 1 first is recommended but not required.

## Key Locations
- **Verden Abbey** — starting point of Part 1; home of Abbot Remus and the stolen golden cross.
- **The Farmstead** — a slaughtered, blighted 7-hovel community at the edge of the Rivenweald; site of Clues 1–6 and the horse-head/bone-totem discoveries.
- **The Rivenweald** — the "vastly unexplored," maze-like ancient woods where Horack fled and the Man Eaters hunt; site of the 12-encounter investigation and the party's capture.
- **The Ritual Glade** — fire-lit clearing deep in the Rivenweald where the Haruspex conducts her sacrifice ritual and the party is held captive (Part 1, Ch.3); contains the sacrificial altar (Point A) and the players' confiscated gear pile (Point B).
- **Guerrin** — fortified hillside town on the Castur River controlling the Hemlock Ford; built in three tiers (lower level of stables/smithies, mid-level town square, upper plateau with the Great Hall and the Flame of Eir). Ruled by Stragovid.
- **The Flame of Eir** — a great signal fire that has burned in Guerrin for centuries; extinguished by the Man Eaters in Ch.3 and must be relit by the party.
- **The Three Watchtowers** (southwest, south, southeast) — first line of Guerrin's defense; each has a signal fire that goes dark when it falls.
- **The Bloody Fields / Burned Fields** — open ground outside Guerrin's gates where the second stage of the defense is fought, ending when the defenders light a ring of tar for a respite.
- **The Palisades** — Guerrin's wooden wall defense, third stage of the siege; includes gates, ramparts, and a ballista.
- **The Great Hall** — Guerrin's final chokepoint/shelter for children, the infirm, and survivors; site of Stragovid's rallying speech.

## Session Flow (per part)

**Part 1 – Man Hunt**
- *Ch.1 – Chimes in the Wind:* Party is hired/motivated to hunt Ivon Horack for the Abbot of Verden Abbey; random road encounters (wolves, bandits, Brother Alcott) while tracking him west; discover the slaughtered, blighted Farmstead and its bone totem, ending with a trail into the woods.
- *Ch.2 – Lights in the Dark:* 12-encounter crawl through the maze-like Rivenweald as day turns to night; players get lost, find minor/major clues of the Man Eaters' hunt and Horack's passage, fight Preycatchers/wolves/a Giant Glutton/The Wicked, and are ultimately ambushed and knocked unconscious.
- *Ch.3 – The Ritual:* Party wakes bound at a Man Eater sacrifice ritual; witnesses the Haruspex butcher captives and nearly reach Horack before a distraction pulls the fiends away; Horack helps the party break free, retrieve gear, and fight off escalating waves (Rounds 1–9) to escape.
- *Epilogue:* Party escapes the woods and decides Horack's fate; hook for the hidden golden cross.

**Part 2 – Red Dawn: The Siege of Guerrin**
- *Ch.1 – Refuge:* Party escorts a refugee caravan (led by young Wylam) toward Guerrin, completing Night Watch tasks that build a Survival Score, then is ambushed by a Man Eater vanguard (Man Hunter + Fleshguards/Flesh Stalkers) and a lone Berzerker attack. Arriving at Guerrin, they meet Stragovid, Baylor, and Rykkard at the War Council, learn the Wild Hunt (2,000–2,500 strong) will strike within 24 hours, help plan the tiered defense, and rest.
- *Ch.2 – The Long Night:* Multi-stage running battle — defend a chosen Watchtower, fall back to the Bloody Fields, retreat behind the Palisades (with Battle Objectives to rescue rangers, kill the Man Hunter/Warlord, and destroy Giant Fleshgluttons via ballista), then a scripted Frenzied Retreat through the city (choose to save Wylam or Rykkard) to the barricaded Great Hall. Guerrin's Morale Score is tracked throughout and shapes later events.
- *Ch.3 – A Red Dawn:* Short rest in the Great Hall; the party watches the Man Eaters ritualistically transform the Hungry Lord into the Avatar of the Carniphage. Stragovid gives a rallying "red dawn" speech and leads a diversion; the party relights the Flame of Eir and kills the Avatar of the Carniphage in the town square, ending the siege as the Penitent Crusade arrives at dawn.
- *Epilogue:* Survivor outcomes (named NPCs and rank-and-file) are determined by 1d20 rolls scaled against Guerrin's final Level of Morale.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Golden display cross — stolen by Horack from Verden Abbey, hidden in the Rivenweald with a trail of small clue-items — worth up to 200gp to the right buyer (Part 1).
- Bounty on Horack — 25gp for capture alive, 20gp for proof of death, paid by Abbot Remus (Part 1).
- Man Hunter loot — Longbow +1 and Arrows +1 (quantity varies by encounter: 20, 10, or 5) if she is slain during any of her Part 2 appearances.
- Warlord/War Leader loot — Maul +1 if slain during any of his Part 2 appearances.

## Secrets & Twists
- Ivon Horack is not trustworthy — he only cooperates with the party because he believes it improves his odds of escape, and will flee or turn on them if he suspects they're hunting him for the bounty.
- The large "amorphous being" glimpsed enthroned during the Bloody Fields battle (the Hungry Lord) is later revealed to be the vessel that the Carniphage manifests through, transforming into the Avatar of the Carniphage in Chapter 3.
- The Man Eaters' god, the Carniphage/Carrion King, is never directly seen or fully explained in either book — deliberately left mysterious for the GM to develop further.
- Cannibalism is the literal trigger for transformation into a Man Eater (per the lore vignette of "Halleck," who is drawn into a tribe after eating a slain neighbor's heart) — a useful in-world explanation for how the tribes recruit and grow.
- The Penitent Crusade, though nominally allies, are feared by the common folk as being nearly as dangerous/merciless as the monsters they fight — a seeded moral complication for future sessions.

## Faction: The Man Eaters
- Cannibal tribes born from Doaden's famine and blight; those who resort to eating human flesh ("Fleshspoil") are called into the wilderness by the whispers of the Carniphage/Carrion King and welcomed into the tribe as "The Wicked."
- Strict internal hierarchy based on diet and status: dominant members eat the best cuts and stay closer to human in appearance; weaker members subsist on offal and degenerate into Manswine, then Lardermen; spiritual leadership is held by the Haruspex (who reads entrails/auguries), assisted by Offalscryers.
- Signature creatures/roles: Preycatchers (vanguard/scouts), Manhounds (feral quadrupedal trackers), Manswine/Lardermen (mounts and pack beasts), Fleshguard (elite bodyguards), Flesh Stalkers and the Man Hunter (ranged skirmishers/snipers), Berzerkers and Mangutters (front-line brutes), War Leaders (siege commanders), and giants (Giant Glutton/Siege Fleshglutton) who join the tribes.
- Individual tribes number roughly 20–30, but "Wild Hunts" occur when multiple tribes band together under a Warlord or the enthroned Hungry Lord to besiege a larger target like a city — the scenario behind the Siege of Guerrin.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Ivon Horack's ultimate fate (turned in, killed, freed, or recruited) should be decided by player choice and tracked for future sessions — check whether the party recovered the golden cross.
- If Baylor's rangers weren't fully rescued during the Palisades Skirmish Event, note how many survived — affects Guerrin's post-siege state.
- Guerrin's final Morale Score at the end of Part 2 determines which named NPCs (Stragovid always survives) and what percentage of soldiers/militia/rangers survive — record the outcome for continuity.
- The Man Eaters are explicitly designed as a recurring, expanding threat across Doaden — a natural hook for a Part 3 or spin-off encounters.
- The Penitent Crusade is now aware of and allied (uneasily) with Guerrin — potential future faction with its own agenda.
