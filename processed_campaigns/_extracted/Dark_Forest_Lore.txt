    elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted
        monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very
                            essence of existence.

It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of
   the glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie

The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be honored

them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless husks and shadows of
                        their once-glorious selves.

     Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn
               tenacity, a last flickering flame within the dark.

A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of eternity,
               a momentary victory before the endless night.

                       Dark Forest

       The Black Forest is expansive and nearly impenetrable, its breadth
    stretching from the Holy Empire all the way to the northern realms
    of Thule. Its trees had grown tall already when the world was young,
    many being already thousands of years old. The strange and isolated
    communities of folk that live here believed that the place has a spirit of its
    own, and that ancient nature spirits still dwell in the unexplored vastness.
       Recently, strange happenings have been reported in the Black Forest.
    Caravans have gone missing, foul creatures are lurking about, and a
    dark chanting echoes from deep within the forest. A goat-like braying
    emanates from the darkness within, calling folk to worship at its sinister
    altar. But what evils corrupt the minds of these folk? Some secrets are
    best left undiscovered.

                                              Domovoy

   Domovoys are mischievous devils often employed by
witches for small tasks such as fetching ingredients or assisting
in preparing spells and potions.
   Some of the older communities of Doaden revere these little
impish creatures, leaving out small bowls of food or alcohol
on their hearths, believing that the Domovoy will bless their
households, or curse them if they should be offended.

     Old Blacksmith


                Aa towering man with wild eyes, this poor sot was
            once a skilled laborer. Hearing the call of madness, he
            has become feral, losing his mind to a dark horror in
            the forest. Like a cornered beast, he lashes out with
            his smithing hammer, a bulky iron sledge requiring
            two hands to hold.
                He charges at any who dare to approach him, his
             screams devolving into a poor imitation of bleats
               as he unleashes his fury. He fights with a savage,
                unrelenting ferocity, with little regard for his own
                 safety.
                     Corrupted Villagers. Territorial and
                  unwelcoming to outsiders, the maddened
              villagers of Essenvald kidnap or attack anyone that
            enters their land, corrupted through their worship of
            the olden deity in the woods.
                Though they are not particularly intelligent, they
            have primal cunning and are able to work together in
            packs, using their numbers to overpower their victims.
                The same holds true for all such small and isolated
            communities spread throughout the northern regions
            of the Holy Empire. For in these backwater parts,
             many dark entities still hold sway in the local lands,
                drawing in and corrupting those who enter their
                   domain.

                                     Mendicant Friar


    The mendicant clergy travel the lands of Holy
Empire, living among the most impoverished
communities.
    They walk far and wide to preach the sermons of
the Azerai faith to the common folk, relying on alms
and the goodwill of those they meet to survive.
    The story of the friar who visited the town of
Essenvald is a sad tale. Originally visiting to bring
the holy word to the isolated villages in the area, he
become twisted and corrupted by the evils that dwelt
in the nearby woods, the same evils that the village
folks worshipped in secret.
    Yet though his mind and soul have become
corrupted, he still wields the powers of the light to
heal and soothe his now-twisted allies, and to burn
away his enemies.

     Mad Woodcutter


                The Mad Woodcutter was once a normal man,
             but transformed into twisted, violent being under
             the influence of the corruptions in the nearby
             woods.
                 His eyes burn with a crazed intensity, and his
             mouth is twisted into a savage snarl.
                He carries with him his axe for chopping
             wood, though now he uses it more frequently to

                                       Crazed Scyther



   Once a docile peaceful farmer, the Crazed Scyther
has fallen to corruption.
   Now, she uses her harvesting scythe to reap the
flesh of any outsider that wanders into her derelict
community.
   Corruption is, unfortunately, a common event,
especially in the dark and isolated corners of the world
of Doaden. In most cases, there is no cure, and the
villagers must simply be purged for the greater good of
the Empire.




                                                           17
     Kikimora


           The Kikimora emerges as a haunting embodiment of
       fear. Their twisted forms and decaying flesh reflect the
       terrors within. They prey upon the desperate and lost,
       reveling in torment and suffering.
           Encounter with a Kikimora is a chilling ordeal, their
       sinister laughter signaling the descent into darkness.
       Sharp teeth line its mouth, and its saliva is deadly
       poisonous, causing paralysis and even death.
           Adventurers can often determine when they are close
       to a Kikimora’s nest by the horrid smell of excrement,
       rotting vegetation, and festering meat spoils that make up
              the creature’s home.
                  The Kikimora is a patient predator, camouflaging
            itself amid the brushes, waiting for unsuspecting prey
       to pass by.
           Once it pounces, it strikes with ferocity and tenacity,
       its jaws clamping down on its victim and injecting them
       with its toxic venom before dragging their paralyzed
       corpses into their dens to be eaten alive.

                                        Village Hag


   Hags are old crones, humanoid but twisted by the
dark spirits of the earth. They are powerful spellcasters
devoted to the worship of the earthen spirits that once
proliferated throughout the less civilized regions of
Doaden.
   Hags are grotesque in shape, their warty flesh sagging
and rotten teeth snarling.
   Generally, hags generally take interest in small,
isolated communities, approaching and offering services
as healers, but slowly corrupting the common folk to
bring them under the influence of whatever
dark entities the hags serve.
   Hags delight in cursing their
enemies with plagues and diseases,
bad luck, and other terrors. Few who
cross a hag’s path come away unscathed, and
those who do often find themselves plagued by
nightmares and haunted by their cackling laughter.

     Chort


           Born from the twisted nightmares of ancient
        evils, this twisted goat-like demon is covered in
        coarse, matted fur, and from its deformed skull
        sprout twisted, bestial horns.
           It is a virile beast with an insatiable carnal
        appetite, and Baphomet takes it as her consort, to
            spawn a string of never-ending evils into the
             wicked world.
                 The Chort is a skilled hunter and fighter,
            capable of leaping great distances and delivering
          powerful strikes with its deadly weapon.

                                                   Bies

    Bies are among the worst of the fiendish offspring of
Baphomet, personifying the corruption of evil upon the natural
world.
    These beasts are solitary hunters, stalking their victims with
ruthless determination. Their razor-sharp claws and jagged teeth
are stained with the blood of countless fallen foes.
    Legends speak of their insatiable hunger, as they consume
flesh and bone, leaving nothing but carnage in their wake. These
monstrous creatures possess an uncanny resilience, shrugging
off wounds that would fell lesser beings. Their leathery hides are
thick and impenetrable, protecting against all but the most
powerful attacks.
    The mere sight of a Bies is enough to strike terror into
the hearts of even the most valiant warriors. The Bies fight
with little finesse, charging straight at their opponents to
knock them to the ground before biting, kicking, and
striking with their hooves.




                                                                     25
     Baphomet

            Baphomet is a powerful and immortal demonic
        entity, the mother of evils.
            This enormous horned deity holds power over
        nature and fertility and is a great elder spirit, born long
        before the civilizations of man. It is worshiped by dark
        sects, cults, hags, and witch covens who seek to gain
        its favor and harness its power for their own nefarious
        purposes.
                     Broodmare. Baphomet is in a constant
                     state of birthing, giving birth to grotesque,
                       devilish creatures that spread terror and
                       darkness wherever they go, often living
                   in secluded places in the forest or swamps
                surrounding settlements under Baphomet’s
        influence.
            Every once in a while, Baphomet gives birth to
        a Chort, which is seen as a symbol of depravity and
        worshipped by the followers of Baphomet.
                           Corruptor. Baphomet’s mere
                         presence can have a corrupting influence
                          on the environment around it. Its dark
                          energy taints the earth, water, and air,
                         causing plants and animals to mutate
                      and become twisted.
                        In the primeval forests and swamps
              where Baphomet dwells, the trees grow
              unnaturally dense, tall, and twisted, the waters
               turn fetid and poisonous. As for the settlements
                                       that surround its territory,
                                        Baphomet’s influence
                                       spreads like a disease,
                                    infecting the minds of the
                             people with madness and despair.
                                 The people become fearful
                           and paranoid, turning on each other
                      and eventually succumbing to the dark
              influence of the demon. The inhabitants of these
                  settlements are often found performing
                   depraved rituals and sacrifices, all in service
                    to Baphomet.
