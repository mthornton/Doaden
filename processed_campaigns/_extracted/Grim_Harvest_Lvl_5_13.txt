                        The World of Doaden
W         elcome to Doaden, the dark world of tainted lands and twisted monsters, where
          an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very essence of existence.

              It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous heroes of
          glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie with one
                        another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.

             It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
        The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to be honored in
                                       songs of minstrels.

         For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake invariably leave
              them disheartened, broken, nothing more than soulless shadows of
                                  their once-glorious selves.

        Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a stubborn tenacity,
                           a last flickering flame within the dark.

          A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss of eternity,
                         a momentary victory before the endless night.




Content Advisory: This adventure may include disturbing and/or mature content, including violence, psychological horror, gore, and other
sensitive topics. The production may contain vivid descriptions, unsettling imagery, and explore themes of despair, moral ambiguity, and
challenging morality. Please consider your personal boundaries before engaging with this material. You have the option to modify or skip
content to ensure you and your group’s comfort and enjoyment throughout the experience.

The Goblin King
Bestiarum monthly modules include exciting and
dynamic boss fights, each of which come with their
own lair map and loot tables, offering a chance to drop
unique artifacts and magic items. If you choose to run
a boss fight, you should give the party a chance to rest,
and then design a few encounters before they reach
the boss to soften them up and begin to strain their
resources. The more they are strained, the more intense
the fight will be, but don’t overdo it or they will be too
weak to defeat the boss!

The Goblin King. The boss module connected to this
adventure is the Goblin King. Goblin communities do
not live in isolation. Each tribe is ruled by a dominant
Goblin King. To truly purge the goblin threat, they must
delve back into the dark tunnels, digging further into the
cave to reach this villainous foe. With the goblin leader
slain and their population wiped out, these devious
creatures will plague the lands no longer.
