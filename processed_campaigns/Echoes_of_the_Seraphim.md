# Echoes of the Seraphim
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Part 1_Bestiarum 5E Adventure Archive/B_One-Shot Adventures (Lvl 5-20)/Echoes of the Seraphim (Lvl 8-20)/Echoes of the Seraphim (Lvl 8-20).pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 8-20
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A one-shot (or Path of Penitence campaign slot) set in the ruined Cathedral of Echoes, where an imprisoned seraph — a being so alien its mere presence shatters mortal minds — is chained by invisible deva guardians circling the twin bell towers. The Cult of Dogma seeks to ring both bells and free it to "pass judgment" on the world; the party must stop them or, if too late, defeat the freed seraph in a lair boss fight. Difficulty scales by APL: Hard (8-10), Nightmare (11-13), Torment (14-16), Hell (17-20).

## Key Locations
- Main Gallery (1) — entry hall with pews; twin fountains (1a/1b) bear bloody cultist warnings about the twin bells and the petrifying "gaze of the holy"
- Chapel of the Seraph (2) — houses the imprisoned seraph in stasis rings; becomes the boss arena (Lower Level 1 = altar/cover, Upper Level 2 = 45-ft mezzanine)
- Left Sacristy (3) — cult research room; Cult Encounter (knights + mage); a cultist here carries the cathedral keys
- Left Bell Tower (4) — 300-ft spiral stair; first bell; Bell Encounter (deva/planetar)
- Right Sacristy (5) — locked room (DC 15 thieves' tools or DC 20 STR); Investigation table can reveal a secret door to area 6, healing potions, or gold
- Right Bell Tower (6) — second bell, only reachable via undercroft (8) or the secret door in area 5; guardians here are pre-alerted
- Chapel of Reflection (7) — mirrored room with a cultist clue about the eye cherubs' petrifying gaze
- Undercroft Stairs (8) — locked passage beneath the cathedral, lined with petrified victims of the seraph's original descent; leads to the right bell tower

## Plot / Quest Threads
| Thread | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Prevent the Cult of Dogma from ringing the twin bells | Central objective | Cryptic clues at fountains (1a/1b) hint at the ritual; cathedral keys needed to open locked rooms |
| Free/destroy the seraph if bells are rung | Triggers boss fight | Seraph respawns eventually even if "destroyed" — it is functionally eternal |
| Cult of Dogma revenge | Open-ended aftermath | Cult will see the party's actions as sacrilege and may retaliate later |

## Session Flow
1. Hook (city council alarm, slain cultist's note, or PC's prophetic dreams) draws party to the Cathedral of Echoes.
2. Main Gallery — explore, find fountain clues about the bells and petrification.
3. Sacristies — fight/loot Cult of Dogma cells; obtain keys; investigate for secret door/loot/blueprint-style clues.
4. Ascend Left Bell Tower — Bell Encounter #1, ring first bell.
5. Reach Right Bell Tower via undercroft or secret sacristy door — Undercroft Encounter, then Bell Encounter #2 (guardians pre-alerted), ring second bell.
6. Return to Chapel of the Seraph — Seraph Boss Encounter (multi-phase lair fight).
7. Resolution — seraph slain/driven off (temporarily), loot rolled, Cult of Dogma left as a recurring enemy.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Sacristy Investigation (area 5, roll d100): 25+ reveals secret door to right bell tower; 15+ grants 1d4 potions of superior healing; 10+ grants 40d100 gp
- Seraph Boss Loot (roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus: Hard +0/Nightmare +1/Torment +2/Hell +3): 32+ Cherub Eye Charm* (very rare); 1-27 nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- The seraph is not evil in an intelligible sense — it is utterly indifferent to mortals and cannot be reasoned with or bargained with; even "killing" it only banishes it temporarily
- The bell towers are guarded by an invisible army of deva that the party may never fully perceive until the guardians manifest
- The undercroft's petrified statues are victims of the seraph's original descent centuries ago, a grim preview of what happens if it's freed

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Seraph stat block: AC 18, HP 243, CR 16 base; Immutable Form, Magic Resistance, Commander of Heaven (summons eye cherubs each turn, no limit); legendary actions Rush/Quick Cast/Call Cherub/Flurry
- Boss Phases table: Hard = Phase 1 only; Nightmare = Phase 1, 1.5; Torment = Phase 1, 2; Hell = Phase 1, 2, 3 (each phase restores HP and strengthens Divine Lamentation/legendary actions)
- Eye Cherub stat block: AC 13, HP 38, CR 1; Petrifying Iris (DC 11 CON save or turn to stone) and Radiant Beam (30-ft line, DC 13 DEX save, 3d6 radiant)
- If bell towers are accessed out of order or party tries to leave the cathedral exterior to reach the second bell, guardians materialize and force them back inside
