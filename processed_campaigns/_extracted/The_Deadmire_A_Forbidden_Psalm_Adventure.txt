      Lunden is a genius engineer that studied in Allstein for years. The Penitent Crusade has urgent need

     THE BLACK FORTRESS
       The warband has made it! You are finally here at
     the Black Fortress, an enormous stronghold with
     towering walls that reach to the north and south,
     stretching the length of a mountain pass heading
     into the region known as the Ashen Wastes. The
     fortress itself is highly restricted, but there is
     a Penitent Crusade encampment outside of the
     fortress.
