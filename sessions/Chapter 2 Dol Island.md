# Chapter 2 : Dol Island

## DM Notes
Dol Island was once knows as Saraswati's Island.  Over a thousand years ago this Island was a major temple for the worshipers of Saraswati.  A great temple once dominated this small island but has since been reclaimed by nature.  There is no evidence above ground of the once grand temple unless one were to perform an extensive excavation.  Below ground, part of the catacombs remain.  It is here where Sara was lured and imprisoned. 
 
A Saraswati cult of a couple dozen members decided to celebrate Vasant Pancham on Dol island.  Sara, seeing an opportunity to grow her devout followers, decided to make an appearance.  She planned to manifest in the smoke of a bonfire on the last night of the celebration and give her blessing.  What better way to plant the seeds to build a true following again than an appearance? 
 
What Sara did not know was the group was being gently manipulated by an infiltrator.  A priest of Adephagia joined the cult and pretended to worship for years.  Recruited new members and suggested holding a celebration on Saraswati's Island. 
 
The entire plan had one goal: get Sara to the Island and capture her.   
 
Everything went like clockwork.  The Adephagia cultists arrived on the Island ahead of the Saraswati devotees.  They hid in the catacombs shielded from Saraswati's perception by an artifact supplied by Adephagia.  One the final night, Sara appeared in the smoke.  Her worshipers fell to their knees in tears of joy.  The Adephagia cultists then emerged from behind the veil and cast the spell that captured Sara.  The cultists then slaughtered all of Sara's devotees. 
 
Sara, being made flesh by the spell, was magically chained and entombed in the catacombs.  What had once been a place of great power became her prison.  She was then left for 666 days.  At the end of the wait, the cultists returned to harvest her blood to use in a ritual to bring the Jailer to Doaden.  

## Beach Path 
 
Foot print in the sand lead to a break in the thick vegetation.  It is clear this path has been used recently, but not frequently.  Within a few steps you realize there is a stone path.  It is reasonable to assume that path started someplace on the beach and was just covered by sand.  The vegetation is so heavy is block much of the sunlight, but you can still see.  The vegetation is tropical in nature and healthy.  In less than a minute you reach the rocky face of the cliff.  It is not what you were expecting. 
 
This lower part of the cliff is smooth with an ornate façade carved into the stone.  It is dirty, aged, and chipped but was once of remarkable intricacy and beauty.  It is all decorative.  No obvious symbols. 
 
There is an archway in the rock with stairs leading down into darkness. 
 
# Stairs 
 
The walls of the are coved in mosaics made of bits of glass.  Dirty, aged, and shipped, but, again, overwhelming beauty.  Some of the mosaics depict landscapes, some abstract patterns, and a few have multiple panels telling a story. 
 
(See Quest for the Sun-Petal) 
 
# Island 
 
The tunnel leads to a large cave.  The ceiling of the cave is open allowing sunlight to shine on what can be described as an island.  There is a land mass in the center of the cave with vegetation and a large tree.  Surrounding the land mass is a drop off of unknowable depth.  There is a bridge from the stairs onto the island.  It is not clear what keeps the island up.  (It is simply a column of stone, but it can't be seen from the parties vantage point.) 
 
There is a shrine in the center of the island.  Beautifully crafted, but like everything else, nature has done damage.   Its purpose is not apparent.  (Not particularly special, just a nice place to gather people around to worship Sara). 
 
However, it does seem to be less overgrown than you would think compared to the surrounding.  (Perception check 10+) 
 
Any examination of the soundings will find bones and body parts.  The flesh has all rotted away, but some scraps of clothing remain.  Roughly a couple dozen people.  Can't be exact as many of the bodies are in pieces.  There are remains of jewelry, but nothing of value.   
 
Perception check: 
0-10: Not recent.  Some scraps of clothing.  Hard to say if they are a year old or ten years old. 
11-15: Can't be more than 5 years old, otherwise the cloth would  have also rotted away.  The jewelry may have been pretty at one time.  Seems well made but homemade. 
16+: About 2 years old based on the cloth and leather.  You notice there are no weapons.  The remains appears to be women and men that were not fighters.   
 
There is another bridge on the other side leading to a large stone door. 
 
# Entrance 
 
A large door blocks the entrance.  There is no obvious handle or way to open it.  Above the door is script in an unknown language.  On the door are three letter in the same unknown language. 
 
(Translates to "Art comes from the soul".  The three symbols on the door are out of order, but are the letters to spell soul.  Touching the letters in the correct order opens the door.  Anytime a wrong letter is touched a Scythrian warrior comes.) 
 
Strength check: 18+ door is forced open 
 
# Hall 
 
Falling stone trap.  Must walk to spell soul backwards.  This will also close the door.  Walk forward and it reopens. 
 
# Room 1 
 
Large room empty with the exception of two statues of what looks like mummified ancient warriors.  Facing the statues is another statue with an embedded mirror. 
 
There are three cultists in the room when the party arrives.  Two are only glimpsed as they hurry out.  The third says "too late, we have what we need."  He then breaks the mirror and runs out. 
 
When the mirror breaks, the two mummies immediately come alive and attach the party. 
 
Examining the statue: 
Intelligence check 
0-10: Seems old 
11-15: You recognize it from Vasant Panchami celebrations.  Normal to see similar statues as part of the decorations.  You don't remember the name, but another god from mythology. 
16-19: Saraswati is her name 
20: Saraswati, Goddess of the Arts 
 
# Room 2 
 
As you enter you hear a cultist speak "Go, get the blood loaded for transport.  I will delay with the intruders." 
 
There is a statue in this room also with four mummies.  If given a chance, the cultist will break the mirror and try to run. 
 
One cultist.  In way over his head clearly.  If the party captures him, he cackles "Hail Adephagia" then bites his tongue off and chokes to death on his own blood with a smile on his face. 
 
He has a map.  Shows the location of an old church. 
 
Ornate tomb.  A hole has been chipped into the side of the stone.  Blood is oozing out.  If the party looks inside they will see the body of a woman in a bloody white gown with four arms bound in chains.  Her body is still, but does not look decomposed.  She is the most beautiful woman you have ever seen.  There is an open wound in her side where the blood is oozing.  Suddenly, her eyes open and she looks at you.  Sadness and terror cloud her face as you stumble back in surprise and shock. 
 
Regardless of what they try to do, the body disappears shortly. 
 
Approaching the body causes a sense of fear.  DC 15 Wisdom throw.  If fail, terror increases as they approach the tomb to the point they can't get with 5 feet. 
 
Who is Adephagia?  
Intelligence check 10-15: Adephagia is an "old god" worshiped before the God Emperor revealed himself.  You may vaguely remember hearing a song or two, but don't recall the details.  These old gods are part of the mythology of the land and the stories are clearly not true. 
16-19: You remember a song about Adephagia.  The song was comedic about a god whose gluttony was only exceeded by her sex drive! 
20+: You have read stories about her where she is not comical but quite evil and selfish 
 
 
# Room 3 
 
Similar bare room.  The only exit, other than how you entered, is through a break in the wall.  It looks like somebody broke a hole into the wall.  You can hear the cultists fleeing. 
 
In the center of the room is a large thick pool of dark blood.  As you enter, the blood quivers and flows together rising up. A monster stands before you made of coagulated blood.  You have never seen anything like this.  Your mind screams in horror.  Your body is frozen in place. 
 
It leaps towards you.   
 
The group makes a Wisdom throw.  The lowest value is attacked by the monster.  Then combat begins. 
 
After the fight, the party see there is a 10 foot circle carved in the floor with a spiral of ruins carved in the floor in what appears to be the same language as on the entrance door.  If the party gets the amulet of transportation, touching a clear stone immediately brings them to this circle.  This effect takes place from any location in this plane of existence or any other. 
 
# Beach 
 
You emerge from the dense foliage surrounding the hole to see a beach.  This is a different beach than you started on.  The beach is 400 feet wide with 300 feet between you and the water.  You see a boat moored just off shore, single mast about 24 feet long.  There is also 2 smaller 12 foot single mast sail boats in the sand at the water's edge. 
 
There are 4 men in black robes loading barrels into one of the boats.  Two barrels remain to be loaded.  You can't help but wonder why they would wear heavy black robes in the hot sun while loading barrels you can only assume are heavy. 
 
One cultist turns to the others and commands "Finish loading the barrels and get them to the ship.  Set sail soon as they are on board.  I will follow in a sail boat once I dispatch these intruders." 
 
"You, come with me and let's kill them or give our lives in honor." 
 
Two cultists draw weapons and start walking towards you.  The other two continue loading the barrels. 
 
If killed, the lead cultist has a bag with 20 silver pieces and an amulet of transportation.   
 
The amulet has three crystals.  Two are clear and one is red and burnt looking.  Touching a clear crystal causes a loud pop and everything in a cylinder 10 foot in diameter, and 8 feet high are transported back to Saraswati's crypt.  Room 3. 
 
If amulet examined, give a Wisdom check:

1-5: ooh, pretty 
6-10: you can feel a tingle that indicates magic 
11-15:  magic does not feel evil or destructive 
16+: the magic has traces of power you recognize as from one of the Old Gods, but you don't know which 
Nat 20+: Magic is from Saraswati
