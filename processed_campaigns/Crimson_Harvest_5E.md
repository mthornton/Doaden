# Crimson Harvest

**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Bloomshade Monastery has fallen to corruption after receiving a shipment of "crimson fruit" from the mysterious Crimson Grove Abbey. At the annual harvest festival, the monks distribute ambrosia — a soup made from the fruit — to spread their corruption among the local peasantry. The party arrives during the festival, must decide whether to drink the offered ambrosia (infiltration vs. open hostility), then investigate the monastery to uncover a subterranean grove of crimson fruit trees at the corruption's source. A self-contained one-shot; scales APL 1–10.

## Plot / Quest Threads
| Thread | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Decide whether to drink the ambrosia | Opening choice | Drinking eases infiltration (monks treat party as one of their own unless caught snooping); refusing triggers immediate hostility |
| Investigate the source of the corruption | Core objective | Clues from Library research notes, Kitchen journal, and Brother Marrus's resignation point to the catacombs/Underground Chamber |
| Confront the Abbot | Mid-late objective | His key opens the Underground Chamber; his correspondence implicates him further |
| Decide the grove's fate | Ending choice | Harvest fruit for study, destroy the grove, or contain it — player-driven, no single "correct" resolution |

## Session Flow
1. **Harvest Festival** — party arrives, decides to drink or refuse the ambrosia, meets the Abbot's public face
2. **Monastery exploration** — Entrance Hall, Dining Hall, Kitchen, Dormitory, Library: gather clues and loot while avoiding (or embracing) suspicion
3. **Abbot's Quarters** — confrontation with the still-lucid Abbot; obtain the Underground Chamber key
4. **Bell Tower (optional)** — discover Brother Marrus's fate, reinforcing the corruption's inevitability
5. **Underground Chamber** — descend to the Subterranean Grove, fight the caretakers, and decide what to do with the crimson fruit trees

## Secrets & Twists
- The ambrosia corrupts over 14 days of daily DC 10 Constitution saves (Crimson Decay Effects table: nausea → pale/itchy skin → disadvantage on Str/Dex → tumors → cravings → disadvantage on Wis/Int → bulbous swelling → full GM control, drawn toward Crimson Grove Abbey)
- Corruption can be halted by purging the fruit from the body (induced vomiting, extraction) but only reversed via Crimson Grove Abbey indoctrination (permanent) or a strong cure (Greater Restoration or similar) before that point
- Crimson Grove Abbey itself — the true origin of the fruit — is deliberately left undeveloped, explicitly flagged as a hook for the DM to build out later
- The Abbot is notably more lucid/in-control than his flock, implying deeper complicity rather than simple corruption

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Three adventure hooks (Sinister Invitation, Desperate Plea, Arcane Research) change what the party is motivated to do post-grove — confirm which was used
- If a PC drank the ambrosia and is mid-corruption, track their Crimson Decay Effects and the 14-day clock across future sessions
- Crimson Grove Abbey is unexplored — a natural next adventure if the campaign continues
