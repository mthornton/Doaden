# Prison Breakdown

**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A one-shot siege/infiltration adventure: the outlaw Blood Reavers, led by the imprisoned Urvakh the Betrayer, engineer a riot at the Iron Gauntlet prison fortress to break out and seize the key to the high-security Panopticon. The party must fight through the prison complex, gather allies and intel, and take down Urvakh before he tortures the location of the key out of the warden. Scales via APL: Beginner (1–2), Easy (3–4), Medium (5–7), Hard (8–10).

## Key Locations
- Area 1, Entry Gate — false "guards" (disguised bandits) try to turn the party away; ambush if the party pushes in.
- Area 2, Ward — outer courtyard, hidden Blood Reaver ambush among crates/debris.
- Area 3, Gallows — three guards (incl. Barle) making a last stand against a mob; rescuing them yields plot info and a prison map.
- Area 4, Guardhouse — drunk bandits reveling; captive guard in a jail cell; hides armory key (secret drawer, DC 10 Investigation) and prison keys.
- Area 5, Armory — locked (needs guardhouse key or DC 15 Dex / break DC 20, 20 HP); ambush inside; watchtower overlooks Warden's Office for recon.
- Area 6, Cell Block A — corridor of cells; Grolo held at the end; scene of dead guards/prisoners.
- Area 7, Cell Block B — mob fighting two guards; the Ghost's quiet, undisturbed cell near the entrance.
- Area 8, Warden's Office — walled courtyard (locked gate) → ground floor (Urvakh's men) → upper floor (Urvakh + Ingfar).
- Area 9, Secret Tunnel — hidden passage (DC 20/15 Perception) from outside the outer wall to behind the Warden's Office, bypassing the courtyard; contains 3 traps and Grolo's hidden stash.
- Area 10, The Panopticon — high-security block, inaccessible this adventure; serves only as a looming time-pressure element.

## Plot / Quest Threads
| Thread | Status | Notes |

| Quell the Blood Reavers' prison riot | Resolved by defeating Urvakh | Bandits/mobs surrender or flee once he falls |
| Recover/secure the secret tunnel | Optional | Learned from Grolo or found via Perception (DC 20/15); bypasses courtyard fight |
| Protect the Panopticon key | Background tension only | Panopticon stays secure this adventure — a ticking-clock narrative device, not resolved on-screen |
| Free the Ghost as an ally | Optional | If freed, kills 6 bandits on office ground floor before vanishing |
| Free Grolo for tunnel intel | Optional | He lies about being framed and about his own hidden stash/traps |

## Session Flow
1. Opening: party arrives at the Iron Gauntlet mid-riot; false guards at the gate try to turn them away (Insight/Perception clues expose the ruse).
2. Ward ambush, then optional rescue of guards at the Gallows (plot info + prison map).
3. Explore Guardhouse (free captive guard, get armory/prison keys, learn of the secret tunnel and "the Ghost").
4. Secure the Armory (fight ambush, loot gear, use watchtower to scout Urvakh's forces).
5. Optional: Cell Block A (free Grolo for tunnel intel) and/or Cell Block B (free the Ghost as an ally).
6. Approach the Warden's Office via courtyard assault or the secret tunnel (bypass); fight through ground floor.
7. Confront and defeat Urvakh on the upper floor while he interrogates the warden; possible surprise round.
8. Ending: riot collapses, warden freed, party hailed as heroes; Panopticon remains a looming unresolved threat.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Armory search (1d20 per PC): 15–19 a potion of healing; 20 a +1 weapon/armor/shield (GM's choice); 0–14 nothing.
- Grolo's parting gift: Grolo's Stinky Sack* (attracts rat swarm in the tunnel; secretly gives enemies +5 passive Perception vs. the carrier — don't reveal this to players).
- Grolo's Stash (Area 9, DC 20 Perception / DC 15 with a clue): 500 gp.
- Standard weapons/armor ≤100 gp freely available in the Armory.

## Secrets & Twists
- Urvakh deliberately allowed himself to be captured a week prior specifically to orchestrate this riot from inside.
- Grolo secretly sold the tunnel's location to the Blood Reavers and seeded it with traps hoping the party (or anyone else) dies so he can loot their remains.
- Grolo's "protective" gift (Stinky Sack) is actually bait that lures the tunnel's rat swarm and boosts enemy Perception against the carrier — a hidden mechanic GMs should not disclose to players.
- The Panopticon threat is a narrative-only ticking clock in this adventure — it never actually gets breached on-screen.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Decide up front whether the party discovers the secret tunnel via Grolo, via Perception, or not at all — it changes the difficulty of the final assault significantly.
- If both Grolo and the Ghost are freed, note the combined effect (tunnel access + 6 ground-floor kills) trivializes much of the final approach — pace accordingly.
- Urvakh's stat block scales notably (bandit captain → bandit khan* with allies) — confirm difficulty tier before the final fight.
- The prison map given by Barle deliberately omits the secret tunnel location per GM note.
