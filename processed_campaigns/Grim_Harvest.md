**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Farmers in the northern territories are vanishing, food production is collapsing, and survivors report chittering in the dark and stray mushrooms near the disappearances. The trail leads to a goblin-infested cave network where captives are ground into mulch to feed the tribe's fungal crops. The party must brave a hazardous descent, fight across a goblin-held cavern of rope bridges and giant mushrooms, and defeat a troll guarding a human survivor — with an optional extended finale against the tribe's ruler, Grothak Wyrm'Killa the Goblin King, and his Bugbear Enforcer bodyguard.

## Key Locations
- Cave Entrance / Dark Descent tunnels — single-file passage riddled with environmental hazards
- Goblin Infested Traverse (Map A) — vaulted cavern of giant mushrooms and rickety rope bridges over deep pits; goblin patrols posted at A1–A7
- Troll Lair (Map B) — cavern where a Feral Cave Troll or Siege Troll feasts on victims; corpse pile hides survivor Beryn; cracked floor can collapse into a new pit
- Fungal Farms (Taking It Further extension) — sprawling mushroom valley grown on human mulch; home of the Fungal Hermit and Skulk
- The Blud-Thrown (Goblin King's throne room, boss extension) — labyrinthine deep tunnels lined with trophies, lit by phosphorescent fungi; lair actions trigger on initiative count 20

## Session Flow
1. **Encounter 1 — Dark Descent:** Party picks a marching order and rolls the d6 hazard table per member while descending into the goblin tunnels.
2. **Encounter 2 — Goblin Infested Traverse:** Combat crawl across Map A's mushroom platforms and rope bridges from entrance (A1) to exit (A7); a human cry for help is heard as they near the exit.
3. **Encounter 3 — Troll Lair:** Party fights the troll(s) and rescues Beryn from beneath the corpse pile in Map B.
4. **[Optional] Fungal Farms detour:** Non-combat encounter with the Fungal Hermit and Skulk; can yield magical services or a humanitarian-aid subplot.
5. **[Optional] Goblin King finale:** Party rests, then pushes deeper to the Blud-Thrown for the Grothak Wyrm'Killa / Irn'Hyd dual boss fight (with optional Phase II reinforcement wave).

## Secrets & Twists
- The goblins don't eat their human captives — bodies are used purely as mulch fertilizer for the fungal crops
- The Fungal Hermit's prized Cerebrum Cap fungus grows only when planted on human skulls
- Killing Grothak doesn't end the broader goblin threat in Doaden — each tribe answers to its own Goblin King elsewhere

## Faction Notes (Goblin Warrens)
- Goblin society here is single-tribe and hierarchical: common Grunts and Spore Sowers (CR1) support specialized Strikers/Marauders (CR3), elite Shadow Stalkers/Blood Seers (CR5), and Raid Leaders (CR7), all ultimately answering to the tribe's Goblin King
- Goblins fight with Pack Tactics (advantage when allies are adjacent) and are noted as relentless once alerted, but won't chase intruders past their own chamber boundaries
- The tribe's economy is built on captive-fed fungal agriculture — the Fungal Hermit sits outside the goblin hierarchy as a tolerated, semi-independent trade partner
- A Goblin King is always guarded by a dedicated Bugbear Enforcer bodyguard — expect any King encounter elsewhere in Doaden to follow this same dual-boss pattern

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Beryn's mention of "more survivors deeper in the cavern" is the built-in hook for extending this into a longer dungeon crawl
- The Fungal Hermit/Skulk detour is neutral, not a fight — useful as a breather or roleplay beat before the Troll Lair or Goblin King push
- If running the Goblin King finale, let the party rest first and consider a few softening encounters beforehand — the fight is tuned to be tough even for a strained party
