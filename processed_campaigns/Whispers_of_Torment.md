## Overview
St. Pyotr's Sanitorium, once a respected medical institution, hid a dark secret: head priest Ignatius and his staff conducted torturous experiments on patients. A few months ago the Tormentor Cults — worshippers of the Prison Lord (the Jailer), an entity that feeds on anguish — invaded and are performing a ritual to merge their eldritch Prison Realm with reality. The party must infiltrate the three-level sanitorium, survive escalating madness, and stop the ritual by defeating the Herald of Agony in the chapel.

## Session Flow (chapter beats)
1. **Hook** — pick one: Lost Refuge (storm shelter), A Cry for Help (mad survivor), or Path of Penitence (Crusade mission); read Opening Script at A1
2. **Level A (Ground Floor)** — explore rooms A1–A12; find priest corpses (A6, A9), Cloister Garden patrol fight (A7), locked Chapel (A8) awaiting the Keyring
3. **Level B (Upper Floor)** — Prison Realm effects intensify (B3); deranged patient Madness Roll (B4); loot dormitory/library (B5–B6); fight Ignatius's guards, obtain journal + Keyring (B7)
4. **Level C (Dungeons)** — optional deep dive: Undercroft (C1) → Body Disposal Pit (C2) and/or Hidden Laboratory (C3) for backstory and the "corruption" lore reveal
5. **Final Encounter** — Cult Chapel (A8): Herald of Agony boss fight using lair actions; encourage finishing remaining C2/C3 exploration before this if not yet done
6. **Ending the Adventure** — corruption/madness/illusions fade over 1d4 days after the Herald falls; if not defeated, Prison Realm continues merging with reality (calamitous escalation, sets up sequel)

## Secrets & Twists
- The sanitorium's caretakers (led by Ignatius) were torturing/dissecting patients *before* the cult ever arrived — the cult was drawn there by the site's existing atrocities, not the other way around
- Doaden lore reveal (via C3 medical notes): "Corruption" is a real, physical, inescapable phenomenon that grows in all mortal flesh from birth to death — a deeper campaign-setting mystery
- The Body Disposal Pit (C2) isn't just set dressing — it generates a ley-line-like psychic energy from accumulated suffering that actively feeds/strengthens the Prison Lord
