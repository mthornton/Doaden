**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–16
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
Once every several years a maelstrom drains a coastal gulf, exposing the ancient, fish-folk (N'gorroth) temple hidden beneath. The party explores its three descending levels, solves a tide-based puzzle, and confronts an ancient horror — the Avatar of the Sunken God — while the temple begins flooding and sinking back beneath the waves around them. Tier II, APL-scaled (Medium 5–7 / Hard 8–10 / Nightmare 11–13 / Torment 14–16). Set anywhere in the coastal regions (western/southern/eastern diocese) of the Empire of Azerai.

## Key Locations
- Risen Pagoda (Level A) — surface entrance; frescoes depict fish-priests and marine behemoths; contains the first Descending Shaft (20x20 ft, DC 15 Athletics to climb safely)
- Maze Chamber (Level B) — domed octopus-head-shaped chamber with 7 branching tunnels + a hidden 8th (Area 2i, revealed by solving the Tide Wall Puzzle)
- Tunnel Branch 1 (2a) — leechworm swarm breeding ground guarding a High Tide Cube
- Tunnel Branch 2 (2b) — prophecy fresco room; Low Tide Cube on pedestal with N'gorroth rune prophecy ("The world shall be consumed by the depths once more")
- Tunnel Branch 3 (2c) — decaying cadaver room guarding a High Tide Cube; sunken cadavers/priests animate
- Tunnel Branch 4 (2d) — cursed black chest (10d100 gp + 1d4 gems worth 250 gp each), locked (DC 15 Sleight of Hand), cursed with chill touch if touched
- Tunnel Branch 5 (2e) — small pool with a magical tentacle guarding a golden spell scroll case (water breathing scroll, case worth 150 gp)
- Tunnel Branch 6 (2f) — zhathogga's lair guarding a Low Tide Cube
- Tunnel Branch 7 (2g) — fish-folk priest statue shrine; cursed if looted, blesses if given offering
- Tunnel Branch 8 (2h) — leads to the second Descending Shaft down to Level C
- Flooded Chamber (Level C) — deepest chamber; pool gateway to the abyssal depths where the Avatar of the Sunken God dwells; loot: Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals (rare)

## Session Flow
1. **Opening/Hooks** — Treasure Hunt, Fisher's Tale, or Stumbled Upon draws the party to the exposed temple during the maelstrom.
2. **Level A: Risen Pagoda** — party explores frescoes, finds the first descending shaft down to Level B.
3. **Level B: Maze Chamber** — party gathers 4 tide cubes from tunnel branches 1–8 (each with its own encounter/puzzle/treasure), solves the Tide Wall Puzzle (DC 15+/20+/25+ Investigation/Nature hints available) to reveal the hidden 8th tunnel and trigger the temple's flooding.
4. **Descend to Level C** — party has limited time before the temple submerges.
5. **Flooded Chamber climax** — final boss encounter with the Avatar of the Sunken God (or scaled alternative) while water rises each round.
6. **Escape** — party flees upward through the shaft as the temple returns to the sea.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Cursed black chest — 10d100 gp + 1d4 gems (250 gp each); cursed with chill touch, DC/level scales with difficulty
- Golden spell scroll case (150 gp) containing a water breathing scroll — guarded by a magical tentacle (DC 20 Str save or grappled, piercing damage scales with difficulty)
- Fish-folk priest statue offerings — 500 gp total (250 gp scepter, 1,000 gp emerald); taking treasure without offering triggers bane/bestow curse
- Boss loot (Flooded Chamber, roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus): 30+ = Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals (rare); 1–29 = nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- The N'gorroth runes reveal a cyclical prophecy: the world has been "consumed by the depths" before, an unknown number of times
- Completing the Tide Wall Puzzle triggers the temple's self-flooding/re-submersion mechanic — a built-in ticking clock
- The zhathogga can burrow and ambush from an entirely new location, making it a recurring threat throughout Level B

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Fish-folk (N'gorroth) language is nearly unreadable without magic — a good hook for scholar NPCs later
- Tide cube puzzle requires solving lunar tide mechanics (high tide cubes above/below the planet mural, low tide cubes to left/right)
- Ending varies by adventure hook used (Treasure Hunt / Fisher's Tale / Stumbled Upon) — tailor the epilogue accordingly
