# Chapter 6 Swine Raid

The party has agreed to accompany Lady Ysabel Rockfort to Solteres.  With the recent death of her bodyguard Ulfric, she is in need of protection on the road.  The party benefits by having access to carts, horses, supplies, and somebody that knows the road and is established in the city. 
 
Traveling with Lady Ysabel are 4 male servants and 1 lady in waiting.  The male servants also serve as guards, but are not skilled fighters. 
 
To the parties surprise, Master Thaddeus and his wife Marion are joining for the first half the trip.  They received word a colleague obtained a cart of pumpkins that show signs of innate magical properties.  They are meeting him on the road in a few days time then turning back to head back to Haleyon. 
 
On the third night, the party rests and Master Thaddeus approaches Virgil to discuss his problem.  Over the past few days their acquaintance has built some trust. 
 
"Well, you see, I was not truthful about my, um, transformation.  The whole thing is humiliating.  I never meant for this to happen."  From there he can tell his backstory. 
"The village of Haleyon is in desperate need of help.  They begged for help, but their attitude is starting to change as the infestation gets worse." 
"If you can come help, I am sure it would go a long way towards getting you back in the good graces of the Alchemistic Order!" 
 
The next day the party hears a screaming ahead.  The road bends, so they can see that cause. 
 
A group of Beastmen, Porcine specifically, have attacked a wagon of produce.  3 raders and 4 piglets. 
 
The four servants draw their short swords and surround Lady Ysabel.  Thaddeus and Marion slip behind them to hide. 
 
Roll for initiative 
 
If the fight is too easy, have another Rader come I from the woods. 
 
The wagon raided was the people Thaddeus was meeting.  The leader, Willowbread, is moments away from death.  His gut has been torn open and half eaten.  There is no possibility of saving him.  He tell Thaddeus the rough location where the pumpkins were found.  Warns him there is a coven of witches growing the pumpkins.   Rather than confront them, they created a distraction then snuck into the fields and stole the pumpkins.  There is nothing else Willowbread is able to say before he dies. 
 
The small cart held about a dozen pumpkins, but they are mostly destroyed from the rutting of the Porcine.  Thaddeus collects a few scraps but hold out little hope of learning anything. 
 
The pumpkins look, smell, and taste normal at first glance.  It is not apparent why Willowbread thought these had magical properties. 
 
The pumpkins themselves are begnine, but ingesting the seeds is deadly.  The seeds take root in the stomach.  After 24 hours, the roots are so intwined with the stomach there is no way to remove them.  The plant grows until it causes the person to die from starvation or suffocation.  The death is not painful, the plant gives off a narcotic that puts the victim into a mindless stupor.  The plant controls the victim such that they will fight off any attempt to interfere and will cause them to find a fertile spot to lay down and die. 
 
Perception check (Virgil gets +3 and advantage if he commits to an extended study of half a day). 
DC 12+ : there is a trace to magic.  Mix of Enchantment, Necromancy, and Transmutation 
DC 15+ : the seed are the source of magic 
DC 20+ : the plant seems to have the ability to take over a person
