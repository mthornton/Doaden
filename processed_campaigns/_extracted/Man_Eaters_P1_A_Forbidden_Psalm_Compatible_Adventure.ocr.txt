Goal:

Survive the night for 5 rounds until daylight returns and make your escape.
Your warband may escape the ritual area beginning on round 6 by traveling to any of the 4 edges of
the map.

However, Man Eaters hide among the trees waiting to pick off stragglers.

After all warband members escape the ritual area, each surviving member must make a presence roll
with +1 bonus for each warband member (including the first) escaping from that same edge.

Ona fail, they are picked off by the Man Eaters and die.

Reward:
You survive.
Setup and Treasure:

Place 1 Man Eater Altar at the center of the map.

Then, each player places terrain per the normal terrain rules (Forbidden Psalm, p.38) by first placing
any Man Eater Totems and Man Eater Fleshpoils that are available, and then by placing trees, rocks,
rubble, or any other forest themed terrain available until all terrain is placed.

During the mission, the first two Offalscryers each player slays carry 1 treasure.

Deployment:

Versus and Coop - Players place all warband members within 6 inches from the center of the map.
Threats:

For this encounter, where you see the word “Randomly” roll 1d4 and refer to the combat map to
determine the map edge.
Monsters may be spawned anywhere within 4 inches of that edge.

Before combat: each player Randomly spawns 2x Preycatchers.

Round 1: each player Randomly spawns 2x Manhounds.

Round 2: each player Randomly spawns 2x Preycatchers.

Round 3: each player Randomly spawns 1x Offalscryer.

Round 4+: each player Randomly spawns units cycling through Rounds 1 - 3 again.

Game Ends: ,

  
 

The game ends on or after Round 6, when all warband members escape or die.
If any warband members do not escape by round 9, those remaining members automatically die and.
the game ends.
