## 2. ⚠️ THE SUPPLY IS FINITE — and the party ended it without knowing

**This is the spine of the whole thread.**

**There is no more blood coming. There will never be any more.**

The harvest ran for roughly **seven weeks** — late June to mid-August 1126 — and it stopped because **the party walked onto that beach.** Four robed men on the sand, a ship standing off, and a unit of branded strangers coming out of the treeline. The cult did not lose a battle; **it lost its privacy**, and a secret operation that has survived thirty-eight years by being boring does not stay in a place where armed men now know the way down.

**So they finished early and moved her.** Her body is not under Dol any more — the party watched it disappear — and **she is in the Prison Realm now**, which is where every other account places her.

> ### 🔑 What this means
>
> **Everything the cult will ever have is already in casks.**
>
> They cannot harvest more. They cannot find another god weak enough to catch. **The fuel for the Hecatomb is a fixed quantity sitting in somebody's warehouse, and every cask the party destroys is a cask that does not exist for the finale.**

**And the party thinks Chapter 2 was a failure.** They let the ship go. They have carried that for two and a half months.

> ⚠️ **They were the reason the harvest stopped.** Do not tell them. **Let them work it out from the records** — a run of consignments that goes weekly, weekly, weekly, and then stops dead in the third week of August and never resumes. **Let a player put the date together.**

## 3. 📅 The chronology, fixed

**The review flagged a real problem: 666 days after an early-spring festival does not land in late June. Here is the one-sentence repair.**

> **The 666 days did not start when they took her. They started when the binding was finished.**

The cult took her at **Vasant Panchami, spring 1124.** Chaining a god is not a night's work — it took them the better part of a year to build and consecrate it. **The count began the day the last chain closed**, and it ran out in **late June 1126.**

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Spring 1124** | Vasant Panchami on Dol. She manifests in the bonfire smoke to bless her followers. **The cult is waiting.** Two dozen worshippers killed. |
| **1124 → mid-1124** | The binding is built and consecrated in the catacombs. **The count begins.** |
| **~2 years ago** | ⚠️ **The Ashgate purchases surge** — from three or four a year to thirty. *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md`.)* **They began stockpiling the Catalyst the moment they had the mana source.** |
| **Late June 1126** | 666 days out. **The harvest begins.** Vozruk's first team lands the same week, is taken, and four are bled into the same casks. |
| **Late June → 13 Aug** | **Seven weeks of industrial harvesting.** Ships, boats, crews, a schedule. |
| **14 Aug 1126** | ⚠️ **The party arrives.** The last two casks are on the sand. The ship sails. **The operation abandons Dol and moves her.** |
| **18 Aug 1126** | St. Pyotr's. **The party interrupts an attempt** — five cultists dead, the ritual broken, the mist disperses. ⚠️ **The herald escapes out the back of the chapel. The building is not destroyed.** |
| **Oct 1126** | A cask still sitting at Bramblefen, undelivered. |

## 4. How much exists

**Let the records give them a real number. The payoff of a paper chase is a figure.**

> **Four consignments out of the fen between July and the third week of August 1126. Total declared weight: a little over eleven tons.**
>
> **Call it fifty-one casks.** ⚠️ **Two of those are accounted for** — the pair standing in the chapel at St. Pyotr's, spent on the ritual the party broke. *(Whether they were emptied, spoiled, or are still sitting in that building is your call — see Open Decisions.)* **One was at Bramblefen and is now soaked into a floor.**

**Forty-eight casks are unaccounted for.**

| Why this number works | |
|---|---|
| **Too many to burn by hand** | They cannot solve this in an afternoon with a torch. |
| **Few enough to matter** | **Every single cask is 1/48th of the finale.** Destroying nine of them is not flavour, it is a measurable blow, and the party can be told so. |
| **Countable** | A player can track it. ⚠️ **Put the number on a card and cross it off.** |

## 5. 🚢 The route

**The cult does not move its own freight.** It never has. **It pays the tariff like everybody else and ships boring.** *(`The Blood of Saraswati.md` §3.)*

> **Dol Island → by ship up the coast → into the fen by water → BRAMBLEFEN → by cart on the east road → St. Pyotr's.**

### ⚠️ And the fen channel is the Drain

**The smuggler's channel under Bramblefen's wall — the one Erwana escaped through — is not primarily an escape route.**

**It is a loading dock.**

A cask comes off a shallow boat, goes up the channel, through the grate, into the Deeps. **That is what the Deeps was being used for**: three storeys of stone with no windows, in a compound nobody visits, owned by the man who taxes everything that moves.

> 🔑 **Bramblefen was a transfer point in the cult's supply line, and the party has already walked every inch of it.**
>
> ⚠️ **Which means Vaskar knew.** Not what was in the casks — he was paid not to know — but that something came up his channel and went out his gate, regularly, for years. *(See §9: this makes him worth taking alive for a second reason.)*

## 6. Why one cask was still in the Deeps

**Because the compound filled up with guards.**

Ironwake's wagon arrived with the Juggernaut a few weeks before the party did. Twenty men instead of five, a fighting ring going up in the yard, visitors expected, and a crowd. **You do not move a consignment nobody is allowed to look at through a yard full of other people's professionals.**

> **So Vaskar held it, in the dark, in a building he kept locked, and waited for the giant to leave.**
>
> **It was still there when the party took the compound.**

## 7. ⚠️ What the party already diagnosed by accident

**They went into the Deeps because of the feeling coming off it. They said out loud that it was like St. Pyotr's, and less intense.**

**They were exactly right, and they do not know why.**

| St. Pyotr's | The Deeps |
|---|---|
| A circle of a god's blood | **One cask of it** |
| Decades of deliberate, industrial torture by Father Ignatius | **Three centuries of ordinary, careless misery** |
| **The barrier worn through** | **The barrier thinned a little** |

> ### 🔑 They have felt the recipe twice now and described it accurately both times.
>
> **Mana plus suffering, in the same room, thins the world.** That is the Hecatomb in miniature and they walked into it twice.
>
> ⚠️ **If you take §8c of `The Ashgate Purchases.md` — the torture as the drill — this is the proof the party already has in hand.** All they need is somebody to say it out loud, and **Virgil is the man who would.**

**Optional and nasty:** forty gallons of a god's blood went into the floor of the Deeps, in a building soaked in three hundred years of misery, and **nobody lives at Bramblefen any more.** *(Decide whether that matters. It does not have to.)*

## 8. 📜 The paper trail

**Three steps, same shape as the women, and the third is the same raid.**

| Step | What it gives |
|---|---|
| **1 — The Working Ledger** | ⚠️ **Not the barrels themselves** — Vaskar's book is a *slave* ledger. What it holds is **the carriage entries**: dates, a carter's name, and a tariff-mark, because everything that left that fen was taxed. **A mark, not a manifest.** |
| **2 — The carter** | A real man with a real yard in Solteres who hauled casks on the east road and was paid well not to wonder. ⚠️ **He is alive, findable, and frightened**, and he remembers that one load was *warm.* |
| **3 — Ironwake's transport records** | ⭐ **The whole thing.** Weight, date, destination, buyer's name. **Four consignments, eleven tons, and where they were unloaded.** *(Arc Plan §5. This is Chapter 10.)* |

> ⚠️ **The tariff-mark is the join.** The same mark is burnt into the head of the cask in the Deeps *and* written beside the carriage entry for eleven women on the east road. **Two threads, one stamp, and the party can match them without being told.**
>
> *(See `Cobbs Ledger.md` — Ashgate is Cobb's invented filing name and does **not** appear on freight. **The tariff-mark does.** Use the mark, not the name.)*

## 9. What destroying barrels accomplishes

**Tell the party plainly, once somebody works out the supply is finite.** This is the one place in the campaign where progress is arithmetic.

- **48 casks is the entire remaining fuel supply for giving Brudda Death a body in this world.**
- **There is no replacing it.** Saraswati is beyond their reach now, in the Prison Realm, which is the one place they cannot go and come back from — ⚠️ **except with the amulet, which is the finale.**
- **Every cask destroyed weakens the manifestation.** Not abstractly. **Make it mechanical at the finale** — fewer casks, weaker Jailer, shorter duration, fewer lair actions. **Put the conversion on a card.**

> ### ⭐ This is the answer to the Chapter 7 review's complaint
>
> *"Destroying a shipment, exposing a financier, or freeing artists should measurably reduce the cult's capabilities."* **Here is the mechanism. Use it.**

**And it gives Vaskar a second reason to be worth keeping alive:** he is the only living man who knows how many boats came up that channel and when.

## 10. Virgil's sample

⚠️ **Virgil walked out of Bramblefen with a vial of Saraswati's blood and a cutting of the green, and he went to Underwood.**

**He is the only person in the world holding both halves.**

> **This is your Chapter 9 or 10 letter**, and it should arrive at the worst possible moment:
>
> *the blood is not inert — it is still hers, it is still alive in some sense, and the growth from the burn circle responds to it.*
>
> **That is a horrifying and useful thing to learn**, and it is the first time the cosmic plot and the green have touched in the party's own hands.

**Decide what he actually finds** — but the cheapest and best version is: **the blood and the green are the same substance at different stages.** A god bled into the world, and the world growing something out of it.

## 11. 🔗 How this ties to the women

**Three joins, and they are already built.**

### a) One tariff-mark

**§8.** The casks and the carriage of eleven women left the same fen on the same east road under the same stamp. **One raid on Ironwake's records answers both questions**, which is exactly why Chapter 10 is Ironwake.

### b) Two sites, one convergence

> **Mana in casks. Suffering in people.** ⚠️ **The rite needs both in the same place.**
>
> - **The women are held and harvested inland, east of Underwood.** *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md` §5.)*
> - **The casks are stored elsewhere**, somewhere dry and defensible on the east road.
> - **Both move to St. Pyotr's for the summoning itself.**

**That gives the party two separate places they can hurt the cult before the finale** — which is what the Chapter 7 review asked for.

### c) ⚠️ The same two-year clock

**Look at the dates together and they are one decision:**

| ~2 years ago | |
|---|---|
| The binding on Dol is completed and **the 666-day count begins** | They have secured the mana |
| **The Ashgate purchases jump from 3–4 a year to 30** | They begin stockpiling the Catalyst |

> **The cult did not start buying more people because business improved. It started buying more people because it had just chained a god and started a countdown.**
>
> **Two lines in two different books, two years apart, describing one decision.** ⚠️ **The party holds one of those books and Ysabel holds the other.**
