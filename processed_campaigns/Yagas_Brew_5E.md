**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 1–10
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting)

## Overview
A one-shot wilderness adventure: the isolated peat-harvester village of Wrozek stands abandoned, and two "children" beg the party to rescue their families from the witch Baba Yaga. In truth, the "children" are Yaga's shapeshifting domovoy servants luring travelers into her swamp den as her latest sacrifices. Scales via APL: Beginner (1–2), Easy (3–4), Medium (5–7), Hard (8–10).

## Key Locations
- Wrozek Village — abandoned peat-harvester settlement; investigable cabins reveal signs of forced abduction (DC 5/10/15/20 checks).
- The Woods/Swamp — disorienting terrain between Wrozek and Yaga's den; navigated via Survival checks and a random Woods Encounters table (d10).
- Baba Yaga's Grove — the final den: a sunken ancient altar/ruin with Yaga's cauldron at the center, surrounded by waist-high water and 10-ft collapsed-ruin cover.

## Session Flow
1. Opening: party arrives at abandoned Wrozek; two disguised domovoys ("Tomek and Zosia") plead for help finding their "kidnapped" families.
2. Optional: investigate cabins for clues (DC 5/10/15/20 reveals abandonment signs, struggle marks, a diary referencing an ancient shrine).
3. Optional: unmask/confront the domovoys — they flee or bargain, potentially handing over loot or (deceptive) directions.
4. Journey through the woods/swamp: 3 successful Survival checks (2 with the village journal, advantage/disadvantage if a captured domovoy "guides") to reach Yaga's den, triggering Woods Encounters (d10) along the way.
5. Final battle in Baba Yaga's Grove against hags and (on Hard) the animated cauldron.
6. Ending: Yaga dissipates on defeat; party discovers there were no survivors — only bones and hung organs remain.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Domovoy capture reward: a bag of holding (one domovoy) or 10d10 gp (the other), offered as ransom or looted if defeated.
- Screaming Log (Woods Encounter #10, DC 15 Perception): 1d4 of trinkets (50 cp), jewelry (50 sp), gems (50 gp), or potions of healing.
- Final loot roll (4d10 + difficulty bonus): 28+ yields a Hexing Wand* (uncommon); otherwise nothing.

## Secrets & Twists
- The "children" are not human — they are Yaga's shapeshifting domovoys sent specifically to lure adventurers to their doom.
- There are no surviving villagers of Wrozek; the entire rescue premise is a trap, confirmed grimly at the adventure's end (bones and organs only).
- If the party doesn't pursue Yaga on their own, she actively manipulates the forest (closing/opening paths, fog, distant cackling) to herd them toward her den anyway.
- A captured domovoy's "guidance" during travel is a coin flip (1d20 even = truth, odd = lie) — following bad advice gives disadvantage on the Survival check.

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Track successful/failed Survival checks carefully — 3 needed normally (2 with the journal); each roll also triggers a Woods Encounters table entry (d10).
- Baba Yaga's exact statline depends on difficulty tier — confirm before the final fight, especially whether the Cauldron* (Hard tier) is in play as a construct hazard, not Yaga herself.
- Note the Bewitching Lights encounter (#3) can cost the party 2 extra Survival checks on a failed Wisdom save — factor into pacing.
- Cursed Totem encounter (#7) triggers a roll on the long-term madness table — have that table handy.
