## Overview
The town of Matrielle is overrun by a demonic incursion pouring from a burning void rift. The party enters the portal itself to fight through waves of hellspawn on a floating platform in a hellish dimension and defeat the pit lord leading the assault, sealing the rift. Tier III, APL-scaled (Hard 8–10 / Nightmare 11–13 / Torment 14–16 / Hell 17–20). Includes an epic phased boss lair encounter.

## Key Locations
- Matrielle (town square) — site of the void rift, overwhelmed by demons; optional pre-portal defense scene
- The Rift / Floating Platform — hellish demiplane where the boss fight occurs; columns provide cover; falling off (without flight) is fatal
- Rift Portal — remains open during the fight; party can retreat through it if losing and try again later
- Blood Shrine — obsidian shrine with a blood basin; usable for an instant short rest, but each use grants 1 level of Soul Corruption (24 hrs); using it while corrupted triggers Long-Term/Indefinite Madness table rolls

**Pit Lord Boss Phases:**
- Phase I (base): CR 16 / 15,000 XP
- Phase II – Nightmare+: restored to half HP, Flame Geyser damage increases to 21 (6d6)
- Phase II – Torment+: CR 22/41,000 XP, restored to full HP, Assault legendary cost reduced to 4
- Phase III – Hell: CR 25/75,000 XP, restored to half HP, Flame Geyser damage increases to 35 (10d6), pit lord can create a new flame geyser each round via lair action

## Session Flow
1. **Optional lead-in** — party fends off demon waves in Matrielle town square, or is drawn in via survivor/vision hooks.
2. **Opening script** — party steps through the void rift into the hellish demiplane.
3. **The Rift battlefield** — party gets a moment to survey the floating platform, columns, Blood Shrine, and open portal before combat begins.
4. **Waves 1–3** — successive demon waves (hellhounds, hellfiends, hellfiend ascendants), with a minute's respite between waves to reposition or use the Blood Shrine.
5. **Wave 4: Pit Lord boss fight** — multi-phase lair encounter with lair actions, Dreadful Gaze, Flame Geyser hazards, and Assault mobility attacks.
6. **Ending** — pit lord's death destabilizes and seals the rift; party rests and regroups.

## Secrets & Twists
- The rift can only be closed by killing its anchoring entity (the pit lord) — a DC 20 Arcana check reveals this to a PC
- Repeated Blood Shrine use is a tempting but escalating risk/reward (short rest for Soul Corruption, leading to madness effects)
- Pit Lord's "Blood for Blood" trait reduces its own Assault legendary action cost whenever it takes damage — punishes attrition tactics

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Wave spawning should be staged cinematically (crawling over the platform edge, flying down, etc.) — no fixed spawn points required
- Losing the fight isn't a dead end — the open portal allows retreat and a second attempt
- Pit Lord phases are keyed to adventure difficulty tier, not fixed HP thresholds — use the tier appropriate to your party's APL
