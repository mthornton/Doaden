# Doaden — World Rules
### Setting-wide facts that apply everywhere, not to one place or NPC

> Short on purpose. Add a rule here when it is true across the whole setting; put place-specific rules in that place's file. **All of these are DM-settled.**

## Childhood and coming of age
- **A child is anyone under 15.** At **15**, a person is legally an adult and **on their own** — no guardian, no claim on anyone. *(DM, 15 Oct 2026.)*
- **The Church apprentices the children in its care.** Orphans — including a child whose only guardian has become a Penitent — are taken in and **placed into apprenticeships** with licensed trades until they turn 15. An indenture, a fee to the house, a blessing on the paperwork. Nobody thinks it sinister. *(See `Barney Soot.md` §8, `What To Do With Rowan.md` §2.)*

## The Emperor and the throne
- **Emperor Fredric IX** reigns. **Fredric I**, a mortal, took the title of Emperor in **707 PA** at the end of the War of the Five Kingdoms and made it **hereditary.**
- **Church doctrine:** the Fredric line is **the mortal representation of the true Emperor** — a bloodline **chosen by the Emperor to carry on his will until his return.** He will return **by being born into the Fredric line**, and the proof will be that **that Emperor is immortal.** Every reigning Fredric may be the one; only his death proves he wasn't, and then the heir may be.
- **"May he rise again"** — said in every village — is **a prayer that the reigning Fredric is the true Emperor returned, and will prove it by not dying.** Common folk put it simply: *"we pray for his return."*
- Below the throne, **almost nobody in the Church believes there was ever actually an immortal Emperor**; the doctrine holds Church and throne together.
- ⚠️ *DM only:* the line was never chosen — Fredric I seized an opportunity. The heirs **believe their own divinity** and **secretly fund life-extension magic** with Church money. The true Emperor is real, an Old God, and not a Fredric. See `The Emperor.md`.

## Bondage
- **Two doors: Stock and Terms.** Non-humans and half-humans can be owned outright (*Stock*); humans serve a purchased sentence (*Terms*), or are made permanent through the 50-year rule. The Church's ruling against slavery is read as applying only to *people*. Full model in `Solteres.md`.
- **Penitents** owe the Church a term of service, branded with the Mark of Atonement, which is also a leash. *(`The Office of the Lost Flock.md`.)*

## The calendar
- The Church's era is **PA** (Post Apocalypse). **Modern day is 1126 PA.** *(`world_lore/Timeline.md` mixes in "PC" for the same era — use PA.)*
