             THE WORLD OF DOADEN


W    elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted lands and twisted
     monsters, where an ever-creeping affliction corrodes the very essence of
                                     existence.

It is a world of decay and depravity where once-righteous men and heroes of
glorious purpose now struggle to survive as cults and dark gods vie with one
              another to consume the last vestiges of humanity.

                                  CAPRA HUNTERS

     HP: 9

     Morale: 9

     Dagger: 1D4 / Bow: 1D6

     Armor: 1



     Special:

      Hunters do not move towards
     models instead standing back and
     using their bows.
     If they are within 12 inches they do not
     move and attack with their bow as a
     ranged attack.
     If they are over 12 inches, they move
     1D6 towards a model and then attack
     once they are within 12 inches.
