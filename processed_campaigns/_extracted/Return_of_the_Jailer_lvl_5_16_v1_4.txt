Why is Mercereau Doing This?
Mercereau has studied ancient texts for much of
his life. In his youth he worked as a scribe,
transcribing old texts recovered from ancient
archeological sites. Some of these were
blasphemous, containing dark philosophies and
spells that opened Mercereau’s eyes to the true
nature of the world – inherent and inevitable
suffering. They sparked his interest in the Prison
Realm and an insatiable thirst for more
knowledge. The Prison Lord began to whisper
between the realms to Mercereau, promising him
great power and enlightenment if he completes
the ritual and opened a great rift to the Prison
Realm. Now finally, all of Mercereau’s
preparations have come to fruition. He has
constructed the Hecatomb, filled it with the
requisite flesh materials, and need only complete
the five blood sacrifices to thin the veil and
achieve his goals.

Final Investigation Notes
The last notes you should keep in mind while
running this adventure are related to creating a
satisfying story. Investigations rely on a balance of
hiding and revealing evidence. What happens for
each party can vary, so improvisation is required.
Try to make sure your players always have a clue
to follow but allow them to deduce the truth
themselves. You can also make up some of your
own suspects, clues, and red herrings to make the
story your own.

Guiding Players. Your party might feel lost at
times. Provide hints as needed so that they are
engaged and have fun. However, the scenario will
come to its natural end no matter what, so they
will not miss out on the narrative in any case.

Ending the Adventure
After the Jailer’s defeat, the rift that has opened
below New Heldon will start to fade. The dark
energies will slowly recede, signaling the retreat of
the Jailer’s sinister forces. Here are the next steps
and considerations in ending the adventure or
continuing the experience with the Tormentor
Cults.

Clearing the Corruption. With the Jailer's fall, the
ritual effects will start to unravel. Had the party
not defeated the cult, the Prison Realm would
have continued to merge with reality, creating a
bleeding-over of the dimension and allowing
many hellish creatures from that realm to enter
the world—a surely calamitous event.

Debriefing with Aquilon. Upon returning to the
surface, the party should report to Primus
Aquilon. The Penitent Crusade will want a full
account of the events that transpired. Aquilon
should reward the party for their deeds with gold
or a magic item of your choice. He should also
swear the party to secrecy, demanding that they
never speak to anyone about the corruption of
Abbot Mercereau and his followers. Spreading
gossip about the fallibility of the empire’s religious
leaders is not something the Crusade takes lightly,
and if word reaches him of their loose lips, their
tongues will be removed, or worse.
