## Part 1: The Mark of Atonement 
The world has returned to you in fragments. A blur of shouted orders, the clang of iron bars, and the gnawing emptiness in your gut. You have each walked a different path of bad decisions and worse luck, yet all roads have led you here. You are no longer who you were. You are now a Penitent in the service of the Church of Azerai. 
Your first moment of clarity in days finds you in a cavernous, stone-walled dining hall, seated on a rough-hewn bench. The air is thick with the smell of boiled vegetables, damp stone, and unwashed bodies. Dozens of others, a motley collection of races with the same battered and hollow-eyed look you imagine you wear, are bent over wooden bowls. The only sounds are the scrape of spoons and the desperate, rhythmic slurping of the hungry. 
In your hands is a bowl of warm, greyish stew. It’s bland, with only a hint of salt, but it’s the most generous portion of food you’ve seen in weeks, and you devour it with a primal urgency, a deep-seated fear that it might be snatched away at any moment. For the first time in what feels like an eternity, your belly is full. 
The warmth spreads through you, chasing away some of the chill, and you finally look up, taking stock of the three others at your table. They look as lost and confused as you feel. Their faces are a mixture of defiance, fear, and weary resignation. 
Four sit at your table.  All dressed in rags and focused on the food in their bowl.  One man stands out because he can barely still be recognized as a man.  His body is covered in fungus.  Mushroom and odd growths sprout from his skin.  Most disturbing, cones of plant material grow out of the sockets where his eyes once were.  A musty smell surrounds the creature.  Across from him is a large man.  Hard to judge height with him seated, but he is clearly a large muscular young man.  Not unnaturally large, but on the upper range of normal.  Beside the large man is a study in contrast.  An elf sits delicately sipping broth from the bowl.  His pale hairless body is dressed in the same rags and he dirty as everyone else in the room, but his demeanor does give the vibe of desperation as his compatriots.  Beside the fungus infested man sits a skinny man with long dirty blond hair matted with filth.  He licks the last few drops of the stew from his epic Fu Manchu style mustache and turns to the fungus man. 
"Dude, like, are those mushrooms edible?  The soups was great, but, like, I am still hungry."  Luthien asks. 
"Ah, yes.  I specifically engineered the symbiotic fungus to be edible."  exclaims Virgil.  He snaps a piece of mushroom of the side of his leg and drops it in Luthien's bowl.  Luthien, without hesitation, uses the mushroom to sop up the dregs left in the bowl and eats it. 
Before any real conversation can begin, the heavy doors to the hall grind open. A half-dozen guards, clad in steel plate bearing the sigil of Azerai, stride into the room. Their presence instantly chills the atmosphere. 
A voice like grinding stones cuts through the silence. "Jon Lief. Virgil. Luthien. Wolfram. With me. Now." 
The names echo in the hall. A jolt runs through you as you realize he's called the names of the three people at your table. And yours. Was this a deliberate grouping? A stroke of luck? Or is the hand of fate already beginning to show its design? 
The four of you are escorted out, marching between the hulking guards. You're led to a small, soot-stained building from which rhythmic hammering and the roar of a great fire emanate. A blacksmith's forge. Inside, the heat is a physical blow. A thick-armed smith, sweat glistening on his brow, stokes the coals of the forge, his face illuminated by the hellish glow. Three more guards stand waiting. Scattered around the workshop are the tools of the trade: half-finished blades, dented armor, and chains hanging from the walls. 
Without a word, two guards seize Luthien from behind, their grip an iron vise. Another guard pulls his shirt sleeve up exposing the skin of his shoulder. The blacksmith pulls a long iron rod from the roaring fire. Its end glows a malevolent, white-hot orange. 
He presses the brand to Tyler's shoulder. 
The hiss of searing flesh is drowned out by a raw, agonized scream. The smell of burned hair and meat fills the air. Tyler collapses to his knees, trembling, as the guards release him. On his shoulder, raw and blistered, is the Mark of Atonement: a stylized sun with a single tear falling from its center. 
One of the guards turns to the rest of you, his face an impassive mask. "Who's next?" 
Jon Lief recognizes the futility of fighting back.  The last few weeks have shown him that now is not the time to fight.  Better to accept his fate for now.  After all, he has a real battles to fight and will not waste his life in one that is meaningless.  He steps forward:  "Please allow me to go next." 
"Smart for an Elf!" chuckles the guard.  "Every Penitent leaves this smithy with the Mark. Whether you leave on your feet or dragged out as a corpse is the only choice you have left to make.  The smarts ones accept the brand.  The guard lifts his own steel pauldron to show a faded, silvered scar on his shoulder—the same mark. 
 
## Part 2: The Redeemer's Bargain 
Once the brutal ritual is complete and each of you bears the stinging, fresh brand, you are marched to the office of your new master. The man who sits behind a large, oak desk is Vozruk the Redeemer. He is a man of severe features, with piercing eyes and a mouth set in a permanent sneer. He doesn't rise as you enter. 
"Welcome, new lambs for the flock!" Vozruk exclaims, his voice dripping with an irony so thick you could cut it with a knife. He stands and begins to pace, looking you over like a butcher inspecting cattle. "This is a day for celebration! Your worthless, misspent lives now have a purpose. You will help, in your own small, insignificant way, to make the world a better place." 
He stops and leans forward, his hands on his desk. "I have assembled you four into a unit. A team. You will be assigned missions by the Church. You will fight our enemies. You will protect the faithful. You will, in all likelihood, die for our cause." He chuckles, a dry, humorless sound. "I expect the heretics are already shaking in their filthy robes at the thought of you." 
Vozruk straightens up and clasps his hands behind his back. "Let's be clear about the terms of your service. Each of you owes a debt to society and the gods. The Church has calculated that debt at five hundred years of service. A pittance, really." 
He lets that sink in. "Most sinners die with their debt unpaid, their souls damned to eternal torment. But you... you are the lucky ones. We are giving you a chance at redemption. Complete a mission successfully, and I will graciously reduce your sentence. Survive long enough, perform great deeds, and you may one day walk free. A slim chance, I grant you, but a chance nonetheless." 
He waves a dismissive hand. "The guards will now take you to the armory. You'll be issued proper gear. It's old, but it's probably seen more battles than all of you combined. Afterward, you'll be shown to the Penitent barracks. A significant upgrade from your previous accommodations, I assure you." 
"Get settled. Visit the dining hall. Have a good night's rest. Your first mission begins at dawn." With that, Vozruk turns his back on you, already engrossed in a map on his wall, your dismissal complete. 
 
 
## Part 3: The First Mission 
 
The guards lead you to the armory, a squat, one-story building with a thick, iron-banded door. The armory is a single, vast room, a labyrinth formed by towering, unstable stacks of armor, crates of weapons, and mismatched equipment. Shelves line the walls, groaning under the weight of centuries of accumulated gear. 
An ancient halfling named Finnan Quickstep presides over a kingdom of chaos. He comes hobbling from between a narrow passage between piles of shields and breast plates.  A faded, barely-visible Mark of Atonement peeks from the collar of his tunic. 
"I'm coming, I'm coming." he grumbles.  "What do you want?  I got everything.  Tell me what equipment you want and I'll get you outfitted and on your way.  Start with you big boy and be quick about it." 
Wolfram requests a suit of armor, shield, long sword, and set of javelins. 
"Big surprise in that request."  Finnian mumbles under his breath as he hobbles back into the stacks.  Before he is out of sight he stops and turns. "Don't touch anything!" he commands and is then gone. 
 
You hear banging and rattling as Finnian apparently is searching through the piles with frequent curses from the old halfling. 
 
The guards and new penitents look around while they wait. 
 
A guard speaks up, "Finnian is always like this.  I heard he has managed this warehouse since this camp was established and that was over 120 years ago!  The longer we are here the crankier he will get.  Have your request ready and just do as he tells you and we will be done quickly." 
 
"What about that guitar?" ask Luthien as he points to a guitar sitting on top of a seven foot tall pile of backpacks, climbing gear, pots, pans, and other miscellaneous gear.  The guitar looks to be made of bone. 
 
"Don't know" says the guard.  "Ask Finnian for it when he gets back." 
 
After several minutes he returns with the request equipment.  It is not new.  The sword has several deep scratches and the amour is dented, but Wolframs trained eye detects this equipment is fully functional. 
 
"Emperor's left nut, what are you?" Finnian asks of Virgil. 
 
Virgil's face lights up.  "Why I am former member of the Alchemetic Order.  I have dedicated my life to the study of human potential and discovered…"  
 
Finnian cuts him off.  "Don't care.  What equipment do you want?"   
 
"Ah, a cloak and staff will suffice I suppose." states Virgil in a disappointed tone. 
 
"Ok, one moment" says Finnian. 
 
"Hey, little man, like can I have that bitchin' guitar?" asks Luthien with clear excitement. 
 
"Wait your turn." snaps Finnian as he hobbles back into the stacks. 
 
Again, you hear the clattering and curing as Finnian gathers the equipment.  Everybody continues to look around at the thousands of items laying all around.  Luthien casually moves closer to the pile with the guitar.  Before anybody can react, he reaches up and grabs the guitar. The guitar brushes against a pot as Luthien lowers it. 
The avalanche starts slowly.  The pot falls forward pushing a backpack over.  The flap on the backpack opens and several bottle come rolling out into a grappling hook.  The hook, with rope attached, falls to the floor.  The rope pulls down and the entire pile starts to crumble washing forward like a wave on the beach.  The noise is defining. 
Luthien, with the guitar in hand, leaps like a scared cat to the side avoiding the cascade.  A guard that was rushing forward in an attempt to stop the crumble was not as lucky.  Something sharp from the mess cut his arm. 
The guard looks at the blood running down his arm, looks at the mess, then looks back into the stacks with concern.  "Finnian is not going to like this." the guard warns. 
Finnian come out hobbling quickly dragging Virgil's equipment.  "What in the name of the Emperor's left nut is going on?  I gave you one simple instruction.  Don't touch anything!  Who did this?" yelled Finnian as his liver spots turned red. 
Nobody said a word, but everyone looked at Luthien holding the guitar.  Finnian bounded forward and leapt up to land on Luthien's back and started smacking him in the side of the head yelling curses.  A guard gently grabs Finnian from behind puts him back on the floor. 
"Guards, detain this fool." fumes Finnian.  "You will not leave this building until everything is put back where it belongs." 
"Elf, what do you want?"  asks Finnian as he turns to Jon Lief. 
"Bow, arrows, sword, light armor, and a bear fur lined cloak." politely requests Jon Lief. 
Still muttering under his breath about fools that don't do as they are told, Finnian returns to the stacks.  He returns a few moments later with everything requested.  Jon Lief dresses noting how perfectly everything fits. 
Finnian faces Luthien, still being held by the guards.  Luthien starts to speak "My most righteous little dude I would like…" 
"Shut up!" snapped Finnian.  He turns are goes into the stacks returning quickly with heavily used leather armor that is missing pieces, a dagger with a clear crack down the middle, and a short sword made even shorter where the tip was broken off. 
"Everybody else leave except this idiot that will help right the mess he made." 
"When might Luthien rejoin us?" asks Jon Lief? 
Finnian response by throwing a pot.  The guard mutters quietly "Probably not until morning.  Finnian will work him like a dog."

Travel 
 
Dol Island is off the coast of the Southern Dioscese in an area known as the Shattered Coast. 
 
The military camp you are stationed borders Castur River.  You board a 30 foot ship, The Hreowan, that seems more suited for fishing than a military excursion. 
 
The three week trip is uneventful until the night before you reach the island.  A violent storm comes to life making controlled navigation impossible.  The ship smashes into partly submerged reefs sending you and the crew into the water.  You wake on a beach.
