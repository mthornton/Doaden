## Overview
The Tormentor Cults are a dark-fantasy antagonist faction for the World of Doaden, born after "the Cataclysm" from worship of the **Prison Lord**, a malevolent entity dwelling in an extraplanar **Prison Realm**. Cultists believe suffering thins the veil between worlds; through torture, sacrifice, and ritual they aim to force a convergence of Doaden with the Prison Realm. The cult is not a single group but a hydra of regional cells, each commanded by one of six **Harbingers** (once-mortals remade by the Prison Lord), backed by mortal **Adherents**, monstrous **Ascendants**, and alien **Collector Entities** from the Prison Realm itself.

## Cults / Factions (the six Harbingers)
| Harbinger | Theme / Domain | Origin | Goals / Tactics | Stronghold flavor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Court of Three** | Foresight, manipulation, judgment | Three oracle-sisters sacrificed by the cult, fused into one entity after surviving the Prison Realm | Infiltrate as advisors/diplomats; use precognition to set traps/ambushes; leave public gruesome displays | Libraries, temples, tomb-complexes full of arcane wards and puzzles |
| **The Confined** | Brute violence, chaos | Once-human warchief fused with prison bars | Frenzied hit-and-run raids on markets/cities; no subtlety | Torture-fortress with cages, maiming (non-lethal) traps |
| **The Oubliette** | Stealth, assassination, theft | Former master of infiltration bound in an iron gibbet | Assassinations, ambushes, poison, guerrilla tactics; gruesome signature displays | Labyrinth of hidden passages and disorienting traps |
| **The Punishment** | Fire, arson, chaos | (Female Harbinger; burning scimitars) | Erratic, relentless melee; burns populated areas (inns/homes) for distraction and fear | Burned ruin / foundry / permanently-ablaze forest |
| **The Sentence** | Corrupted religion, fanaticism | Former devout priest turned zealot demagogue | Stays back casting support spells/auras; whips cultists into a suicidal fanatical mob | Corrupted cathedral/monastery/temple, blood-stained altars |
| **The Stockade** | Military discipline, sieges | Former Penitent Crusade officer (female) | Defensive warfare, shield-and-mace, pincer movements, disciplined cultist units | Fortified military outpost/castle with kill-zone traps |

Pick one Harbinger to anchor a short adventure, or use all six across a full campaign (see Hooks section).

## Legendary Lairs
- **Herald of Agony** — corrupted churches/temples/sanctuaries; lair actions: Necrotic Pulse (DC16 Con, 4d8 necrotic), Shadowy Chains (DC16 Str, restrain 2).
- **Tribune Carcerus** — captured fortress/military outpost; lair actions: Call Reinforcements (1d4+2 cultists), Activate Traps (DC18 Dex, 4d8 piercing), Commanding Presence (advantage buff).
- **The Caged** — ancient dungeon/forsaken prison; lair actions: Call Chains (DC20 Dex, grapple/restrain), Soul Drain (DC20 Con, 6d8 necrotic, self-heal), Horrifying Wail (DC18 Wis, 5d6 psychic + disadvantage).
- **Old Shacklegut** — corrupted wasteland/derelict arcane ruins; lair actions: Arcane Barrage (DC18 Dex, 60-ft cone, 6d8 fire), Arcane Explosion (DC18 Dex, 20-ft radius, 4d8 force + difficult terrain), Toxic Fumes (DC18 Con, poisoned).
- **Snatcherlord** — dark catacombs/abandoned fortresses; lair actions: Summon Shadows (DC17 Str, restrain), Whispers of Dread (DC17 Wis, frighten), Shadow Step (teleport + 25-ft obscuring cloud).
- **The Jailer** — chamber containing a **Hecatomb** (blood-pit ritual site); lair actions: Call Cultists (6 non-combat sacrificial cultists), Subsume Flesh (kills cultists in the pit to heal 1d12 each). CR 21 base, CR 23 (50,000 XP) with the soul-rending blade variant.
- **Wailing Leviathan** — wastelands/ruins amid mist; lair actions: Fearful Wail (DC24 Wis, 120-ft cone, frighten+disadvantage), Agonizing Wail (DC24 Wis, 60-ft radius, 10d6 psychic).

## Harbinger Brands
Each Harbinger can mark favored lieutenants (a head Demagogue or Bruiser) with a unique **Cult Brand** — a physical mark granting powers echoing that Harbinger's own abilities. Rule of thumb: only one branded cultist per encounter, and only a handful across the Harbinger's whole following.

|---|---|---|

## Hooks & How to Use
- **Adventure Hooks table** — roll or pick a hook to open with; can also build a hook around a specific Harbinger.
- **Scaling structure:**
  - *One-shot* — 1 "ritual sign" clue, ends in a fight vs. a small cultist band/weak fiends.
  - *Standard adventure* — 2–3 signs, ends vs. a Harbinger-backed force.
  - *Campaign* — multiple hooks, one Harbinger per adventure arc.
  - *Torment Unleashed* — full campaign confronting all 6 Harbingers plus the strongest fiends.
- **Ritual Signs** — escalate clues from subtle (disappearances, bad dreams, symbols) to overt as investigation deepens.
- **Progressive Rifts** — cult rituals physically corrupt the region (blight, twisted terrain, black rivers); roll periodically for environmental effects that intensify as the ritual nears completion. Minor rifts = small adventures/weak fiends; major rifts = campaign climax/godlike fiends.
- **Cult Strongholds** — each Harbinger should have a themed stronghold (fortress, asylum, ruins) reflecting their personality; larger campaigns can feature multiple strongholds (recruitment, sacrifice, interdimensional-comms sites) feeding a primary rift site.
- **Final Battle scaling** — low level: a blessed Demagogue; mid: the Harbinger themself or a room of fiends/cultists; high: legendary Prison Realm fiends (Jailer, Wailing Leviathan, etc.).
- **Burdun & the Brute** — drop in as a recurring quest-giver/merchant duo; morally ambiguous, not overtly hostile, good for selling/awarding artifacts.
- Companion published adventures for reference/inspiration: *Whispers of Torment* (Lvl 1–10) and *Return of the Jailer* (Lvl 5–16), both already in raw_campaigns/.

## Things to Remember
- Only **one** branded cultist per encounter, and only a few branded cultists exist per Harbinger's entire following — don't overuse the brand mechanic.
- The Jailer only gets legendary lair benefits while in a chamber containing a **Hecatomb** (ritual blood-pit); without one it's just a very dangerous CR 21/23 monster.
- Demagogues are the cult's "antenna" to the Jailer's will — killing all of them can send a cult cell into catatonic leaderless madness (good plot lever).
- Item tables (Uncommon/Rare/Very Rare/Legendary, pp. ~90–99) are visual/graphic layouts not captured by text extraction — revisit via rasterization if exact item names/effects are needed at the table.
- CR/statblock numbers besides the Jailer (CR 21/23) were not present as extractable text — treat other creatures as "GM discretion, scale to party" until statblocks are visually confirmed.
- Setting tie-in: cult origin traces to "the Cataclysm"; opposed by an in-world faction called the **Penitent Crusade** (several Harbingers are ex-Crusade members) — worth cross-referencing if the Penitent Crusade summary is used in the same campaign.
