# Return of the Jailer
**Source:** raw_campaigns/Return of the Jailer (Lvl 5 - 16) - A 5E Adventure/Return of the Jailer (lvl 5 - 16) v1.4.pdf
**Last processed:** 2026-07-23
**Level range:** 5–16
**System:** D&D 5e (Doaden setting, Bestiarum Miniatures / Tormentor Cults line)

## Overview
The town of New Heldon suffers a string of ritual murders. The party, hired by Penitent Crusade general Primus Aquilon, investigates five sequential sacrifices to unmask the cult (Act 1), then confronts the cult's dark god, the Jailer, as it erupts from a rift in catacombs beneath the ruined Old Heldon Cathedral (Act 2). Secretly, the town's beloved founder and abbot, Mercereau, is the cult leader.

## Key Locations
- **Apostle's End Tavern** — run-down bar; Pavel first approaches party here; Gorab drinks here
- **Town Hall** — Aquilon's commandeered HQ; mission briefing and payment
- **Stonemason Guild Hall** — murder #1 scene; mine's owning guild, closed after cave-in
- **Weaponsmith Shop** — murder #2 scene; Bjorq's shop, ID's evidence
- **Guardsman's Barracks** — murder #3 scene; guard HQ, off-limits except to Crusade
- **Dragon's Eye Mine** — closed mine below the cathedral; cultists caused the "cave-in" to tunnel into the catacombs and harvest miners for the Hecatomb
- **Old Heldon Cathedral** — ruined cliffside cathedral; Mercereau's cover location; site of Act 2's opening battle; houses the unusable Grand Bell
- **Monastery** — Mercereau's residence and monk quarters; Hyle found here
- **Mead Hall** — murder #4 scene (midnight ambush possible)
- **Market** — murder #5 scene (cannot be prevented, happens in daylight)
- **The Descending Passage (Map C)** — cavern ring beneath the cathedral, connects mine tunnel to the catacombs
- **Hecatomb Catacombs (Map D)** — final area; the Hecatomb (blood-filled ritual pit/rift) and Blood Altars

## Session Flow
**Act 1 — The Investigation** (track time: travel 0.5h, NPC talk 0.5h, investigate scene 0.5h; start 9:00 AM)
1. Apostle's End Tavern — Pavel approaches with Aquilon's summons
2. Town Hall — Aquilon briefs the party, offers pay + torture authorization
3. Stonemason's Hall — murder #1 scene, meet Niles
4. Weaponsmith's Shop — murder #2 scene, meet Bjorq
5. Guardsman's Barracks — murder #3 scene, meet Lander
6. Apostle's End Tavern (return) — find Gorab
7. Dragon's Eye Mine — meet Dolan
8. Old Heldon Cathedral — meet Mercereau (unsuspected)
9. Monastery — meet Hyle; recovering his note triggers the countdown to murder #4 (next midnight) and #5 (following noon)
10. Mead Hall — murder #4 (midnight)
11. Market — murder #5 (noon next day) → transitions to Act 2

**Act 2 — The Jailer**
- Ruined Cathedral: wave combat vs. fiends pouring from the rift
- Descending Passage: fiend + cultist ambush in the caverns below
- Hecatomb Catacombs: boss fight vs. the Jailer amid Blood Altars; loot roll (1d20, table not text-extracted) on victory
- Debrief with Aquilon: reward (gold/magic item, DM's choice); party sworn to secrecy about Mercereau's corruption

## Treasure & Rewards
- Investigation fee from Aquilon: 20 gp retainer per PC + 80 gp on completion
- Silver dagger — Gorab's stolen family heirloom (badger crest); returnable, no stated magic properties
- Post-Jailer loot: 1d20 roll against a table (contents not captured by text extraction — check PDF/rasterize page if needed), bonus based on chosen adventure difficulty
- Final reward from Aquilon: gold or a DM-chosen magic item

## Secrets & Twists
- Abbot Mercereau, New Heldon's revered 40-year founder, is the true cult leader
- Pavel is Mercereau's embedded spy inside the Crusade's own investigation
- Hyle (a monk) is a cultist directly involved in all three initial murders
- The Dragon's Eye Mine "cave-in" was a deliberate cultist attack to trap and sacrifice miners and tunnel into the catacombs
- Dolan's "madness" is real — he genuinely heard cultists chanting before the collapse
- The stolen silver dagger is a deliberate frame job against innocent Gorab
- The Jailer cannot be permanently killed, only banished — the cult can always resummon it

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- Aquilon threatens the party to silence about Mercereau's corruption ("or worse") — sets up future leverage/blackmail
- Aquilon hints at future Crusade missions against the Tormentor Cults — sequel hook
- Ties into Bestiarum's larger **Path of Penitence (PoP)** campaign system and the **Tormentor Cults Faction Guide** supplement for expanded content
- If Mercereau is confronted/exposed before Act 1 ends, his followers will still complete the ritual — the story continues regardless
