WOODS ENCOUNTERS TABLE
    d10   Random Encounters

    1     Bramble Brush. A dense thicket of thorny brambles block the party's path, too high to
          climb over and stretching out on both sides as far as the eye can see. The party members
          must navigate through the thorns, requiring a Dexterity saving throw (DC 10). On a failure,
          they take piercing damage from the thorns, scaling with difficulty.
              • Beginner 4 (1d8) piercing damage.
              • Easy 9 (2d8) piercing damage.
              • Medium 13 (3d8) piercing damage.
              • Hard 18 (4d8) piercing damage.

    2     Toxic Blossom. The party stumbles upon a large, camouflaged flower by the side of the
          path. As they approach, the flower bursts open, releasing a cloud of toxic gas that erupts in
          a large area. Each party member must make a DC 5 Constitution saving throw, becoming
          poisoned for 24 hour on a failure.

    3     Bewitching Lights. A billowing fog heavily obscures the area, making it difficult to see
          more than a few feet. Glowing lights flicker in the distance, creating a mesmerizing pattern
          that draws the party's attention. They must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15). On a
          success, they recognize the lights as a distraction. On a failure, they are led astray and
          must succeed on two additional Survival checks to reach Baba Yaga. If they fail once, they
          may choose not to follow the lights in the future, automatically succeeding on the check.

    4     Snakes. The party comes across a nest of snakes, disturbed by their passage.
             • Beginner: 3 poisonous snakes.
             • Easy: 3 giant poisonous snakes.
             • Medium: 1 giant poisonous snake and 2 swarms of poisonous snakes.
             • Hard: 3 giant poisonous snake and 3 swarms of poisonous snakes.

    5     Boggy Quagmire. As the party travels through a mucky section of ground, the earth
          unexpectedly gives way to a deep, watery pit with sinking mud at the bottom, snaring their
          feet and legs, and dragging them down. Party members must make a DC 20 Strength
          saving throw to pull themselves free or become restrained under the water for 6 seconds
          without oxygen. If a PC runs out of oxygen, they begin Suffocating. Every round they may
          reroll the check. PCs that escape may assist ones that are still stuck.

6    Bone Chimes. The party comes across an area strewn with bone chimes hanging from the
     trees and crude wooden dolls scattered on the ground, clear signs of Baba Yaga's
     influence. As they pass by, an unsettling voice murmurs in the wind, shaking the chimes
     striking with a deep sense of psychological horror. The party must make a Wisdom saving
     throw (DC 10). On a failure, they take psychic damage, which scales with difficulty.
          • Beginner 5 (2d4) psychic damage.
          • Easy 9 (2d8) psychic damage.
          • Medium 13 (2d12) psychic damage.
          • Hard 21 (2d20) psychic damage.

7    Cursed Totem. The party stumbles upon a grotesque wicker totem shaped like a twisted
     goat standing on its hind legs with a swollen belly. The creature's eyes glow ominously red
     as the party approaches. Each PC must roll once on the long-term madness table.

8    Twisted Beast. Deformed creatures, their bodies and mind corrupted by dark magic, block
     the party's path. Their eyes glow a baleful red, and its shaking body is a pitiful sight, it has
     become rabid and overcome by some dark curse.
         • Beginner: 2 wolves.
         • Easy: 1 wolf and 2 dire wolves.
         • Medium: 3 giant boars.
         • Hard: 3 giant boars and 1 giant elk.

9    Sunken Ruin. The party encounters an ancient ruin half-sunken in the swamp. It is
     infested with undead creatures that attack as the party passes.
     • Beginner: 1 specter.
     • Easy: 1 shadow and 2 specters.
     • Medium: 2 shadows and 1 ghost.
     • Hard: 1 ghosts and 1 wraith.

10   Screaming Log. The party comes across a rotting log with a grim likeness of a screaming
     face. A DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check reveals something inside its mouth. On a
     success, roll 1d4 and have the party find the result.
