# Denrick

**Type:** NPC — enslaved overseer / "trusty" at **Bramblefen**; a treacherous informant the party will be tempted to trust.
**Function:** offers the party the drainage-tunnel shortcut and camp intel — but has already sold that tunnel to Vaskar (see [[Vaskar the Brand]]) and seeded it with traps, hoping to loot whoever dies in it.

> Reskin of "Grolo" from *Chapter 7: The Chained Coast*. Rename freely.

## The Small Betrayer

Denrick is what most people become in a place like Bramblefen, if they lack the intelligence to become a Vaskar and the heart to become a [[Durgan Half-Ear]]. He is a slave still — collared, owned, disposable — but he bought himself an inch of comfort the only way the pens sell it: with other people. He points out the ones planning to run. He names the weak, the sick, the ones hiding food. In return he gets a dry corner, a fuller bowl, and the overseer's small, poisonous privilege of standing one rung above the wretches he informs on. He tells himself everyone would do the same. He is genuinely puzzled by those who don't.

He is not brave and he is not clever — not really. He is *quick*: quick to read who holds power in a room, quick to grovel, quick to threaten someone weaker, quick to find the angle. He has survived a long time on quickness. He mistakes it for wisdom.

## The Tunnel Con

Denrick knows Bramblefen's bones — the guard rotations, the soft spots in the palisade, and the old **drainage tunnel** that runs from outside the wall to behind the Chainmaster's Hall. It's real, and it's a genuine shortcut. It is also **already spent**: Denrick sold its location to Vaskar for favor, and then, hedging as always, seeded it with traps and stashed a little loot at the far end — betting that sooner or later *someone* desperate would try it and die there, and dead men's purses are the overseer's tip. When the party comes asking for a way in, he sees exactly that someone. He'll trade the route for coin or a promise of freedom, swear it's safe, and send them down a killing chute with a straight face.

## Personality & how to run him

- **Ingratiating and twitchy.** He fawns on whoever's winning and sneers at whoever's losing, and the switch can flip mid-sentence. Lots of "friend," "of course," "anything you need."
- **A liar, but not a perfect one.** He lies smoothly about big things and fumbles small ones — he'll swear the tunnel's clean while not *quite* meeting anyone's eye, or over-sell how much he "hates the masters, truly." A good Insight check (or catching him in a detail) cracks him.
- **Faithless by policy.** Any deal he makes, he's already planning to break if a better one comes along. Trust him and he betrays you at the worst possible moment; corner him and he grovels and sells out whoever's next.
- **The cautionary mirror.** He's the arc's third answer to "how do you survive slavery" — betray everyone, a little at a time. Set beside Durgan and Vaskar, he's the most human and the most pitiable.

## Stat block — Denrick

*Medium humanoid (human), neutral evil — CR 1/2 (100 XP)*

- **AC** 12 (leather) · **HP** 22 (4d8 + 4) · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

- **Skills** Deception +6, Persuasion +4, Insight +2, Stealth +3, Sleight of Hand +3
- **Senses** passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common, Thieves' Cant

**Traits**

- **Silver-Tongued Traitor.** Denrick has advantage on Deception checks made while trading information or striking a bargain he means to break.
- **Knows the Ground.** He knows Bramblefen's layout, guard rotations, and the drainage tunnel intimately — but has already sold the tunnel to Vaskar and trapped it (see the *Chapter 7* Drain traps). Any "safe route" he offers routes through danger he profits from.
- **Coward's Instinct.** The instant a fight turns against him or he drops below half HP, Denrick surrenders, grovels, or bolts — always with a fresh deal on his lips.

**Actions**

- **Cudgel.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d6 bludgeoning. He fights only when cornered, and badly.
- **Light Crossbow.** *Ranged:* +3 to hit, range 80/320. *Hit:* 1d8 + 1 piercing. Prefers to shoot from behind someone else.

**Reaction**

- **Shove the Weak.** When Denrick is targeted by an attack he can see and another creature is within 5 ft., he can shove that creature into the path of the blow; the attacker may redirect the hit to that creature. (A pettier echo of Vaskar's *Someone Else's Skin* — he learned it from the master.) Usable once per round.

## Hooks & how he plays out

- **The tempting shortcut.** Denrick is the party's easy way in — and the trap. Reward players who vet him (Insight, cross-checking with Maeve or another captive) with a chance to catch the con before they're in the chute; punish blind trust with the trapped tunnel.
- **Turnable, briefly.** Scare him badly enough, or outbid Vaskar (real freedom, real coin), and he'll flip — but only until the wind changes. He can hand over true intel (the tunnel traps, Vaskar's routine, where Old Cobb is held) if the party makes betrayal the safer bet for him.
- **His stash.** At the tunnel's far end sits Denrick's hidden purse (~500 gp) — bait for the dead, and a small reward for a party that survives the route or wrings the location out of him.
- **Contrast, deliberately.** If your table meets [[Durgan Half-Ear]] and Denrick in the same session, let the difference land: two enslaved men, two ways to survive. It sharpens what the party is fighting for.
