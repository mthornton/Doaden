## Overview
A trap-gauntlet dungeon crawl through the 10th floor of the Panopticon, a high-security magical prison, where the Iron Juggernaut — a near-invulnerable ogrish giant prisoner — is on the verge of breaking free. The party must navigate a labyrinth of fire traps, a pendulum bridge, and a construct ambush, then subdue or kill the Juggernaut in a boss fight. Difficulty scales by APL: Medium (5-7), Hard (8-10), Nightmare (11-13), Torment (14-16).

## Key Locations
- Floor 10 Landing (1) — massive iron door warded against Transmutation and Conjuration magic (double spell slot cost, 6-second max duration within the ward)
- Flame Corridor (2) — motion-sensor fire trap; DC 15 lever at the hall's end disables it, or dispel the sensor (4th-level spell)
- Watch Post (3) — bandit ambush room; safe short rest afterward; hidden healing potion stash and a Thieves'-Cant blueprint giving advantage on later trap checks
- Pendulum Bridge (4) — three scythed pendulums swing across bridge gaps; hidden wall targets (AC 10, 1 HP) can permanently disable each pendulum for 24 hrs; failed jumps risk a 200-ft chasm fall
- Blazing Pillar Hall (5) — four rotating fire-spewing pillars; no way to disable them here, only pattern-read to reduce required saves
- Control Room (6) — Weeping Maiden ambush; contains the master lever that disables all traps on the floor for 24 hrs (not the maidens themselves) and the vault door to area 7
- Juggernaut Cell (7) — boss arena with 8 stone pillars the Juggernaut can Ram into (destroying the pillar and ending its charge)

## Session Flow
1. Party responds to Warden Ingfar's distress call at the Panopticon; escorted down to floor 10 with a key and torch.
2. Floor 10 Landing — interpret warding glyphs (Arcana DC 15), force the heavy iron door open.
3. Flame Corridor — timed fire trap; spot/disable via lever or dispel the sensor.
4. Watch Post — bandit fight; short rest; find healing potions and blueprint notes (advantage on later trap checks).
5. Pendulum Bridge — timed jump-and-dodge trap across a 200-ft chasm; optionally disable pendulums via hidden targets.
6. Blazing Pillar Hall — no disable option here; cross via DC 10 DEX saves or reduce required saves via pattern analysis (DC 15 INT Investigation x4).
7. Control Room — Weeping Maiden ambush; pull the master lever to disable remaining floor traps; open the vault.
8. Juggernaut Cell — boss fight; use the 8 stone pillars to block his Ram charges.
9. Resolution — Warden Ingfar restores order; party decides the Juggernaut's fate.

## Treasure & Rewards
- Watch Post (Perception DC 10): 1d4 potions of greater healing hidden stash
- Watch Post (Investigation DC 15): blueprints granting advantage on Perception checks in areas 4/6 and Investigation checks in area 5
- Boss Loot (roll 4d10 + difficulty bonus: Medium +0/Hard +1/Nightmare +2/Torment +3): 32-40 Iron Juggernaut Helm (rare); 27-31 Iron Juggernaut Chains (uncommon); 1-26 nothing

## Secrets & Twists
- The bandits attacking the party throughout the level are actively trying to free the Iron Juggernaut, not merely guarding him — some may be positioned as reinforcements in the final boss fight at higher difficulties
- The Iron Juggernaut's iron helm grants full psychic immunity; removing it (contested STR check) exposes his Vulnerability to psychic damage

## Open Threads / Things to Remember
- The Panopticon has 25 floors total, each housing a different single prisoner — potential hook for future one-shots on other floors
- Transmutation/Conjuration ward on the entire floor 10 area: double spell slot cost, spell effects capped at 6 seconds duration
- Flame Corridor damage scales: Medium 2d10, Hard 3d10, Nightmare 4d10, Torment 5d10 fire
- Pendulum Bridge damage scales: Medium 1d8/20ft fall, Hard 2d8/20ft, Nightmare 3d8/40ft, Torment 4d8/40ft
- Blazing Pillar damage scales: Medium 1d6, Hard 2d6, Nightmare 3d6, Torment 4d6 fire
- Iron Juggernaut stat block: AC 16, HP 175, CR 10; Slam melee, Ram (1/round, 8d6 bludgeoning), Throw Boulder (2 legendary actions), Reinvigorate (1 legendary action, +5 HP)
