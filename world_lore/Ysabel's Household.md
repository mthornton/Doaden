# Ysabel's Household
### Five people the party has been responsible for since Chapter 6

> **Moved here from the archived `Chapter 7 DM Notes.md` (Oct 2026)** because all five are still with the party in Chapter 8. For Ysabel herself — her history, her slaveholding, how to run her — see `Solteres.md`.

Per Chapter 6, Ysabel travels with **four male servants who double as guards** and **one lady-in-waiting.** They came through Bramblefen with the party **and no deaths among them were recorded.** They are not fighters.

## Dov Brandt — the household's Terms man

**Thirty-one, from a river town north of Solteres.** Militia spear for a few years, then dice. To clear the debt he tried to rob a merchant on the Solteres road and was so bad at it the merchant's man broke his arm and sat on him until the watch came. Everything his family owned was sold for restitution, **and then five years.**

**He chose to discharge it by selling himself**, as the law allows. Voluntary, lawful, and nobody involved calls it slavery. **He was sold at Bramblefen a little over two years ago and Ysabel bought him. Three years left.** *(Ysabel told the party all of this over breakfast in Session 1, by way of illustration.)*

**What the party does not know yet:**
- **He remembered Old Cobb** — the clerk who asked him how to spell *Brandt* and was decent to him on the worst day of his life. Cobb's body was thrown in the yard and nobody at the table said they buried him. ⚠️ **If anyone did, it was Dov.** Use it or don't.
- **His family is gone** — a mother and younger sister who lost the house when it was sold out from under them. He has not seen them since and does not know where they went. **JonLief is looking for a son; Dov stopped looking.** The two have never compared notes.
- **When the collars came off the thirty, nobody thought to include him** — he was standing with the nobility in a clean coat. He did not raise his hand either.

### ⚠️ Chapter 8 — he transfers with the estate

**If Ysabel is forced into the marriage, Dov's remaining three years pass to her husband**, which means to Ironwake *(`sessions/Chapter 8 DM Notes - Planning.md` §3)*. Nobody has told him. He may have worked it out. **He is one of the costs of letting her fall** — and the one most likely to ask Wolfram for help, because he has been asking Wolfram how to stand since before Bramblefen.

### Ysabel's defence, which is entirely true

She paid a debt that would have ruined him. He eats at her table. Nobody has raised a hand to him. In three years he walks away clear, with money and a reference, which is more than a cell would have given him.

**All of it is true. None of it is the point. Let her be reasonable — it is far more uncomfortable than letting her be cruel.**

## Running the household in Solteres

- **They are Ysabel's credibility.** A noblewoman's word opens doors for the party; her retinue is part of what makes the word believed.
- **If Ysabel is taken** *(Chapter 8 §8 "the turn")*, **Perrin will not stop looking for her** and the others follow Perrin. That is either help or a liability, depending on what the party decides about the wedding.
- **Give them jobs, not initiative.** A PC who assigns a task well should feel it pay off.
- **Their deaths are the party's.** They knew these names before Bramblefen.
