# Chapter 7 : Session 1
Played 8/16/2026
Players for JonLief, Virgil, Wolfram, and Luthien were present at the table.
The session lasted 2.5 hours.

# Around the campfire
The session started by introducing Ysabel's entourage. The DM explained that they have been traveling together for a while now and the party has gotten to know them all a bit. In particular, Wolfram and Dov struck up the beginnings of a friendship. Dov looks up to Wolfram and is inspired to be like him.

Ysabel made her plea as described in the DM notes. The party's stance of not helping remained. Ysabel's appeal — including her claim that Lord Ironwake's champion was easily twice the size of the largest man she had ever seen — did not help her case. When Wolfram incredulously stated, "The Juggernaut is twice the size of me?" Ysabel replied that the champion was three times the size of Wolfram, as Wolfram is not the largest man she has ever seen.

The party discussed the matter. Luthien pointed out that three of the four of them are not really suited to combat in an arena. JonLief asked a clarifying question about the use of magic. Ysabel confirmed that magic can be used.

When asked for details about the trial, Ysabel explained that a trial by combat is not unusual in Solteres. They typically take place in the coliseum on festival days. She explained that throughout the entire festival day there is entertainment in the coliseum. Plays, acrobats, and animal acts are just a few of the variety of possibilities. The trial by combat is the grand finale. A trial is guaranteed to be brutal and to end in death, making it very popular with the crowds.

Ysabel also shared more detail about the root of her conflict with Ironwake. She is a merchant dealing in textiles. Initially she imported exotic fabrics from far-off lands and resold them to dressmakers. Some of the goods she imported were from lands it is illegal to import from. She arranged to have them smuggled into Solteres. This is how she met Ironwake. He controls all goods — in particular illegal goods — that come in and out of Solteres. Ysabel had to pay "taxes" to Ironwake, the criminal overlord, to guarantee the goods arrived safely. She first ran afoul of Ironwake when she tried to have textiles smuggled in without paying him off. Ironwake discovered this, stole a large shipment from her, and warned her that any further disrespect would result in her meeting an unpleasant end. She then opened a factory to have her own fabric woven. She used raw goods she had smuggled in and slave labor purchased from Bramblefen. This increased her profits twofold. But her real success came when she opened another factory to make dresses. Her dresses were of better quality and style than anything else available and overnight became the most sought-after dresses among the noble class along with wealthy merchants. She charged exorbitant prices and went from being a successful and wealthy merchant to one of the wealthiest women in Solteres. Her success led her to think she had enough power to bypass Ironwake. Once again she started to smuggle in goods without paying him off. Once again he discovered this. This was her second offense, and Ironwake came down hard. He had her niece poisoned and set Ysabel up to be found guilty of the murder. (This was described in an earlier document.)

The decision not to stand for Ysabel was the hardest for Wolfram. He is a protector of the weak, and not helping goes against everything he stands for. But he is also a man of duty. He belongs to the Church. They sent him on a mission that did not include fighting a duel to the death for a wealthy merchant he barely knows.

Luthien's decision was easy. He could not care less about Ysabel's fate. JonLief and Virgil were more practical. First, they agreed with Wolfram that they have orders from the Church. Second, none of them stand a chance against The Juggernaut.

The night ended with the party settling on parting ways with Ysabel once they reach Solteres. They will then find the Church headquarters in Solteres and report in.

# Early morning disruption

The party was awakened early in the morning as described in the DM notes. Erwana made her plea for mercy.

Ysabel dismissed Erwana as a subhuman and not worth believing anything she says. She will have Perrin keep her tied up and take her to Solteres. Once there, Ysabel will take her to one of Lord Ironwake's lieutenants and return the escaped slave. It's not enough to get her out of the conflict with Ironwake, but maybe it will help.

JonLief asked Erwana where she came from and she explained she escaped from Bramblefen. With some coaxing, she told the story of Old Cobb giving her the second ledger and arranging her escape via the secret tunnel below the drainage grate.

She explained that Old Cobb told her to run as far from Solteres as she can, then to find a Church and give them the ledger. The Church in Solteres turns a blind eye to slavery and Old Cobb does not trust them. However, he believes in the overall goodness of the Church and thinks that if she can get to another major city and give them the ledger, they can help. She does not know what he thinks they can help with, and she can't read.

Erwana offers the party the ledger if they will give her food and some coin and then let her run. The party agrees and gets the ledger. JonLief examines the ledger and discovers it records the buying and selling of slaves for many years. The last entry in the ledger is from six weeks ago. He also sees an entry from two months ago: an elf woman and her son were brought to Bramblefen. They were named Elizabeth and Rowan. These are the names of JonLief's wife and son, and the ages are correct. He asks Erwana if she knows anything. At first she says she does not, then notices the pendant around his neck. She recognizes it as the same design worn by the elf woman. They were in a cell near hers. The woman was very sick and coughing all the time. The guards came and got both of them one day, and she does not know what happened after that.

In the conversation with Erwana, the party learned from her that all the women were sold. All but her, that is. The guards said she smelled so bad that even filth like the buyers would not take her. The buyers were a small group of men that looked like nothing Erwana had seen before. Their bodies were covered in scars, both old and new. They were all wearing dirty hooded cloaks. They bought all the other women, and Erwana heard nothing else after this. It was shortly after the guards took the elf woman and boy. She does not know if the elf woman was part of the sale.

This changed the party's plans. JonLief desperately wants to go to Bramblefen to liberate his wife and child — liberate them if they are still there, and if not, find out where they were taken.

In the exchange between the party and Erwana, Ysabel chimed in a few times. She wanted the ledger. With that, she could blackmail all the politicians, noblemen, and merchants who purchased slaves. Nobody really cared that people bought slaves, but Ysabel felt confident the ledger contained secrets that some important people did not want to get out. Otherwise, why would there be two sets of books? Simple skimming of profits does not explain the secrecy.

Ysabel was dismissive of Erwana several times, explaining that a slave is ignorant and untrustworthy. Subhumans were below humans and were good for nothing more than serving their masters. Slavery also served a greater good when applied to humans who have committed crimes. She pointed out that Dov is her slave for three more years. She gave a brief summary of Dov's background as explained in an earlier document.

The party started working on a plan to break out JonLief's family. To get more information, they talked to Dov. Dov explained that he did volunteer to enter into slavery in lieu of going to prison. He was at Bramblefen for just a few days. It is a rough place where people are treated poorly. As a human, it was not as bad for him as it is for other races. He told them that he only ever saw four or five guards at the compound, and that the guards are more brutes than soldiers — nasty men who enjoy dominating others. However, Vaskar is another matter. Dov never saw the leader of Bramblefen fight, but he looked fierce.

The party discussed the possibility of storming Bramblefen and breaking out JonLief's family. One possibility was to sneak in through the escape tunnel. Erwana says it would be a tight fit for Wolfram, but he would fit.

Ysabel offers another option. She will come with them and present herself as somebody there to purchase slaves. They will all pose as her staff. If they find JonLief's family, she can buy them and they can all leave quietly. If his family is not there, she can help get information. In exchange for this, she wants the ledger.

The party agrees to this plan. They set out immediately and arrive at 5:30 pm. Bramblefen sits in a valley. The party has a good view of the facility. As was described to them already, a 15-foot stone wall surrounds the compound. Four buildings sit inside: a large stone prison; a smaller brick building converted into a prison; a combination blacksmith and armory; and last, a large house where they are told Vaskar lives and has an office.

What is different from what they were told is the number of guards. They can see at least eight guards. There is also a group of slaves being directed to arrange some type of barrier.

The party decides to continue with their plan. Ysabel approaches the guards at the gate and tells them she is there to buy slaves for her factory. The guard initially tells her today is not a good day and to come back tomorrow. She asks why, and they explain that Lord Ironwake sent his champion The Juggernaut to Bramblefen. They are holding a sparring match at sundown to get The Juggernaut warmed up for an upcoming trial by combat. The guards seem very excited to watch. The Juggernaut is going to fight a gnome slave who has been particularly stubborn and prone to fighting back.

Ysabel presses to be let in and the guard agrees. As the party steps into the compound, they see the yard bustling with activity. One drunken guard is beating a slave who is tied to a post. The guard escorting the party says not to worry about it — the slave is a half-orc and can take a beating. It is unlikely to kill him. Other slaves are being instructed to build a temporary ring made of very large sharpened wooden stakes.

The yard is muddy, and the air is humid and heavy with the smell of sweat and fear. It is loud with the shouting of the guards, the hammering of the barrier construction, and the moans of the half-orc being beaten. In this confusion Wolfram takes a second look at one of the slaves. The man seems familiar, but Wolfram can't place him.
