6) Float Town
This unique segment of Siverfell once bustled
with activity. A series of rotten wooden
platforms remain afloat above the black
waters, interconnected by creaky, half-
collapsed walkways. These platforms, topped
with old buildings, once served as the heart of
the town's trade. Merchants hawked their
wares, shouted out prices, and orchestrated
swift exchanges, all while the waters of the
dark ravine lapped gently beneath them.

Ending the Adventure
Whether the curse is lifted or not depends on
the party. If Lilac’s bones are incinerated or
the necromantic magics afflicting them
dispelled, the weight of despair that hangs
over Siverfell begins to lift, replaced by a
sense of peace and closure. The black waters
may recede over time, and the specters that
once haunted the city slowly fade, their
tormented souls finally finding rest. Yet not all
parties must take on such a risky endeavor.
    You can use the adventure hook to
emphasize different sections of the adventure.
If you used the Lost Histories prompt,
collecting the Annals of Siverfell may be the
party’s priority. If you used the Cursed Spirits
prompt, lifting the curse would certainly be the
