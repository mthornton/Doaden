          THE WORLD OF DOADEN



W
             elcome to Doaden, the dark fantasy setting of tainted
             lands and twisted monsters, where an ever-creeping
               affliction corrodes the very essence of existence.


             It is a world of decay and depravity where once-
righteous men and heroes of the glorious purpose now struggle to
survive as cults and dark gods vie with one another to consume
                   the last vestiges of humanity.


It is a world of death, trapped in the fading dusk of a fallen empire.
The heroes that wander these lands are no grand adventurers to
                  be honored in songs of minstrels.
For the heavy deeds and decisions these wanderers undertake
invariably leave them disheartened, broken, nothing more than
     soulless husks and shadows of their once-glorious selves.
Yet as dark as Doaden may be, humanity clings to life with a
     stubborn tenacity, a last flickering flame within the dark.
A last hurrah, a final war cry that shall echo in the black abyss
     of eternity, a momentary victory before the endless night.

     The Black Fortress
        To stem the threat of the Eternal Legions, the
     Penitent Crusade mans the Black Fortress, a large
     bastion with sprawling walls, and heavily defended
     towers and outposts.
         This fortress spans the length of the
     border between the Western Reaches and the
     Ashen Wastes, keeping the evils of the Wastes out.
     Yet the Eternal Legions grow ever stronger, their
     numbers swelling by the day, and the immortal
     warriors rising again and again. It is clear to the
     generals that it will be only a matter of time before
     the Crusade is defeated, and when the day comes,
     there will be nothing left to stop the apocalypse.
        To prevent the spread of fear about the
     inevitability of the end, the operations of the Black
     Fortress, and any information of what lies beyond, is
     kept highly confidential. The general population has
     no idea about what lies beyond the Black Fortress,
     other than a desolate wasteland and the remnants of
     the Old Empire.

Eternal Legion Part II
    This adventure is the first of two parts. In the next
installment, the adventure continues the harrowing
journey through the Ashen Wastes. The party is
caught by the dust storm as they’re on their back
to the Black Fortress. None can survive in such
conditions for long, being exposed to extreme levels
of magical energies that corrode and sicken the
body. Luckily, they stumble upon a Penitent Crusade
Outpost where they take shelter. Yet, the Eternal
Legion army is hot on their heels, and will arrive
at the outpost in two days, giving the party few
precious moments to recover from their wounds and
prepare the outpost for the imminent assault.
