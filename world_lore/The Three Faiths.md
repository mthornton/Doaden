## 1. The shape of it in one paragraph

**Three faiths, one hostage, and a Church that denies all of it.**

**Saraswati** is a prisoner whose congregation does not know it is a congregation. **Adephagia** is a predator who needs no congregation at all. **The Tormentors** are a movement that wants to tear down the only institution holding the world together, and they have been handed a god to burn as fuel.

**Adephagia gave them that god.** She is not their victim and she has not been betrayed. **She is their patron**, for two reasons that both pay her, and she has exactly one limit they do not intend to respect.

## 2. 🎨 Saraswati — the faith that does not know it exists

### What she is

Chained in the **Prison Realm**, and has been for about two years. Not under Dol Island any more — *(see `The Barrel Trail.md` §2)*. She cannot be seen, cannot be named, and cannot ask anyone for anything except in dreams, weakly, usually unnoticed.

### Her mana economy — both halves

**The notes have said appreciation; the DM has said creation. It is both, and they are not equal.**

| | |
|---|---|
| 🖌️ **The artist is the priest** | **Making** something beautiful is an act of devotion, whether or not the maker has ever heard her name. Few people. High intensity. |
| 👥 **The audience is the congregation** | **Genuinely appreciating** great work is the larger and far more numerous act of worship. Thousands of people. Negligible each. ⚠️ **This is the bigger pool by a wide margin.** |
| 🏛️ **The art-items are the temples** | Her last act of real power: brushes, chisels, instruments, pens, scattered. They take someone already good and make them legendary — which produces masterpieces, **and masterpieces last centuries and are admired by thousands.** The trickle never stops and it compounds. |
| 🌸 **Vasant Panchami is the one real festival** | The fifth day of spring. Communal creation at scale. **An annual bump, and the only moment in the year she is worshipped on purpose by people who think it's a folk custom.** |

> 🔑 **So her strategy is: be loved without being known.** Every other old god is starving because nobody remembers their name. **She does not need anybody to remember hers.**

### Why she is happy for her followers to stay ignorant

**Because if the Church noticed, it would close the taps.**

The Church has already strangled Vasant Panchami by inches — *(the "donation of renewal," the banning of the idols; `Saraswati and Adephagia.md`)* — and that was done with no idea she was real. **If the Church learned there was a living entity behind the art, it would ban the festival outright and name appreciation of beauty a heresy**, and she would be dead inside a generation.

⚠️ **This is the sharpest thing about her faith: secrecy is not caution, it is the business model.** A loud follower is worse for her than no follower.

### The followers, in three tiers

| | Who | Count | Knows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The unwitting** | Everyone who makes or loves beautiful things | ⚠️ **Hundreds of thousands** | Nothing. She is a spring festival. |
| **The devout** | `Cult of Saraswati.md` — starving artists plus a few wealthy patrons. **No structure, no meetings, no self-identification as a cult.** Most know two or three others, and only because of who recruited whom. | **Dozens** | That she is real and worth praying to. Mostly not that she is imprisoned. |
| **The few who worked it out** | ⚠️ **Corbin Wainwright.** The two dozen on Dol Island — and they were slaughtered for it. | **A handful** | That she is held in the Prison Realm, that she made items, and that she is begging for help. |

> ⚠️ **Notice what happened to the last group that figured it out.** The unarmed dead in the sunlit cave are the Dol congregation. **Understanding Saraswati has a mortality rate.**

## 3. 🍖 Adephagia — the faith that needs no faith

### What she is

**The only old god with real power left**, because she is the only one who does not depend on being remembered. **Greed, gluttony and lust generate her mana whether anyone names her or not.** As long as people are people, she eats.

Her appearance is monstrous. The folk song about her is sung in taverns as a bawdy joke. ⚠️ **That is her ideal situation** — a dirty song is not worship and the Church does not ban dirty songs.

### Her "cults" are not religious

**She has no congregation and does not want one.** What she has:

- **Gourmand clubs and merchant syndicates** — the wealthy ritually wasting resources.
- **Solitary pacts.** Individuals in positions of power who have privately traded for a life of luxury. ⚠️ **These people do not know each other and there is no network to infiltrate.**
- **Corrupt clergy.** Decades of flattering not-very-pious churchmen as "a wisp of light" praising their wisdom — which is how she throttled Vasant Panchami without ever showing her face.

> 🔑 **She does not recruit. She rewards behaviour people were going to engage in anyway.** Which means **there is no Adephagian cult for the party to find and destroy.** The thing they would have to fight is human appetite.

### ⚠️ Why she is backing the Tormentors — two reasons, both profitable

**1. Spite, and a job she wants finished.** She beat Saraswati over decades of patient political work and finally needed somewhere to put her. **The Tormentors had the Prison Realm.** The price of that bargain was access — and now they are draining her prisoner to fuel their own project, and **she does not mind, because keeping Saraswati bound and bled is exactly her plan.** *(`Saraswati and Adephagia.md`: "a deal was made to have her imprisoned in the Prison Realm.")* **They are executing her strategy for her, for free.**

**2. Chaos feeds her.** And she is almost certainly right. **Frightened, desperate people act on their base natures** — they hoard, they gorge, they take, they grasp at comfort. A society convulsing is a society indulging.

> **So she is not a reluctant ally and she has not been double-crossed.** She is a patron with an excellent return on investment.

### 🔑 But she has one limit, and it is the whole of Act Three

**Adephagia wants a brief Aspect. The Tormentors want convergence.**

| | What they want | What it does for her |
|---|---|---|
| **Adephagia** | **A short, loud manifestation.** The Church exposed, the faithful terrified, the world panicking and indulging. **Then it goes back.** | **A feast.** |
| **The Tormentors** | ⚠️ **To merge the Prison Realm with Doaden. Permanently.** | **Starvation.** A world owned by Brudda Death is a world of orderly, administered suffering — and **there is nothing in a prison for her to eat.** |

> **She financed a fire and they are building a furnace.**
>
> ⚠️ **This is the motive the Chapter 7 review said was missing**, and it means her late-campaign offer to the party is not a reversal of character. It is a patron discovering that her contractors have their own plans. **She has to ask mortals to clean it up, and she will hate that, and she will do it anyway.**

## 4. ⛓️ The Tormentors — the faith that wants the pillar pulled down

### What they believe, in two layers

**Layer one — the metaphysics.** *Suffering thins the veil between worlds.* Through torture, sacrifice and sustained ritual they intend to force **a convergence of Doaden and the Prison Realm.** This is the Harbingers' actual goal and it is not metaphorical. *(`Tormentor_Cults_Faction_Guide.md`.)*

**Layer two — the recruitment argument, and this is what a mortal cell leader will actually say to you:**

> **The Church of Azerai is the pillar that holds this world together. And the Church teaches that things like Brudda Death do not exist.**
>
> **So bring one through, in front of witnesses, and the Church's authority does not erode. It ends — overnight.**
>
> **Everything built on that lie comes down with it. Then something true can be built.**

> 🔑 **Run their leaders as sincere, articulate and wrong.** This is not raving. It is a coherent political argument made by people who have correctly identified that an institution's power rests on a claim that is false.
>
> **And it is the argument most likely to reach this party.** Five branded Penitents who have been told the old gods are myth, who have personally met three of them, and who owe five hundred years to the institution doing the lying. ⚠️ **A Tormentor recruiter would consider them extremely promising.**

### ⚠️ Which means the manifestation has to be SEEN

**This is the operational consequence and it reshapes the finale.**

A summoning in a sealed catacomb under a ruined asylum in a black fog **proves nothing to anybody.** If the point is to expose the Church, the Aspect must appear where it cannot be denied.

> ### 🔑 The Hecatomb is the door, not the stage.
>
> **It comes through at St. Pyotr's — and then it walks.**
>
> An Aspect crossing open country, eating villages, in front of hundreds of witnesses, is the demonstration. The blood-pit is just where it gets in.
>
> **And that gives the finale its real clock.** Not *stop the summoning.* **It is already through, and it is nine miles from Ald's Star.**

*(⚠️ `Campaign Arc Plan.md` §8.2 should be read with this in mind — the objective is still freeing Saraswati from the pit, but the pressure is a thing already loose in the world.)*

### Structure

**A hydra, not an organisation.** Six **Harbingers** — once-mortals remade in the Prison Realm — each commanding regional cells of mortal **Adherents**, with monstrous **Ascendants** and alien **Collector Entities** behind them. ⚠️ **Cells do not coordinate and often do not know of each other.** Killing one changes nothing structural.

**The ones that matter to this campaign:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The cell on Dol Island** | Harvested Saraswati for seven weeks. Industrial, scheduled, unhurried. **The men in hoods with layered scars.** ⚠️ **They are devout and they practise on themselves** — the suffering is the sacrament, and they do not exempt their own flesh from it. |
| **The cell at St. Pyotr's** | Took over an asylum where a priest had spent years thinning the barrier with torture and **did not have to explain the method to anyone.** ⚠️ **The party broke their ritual in Chapter 4 and killed five of them — but the herald escaped and the building still stands.** They moved the work east and left the door where it was. |
| **The Herald** | ⚠️ Runs the holding east of Underwood where the Ashgate women are kept. Arc Plan Chapter 13. *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md` §5.)* |
| **Corbin Wainwright** | **Not one of them.** See §6. |

### Why they buy people

**Because suffering cannot be stockpiled, only sustained.** *(`The Ashgate Purchases.md` §2.)* Thirty-eight years of quiet, well-paid, boring commerce — because the rite needs people who are alive, held, and hurt again tomorrow.

## 5. ⛪ The fourth party — and the joke at the centre of it

**The Church of Azerai is not a faction in this war. It is the battlefield.**

- **It knows the old gods are real.** It has known for centuries and has been deliberately starving them — folding the one true god into every festival, banning the idols, letting each decade take a little more. *(`Old Gods.md`: "The Church is very aware of the situation.")*
- **It teaches the public that they are folklore**, because a myth draws no worship and a myth generates no mana. **The lie is the weapon.**
- ⚠️ **And the lie is the vulnerability.** The Tormentors' entire plan is aimed at the single load-bearing falsehood the Church has to maintain.

> 🔑 **The Church's most effective strategy against the old gods is also the thing that will destroy it.**
>
> It cannot warn anyone. It cannot prepare anyone. **The moment it admits Brudda Death is real in order to fight him, it has already lost what it was protecting.**
>
> ⚠️ **That is the trap Vozruk is standing in, and it is why Chapter 11 matters.**

## 6. 🔗 The overlaps — and the man standing in all three

### Who knows what about whom

| | Knows Saraswati is real | Knows Adephagia is real | Knows the Tormentors exist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unwitting artists & audiences** | No | No | No |
| **The devout (dozens)** | Yes | Mostly no | No |
| **Adephagia's pact-holders** | No, and don't care | **Yes** | ⚠️ Some. The ones financing them. |
| **Tormentor cells** | ⚠️ **Yes** — they are draining her | ⚠️ **Senior cells know they have a patron.** Rank and file do not. | — |
| **The Church** | Knows *an* entity is behind the spring festival | Yes | **Yes, by name — and hunts them** |
| **The party** | Suspects | Has heard the name | ⚠️ **Has fought two cells and does not know they are one movement** |

### ⭐ Corbin Wainwright is the diagram

**One man in all three circles, and he is not a member of any of them.**

- **He is a Saraswati devout** — one of the only people alive who knows she is imprisoned, because she reached him in dreams for years.
- **He murdered Fenna Pembrook for her brush.** Six months of planning, a journey, a break-in, a killing. ⚠️ **He destroys his own best work** so nobody traces the skill. Not even Orla knows.
- **He went to the Tormentors because they are the experts on the Prison Realm**, offered them resources and connections in exchange for freeing her — and **they counter-offered: help us bring Brudda Death through and he will free her too.**
- **He agreed without understanding what he was agreeing to.**

⚠️ **THE BARGAIN IS NOW FLESHED OUT — `Corbin Wainwright and Orla Miller.md` §4.** The old one-line version (*“help us and Brudda Death will free her too”*) was unplayable: no consideration, no enforcement, nothing binding. What actually happens:

- 🔁 **THEY PAY HIM IN ACCESS TO HIS GOD.** Suffering thins the barrier, so the Tormentors can make Saraswati's dream-contact **clear** — a voice in the room instead of a half-remembered flash. **Metered, revocable, and it compounds**, which is the growing fanaticism being fed to him deliberately. **An addiction, not a contract.**
- ⚠⚠ **He does not know what pays for it.** They told him the places are consecrated, or old, or naturally thin. **He has never asked what is in the next room — for two years.** That is a decision he remakes every visit, not ignorance, and **showing him the room is how you break him.**
- 🗣️ **Their argument is TRUE with the motive left out:** she is in the Prison Realm, Brudda Death rules the Prison Realm, **the jailer can open any cell he likes.** ⚠️ **Do not let the party win this on logic. They have to win it on evidence.**
- 💰 **His side of it: THE NAMES.** He paints the wealthy of Solteres and knows whose counting house will guarantee a draft and never ask. ⚠⚠ **CORBIN IS THE TWO-YEAR SURGE IN COBB'S LEDGER** — ~60 of ~200 Ashgate purchases, Symeon Tallow among them. **The party is carrying his work product and has no idea.**
- ⛓️ **He cannot walk away** because the people he named are already dead or collared and stopping means adding it up. **The sunk cost is human.**
- 😶 **And he does not know he is also feeding them VESSEL-HOLDERS** — his researchers hunt extraordinary artists, Ashgate buys extraordinary artists; **same search, run twice, and he hands over the results.**
- 👤 **He was recruited by a client who commissioned a portrait** — weeks of sittings, cultured, pleasant, and knew things about the Prison Realm nobody should. ⚠️ **He was marked exactly the way he now marks other people. His method was taught to him by being used on him.**

⚠⚠ **AND SARASWATI KNEW ABOUT FENNA ALL ALONG.** She is connected to the items; she felt Fenna die and felt the brush change hands, deduced what happened, **and does not care** — the brush is being used, mana is arriving, that is the whole of her interest. 🔑 **She has never mentioned it to him, so Corbin believes he is keeping a secret from his god, and has carried it alone for two years.** **That is the finale's turn lever: you do not persuade Corbin his goddess is self-serving — you tell him she watched.** ⚠️ **She does NOT know about the cult bargain** — she is funding her own destruction through an instrument she has never audited. And **she chose him because he was DESPERATE, not because he was good**: a god with almost no power can only reach someone straining to hear, and the gifted were not listening. **He is not a corruption of her worship. He is its product.**

> 🔑 **He is doing something monstrous for a reason he believes in, and he mirrors the party exactly.** That is why he works as the finale's antagonist and why he has to be **likeable for three chapters first.**
>
> ⚠️ **And note the shape of the trade: the Tormentors are using a Saraswati worshipper to fuel a ritual powered by Saraswati's blood, which Adephagia supplied.** All three faiths in one transaction, and only Adephagia is getting what she wanted.

**None of this needs correcting on screen.** It is what makes Corbin's eventual explanation land.
