# Corbin Wainwright and Orla Miller

Corbin was an aspiring painter.  He trained under local craftsmen, but they were sign makers, not true artists.  Corbin's technical skill grew to a level of competency but not mastery.  His work lacked true inspiration.  He was good enough to make a meager living from painting portraits for wealthy merchants and the like.  Even this was limited to those just wealthy enough to afford a portrait.  The truly wealthy went to artists of much more acclaim than Corbin. 
 
Corbin's frustration and disappointment in himself grew but it did not stop his drive to become great.  He continued to study and practice his craft with little noticeable improvement.  He also researched everything he could about the great artists and their process.  This is how he eventually learned of Saraswati the goddess of the arts.  Like everybody, he knew of her from the annual festival of Vasant Panchami, and, like most people, thought she was just a folklore figure with something to do with Spring.  He learned of her true nature and stories of how she would bless her devout followers with inspiration. 
 
Corbin started to pray to Sara.  He felt reenergized and hopeful.  This showed in his work.  It was not magic or anything divine, just the result of being truly hopeful again.  But, he thought the small improvement in his work was because of Sara which led him to become more devoted.   
 
As his faith grew, Sara started to reach out to him from the Prison Realm.  Mostly to beg for his help, but also to occasionally bless him with a small bit of inspiration.   
 
After a small blessing, any painting he created over the next day was exceptional.  Not masterwork, but at the top end of what he was currently capable of.  This started a self feeding.  Each time he felt her touch he believed more.  As he believed more she could reach him in his dreams easier. 
 
From the beginning he included his girlfriend Orla.  She was an aspiring actress, but roles for women that could not also sing and dance well were limited.  But she had dreams and every now and then got an unpaid roll with small performing troops and this was enough for her.  She earned a livable wage as a server at a tavern.  She was young, pretty, and full of life with a touch a naivete.  The kind of woman men love to tip. 
 
She was very supportive of Corbin.  His stories of Sara and how she was helping him were not believable.  Orla thought it was not divine inspiration but Corbin hard work that was making him successful.  But, she kept those thoughts to herself and supported Corbin by participating in the prayers. 
 
When Corbin told Orla about Sara being trapped in the Prison Realm and needing help Orla began to question Corbin's sanity.  It seemed much too far-fetched.  Still, what harm was there in his fantasies?  His work was getting better.  Sometimes much better.  He got regular work painting portraits and most weeks brought in more money than she did. 
 
He began to focus on the magic items.  First, if he could wield the brush he would have access to wealth that could be used to free Sara.  Also, once freed, the items could be absorbed by Sara making her immediately stronger. 
 
He studied all the renowned painters in the land.  He found three that regularly produced master works and has shot up almost overnight into the spotlight: Orin Longacre, Fenna Pembrook, Gareth Rivers 
 
He visited Orin first requesting to work with the master to learn more.  Orin accepted Corbin into his shop.  Once there, Corbin discovered the Orin was a fraud.  The masterworks were created by a talented young mans in Orin's employ.  
 
Next was Fenna.  He told her one of his wealthy patrons wanted to hire her for a portrait and he was there to negotiate the deal.  This gave Corbin access to the studio.  Corbin found that while she did produce some spectacular works, none of them were to the level he would expect from the brush. 
 
Last he investigated Gareth.  Being the last on his list he felt this had to the correct person.  He tried the same ploy of claiming he was there to negotiate but Gareth was not interested in the commission.  But he was interested in Corbin.  Corbin swallowed his pride and engaged in an affair with Gareth to learn his secret.  Disgusted with himself Corbin did learn that Gareth's success came over night, but his talent did not.  He had many unreleased masterworks in his studio that went back to his youth.  In conversations with Gareth about people that seem to become great out of the blue Gareth mentioned Fenna.  Corbin told Gareth that he met Fenna and while her work is good, it is not that good.  Gareth then told Corbin about the secret stash Fenna has.  He said they are masterwork unlike anything he has seen by any modern artist.  For reasons that are her own, she keeps them private.  Gareth only knew this because he was close friends with Fenna.  Gareth split this secret only because Corbin was his lover. 
 
Corbin now new the truth.  Fenna had the brush but was hiding its true power.  She must have felt there was magic involved and did not want people to know. 
 
Corbin waited six month then planned a robbery.  He traveled to the city of her studio, broken in, murdered her, then took the brush. 
 
Corbin then started creating masterworks of his own.  He also took a lesson from Fenna.  The painting that were too good he destroyed.  Not even Orla knew of those.  He did sell paintings thar were better than his previous works.  With each painting a little better than the last.  This allowed him to show steady growth not a sudden jump in skill. 
 
Still not well known, his reputation is growing.  So is his wealth.  With this wealth he is employing people to research and learn about other artists in search of the other magic items. 
 
He is also getting involved with the occult.  The magic items will not save Sara.  He has to find a way to rescue her from the Prison Realm.   
 
The experts in the Prison Realm is the Tormentor Cults.  A group of heretics that are insane.  He gets close to them and strikes a bargain - he will support the cult with resources and connections in exchange for freeing Sara.  A counter offer is made.  Help the cult and when Brudda Death is freed he will also free Sara. 
 
Corbin agrees not truly understanding the consequence of bringing Brudda Death to our realm.

# 🎭 DM SECTION — How to play Corbin

> **Settled October 2026.** Everything above is the backstory as written. Everything below is how to run him, and it fleshes out the cult bargain, which was previously one sentence.
>
> Cross-reference: `The Three Faiths.md` (he is the diagram), `The Art-Items.md` §3 and §8 (his detection method, what he knows), `Campaign Arc Plan.md` §8.6 (reveal schedule), `Cobbs Ledger.md`, `sessions/Chapter 8 DM Notes - Planning.md`.

---

## 1. 🔑 THE CORE OF HIM

**His ambition came first. The goddess arrived afterwards, as an explanation.**

He was researching *great artists and their process* — looking for the trick — and found a god at the bottom of it. Then he prayed, felt hopeful, and his work improved, and the file is explicit that **this was not divine**: *"just the result of being truly hopeful again. But, he thought the small improvement in his work was because of Sara."*

> **He got better by himself and gave her the credit.** Every single thing he has done since rests on a man who cannot tell his own effort from a gift.

And note the order of his own reasons for wanting the brush: ***first***, wealth and the ability to paint like a master; ***also***, she could absorb it later. **He ranked them, and the one where he becomes great came first.**

⚠️ **He is not a hypocrite and must never be played as one.** He is sincere, hardworking, and spends his money on the mission rather than on himself. **That is what makes him dangerous.** A man who believed less would have done less harm.

---

## 2. 🖼️ APPEARANCE, VOICE, AND THE THREE THINGS THAT DON'T MATCH

**Young. He would be handsome if he were not so thin.**

- **The thinness is a tell, not a look.** He was poor for years and learned not to eat; now he has money and still doesn't. He forgets food when he is working, and he is always working. ⚠️ **He got what he wanted and is visibly not enjoying it** — put that in front of the party early and let them read it as dedication.
- **He speaks like an aristocrat and he is not one.** Self-taught, assembled over years of sitting in rich people's drawing rooms. He copied their vowels the way he copied their faces.
  > 🔑 **He is a superb mimic who has never had a voice of his own — which is exactly the flaw in his painting.** Technically immaculate, nothing underneath. *The same man, in two mediums.*
  >
  > ⚠️ **A performer would hear it.** Luthien has spent his life listening to how people talk for a living. **Give him a passive read on it, unrolled, the first evening: the accent is learned.** Don't explain why.
- ⚠️ **His hands give him away.** Pigment ground into the creases and under the nails; nothing will shift it. **Noble vowels, a tradesman's hands, and a sign-maker's history** — three things that do not match, all visible in one handshake. *(And it rhymes with Hanne Vetter's unmarked hands. Hands are how this campaign tells you what someone really is.)*
- **He is a practised, comfortable liar.** He talked his way into Orin's shop, lied to Fenna about a commission, and went to bed with Gareth to extract a secret. **Three successful cover stories before he ever killed anyone.** A lie costs him nothing and he does not tell when he is in one.

---

## 3. ⚠️ SARASWATI KNEW ABOUT FENNA, AND DOES NOT CARE

**She is connected to the items.** She felt Fenna Pembrook die, she felt the brush change hands, and she worked out what must have happened between those two facts.

**She does not care.** The brush is being used. Work is being made. Mana is arriving. That is the whole of her interest.

> ⚠️⚠️ **AND SHE HAS NEVER MENTIONED IT TO HIM.**
>
> **Corbin believes he is keeping a secret from his god.** He has carried it for two years — the one thing he did that he knows was monstrous, hidden from the only being whose opinion he cares about.
>
> 🔑 **She has known the entire time and said nothing, because it did not matter to her.**

**This is the turn lever for the finale, and it is better than any argument the party could construct.** You do not persuade Corbin that his goddess is self-serving. You cannot — he has heard worse and won. **You tell him she watched.**

*(See `The Three Faiths.md`: Saraswati's goodness is a property of her power source, not her character. Corbin is the proof, and he is made in her image: self-interest performed as devotion, sincerely, by someone who cannot see it from inside.)*

⚠️ **Why she chose him in the first place, which nobody has said out loud:** a god with almost no power can only reach someone straining to hear. **The gifted were not listening** — Gareth had a studio of unreleased masterworks and needed nothing from anyone. **She found the most desperate man available and made him useful.** Corbin is not a corruption of her worship. He is its product.

---

## 4. 🤝 THE BARGAIN — fleshed out

**The old version was unplayable:** *"help us and when Brudda Death is freed he will free Sara too"* costs them nothing, pays out only if they win, and cannot be enforced. **Corbin planned a murder six months out. He would not accept that.** Here is what actually happened.

### 4a. How he was recruited — he was marked the way he now marks

**A wealthy client commissioned a portrait.** Weeks of sittings. Cultured, unhurried, genuinely interested in the work — the first person who had ever talked to Corbin about painting as though it mattered. Over those weeks the conversation drifted, as it does in a room where one man is standing still and the other is concentrating.

**And the client knew things about the Prison Realm that nobody should know.**

> 🔑 **Corbin was found in a parlour, over hours, by somebody being pleasant — which is precisely what Corbin now does to other people.** His method was not invented. **It was taught to him, by being used on him.** The diagram closes on itself.

### 4b. What they give him — ACCESS TO HIS GOD

**This is the fix, and it runs entirely on doctrine you already have.** Suffering thins the barrier. Ignatius spent years thinning it at St. Pyotr's. The rift is why Brudda Death could reach Elizabeth in a dream at all.

> ⚠️ **So the Tormentors can make Saraswati's contact CLEAR.**

For two years Corbin's faith rested on weak flashes he half-interpreted. They took him somewhere thin, and **she came through like a voice in the room.**

| Why this binds him when a promise would not | |
|---|---|
| **It is the one thing in the world he wants** | Not money, not skill. **Her.** |
| **They are the only suppliers** | Nobody else on earth can do this. |
| **It is metered and revocable** | He has to keep earning it. |
| **It compounds** | Every clear contact deepens the faith that makes him cooperate. **This is the growing fanaticism — it is being fed to him on purpose.** |

**It is an addiction, not a contract.** Play it that way: he talks about the visits the way a man talks about a pilgrimage, and he is restless and thin and slightly wrong in the weeks between.

### 4c. ⚠️⚠️ He does not know what pays for it

**They told him the places are consecrated. Or old. Or naturally thin.** He has never asked what is in the next room, and **he has never gone looking.**

> 🔑 **He is one question away from the truth at all times and has chosen, for two years, not to ask it.**
>
> That is not ignorance. **It is a decision he makes again every time, and the party can name it out loud.** *("You have been praying in that building for two years. What's on the other side of the wall, Corbin?")*

**And it keeps him turnable, which the finale needs** *(Arc Plan: handing the brush over freely is the strongest ending)*. **Show him the room and he breaks.** Not the argument — the room.

### 4d. Their argument, which is not a lie

> *"She is imprisoned in the Prison Realm. Brudda Death rules the Prison Realm. **The jailer can open any cell he likes.** He is the only being in existence who could let her out. You cannot free her. He can."*

**Every sentence of that is true.** What is omitted is why he would, and what a world he owns is worth to a goddess of art. **It is a true statement with the motive left out** — the exact register `The Three Faiths.md` sets for them: *sincere, articulate and wrong.* ⚠️ **Do not let the party win this one on logic.** They have to win it on evidence.

### 4e. 💰 What he gives them — THE NAMES

**He paints the wealthy of Solteres. He sits in their houses for hours.** He knows who is in debt, who has appetites, who can be flattered, and whose counting house will guarantee a draft and never ask what it bought.

> ⚠️⚠️ **CORBIN IS THE TWO-YEAR SURGE IN COBB'S LEDGER.**
>
> `Cobbs Ledger.md` records a steady trickle of Ashgate purchases for decades and then **a surge in the last two years** — roughly **60 of about 200 people**, paid with drafts guaranteed by *"real, respectable, named counting houses in Solteres,"* including **Symeon Tallow**, the chandler who *"was told where to send the paper and never asked."*
>
> 🔑 **Corbin found them. His two years in the cult and the ledger's two-year acceleration are the same two years.**
>
> ⚠️ **Which means the party is carrying his work product in their pack right now and has no idea.** No new prop, no new document. **The reveal is already in their hands.**

### 4f. ⛓️ Why he cannot walk away

**Not because the cult threatens him.** Because the people whose names he gave are already dead or in collars, and **he cannot stop without admitting that.** Every day he continues is a day he does not have to add it up.

> **The sunk cost is human, and that is what the party has to break.**

### 4g. 😶 And the thing he does not know he is doing

He pays researchers to find **extraordinary artists**, so he can identify the other vessels. Ashgate buys **extraordinary artists**, because an unwitting devout generates mana.

> ⚠️ **It is the same search, run twice, and he hands them the results.**
>
> **Corbin has been feeding vessel-holders into the cult's hands while believing he was narrowing his own list.** He does not know this. **Nobody does.** *(`The Art-Items.md` §6 — the Tormentors are the largest collectors of Saraswati's vessels in the world and do not know it. He is why the collection accelerated.)*

---

## 5. 🔥 HE DESTROYS HIS OWN BEST WORK, WHICH STARVES HER

He burns the canvases that are too good, to hide the curve in his skill. **Run that against the mana rules.** The vessels exist so that great work gets made **and appreciated**, and appreciation is the only food she has.

> **Every canvas Corbin burns is mana she never receives.** He is deliberately destroying the highest-yield output of one of her six surviving vessels, **to protect his reputation.**

**Add it up and by his own stated goal he is a net negative for Saraswati on every axis:** one murdered priest *(making art is priesthood — **Fenna outranked him**)*, destroyed masterworks, and a bargain that mortgages the world. ⚠️ **None of this needs to be said at the table.** It is arithmetic the players can do once they have the rules.

---

## 6. 💐 ORLA — and why her view of him is clean

**She does not know about Fenna.** She does not know about the destroyed paintings. She does not know what the cult is.

**Corbin curates himself for her, carefully and continuously.** She gets the version that is hopeful, hardworking, devoted and a little mad about a goddess — and that version is *real*, it is just edited.

> 🔑 **She is the only person alive with a clean view of Corbin, and it is clean because he cleaned it.**

**With the mask now canon** *(`The Art-Items.md` §4)*, this gets much worse and much better:

- He gave her the one thing she ever wanted, and **she became extraordinary overnight.**
- That is what finally made her believe — **not an argument, a thing she felt in her own hands.**
- ⚠️ **So she will not name him**, and now there are two reasons: she loves him, **and he is the man who gave away a priceless object rather than sell it, to her, for nothing.** She knows exactly what that was worth.

⚠️ **She is the party's best route to him and the cruellest one.** Everything they tell her about him is true, and all of it will destroy the only unedited good thing in either of their lives.

---

## 7. 🎬 THE THREE-CHAPTER PLAN — warm, useful, and unhurried

Per Arc Plan §8.6: **likeable for three chapters, then let them find out.**

**What he is, genuinely:**

- **The first person in the campaign to treat Luthien as an artist rather than a jester**, and he talks about work the way Luthien thinks about it. That friendship is not a tactic. ⚠️ **It is also the single most dangerous thing in the chapter.**
- Money and connections the party needs. **He buys dinner.** He can get them in front of people.
- He will **help them chase the cult** — for his own reasons, which he does not share.
- He explains the cosmology: mana as an economy rather than a mystery, and he can **date Vasant Panchami**, turning a footnote into a clock. ⚠️ **Which means the villain teaches them the rules of the world, and they will remember whose voice it was.**

**What he withholds:** that he has a vessel. That he murdered for it. That he has researchers hunting the rest. How many exist — ⚠️ **he genuinely does not know** *(`The Art-Items.md` §8)*. That he knows the Tormentors. That he gave them names.

**The guitar.** He goes still, asks pleasantly where it came from, accepts whatever lie he is told while visibly not believing it, and changes the subject. ⚠️ **He cannot be certain** — his only test is a career that outruns a life, and a bone guitar is not proof. **He has never held two vessels at once and so has never learned the second tell.** *(§3 and §1a of `The Art-Items.md`.)* **He will be back.**

---

## 8. 🗡️ HOW TO BREAK HIM — three things, in this order

**He cannot be argued down.** Telling him Saraswati is self-serving in the abstract will fail; he has heard worse and won. **It has to be evidence, and it has to be in this sequence:**

| | Show him | What it does |
|:--:|---|---|
| **1** | **The room next to where he prays.** | He did not know. He chose not to know, which is worse, and he will understand the difference instantly. |
| **2** | **Cobb's Ledger, open, with his guarantors' names in it — and the count.** | He knew he gave names. **He has never seen the number.** Sixty people in two years, written in a dying man's hand. |
| **3** | ⚠️ **That she knew about Fenna the whole time and never said a word.** | **This is the one that kills him.** Two years of carrying it alone, and she had simply filed it. |

> 🎯 **And the payoff is mechanical, not sentimental.** Per `The Art-Items.md` §9: **belief given freely outvalues an object taken.** A turned Corbin handing over the brush produces a stronger Saraswati than his corpse does. **The party can save the villain and it is the optimal play** — let them work that out for themselves with a god screaming in a pit.

⚠️ **What he thinks happens after she is free:** he has not thought it through, and he has an answer anyway — **he will be at her side when she rises.** It is a fantasy, not a plan, and **nobody has ever asked him a follow-up question about it.** *The first person who does should be a player.*

## 9. 🎯 WHY HE WANTS THE PARTY — settled Oct 2026

**The guitar is not a sufficient motive and it is important to understand why.** It explains him watching Luthien. **It does not explain three chapters of dinners, introductions and money.** A man who identified a target, waited six months, travelled to her city, broke in and killed her does not court five armed strangers to acquire an object. **The efficient play is to learn where they sleep.**

### 9a. ⛓️ Why he does not simply rob them

Five armed people in a city is not one woman alone in a studio, and he is a thin portraitist, not a fighter. But the real answer is better:

> ⚠️ **They are branded Penitents, and killing Church property brings the Office of the Lost Flock.** `The First Penitents.md` already has Vaskar terrified of exactly this. **Corbin is clever enough to have worked it out.**
>
> 🔑 **The collar they resent is the thing keeping them alive, and the villain is the one who understands that.**

### 9b. ⚠️⚠️ THE ENGINE: THEY HAVE SEEN HER, AND HE NEVER HAS

**Corbin has spoken with Saraswati in dreams for years. He has never laid eyes on her.**

The party looked through a chipped hole in a stone tomb on Dol Island and saw a four-armed woman in a bloodied white gown, chained, undecayed — **and she opened her eyes and looked back at them.** Wolfram did it in Chapter 2. **Aldous did it five chapters earlier with the first team, and Gimble can corroborate every word.**

`The Three Faiths.md` pins down exactly what Corbin's tier of devout knows: *"that she is held in the Prison Realm, that she made items, and that she is begging for help."* **Nothing about Dol. Nothing about a body in a crypt. Nothing about a wound in her side.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **HIS OWN ALLIES HAVE HIDDEN DOL FROM HIM, DELIBERATELY, BECAUSE THE BARGAIN DIES THE NIGHT HE FINDS OUT.**
>
> 🔑 **So the party carries the single fact that would end his deal, and neither side knows it.**

**For a man whose entire faith rests on unclear flashes he half-interprets, five people who have seen his goddess's face are the most valuable thing in the world.** He will want every detail. He will want it again next week, phrased differently, to check it. He will ask Wolfram and Gimble separately and compare.

> 🖼️ **AND HE WILL PAINT HER FROM THEIR DESCRIPTIONS.** ⚠️ **Put that on the table.** A scene where a stranger reconstructs, stroke by stroke, over an evening, the thing Wolfram has been carrying alone and silent since Chapter 2 — and gets it right, because they are telling him how. **It is the warmest scene Corbin will ever have and it is the most dangerous thing in the chapter.**

### 9c. 🪤 His motive is the mechanism of his own destruction

**He is courting the only people alive who can tell him the truth about his bargain, and he is doing it on purpose, and he brings it entirely on himself.**

⚠️ **But he will NOT break the first time.** Tell him about the hole in her side and the barrels going aboard a ship and he will rationalise. **And he has a genuinely defensible out:** the Tormentors are a hydra of six Harbingers and uncoordinated cells *(`The Three Faiths.md`)*, so *"those were not my people"* is plausible and may even be true.

> 🎬 **Run it as three refusals.** The crack opens in Chapter 8. It does not break until the finale. **In between, the party watches a man refuse the same evidence three times** — and each refusal is a little less convincing than the last, to them and to him.
>
> **This is also what §8 means by breaking him with evidence rather than argument.** By the time it lands he will already have heard it and dismissed it twice, and he will remember doing that.

### 9d. 🔧 The two practical motives underneath

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ **He needs an acquisition team** | He has researchers who can *find* things and **nobody who can take them.** He is a thin painter with money. **Five armed Penitents with a Church writ who kick doors in are precisely the instrument he lacks.** |
| 🕵️ **He cannot audit his own partners** | He bet everything on a cult he met two years ago and has no way to check what they actually do. **The party is an intelligence probe he can aim at his own allies without being seen to aim it.** ⚠️ *This is what Arc Plan §8.6's "for his own reasons, which he does not share" always meant.* |

### 9e. 🤝 Genuine AND tasked, at the same time

**His handler saw five branded Penitents with a five-hundred-year grievance against the Church and told him to work them.** `The Three Faiths.md` wants the recruitment pitch made once, late, by somebody competent, **and it is the strongest argument anyone will ever make to this party.** Corbin is the delivery mechanism.

**And he genuinely likes Luthien.** He is the first person in the campaign to treat the man as an artist rather than a jester, and that is not a tactic.

> 🔑 **He was recruited by someone being sincerely pleasant on assignment. He is now doing that exact job to somebody else, and he cannot tell which part of himself is which.** Neither should the table.

### 9f. 🗣️ HIS PITCH — he admits heresy, in stages

**He needs a cover story for a lot of generosity, and the one he chooses is the truth, released slowly.**

| Step | What he does | What he is watching for |
|:--:|---|---|
| **1** | **Nothing.** Warmth, the guitar question, dinner. He is pleasant and useful and asks for absolutely nothing. | Whether they take the dinner. |
| **2** | *"An interest in the old faiths."* Framed as scholarship — a gentleman's harmless hobby. | Faces. Who tenses. **Who doesn't.** |
| **3** | He says a name out loud. **Saraswati.** | Again. And whether anyone says *we have seen her.* |
| **4** | **The admission** — privately, probably to Luthien first. ⚠️ **And he frames it as the risk it is:** *"I am telling you a thing that could hang me."* | Nothing. By now he has decided. |
| **5** | **The ask.** *"You have seen her. I never have. Tell me."* | |

> ⚠️ **Admitting heresy to Church Penitents is an enormous calculated risk, and it is the most perceptive thing Corbin ever does.** He is betting that **people the Church owns will not run to the Church.** Four of them have been handed five hundred years by that institution. **He has read the room correctly, and it should land** — confessing a hanging offence is the fastest way to make five suspicious people trust you.
>
> 🛡️ **And it is deniable, which is his insurance.** An accusation from a branded criminal against a fashionable portraitist in Solteres goes nowhere, **and he knows it.**

⚠️ **THE REAL TABLE RISK IS WOLFRAM.** Oath of Devotion, and the Duty tenet is *obey those who have just authority over you.* **He is the one who might report Corbin**, and Luthien is the one who won't. **Do not resolve that for them.** *(JonLief is a father looking for his son, not a zealot. Virgil would want the knowledge. Gimble has the least reason of anyone to help the Church.)*

### 9g. 🪑 Use the sittings

**He paints people for a living, which means hours of someone held still while he studies them.** That is how he was recruited, in a parlour, over weeks.

> ⚠️ **He will offer to paint them.** A group portrait, or Luthien alone with the instrument. **It is a recurring scene with a built-in reason for long, unhurried conversation**, it gets him close to the guitar without reaching for it, and it is the same trap, reused, by the man it was used on.

### 9h. 💀 The thing he does not know about his own survival

`The Three Faiths.md`: the **two dozen on Dol Island** worked out she was imprisoned and **were slaughtered for it.** Corbin worked out the same thing and **was recruited.**

> 🔑 **The difference is that he arrived offering money and names instead of a rescue attempt. He is the only person in his category still breathing, and the reason is that he made himself useful to her captors.**
>
> **He does not know that is why. Nobody has ever told him there were others.**

### 9i. ⚠️ What he must not learn in Chapters 8–10

- **That Maelis Vire exists**, or that a dress house in this city got extraordinary overnight. *(`The Art-Items.md` §5.)* **He will get there first, and the party will learn what he did to Fenna Pembrook the hard way.**
- **That Orla's mask is a vessel** — *he already knows, he gave it to her;* ⚠️ **what he must not learn is that the party has worked out the pattern**, because the moment they can identify vessels on sight they are competitors rather than instruments.
