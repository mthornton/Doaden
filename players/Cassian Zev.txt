Cassian Zev has spent his entire life in service to the Church of Azerai, raised on tales of holy warriors whose deeds became legend. From a young age, he trained to join the Church's military forces, and he proved unusually talented with a blade. When magic began to manifest alongside his swordsmanship, Cassian accepted it as another blessing from the Emperor. He has never questioned this explanation; to him, his abilities are simply proof that he has been given the tools to accomplish something extraordinary.

Cassian is confident, determined, and deeply devoted to his faith. He has little doubt that he is destined for greatness, and he can be frustratingly certain of his own judgment. He is not especially interested in winning people over through charm. Instead, he pushes forward with conviction, speaks his mind plainly, and expects others to recognize the value of his plans. He means well, but his certainty can come across as arrogance, especially to those who have little patience for his grand ambitions.

His assignment to the Office of the Lost Flock initially seemed a curious detour from the heroic future he had imagined. Yet serving under the renowned Shepherd Baltus Thole has given him a new opportunity. As an apprentice Shepherd, Cassian has been tasked with accompanying a group of Penitents, helping them when he can, observing their activities, and reporting back to the Church. He sees no contradiction in these duties: the Church has entrusted him with a responsibility, and he intends to carry it out faithfully.

Cassian believes this journey may be the beginning of the deeds that will make his name known. He genuinely wants to do good, but he also wants to be remembered for doing it.
