# Corbin Wainwright and Orla Miller

Corbin was an aspiring painter.  He trained under local craftsmen, but they were sign makers, not true artists.  Corbin's technical skill grew to a level of competency but not mastery.  His work lacked true inspiration.  He was good enough to make a meager living from painting portraits for wealthy merchants and the like.  Even this was limited to those just wealthy enough to afford a portrait.  The truly wealthy went to artists of much more acclaim than Corbin. 
 
Corbin's frustration and disappointment in himself grew but it did not stop his drive to become great.  He continued to study and practice his craft with little noticeable improvement.  He also researched everything he could about the great artists and their process.  This is how he eventually learned of Saraswati the goddess of the arts.  Like everybody, he knew of her from the annual festival of Vasant Panchami, but, like most people, thought she was a folklore figure with something to do with Spring.  He learned of her true nature and stories of how she would bless her devout followers with inspiration. 
 
Corbin started to pray to Sara.  He felt reenergized and hopeful.  This showed in his work.  It was not magic or anything divine, just the result of being truly hopeful again.  But, he thought the small improvement in his work was because of Sara which led him to become more devoted.   
 
As his faith grew, Sara started to reach out to him from the Prison Realm.  Mostly to beg for his help, but also to occasionally bless him with a small bit of inspiration.   
 
After a small blessing, any painting he created over the next day was exceptional.  Not masterwork, but at the top end of what he was currently capable of.  This started a self feeding.  Each time he felt her touch he believed more.  As he believed more she could reach him in his dreams easier. 
 
From the beginning he included his girlfriend Orla.  She was an aspiring actress, but roles for women that could not also sing and dance well were limited.  But she had dreams and every now and then got an unpaid roll with small performing troops and this was enough for her.  She earned a livable wage as a server at a tavern.  She was young, pretty, and full of life with a touch a naivete.  The kind of woman men love to tip. 
 
She was very supportive of Corbin.  His stories of Sara and how she was helping him were not believable.  Orla thought it was not divine inspiration but Corbin hard work that was making him successful.  But, she kept those thoughts to herself and supported Corbin by participating in the prayers. 
 
When Corbin told Orla about Sara being trapped in the Prison Realm and needing help Orla began to question Corbin's sanity.  It seemed much too far-fetched.  Still, what harm was there in his fantasies?  His work was getting better.  Sometimes much better.  He got regular work painting portraits and most weeks brought in more money than she did. 
 
He began to focus on the magic items.  First, if he could wield the brush he would have access to wealth that could be used to free Sara.  Also, once freed, the items could be absorbed by Sara making her immediately stronger. 
 
He studied all the renowned painters in the land.  He found three that regularly produced master works and has shot up almost overnight into the spotlight: Orin Longacre, Fenna Pembrook, Gareth Rivers 
 
He visited Orin first requesting to work with the master to learn more.  Orin accepted Corbin into his shop.  Once there, Corbin discovered the Orin was a fraud.  The masterworks were created by a talented young mans in Orin's employ.  
 
Next was Fenna.  He told her one of his wealthy patrons wanted to hire her for a portrait and he was there to negotiate the deal.  This gave Corbin access to the studio.  Corbin found that while she did produce some spectacular works, none of them were to the level he would expect from the brush. 
 
Last he investigated Gareth.  Being the last on his list he felt this had to the correct person.  He tried the same ploy of claiming he was there to negotiate but Gareth was not interested in the commission.  But he was interested in Corbin.  Corbin swallowed his pride and engaged in an affair with Gareth to learn his secret.  Disgusted with himself Corbin did learn that Gareth's success came over night, but his talent did not.  He had many unreleased masterworks in his studio that went back to his youth.  In conversations with Gareth about people that seem to become great out of the blue Gareth mentioned Fenna.  Corbin told Gareth that he met Fenna and while her work is good, it is not that good.  Gareth then told Corbin about the secret stash Fenna has.  He said they are masterwork unlike anything he has seen by any modern artist.  For reasons that are her own, she keeps them private.  Gareth only knew this because he was close friends with Fenna.  Gareth split this secret only because Corbin was his lover. 
 
Corbin now new the truth.  Fenna had the brush but was hiding its true power.  She must have felt there was magic involved and did not want people to know. 
 
Corbin waited six month then planned a robbery.  Her traveled to the city of her studio, broken in, murdered her, then took the brush. 
 
Corbin then started creating masterworks of his own.  He also took a lesson from Fenna.  The painting that were too good he destroyed.  Not even Orla knew of those.  He did sell paintings thar were better than his previous works.  With each painting a little better than the last.  This allowed him to show steady growth not a sudden jump in skill. 
 
Still not well known, his reputation is growing.  So is his wealth.  With this wealth he is employing people to research and learn about other artists in search of the other magic items. 
 
He is also getting in volved with the occult.  The magic items will not save Sara.  He has to find a way to rescue her from the Prison Realm.   
 
The experts in the Prison Realm is the Tormentor Cults.  A group of heretics that are insane.  He gets close to them and strikes a bargain - he will support the cult with resources and connections in exchange for freeing Sara.  A counter offer is made.  Help the cult and when the Prison Master is freed he will also free Sara. 
 
Corbin agrees not truly understanding the consequence of bringing the Prison Master to our realm.
